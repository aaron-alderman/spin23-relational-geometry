Why a multiplet brings unwanted companions

Buying a complete toolkit gives both the tool you wanted and the tools packed beside it. Unified gauge multiplets behave similarly: symmetry relates desired leptoquark interactions to diquark partners capable of destabilizing the proton. The problem is not merely finding a useful mediator but explaining why its dangerous companions are absent or ineffective.
