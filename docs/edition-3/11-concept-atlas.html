A compact vocabulary

Carrier: the concrete space on which transformations act. Selector: a physical rule that chooses one option from a symmetric family. Domain: the boundary and regularity conditions that make an operator well defined. Residue: the strength with which a mode appears in a response. Anomaly: an obstruction that appears when a classical symmetry is required to survive quantization.
