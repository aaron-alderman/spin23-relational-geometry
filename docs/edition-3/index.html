New physics by preserving distinctions

The next frontier may require more finesse, not only more energy. Preserve the phase, delay, boundary order, memory and cross-channel relation that ordinary measurements erase; then ask whether the recovered record closes under established physics or exposes a reproducible residual.

Authority boundary. This volume is not empirical confirmation, peer review or a derivation of the Standard Model. Admissibility is an executable research method here; its stronger ontological status remains open.
