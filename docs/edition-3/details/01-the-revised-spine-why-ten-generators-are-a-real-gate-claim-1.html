Why ten generators are a real gate

✓Established⌕Auditor

Exact assertion

Four real coordinates do not automatically imply \(\mathfrak{sp}(4,\mathbb R)\). A pair of ordinary oscillators can explore only a three-generator squeezing/rotation subalgebra. Reaching ten requires independently available anisotropies and shears. The corpus therefore treats generator rank as a kill test: if commutators close at three, the model is a lower-rank control, not hidden full Spin(2,3).
