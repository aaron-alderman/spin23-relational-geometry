The authority boundary

This volume reports calculations and constructions internal to declared models. It is not empirical confirmation, peer review, or a derivation of the Standard Model. Every strong sentence carries a nearby statement of what it does not establish.

A book of layers

A ruler can tell you the length of a table without telling you why the table exists. A compass can preserve a direction without choosing which direction should count as north. Much of this programme has the same layered character. It contains exact measuring structures, conditional mechanisms and proposed physical interpretations. The reader should not flatten those layers into one claim.

Each chapter therefore moves in the same order: ordinary picture, defined object, exact result, and authority boundary. A term such as symplectic is introduced only after the familiar idea it formalizes: preserving oriented area in a phase-space plane. A term such as chirality is introduced only after the familiar idea: left- and right-handed patterns cannot always be superposed by rotation.

Four labels used throughout

Established means that a calculation follows from its written assumptions and has an executable or inspectable certificate. Conditional means that a construction works after a named field, coefficient, boundary condition or control is supplied. Externally open means that the missing ingredient is not contained in the current corpus. Internally exhausted means that further rearrangement of the same ingredients no longer addresses the missing datum.

None of these labels means true of nature. Even an exact theorem can describe the wrong physical system. Conversely, a blocked physical programme can contain useful mathematics, simulation tools and experimentally transferable methods.

The route through the book

Chapters 1–3 build the mathematical spine from response measurements and relational loops. Chapters 4–7 ask whether that spine can carry Standard-Model matter, safe interactions and exceptional structure. The ledger then records both positive results and reversals. The frontier chapter ends with concrete reopen conditions rather than an invitation to continue indefinitely.
