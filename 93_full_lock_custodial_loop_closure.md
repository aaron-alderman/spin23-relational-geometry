## 1. Custodial quartic basis

On the enhanced \(SO(3)_L\times SO(3)_R\) locus, define

\[
S=\operatorname{tr}(L^TL),
\qquad
Q=\operatorname{tr}[(L^TL)^2].
\]

The quartic potential is

\[
V_4=\lambda_1S^2+\lambda_2Q.
\]

The determinant term is cubic and the norm term quadratic, so they do not enlarge the quartic invariant basis.

## 2. Exact scalar one-loop contraction

For the nine real components of \(L\), let \(H_V\) be the field-space Hessian of \(V_4\). The scalar one-loop quartic divergence is proportional to \(\operatorname{Tr}(H_V^2)\). Exact polynomial contraction gives

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{Tr}(H_V^2)
={}&\left(
272\lambda_1^2
+224\lambda_1\lambda_2
+48\lambda_2^2
\right)S^2\\
&+\left(
192\lambda_1\lambda_2
+160\lambda_2^2
\right)Q.
\end{aligned}
}
\]

No Jordan-native custodial-breaking invariant is generated by scalar quartic self-interactions when all breaking couplings are initially zero.

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\text{the exact custodial scalar locus is closed under scalar one-loop quartic renormalization.}
}
\]

This is the opposite outcome from the two-column hierarchy, where the shape coupling regenerated the radial invariant. Here the larger symmetry genuinely protects its own coefficient relations within the scalar sector.

## 3. Why the complete theory still breaks the protection

Electroweak gauging uses only the right generator \(T_{R3}\). In the Jordan split \(L=(u,v,a)\),

\[
\|LT_{R3}\|^2=u^2+v^2=B,
\]

not

\[
u^2+v^2+a^2=A+B.
\]

The gauge spurion therefore distinguishes the charged complex triplet from the neutral real triplet. Once \(g_E\ne0\), no exact \(SO(3)_R\) symmetry forbids independent \(A\) and \(B\) counterterms. Yukawa couplings generally distinguish the sectors as well.

The scalar result is consequently necessary but not sufficient. The exact gauge-mass calculation now shows that the vector-loop kernel adds \((2g^2g_E^2+g_E^4)B^2-2g^2g_E^2D\), explicitly leaving the custodial locus whenever \(g_E\ne0\).

## 4. Branch decision

The custodial-completion branch advances from “tree-level tuning with unknown stability” to:

- scalar self-loops: protected by the enhanced symmetry;
- Abelian gauge and Yukawa loops: symmetry-breaking spurions, quantitatively open;
- Jordan determinant-sensitive couplings away from the custodial relations: native sources of splitting.

**Stop-everything positive:** the complete gauge/Yukawa embedding organizes its custodial-breaking spurions so that the induced \(w/k-1\) is finite, predicted and acceptably small.

**Negative:** the required counterterms are independent free tunings at every order or drive vacuum splitting above the permitted range.

**Unsafe shortcut:** infer full technical naturalness from scalar-only closure while omitting hypercharge and Yukawa spurions.
