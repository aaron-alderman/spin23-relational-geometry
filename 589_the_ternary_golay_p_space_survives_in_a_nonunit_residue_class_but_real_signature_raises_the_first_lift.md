# The Ternary Golay \(P\)-Space Survives in a Nonunit Residue Class, but Real Signature Raises the First Lift

**Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Primary sources:** Shiroman Prakash and K. L. Ramya, [“One generation of the Standard Model as a quantum error-correcting code,” arXiv:2610.21879v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21879v1); [GUAVA documentation for the ternary Golay code](https://gap-packages.github.io/guava/doc/chap5_mj.html)  
**Branches:** B03, B04, B07, B09, B10, B11, B12, B13 and B14  
**Claim status:** exact finite Golay reconstruction, exact residue classification, first real-signature obstruction and a sharpened integral target

## Executive result

The global unit-sign no-go does not remove the ternary Golay \(P\)-space. It identifies where that space actually lives.

Let \(C\) be the cyclic ternary Golay code with parameters \([11,6,5]_3\), generated by

\[
g(x)=2+x^2+2x^3+x^4+x^5.
\]

Its dual

\[
S=C^\perp
\]

has parameters \([11,5,6]_3\), is self-orthogonal, and every row has zero coordinate sum. Taking every coefficient residue to be \(-1\) therefore gives the \(P\)-conditions. Logical errors lie in \(S^\perp=C\), and exact enumeration gives

\[
\boxed{d_P(S)=5.}
\]

Thus the finite Golay target survives in the all-negative coefficient residue class.

The naive smallest exact integer coefficients in that class are four \(+2\)'s and seven \(-1\)'s, with sum \(N=1\). They pass every first mod-nine lifting test, but they cannot support a rank-five real isotropic lattice: after removing the positive all-one direction, the weighted form has signature \((3,7)\), whose Witt index is only three.

The first signature-compatible all-negative-residue targets instead have six positive and five negative coefficients. At minimum \(L^1\)-norm they are

\[
\boxed{
(2,2,2,2,2,2,-7,-1,-1,-1,-1)}
\]

or

\[
\boxed{
(2,2,2,2,2,2,-4,-4,-1,-1,-1),}
\]

up to permutation. Both sum to one, every entry is \(-1\pmod3\), and their all-one complements have real signature \((5,5)\).

## 1. Exact finite Golay reconstruction

Using the GUAVA cyclic convention, six shifts of the coefficient vector

\[
(2,0,1,2,1,1)
\]

generate \(C\). Exhausting all \(3^6=729\) words gives the weight distribution

| weight | 0 | 5 | 6 | 8 | 9 | 11 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| words in \(C\) | 1 | 132 | 132 | 330 | 110 | 24 |

The nullspace \(S=C^\perp\) has dimension five and weight distribution

| weight | 0 | 6 | 9 |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| words in \(S\) | 1 | 132 | 110 |

Direct calculation verifies

\[
SS^T=0,
\qquad
S\mathbf1=0.
\]

There are weight-five words in \(C=S^\perp\) with nonzero coordinate sum. One is the cyclic generator itself:

\[
(2,0,1,2,1,1,0,0,0,0,0),
\qquad
\sum_i v_i=1\pmod3.
\]

No nonzero word of \(C\) has weight below five. This proves the \(P\)-distance exactly.

## 2. The complete residue arithmetic

Assume all eleven integer coefficients are nonzero modulo three, and let \(n_+\) count residues \(+1\). Then

\[
\sum_\ell\mu_\ell\equiv 2n_+-11\pmod3.
\]

For exact \(N=+1\),

\[
n_+\in\{0,3,6,9\}.
\]

For exact \(N=-1\),

\[
n_+\in\{2,5,8,11\}.
\]

The earlier \(6/5\) unit-sign classification covered only the \(n_+=6\) member of the first list. The omitted \(n_+=0\) member is exactly the scalar-negative version of the ordinary ternary Golay geometry.

## 3. Why the smallest coefficient lift fails

Write an all-negative-residue coefficient as

\[
\mu_\ell=-1+3k_\ell.
\]

The equation \(\sum\mu_\ell=1\) becomes \(\sum k_\ell=4\). Minimizing \(\sum|\mu_\ell|\) without considering real signature gives

\[
\mu_{\min}=(2,2,2,2,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1,-1)
\]

up to permutation, with \(L^1\)-norm fifteen.

All \(330\) placements of its four positive coefficients survive the linearized lift from mod three to mod nine. The common system has \(55\) lift variables, rank \(20\), and nullity \(35\). So there is no first \(3\)-adic obstruction.

But exact integer \(P\)-conditions live over the reals as well. The diagonal form defined by \(\mu_{\min}\) has signature \((4,7)\). Since

\[
\langle\mathbf1,\mathbf1\rangle_\mu=N=1>0,
\]

its orthogonal complement has signature

\[
(3,7).
\]

A real quadratic space of signature \((3,7)\) has Witt index three. It cannot contain the required rank-five totally isotropic row space. Therefore

\[
\boxed{\text{the four-}2\text{/seven-}(-1)\text{ lift is impossible over }\mathbb R,}
\]

despite its successful mod-nine lifts.

This is a useful warning: \(p\)-adic compatibility does not imply a global integral solution when an Archimedean signature obstruction remains.

## 4. Signature forces a six/five actual sign balance

For \(N=1>0\), a coefficient form of signature \((p,q)\) restricts on \(\mathbf1^\perp\) to signature \((p-1,q)\). A rank-five real isotropic space requires

\[
\min(p-1,q)\ge5.
\]

At length eleven this forces

\[
p=6,
\qquad
q=5.
\]

Thus the residues may all be negative, but the actual integers must have six positive and five negative entries.

Write the six positive coefficients as \(2+3a_i\) and the five negative coefficients as \(-1-3b_j\), with \(a_i,b_j\ge0\). The sum condition is

\[
7+3\left(\sum_i a_i-\sum_j b_j\right)=1,
\]

so

\[
\sum_jb_j-\sum_i a_i=2.
\]

The minimum \(L^1\)-norm occurs at \(\sum a_i=0\), \(\sum b_j=2\). The two partition types of two give precisely the two boxed coefficient multisets in the executive result. Their \(L^1\)-norm is twenty-three.

## 5. Updated lifting problem

The next exact problem is now much smaller and better posed:

1. choose one of the two minimal signature-compatible coefficient multisets;
2. scan its inequivalent placements against the Golay coordinate action;
3. lift the displayed five-dimensional Golay dual from \(\mathbb F_3\) to an integral rank-five isotropic lattice in \(\mathbf1^\perp\);
4. preserve rational distance five;
5. feed any survivor into the Prakash–Ramya doubling theorem.

The equations are

\[
G\mu=0,
\qquad
G\operatorname{diag}(\mu)G^T=0,
\qquad
G\bmod3\sim S.
\]

The real signature gate is now open. The remaining issue is arithmetic existence, not finite-code existence or Archimedean geometry.

## 6. Relation to the broader programme

This branch now exhibits three genuinely different layers:

- the ternary Golay \(P\)-space exists;
- unit-signed integral ancestry is globally impossible;
- nonunit ancestry must pass both local congruence tests and the real signature gate.

That layered structure is close to the programme's relational-carrier intuition. The coefficient vector cannot be only a list of binary chiralities. It must carry magnitude data that reconciles a finite mod-three code with an indefinite real polarization.

Nothing here proves that the magnitude data comes from a Jordan algebra, \(Spin(2,3)\), the Leech lattice or moonshine. It does, however, turn “extra structure is needed” into an exact demand: a successful carrier must select one of a very small set of signature-compatible weighted polarizations and then solve the integral isotropy equations.
