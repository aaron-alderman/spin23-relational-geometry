# Maintenance entry points

Use [managed local qualification](managed-validation/README.md) for repeated assembly, Claimipedia and isolated-bootstrap checks:

```powershell
node tooling/maintenance/managed-validation/run.mjs
```

Its owned scratch space is disposable and receipts have bounded retention. Do not copy a historical dated audit driver merely to obtain a new destination: those drivers preserve whole generated copies and caused cumulative disk growth. Historical reports remain immutable records, not the default operational interface.

Source/history recovery, canonical authority decisions, runtime-cache retirement and research-output regeneration remain separate maintenance tasks. They are not automatically pruned by qualification.
