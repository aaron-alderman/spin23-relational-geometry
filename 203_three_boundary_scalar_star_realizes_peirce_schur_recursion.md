# A Three-Boundary Scalar Star Realizes Peirce–Schur Recursion

**Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Branches:** B01, B02, B04, B07 and B08  
**Claim status:** exact Gaussian boundary-action model; derivation from Standard-Model stress dynamics remains open

## 1. Minimal physical network

Take three observable boundary values \(\phi_i\) joined to one interior scalar \(\chi\). Let the positive link stiffnesses be \(g_i>0\), and let \(m\ge0\) ground the interior node. The quadratic action is

\[
S[\phi,\chi]
=\frac12\sum_{i=1}^3g_i(\phi_i-\chi)^2
+\frac12m\chi^2.
\]

This is a finite-element, spring-network or zero-dimensional Gaussian-field model of a three-boundary Dirichlet problem.

## 2. Block kernel

Write

\[
\phi=(\phi_1,\phi_2,\phi_3)^T,
\qquad
g=(g_1,g_2,g_3)^T.
\]

Then

\[
S
=\frac12
\begin{pmatrix}\phi^T&\chi\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
A&X\\
X^T&C
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}\phi\\\chi\end{pmatrix},
\]

where

\[
A=\operatorname{diag}(g_1,g_2,g_3),
\qquad
X=-g,
\qquad
C=m+g_1+g_2+g_3.
\]

For positive links, \(C>0\) even when \(m=0\). The interior equation is therefore solvable:

\[
\chi_*(\phi)
=\frac{g^T\phi}{C}.
\]

## 3. Exact boundary response

Substituting the interior solution gives

\[
S_{\rm eff}[\phi]
=\frac12\phi^TR_3\phi,
\]

with

\[
\boxed{
R_3
=A-\frac1Cgg^T.
}
\]

This is precisely the Peirce--Schur Jordan triple

\[
R_3
=A-\{X,C^{-1},X\}.
\]

The model therefore realizes the abstract recursion as an ordinary classical boundary response with no group assumed in advance.

## 4. Entries and three relationships

The response entries are

\[
(R_3)_{ii}
=g_i-\frac{g_i^2}{C},
\]

and, for \(i\ne j\),

\[
(R_3)_{ij}
=-\frac{g_i g_j}{C}.
\]

All three pairwise relationships are generated by communication through the same interior carrier. Their loop product is

\[
(R_3)_{12}(R_3)_{23}(R_3)_{31}
=-\frac{g_1^2g_2^2g_3^2}{C^3},
\]

which is real. The reciprocal scalar star creates cubic consistency but no intrinsic complex orientation.

## 5. Determinant

The matrix determinant lemma gives

\[
\det R_3
=g_1g_2g_3
\left(
1-\frac{g_1+g_2+g_3}{C}
\right).
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\det R_3
=
\frac{m,g_1g_2g_3}
{m+g_1+g_2+g_3}.
}
\]

For \(m>0\), the boundary response is positive definite and belongs to the interior of \(H_3(\mathbb R)_+\).

## 6. The ungrounded zero mode

When \(m=0\), the original action is invariant under

\[
\phi_i\mapsto\phi_i+\lambda,
\qquad
\chi\mapsto\chi+\lambda.
\]

The boundary response then obeys

\[
R_3
\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\\1\end{pmatrix}
=0,
\qquad
\det R_3=0.
\]

This is a genuine common-shift or gauge-like zero mode. It does **not** make the interior corner singular: \(C=g_1+g_2+g_3>0\), so the Peirce--Schur elimination remains valid.

The result lies on the boundary of the positive cone with rank two. One may:

- quotient the common-shift direction;
- fix one reference potential;
- add a small grounding \(m>0\);
- retain the zero mode as a physical Goldstone coordinate.

Each choice has a different physical interpretation.

## 7. Further reduction to two ports

For \(m>0\), eliminating one of the three boundary ports from \(R_3\) gives

\[
R_2\in H_2(\mathbb R)_+.
\]

Its determinant satisfies the recursive factorization, and its conformal cone is the \(Sp(4,\mathbb R)\cong Spin_0(2,3)\) terminal carrier.

Here the two retained faces are chosen operationally by which boundary values remain controlled or measured. The model does not claim that nature universally chooses the same pair.

## 8. Stress-response reading

In a continuum field theory, \(g_i\) are replaced by boundary-to-interior Dirichlet-to-Neumann kernels and \(C^{-1}\) by an interior Green function. The same algebraic form persists:

\[
R_{\partial,\rm eff}
=A_{\partial}
-XG_{\rm int}X^T.
\]

For stress or current sources, the entries become tensor-valued or momentum-dependent. Gauge constraints and contact terms must be handled before declaring positivity.

The scalar star is therefore a rigorous prototype of the desired mechanism, not yet a Standard-Model stress calculation.

## 9. Consequences

This model supplies all of the response assumptions used in the conditional triple theorem:

- a real symmetric carrier;

It does not supply a unique universal boundary choice, flavor structure, spinor dynamics or gravity.
