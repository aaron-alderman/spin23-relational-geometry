# WP01 — Initial-tranche adversarial review

Status: **review candidate; not human-admitted**

This review applies four perspectives to O1–O8: internal steelman, rival framework, affected party, and reflexive third order. Its function is subtractive as well as constructive. A recommended disposition is not a Claimipedia admission decision.

## Disposition summary

| Claim | Recommended disposition | Material change |
|---|---|---|
| O1 | Narrow | Use a relational working definition; withdraw the unqualified constitutive claim. |
| O2 | Split | Separate attentional weighting/access from alteration of the experienced affordance field. |
| O3 | Narrow and hold | Restrict to incremental prediction by measured identity centrality; do not infer irrationality or falsity. |
| O4 | Narrow | Replace “does not establish warrant” with “does not entail warrant”; permit defeasible evidential relevance. |
| O5 | Split | Separate correction capacity from the normative judgement that a system is better; replace maximal with bounded corrigibility. |
| O6 | Split and hold | Separate recurrence detection from explanations by convergence, diffusion or shared constraints. |
| O7 | Downgrade | Treat frame transformation as a diagnostic for special pleading, not a definition or proof of moral truth. |
| O8 | Hold | Retain as comparative metaphysical interpretation; block advancement until rival criteria are precommitted. |

No original claim is recommended for unmodified retention. That is the first evidence that the programme can subtract rather than merely assimilate criticism.

## O1 — Orientation is relational

### Internal steelman

The claim captures something lost when orientation is treated as a detachable representation: action possibilities depend on embodiment, environment, learned practice, relationships and norms. It unifies ecological, enactive and cultural observations without reducing orientation to articulated belief.

### Rival-framework review

A sufficiently rich predictive-processing or control-theoretic model may represent body, environment and social norms internally and reproduce the same predictions. “Relational” then marks a useful level of description but does not establish that the relation is metaphysically constitutive.

### Affected-party review

Relational classification can illuminate how hostile environments constrain agency, but it can also let an interpreter redefine a person's orientation from outside and discount their articulated self-understanding.

### Reflexive review

The programme benefits from O1 because nearly every later phenomenon can be redescribed relationally. That breadth creates a self-sealing risk: if no observation distinguishes constitutive relation from rich interaction, the claim becomes an identity marker for the programme rather than a result.

### Recommended revision

Use a methodological definition: orientation is modelled as a temporally extended agent–world–action relation. Treat the stronger constitutive thesis as a separate philosophical hypothesis requiring discriminating consequences.

Disposition: **narrow**.

## O2 — Attention discloses affordances

### Internal steelman

Attention changes salience, perceptual organization, action preparation and what appears practically relevant. “Disclosure” usefully joins experiential and behavioural consequences that a thin spotlight metaphor separates.

### Rival-framework review

Bottom-up salience, expectation, affect, interoception and motor preparation may specify affordances before attention. Observed effects may concern report, confidence or response threshold rather than perceptual availability.

### Affected-party review

The claim can validate differing experienced worlds, but can also pathologize people for “attending wrongly” while ignoring environments designed to capture attention or punish alternative perception.

### Reflexive review

The word “discloses” moves too easily between a modest processing claim and a phenomenological or ontological claim. The programme currently gains explanatory range from that ambiguity.

### Recommended revision

Split into:

- O2a: attention changes weighting, access and action selection among candidate features or affordances;
- O2b: under specified conditions, attention changes the experienced affordance field rather than report alone.

O2a is the lower-risk empirical claim. O2b requires independent perceptual discriminants.

Disposition: **split**.

## O3 — Identity-central anomalies produce nonlinear response

### Internal steelman

Identity-central commitments have wider dependency networks and revision costs. Threshold effects are plausible because a local anomaly can threaten belonging, status, vocation and moral self-conception simultaneously.

### Rival-framework review

Material interest, coalition signalling, source hostility, reputational pressure and prior confidence may explain the same response. Nonlinearity may arise from institutional sanctions rather than identity structure.

### Affected-party review

People resisting genuine coercion or epistemically hostile institutions are especially vulnerable to being diagnosed as identity-defensive. The framework must not presume that calm openness is always safe or rational.

### Reflexive review

The programme itself is becoming identity-bearing. It could interpret forceful criticism as evidence of the critic's threatened orientation while failing to measure its own defensive response.

### Recommended revision

Restrict the claim to incremental prediction: measured identity centrality predicts response magnitude or shape after material stake, coalition pressure, source trust, prior confidence and perceived threat are included. Causal explanation supplies no epistemic verdict on the defended proposition.

Disposition: **narrow and hold for operationalization**.

## O4 — Meaning does not establish warrant

### Internal steelman

Coherent fanaticism, meaningful delusion and mutually incompatible worldviews decisively block entailment from meaningfulness to truth or ethical correctness.

### Rival-framework review

Long-run integration across action, relationships and correction may still be defeasible evidence of world-fit. The original wording risks an unnecessary binary between meaning and evidence.

### Affected-party review

Discounting first-person meaning can restore external clinical, bureaucratic or theological authority over people whose experience has already been treated as epistemically negligible.

### Reflexive review

The programme receives meaning from distinguishing itself from both reductionism and credulity. It must not use the distinction to immunize its preferred external standards from experiential correction.

### Recommended revision

Experienced meaning does not entail empirical truth, moral warrant or metaphysical truth. Particular forms of durable, corrigible integration may nevertheless carry defeasible evidential weight and must be assessed separately.

Disposition: **narrow**.

## O5 — Corrigibility is an orientation quality

### Internal steelman

Fallible agents and institutions need functioning feedback, verification, integration, enactment and retention. This process model locates failure more precisely than generic praise of openness.

### Rival-framework review

Commitment, expertise, confidentiality, ritual continuity and decision deadlines require closure. More correction channels can create paralysis, manipulation, surveillance and permanent contestability.

### Affected-party review

Correction labour and exposure to challenge are unevenly distributed. A system may call itself corrigible while requiring vulnerable participants repeatedly to prove harms to powerful reviewers.

### Reflexive review

If every criticism merely creates another module, distinction or future work package, the programme exhibits expansion rather than corrigibility.

### Recommended revision

Split capacity from value:

- O5a: correction capacity is a describable systems property with identifiable bottlenecks;
- O5b: bounded corrigibility is presumptively valuable when correction channels have standing, verification, proportional cost, closure and retention safeguards.

Disposition: **split**.

## O6 — Myths preserve transformation invariants

### Internal steelman

Recurrent human developmental and coordination problems make cross-cultural recurrence plausible at the level of transformation families rather than identical plots or symbols.

### Rival-framework review

Diffusion, archive selection, translation, colonial collection practices, genre conventions and flexible abstraction can manufacture apparent universals. Generic narrative structure is a demanding null model.

### Affected-party review

Universal comparison can overwrite local interpretation and repeat extractive treatment of living traditions. Members of represented traditions need standing in corpus construction and interpretation.

### Reflexive review

The programme was partly motivated by expecting invariants. Its vocabulary—order, anomaly, sacrifice, renewal—could function as a coding template that guarantees the result.

### Recommended revision

Split occurrence from explanation:

- O6a: preregistered transformation families recur above specified null models in a bounded corpus;
- O6b: competing models—diffusion, convergence under shared constraints and coder-induced abstraction—explain the recurrence to different degrees.

Disposition: **split and hold pending preregistration**.

## O7 — Moral truth has the shape of frame invariance

### Internal steelman

Role reversal, temporal consistency, information improvement and affected-party inclusion expose special pleading and domination concealed by a single perspective.

### Rival-framework review

The transformation set already contains moral commitments. Legitimate partiality, repair and role-specific obligations can be non-invariant. The proposal may redescribe reflective equilibrium without solving moral disagreement.

### Affected-party review

Abstract symmetry can erase history and power. Treating positions as exchangeable may conceal why formally unequal treatment is required for repair or protection.

### Reflexive review

The programme controls the transformation grammar and could manufacture invariance by excluding transformations under which its conclusions fail.

### Recommended revision

Use justified frame transformations as one diagnostic for special pleading and excluded perspectives. Do not define moral truth as invariance and do not treat invariance as sufficient warrant.

Disposition: **downgrade to diagnostic normative proposal**.

## O8 — The Christian Logos interprets the landscape

### Internal steelman

The Logos tradition offers a powerful synthesis of intelligibility, truthful speech, self-giving transformation, personhood and reconciliation. It deserves exploration rather than exclusion for crossing into metaphysics.

### Rival-framework review

Naturalist pragmatism, Buddhist dependent origination, non-Christian theism and other traditions can organize overlapping phenomena. The vocabulary may have been selected under Christian influence, making the later “fit” partly circular.

### Affected-party review

Christian framing may illuminate participants formed by that tradition while marginalizing others or concealing Christian institutional violence. Comparative criteria must not treat Christian categories as neutral.

### Reflexive review

The Logos interpretation can become the programme's final attractor: every empirical lane may be arranged to culminate in it. Blocking early metaphysical advancement is therefore a substantive safeguard, not embarrassment.

### Recommended revision

Retain the Logos as one named comparative interpretation. Precommit comparison criteria and serious rivals before assessment. Permit local explanatory defeat and prohibit mythic recurrence from functioning as proof.

Disposition: **hold**.

## WP01 gate assessment

The AI-authored adversarial pass satisfies the structural requirement for four perspectives and produces material subtraction. It does **not** satisfy the human-disposition requirement. WP01 remains active until a human reviewer accepts, rejects or revises the proposed dispositions.
