# WP04 04.3a blinded power simulation: interpretation

The deterministic simulation confirms that the interaction—not the authority main effect—is the binding design constraint.

At the programme's smallest effect of interest, d = 0.20, even 800 independent networks per cell produces only about .668 power for the authority × reliability interaction. That design already entails 3,200 networks and 25,600 participant places. Reaching 90% would require going beyond the tested grid.

At d = 0.40, the first tested grid point above 90% interaction power is 400 networks per cell: 1,600 networks and 12,800 participant places. Selecting d = 0.40 merely because it is cheaper would violate the predeclared rule against effect-size inflation.

## Disposition

- **Hold:** a confirmatory human interaction study until a multi-site design or defensible external prior makes the network count feasible.
- **Narrow:** a single-site study to estimation, manipulation qualification and variance-component measurement.
- **Retain:** the four-cell authority × reliability mechanism and the separation of convergence from accuracy.
- **Do not start:** appeal and scale-transfer studies until the authority manipulation works.
- **Forbidden:** counting synthetic agents, repeated trials or individual participants as independent networks.

This is negative design evidence with real programme force. It does not show that authority has or lacks the proposed effects; it shows that the proposed small interaction cannot honestly be tested by a modest single-site experiment.
