## Physical and engineering experiments

The gravity question is presently theoretical, but nearby structures are testable.

### Modular spectral emulator

Build a programmable photonic or microwave network whose mode multiplicities realize the first finite coefficients of a selected theta/character function. Perturb boundary conditions through modularly related configurations and test spectral invariance.

- **observable:** resonance frequencies and degeneracies;
- **nuisances:** fabrication disorder, loss, mode mixing and finite-size splitting;
- **decision:** whether modularly related controls preserve the declared spectrum within a preregistered tolerance;
- **payoff:** theoretical engineering and a detector for modular-state design, not a test of EFE.

### Pair-Gram response device

Use two coupled response channels and engineer four discrete correlation strata corresponding to the Leech minimal-pair Gram determinants.

- **observable:** calibrated (2\times2) response matrix and determinant;
- **decision:** whether the same physical device realizes positive-cone transport and the predicted stratum multiplicities;
- **payoff:** tests the physicality of the corpus's strongest lattice--Jordan carrier map.

### Seam/defect experiment

Implement a sign-reversed double and affine boundary twist in a topological, acoustic or photonic network.

- **observable:** defect-localized phase, spectral flow or holonomy;
- **decision:** distinguish a true orientation-sensitive response from relabelling;
- **payoff:** tests whether the extra seam data has operational residue.

The [fully specified first implementation](two-quadrature-seam-experiment.md) uses a complex programmable resonator coupling and IQ/homodyne detection. Its preregistered discriminator is linear odd interference versus quadratic even power under coupling-phase reversal.

The [detector reassignment result](modular-sign-detector-reassignment.md) now gives this referenced protocol priority for a direct \(\chi_5\)-character test. The three-mode ring remains the correct detector for a genuinely continuous intrinsic flux, but the binary modular sign alone reaches only reciprocal \(0/\pi\) sectors and cannot predict its directional odd channel.

None of these experiments tests quantum gravity directly. They determine whether the proposed interface can exist as controlled physics before it is asked to carry gravity.
