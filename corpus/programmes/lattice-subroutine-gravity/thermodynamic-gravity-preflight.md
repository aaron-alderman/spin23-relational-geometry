## The attractive route

Thermodynamic and entanglement-equilibrium derivations show that Einstein's equation can arise when every local causal horizon carries entropy proportional to area and satisfies a local first law. A modular state-counting subroutine therefore looks relevant: it supplies precise degeneracies and entropy.

The entropy is initially the wrong type.

For a chiral \(c=24\) theory, Cardy growth gives

\[
\log d(n)\sim
2\pi\sqrt{\frac{cn}{6}}
=4\pi\sqrt n.
\]

This is global microcanonical entropy at fixed conformal grade. It counts how many states occupy an energy/grade shell of the two-dimensional theory.

The entropy needed for the Einstein equation is attached to a local spacetime cut or causal horizon. It must have the form

\[
S_{\rm geom}=\frac{A}{4G}
\]

with a universal density, a local boost-energy variation and the correct conservation identity.

## Why relabelling does not derive gravity

One can impose

\[
A(n)=4G\log d(n).
\]

But this defines an area variable from the answer. The character then determines only \(A(n)/G\). It determines neither area nor Newton's constant separately, and supplies no spacetime region whose boundary has that area.

## Exact missing interface

The thermodynamic route requires:

1. a local operator or subregion algebra;
2. a state and reduced density operator;
3. vacuum entanglement rather than only global level degeneracy;
4. a map from algebraic cuts to geometric area;
5. a first-law or Clausius identity for all local null directions;
6. universal normalization fixing \(G\);
7. conservation and controlled higher-curvature corrections.

The genus-two sewing data may eventually help with factorization and entanglement between handles. But sewing a worldsheet is not yet partitioning four-dimensional spacetime.

## Consequence

The thermodynamic route remains conceptually plausible but cannot begin from \(J\) alone. The next admissible construction is a net of subregion algebras or tensor-factor/defect model derived from the lattice CFT, followed by an entropy calculation. Only if that entropy admits a geometric area interpretation should the Jacobson/entanglement-equilibrium step be attempted.

This also clarifies the “universe as a computer” picture. A global state counter is not enough. Gravity would require the subroutine to know which information belongs to which local boundary and how information flow changes when that boundary is deformed.
