A propagating excitation needs a kinetic term and a physical inner product. A rest-energy gap additionally needs a nonzero, stable mass operator on that same carrier. This audit asks whether the corpus supplies those objects together, rather than interpreting any mixing matrix or potential curvature as a particle mass.

## 1. What can be derived from two sectors

Suppose orthogonal projectors on a positive Hilbert space satisfy

\[
P_1+P_2=I,\qquad P_1P_2=0,\qquad A=P_1-P_2.
\]

Then \(A^\dagger=A\) and \(A^2=I\). A purely cross-sector Hermitian operator has the form

\[
B=P_1BP_2+P_2BP_1
=\begin{pmatrix}0&M\\M^\dagger&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Consequently

\[
\boxed{\{A,B\}=0},\qquad
B^2=\operatorname{diag}(MM^\dagger,M^\dagger M).
\]

This anticommutator follows from the support condition; Pauli matrices need not be independently postulated. However, identifying \(A\) as the physical propagation operator is another condition. The projectors alone neither select the cross block \(M\) nor require it to be nonzero. Diagonal terms allowed by the action can spoil the pure cross-sector form.

If a one-dimensional transport action actually gives \(H(p)=cpA+B\), with constant \(B\), then

\[
H(p)^2=c^2p^2I+B^2.
\]

For equal finite sector dimensions, a uniform positive rest gap exists precisely when \(M\) is invertible; its value is \(\Delta=\sigma_{\min}(M)\). Different singular values give different rest energies, rather than one common mass. The special factorization \(B=\Delta\beta\), \(\beta^2=I\), requires every singular value to equal \(\Delta\). It is additional structure. In an infinite-dimensional theory, a positive lower singular bound and domains must replace mere finite-dimensional invertibility.

For unequal finite dimensions, the odd block has a kernel of dimension at least \(|\dim P_1-\dim P_2|\). This is exactly the obstruction behind the corpus's [8+7 Yukawa grading and its Clifford extension](../../claims/0300-0399/313_the_yukawa_zero_mode_extends_to_a_massless_dirac_type_cone.md): at least one mode remains massless before additional completion or grading-breaking terms. The internal grading in that claim is not already identified with the physical T1/T2 transport grading used in the conditional one-dimensional comparison above.

## 2. The scalar action gives a conditional dynamical gap

The corpus contains a stronger result than an imposed wall profile. [Claim 341](../../claims/0300-0399/341_a_phi4_field_generates_the_fredholm_wall_but_the_rp2_selector_requires_a_loop_lift.md) uses

\[
\mathcal L_\phi=\tfrac12(\partial\phi)^2-\tfrac\lambda4(\phi^2-v^2)^2
\]

and derives \(\phi_k=v\tanh(v\sqrt{\lambda/2}\,x)\), with finite wall tension. Its fermion operator is

\[
D_w=-i\sigma_x\partial_x+g\phi_k(x)\sigma_z.
\]

The mass profile is therefore generated by a field equation once the scalar action, Yukawa coupling and boundary conditions are specified. The asymptotic bulk rest gap is \(|gv|\) in natural units. The wall itself has a normalizable zero mode: this is a gapped bulk with a gapless defect state, not a uniformly gapped complete spectrum.

For a uniform vacuum, the canonical scalar curvature is \(V''(\pm v)=2\lambda v^2\), whereas the fermion rest gap is \(|gv|\). They are distinct quantities. No equality between them follows without a relation fixing \(g\) and \(\lambda\).

A normalization-explicit comparison makes the remaining freedom visible. In natural units, assume a real two-component reduction with

\[
\mathcal L_\psi=
Z_\psi\Psi^\dagger(i\partial_t+icA\partial_x)\Psi
-y\phi\,\Psi^\dagger\beta\Psi,
\quad \{A,\beta\}=0,\quad A^2=\beta^2=I,
\]

and positive scalar kinetic coefficient \(Z_\phi\). For

\[
V=-\tfrac{\mu^2}{2}\phi^2+\tfrac\lambda4\phi^4,
\qquad \mu^2,\lambda,Z_\psi,Z_\phi>0,
\]

one obtains

\[
|\phi_0|=\mu/\sqrt\lambda,\qquad
\Delta=\frac{|y|\mu}{Z_\psi\sqrt\lambda},\qquad
m_\phi^2=\frac{2\mu^2}{Z_\phi}.
\]

Changing the real nonzero coefficient \(y\) to \(2y\) preserves the projectors, Clifford algebra, scalar vacuum and positive kinetic terms but doubles the fermion gap. Even \(y=0\) preserves those structural data while removing the mixing. This is parameter freedom after fixing the displayed normalization, not a field-rescaling artifact. Scalar backreaction, radiative effects and vacuum stability must be reconsidered in a complete interacting theory; the comparison is a classical uniform-background test.

The [electron action gate](../frontier/parallel/frontier_parallel_qft_08_action_gate_for_the_dressed_electron_functor.md) independently makes the same distinction: gauge/Lorentz typing fixes the allowed Yukawa monomial, but neither its presence nor its coefficient follows from the TRO or linking-algebra incidence.

## 3. The selector has a local Clifford lift, with a global cost

[Claim 345](../../claims/0300-0399/345_the_full_selector_has_a_four_component_local_clifford_lift_but_needs_eight_for_a_global_aiii_grading.md) explicitly constructs

\[
\alpha=\sigma_x\otimes I,\qquad
\beta_a=\sigma_z\otimes\sigma_a,\qquad
C=\sigma_y\otimes I.
\]

These five Hermitian matrices square to one and anticommute pairwise. For constant unit selector \(n\), the supplied symbol \(cp\alpha+m n_a\beta_a\) has square \((c^2p^2+m^2)I\). Its coefficient \(m\) is an input, not a derived selector scale.

The selector is a director \(n\sim-n\), so its base is \(\mathbb{RP}^2\). The four-component antipodal transition flips \(C\). An ordinary globally grading-preserving lift requires eight components in this construction, and the additional factor must be identified with existing gauge-compatible content. This cannot be silently counted as new physical matter. The [correction ledger](../../claims/0300-0399/378_correction_ledger_for_selector_index_parity_and_primitive_deck_claims.md) also prevents transferring a wall or vortex invariant to the selector merely because both have a mod-two label.

Furthermore, \(\mathbb{RP}^2\) is connected. Its antipodal representatives are not the two disconnected vacua of a real scalar kink. Claim 341 explicitly leaves their physical identification open.

## 4. The explicit minimal Spin(2,3) action fails this gap route

[Claim 1596](../../claims/1500-1599/1596_the_explicit_minimal_spin23_action_has_only_the_massless_graviton_and_torsion_square_does_not_create_a_gap.md) supplies the fixed-norm Stelle–West action

\[
S=\frac{\ell}{8\kappa}\int\epsilon_{ABCDE}V^EF^{AB}\wedge F^{CD},
\qquad V^AV_A=\sigma\ell^2.
\]

In the selected gauge it becomes Einstein–Cartan gravity with cosmological term and an Euler invariant. The audited local vacuum spectrum has only the two massless graviton helicities. The fixed-norm selector has no independent radial Higgs mode.

Adding the stated parity-even torsion square changes an algebraic 24-component Hessian, but contributes no momentum dependence. Generic torsion remains auxiliary; the exceptional couplings make algebraic blocks degenerate rather than establish propagating massive particles. Thus this action cannot provide the requested new T2 mass pole through that deformation. This is an action-class result, not a no-go for every Spin(2,3)-covariant extension.

This corrects a possible overreading of the Hessian discussion: a positive algebraic curvature is not a particle mass without an appropriate kinetic term, constraints and pole residue. An AdS curvature shift is likewise not automatically a rest mass.

## 5. A derivative extension has a positive pole, but of another type

[Claim 1622](../../claims/1600-1699/1622_the_full_local_pgt_route_contains_a_healthy_critical_pseudoscalar_sector_but_not_an_open_quantum_region.md) retains a healthy massive \(0^-\) torsion sector in a critical first-order action class. In its positive-normalized reduction,

\[
\mathcal L_{0^-}=\tfrac A2S_\mu S^\mu+
\tfrac B2(\partial_\mu S^\mu)^2,
\qquad m_{0^-}^2=A/B>0.
\]

This is a genuine conditional action-level positive-pole result, stronger than the auxiliary torsion Hessian. It leaves the two graviton helicities massless. Its couplings are supplied and its critical equalities lack an established radiative protection mechanism. It does not supply the anticommuting physical T1/T2 spinor operators. [The fermion portal audit](../../claims/1600-1699/1611_the_surviving_0minus_torsion_mode_gives_a_generic_axionlike_spin_portal_not_yet_a_spin23_fingerprint.md) identifies its low-energy coupling as a generic axion-like spin interaction, with unfixed parameters rather than a unique Spin(2,3) fingerprint.
