A changing spectral line can reflect a changing interaction, a changing source field, or a changing detector. This investigation asks which records distinguish those possibilities before attributing them to a selector. Its useful ordinary outcome is a reproducible source dictionary and an independently calibrated response experiment.

The starting objects are a supplied charge-rotor Hamiltonian, physical charge/dipole candidates, electromagnetic boundary conditions, and a named superconducting interference device. Projectors, spectra and loop phases constrain different parts of that setup. None alone supplies the complete physical source action.

## Scope and authority

The original discussion proposed a spin-weighted photon-velocity account of Abraham and Minkowski momentum. The work recorded here does not derive that account, identify photon helicity with backward propagation, or settle a momentum partition in matter. It develops the intervening source, spectrum and calibration requirements. An algebraic anticommutator or a loop phase is not promoted to a physical optical mechanism by this packet.

Likewise the E8 rotor inversion projector, paired-corner locking selector, order-18 transfer/scattering selector and separate Maxwell cochain selector are distinct constructions. A common physical representation/intertwiner has not been established between them.

This packet adds programme documentation and evidence links. Its status labels do not alter existing claim admissions. It follows the [reading contract](../../READING_CONTRACT.md) and [explanatory standard](../../EXPLANATORY_STANDARD.md).
