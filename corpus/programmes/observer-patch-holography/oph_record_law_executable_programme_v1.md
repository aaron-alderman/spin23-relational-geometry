## Gate 2: local closure and schedule

A higher-range result now requires a declared scheduler, quotient or confluence theorem.

### Calculation

Move from one seam to two adjacent seams and then the minimal loop. For each schedule:

\[

\qquad
\Phi_{e_2}\Phi_{e_1},
\qquad
(\Phi_{e_1}\Phi_{e_2})^n,

compute complete outcome histories, final channels, fixed objects and restriction to the overlap. Test:

- order independence of the accepted quotient;

- persistence of the `g` and `k` records;
- dependence on scheduler and feedback;
- gluing from seam closures to a larger patch;

### Decision

- **No-go for global promotion:** different fair schedules, detector interfaces or phase references give inequivalent accepted objects.
- **Reopening trigger:** an existence-and-uniqueness theorem for faithful gluing on a refining family.

## Gate 3: Jordan-corner canonicality

**Outcome (26 October 2026): closed no-go for canonical `H_2(R)`.** `E` fixes a full `M_56(C)` block. Every complex two-plane in that block gives an `H_2(C)` corner, so the choices form `Gr_C(2,56)`; 55 is only the source-basis empty/full subfamily. The source supplies neither a preferred two-plane nor a phase reference selecting a real slice.

### Calculation

Enumerate source-native real two-dimensional operator subspaces generated by accessible preparations and effects, modulo the declared carrier automorphisms. For every candidate `V` test:

\[
A,B\in V\quad\Rightarrow\quad A\circ B\in V,
\]

positivity-cone isomorphism to `H_2(R)_+`, invariance under the selected channel family, and observability of all three real coordinates. Compare candidates under seam relabelling, endpoint swap, phase convention, scheduler and detector restriction.

### Decision

- **Bridge pass:** one orbit is characterized without chosen labels, its projector is source-derived, and the complete accessible operations preserve it.
- **Selector result:** several equivalent or inequivalent corners exist and a preparation, seam or detector chooses one.
- **No-go:** no nontrivial accessible subspace closes under the inherited Jordan product.
- **Current status:** open; generic principal positive minors do not pass canonicality.

## Gate 4: reversible and contractive split

**Outcome (26 October 2026): closed no-go for direct finite-time bounded-generator embedding.** Every classified channel kills a matrix unit between the `00` and `02` seam-key sectors and is non-injective, whereas `exp(tL)` is invertible at finite time. Dilations, collision models and singular infinite-time limits remain distinct routes.

### Calculation

Test whether the selected discrete channel is embeddable in a completely positive semigroup. Only after embeddability passes, compute a generator and split its reversible derivation from dissipative contraction. On a Gate-3 corner, compare the derivation algebra with the exact TKK `sp(4,R)` action.

### Decision

- **Structural bridge:** a source-native invariant corner has the correct derivation algebra and the contractive complement supplies a separately measured selector.
- **Discrete-only result:** the channel is not CP-divisible; retain its exact discrete semigroup without inventing a Lindblad or Lie generator.
- **No-go:** `sp(4,R)` appears only after an arbitrary logarithm, basis choice or enlarged control set.

\[
