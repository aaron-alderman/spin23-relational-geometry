## Kenotic method

The audit grants neither corpus priority nor OPH priority. It asks, for each statement:

1. what object is defined;
2. what theorem or execution actually follows;
3. which inputs are supplied;
4. which translation gives the result physical meaning;
5. which independent record tests that translation; and
6. whether the construction exists, is selected, is unique and agrees with nature.

The same words can occur on both sides without naming the same object. `Closure`, `observer`, `entropy`, `spacetime`, `dimension`, `repair` and `admission` therefore carry no bridge by themselves.

## Entropy firewall

\[
\Delta S_{\rm carrier}
+\Delta S_{\rm record}
+\Delta S_{\rm controller}
+\Delta S_{\rm environment}
+\Sigma_{\rm irreversible}\geq0,
