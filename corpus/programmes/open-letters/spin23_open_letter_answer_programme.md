# Spin(2,3) Open-Letter Answer Programme

**Date:** 21 October 2026  
**Status:** revised after eight standalone AI answers; no expert endorsement inferred  
**Evidence:** [answer packet](../../../evidence/sources/spin23-open-letter-answers/README.md) and [adjudication ledger](../../../evidence/data/spin23-open-letter-answer-assessment.json)

The supplied answers convert eight broad questions into four executable packets.
They improve the mathematical and kinematic baseline, but they do not close the
programme's physical-selection chain.  In particular, algebra is not promoted
to dynamics, an incidence relation is not promoted to a quantum reconstruction,
and a possible product spectral triple is not promoted to a viable action.

## P1 — carrier certificate (OL-001, OL-002)

Machine-check the Peirce decomposition, TKK brackets, real-form conventions and
neighboring closures on the carrier actually generated by the recursion.  Stop
if a larger triple closure is forced.  Success establishes the algebraic
carrier only; the physical choice still needs a dynamical selector.

## P2 — joint module and action (OL-003, OL-008)

Represent the Furey/internal generators and Spin(2,3) generators on one declared
real Hilbert/Krein module.  Compute the commutant, grading, reality and
first-order conditions.  Then carry one fluctuated Dirac operator through its
covariant action, stationary vacuum, residue/Hessian spectrum and RG matching.
This is the highest-leverage physics packet.

## P3 — operational laboratory (OL-004, OL-005)

Declare whether composites use the real-Hilbert tensor product (interactions
with bilocal rather than local tomography) or a locally tomographic disk rule
(with the continuous-interaction obstruction).  Use the exact metaplectic
quadratic representation to calculate preparations, effects, channels and
invariants.  Do not infer 3+1 locality from that finite-mode laboratory.

## P4 — twistor scalar benchmark (OL-006, OL-007)

Fix the cover, orientation, Hermitian reality condition, field equation and
boundary condition.  For a conformally coupled scalar, prove the admissible
boundary trace range, an inverse modulo homogeneous data, the causal
commutator and positivity/wavefront conditions.  Only then extend to spin or
claim a physical chirality preference.

## Priority and non-promotion rules

Execute P1 and P2 first: P1 is the finite integrity check and P2 is the shortest
route to a falsifiable physical model.  P3 is a controlled laboratory; P4 is a
reconstruction benchmark.  A packet may promote a claim only when its stated
deliverable is reproduced.  None of the eight source answers counts as a reply
from a named addressee, peer review, or closure of a physical gate.
