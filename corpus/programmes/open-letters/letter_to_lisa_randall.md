# Letter to Lisa Randall

**Status:** draft; not sent

**Subject:** Does the shared \(Spin(2,4)\to Spin(2,3)\) ladder support more than analogy?

Dear Professor Randall,

I am developing an open research corpus about when a lower-dimensional
physical structure can be recovered as an admissible, observable face of a
larger carrier.

Karch–Randall gives this question a precise comparison case. An \(AdS_4\)
brane with \(SO(2,3)\) symmetry lies in an \(AdS_5\) parent with
\(SO(2,4)\), while effective four-dimensional gravity is selected by the
spectrum and residues of localized spin-2 modes.

The corpus independently contains an abstract
\(Spin(2,4)\to Spin(2,3)\) descent associated with
\(H_2(\mathbb C)\to H_2(\mathbb R)\). I am not identifying that Jordan
carrier with the Karch–Randall brane.

My question is whether the two selection problems admit an invariant,
carrier-respecting comparison, or whether the common group chain ends at a
representation, source or positivity mismatch.

A concrete answer should compare one declared Karch–Randall background—its
metric, embedding, junction data and brane-to-brane spin-2 spectral
measure—with the Jordan carrier, real slice and primitive two-form
representation. If no map preserves dimension, source type, positivity and
observable action, that closes the proposed bridge cleanly.

I would value either the sharpest obstruction or a better-posed version of the
test.

Kind regards,

Aaron Alderman
