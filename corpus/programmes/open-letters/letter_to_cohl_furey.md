# Letter to Cohl Furey

**Status:** draft; not sent

**Subject:** Is the channel rotation intrinsic to the division-algebraic representation?

Dear Dr Furey,

I am developing an open research corpus that tests possible interfaces between
division-algebraic particle representations and a rank-two Jordan response
carrier.

In one declared four-real-dimensional model, a continuous operator built from
the represented Furey product supplies a channel rotation which, together with
Jordan translations and grading, closes
\(\mathfrak{sp}(4,\mathbb R)\). The finite calculation is exact; its
representation-theoretic meaning is not.

My question is whether this channel rotation is natural in the underlying
division-algebraic construction—through its complex structure, conjugation or
endomorphisms—or whether it is introduced only by packaging the response into
four real channels.

A decisive test seems to be whether the operator acts naturally on the particle
ideals, has the right commutant with the Standard-Model action, respects the
relevant conjugations and charges, and gives an equivariant carrier map rather
than a dimension match.

If the question is mistyped, the earliest obstruction would be as useful as a
positive identification.

Kind regards,

Aaron Alderman
