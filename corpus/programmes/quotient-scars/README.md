# Quotient-Scar Programme

Within the wider [dynatology ontology](../dynatology/README.md), this programme governs the realization, observation, scar and recovery coordinates \((\rho,L,D,I,R)\). It does not by itself supply the ambient carrier, admissibility predicate or objective provenance \((X,A,J)\).

This lane is the machine-readable and human-readable matrix of reductions uncovered by the frontier investigations.

The canonical comparison table is [quotient_scar_register_v1.json](quotient_scar_register_v1.json). Its entries do not assert that the listed carriers are physically equivalent. They force each comparison to declare:

- the resolved parent;
- the reduction map;
- what is killed;
- what survives and in which category;
- whether effective dynamics closes;
- whether available detectors are sufficient;
- the evidential status; and
- the decisive falsifier.
