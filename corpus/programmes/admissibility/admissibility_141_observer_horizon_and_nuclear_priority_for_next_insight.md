## Decision

The areas should not be advanced in the order of their conceptual grandeur. They do four different jobs:

1. **TH229 — (^{229})Th controlled nuclear transition:** execute first as the precision parent/channel/detector pilot.
2. **NUCLEAR_OPEN — compound and isomeric open systems:** develop next as the hostile many-channel control.
3. **KERR — spectroscopy and superradiant channels:** pursue as the strongest existing strong-field data analysis and mixed-generator falsifier.
4. **UNRUH — detector calibration:** use as the exact regression standard for observer-indexed local closure.
5. **HAWKING — state-to-detector stack:** build theoretically, but do not make it the next experimental bet.

This ordering is stable under balanced, experiment-first and corpus-bridge weightings. A foundational-insight weighting elevates Hawking, which is exactly why a single scalar priority would mislead: profundity and executable discrimination are different axes.

## Why this is different from the first ranking

The first atlas grouped all nuclear cases together and put Unruh first as a calibration. That was right for logical pedagogy but not for discovery yield. Direct laser excitation of the (^{229})Th isomer was demonstrated in crystals in 2024, with measured resonance, host-dependent lifetime and isotope/off-resonance controls. In October 2026, two independent solid-state nuclear-clock demonstrations closed feedback loops to the nuclear transition. The lane is no longer merely “nuclear decay”: it is a controllable boundary between nuclear, electronic, crystal and photonic degrees of freedom.

Kerr has also moved from a generic future target to a live high-information record. The LVK analysis of GW250114 reports multiple ringdown tones and consistency with a Kerr remnant at current precision. This makes Kerr the best direct strong-field test in the set, although the observer cannot vary black-hole spin, environment or preparation in the laboratory.

Unruh and Hawking retain high formal importance but lower near-term promotion power. Direct spacetime Unruh detection still requires prohibitive accelerations in ordinary settings; simulators test the detector map. Analogue Hawking systems test kinematic mode conversion and correlations but do not supply dynamical gravity, evaporation or the global information ledger.

## TH229 — highest near-term insight density

### What it can teach

The (^{229})Th platform places an unusually low nuclear transition inside a material host and interrogates it optically. It therefore contains, on one carrier stack:

- a well-identified parent and daughter nuclear sector;
- polarization and quadrupole selection structure;
- a crystal environment that shifts, splits, broadens or quenches the record;
- controlled excitation and feedback;
- time-domain survival and frequency-domain line shape;
- independently fabricated samples and isotope controls.

This is almost a physical realization of the corpus ledger: admission is set by nuclear and crystal symmetry; an optical operator prepares the transition; environmental channels alter the realized width and frequency; a detector closes a finite record.

### The next insight

The useful question is not whether the nucleus is “crossing a seam.” It is whether intrinsic nuclear parameters and host-supplied channels can be factorized well enough that one calibrated model predicts, without refitting, the frequency, splitting, lifetime and controlled depopulation in a second host or site class.

### Next decisive work

Build a hierarchical likelihood with shared nuclear parameters and host-specific channel terms across CaF2, LiSrAlF6 and at least one controlled defect/site family. Freeze the nuclear reduced matrix element and radiative component on a training subset; predict held-out temperature, polarization, site and quench records. Compare a genuine shared-nucleus model with a saturated per-sample fit.

**Go:** shared parameters predict the held-out host intervention and expose a stable residual coordinate.  
**No-go:** each host requires unconstrained effective widths and shifts, leaving no transported invariant beyond the transition label.

### Importance

This is the best immediate route to theoretical engineering and the most credible precision-new-physics platform in this set. It does not presently privilege (Spin_0(2,3)). Its value is that it can teach the corpus how a deeply nested detector closure actually factorizes in nature.

## NUCLEAR_OPEN — best hostile control of seam versus channel

### What it can teach

Compound resonances, isomers, shape coexistence and fission make boundaries and outgoing channels independently complicated. Their effective non-Hermitian Hamiltonians, pole trajectories, selection rules and coincidence records are mature enough to punish vague language. In particular, redistribution into broad and trapped resonance combinations tests whether the corpus can distinguish state geometry from continuum coupling.

### Next decisive work

Choose a data-rich isotope or compound-resonance region with several resolved exit channels. Fit entrance scattering and a proper subset of gamma/particle/fission coincidences; predict held-out branching and delay distributions under changed entrance spin/parity or energy. Use standard (R)-matrix, shell-model/open-system and Hauser–Feshbach comparators.

**Go:** an independently specified structural coordinate and channel coupling leave separable held-out signatures.  
**No-go:** one conventional reaction model absorbs the entire proposed distinction or the inference is dominated by missed feeding and detector efficiency.

### Importance

This is less likely than TH229 to reveal fundamental new physics, but more likely to reveal whether the corpus's seam/channel grammar is genuinely useful. A framework that fails here should not be applied to W/Z decays.

## KERR — strongest strong-field falsifier

### What it can teach

Kerr provides exact relations among mass, spin, horizon angular velocity, surface gravity and a family of quasinormal frequencies. The same geometry also distinguishes the horizon, stationary-limit surface, light ring and superradiant threshold. It is therefore the richest real example of a mixed time/rotation generator whose different boundaries leave different records.

### The next insight

The key corpus question is whether one independently inferred ((M,a_K)) predicts several unlike records: inspiral spin effects, ringdown frequencies and damping, absorption, and any channel-specific superradiant constraint. This is a no-refit closure problem across detectors and epochs, not a pattern match to (T_{\rm e}+T_{\rm h}).

### Next decisive work

Reproduce a public-event Kerr spectroscopy likelihood, beginning with GW250114, and express every tested relation as a carrier/boundary/channel/detector row. Then add a deliberately non-Kerr or environmental comparator. Separately audit ultralight-boson superradiance constraints with explicit age, natal-spin, accretion and self-interaction assumptions.

**Go:** a common mass/spin posterior predicts held-out modes or absorption structure and leaves a model-specific residual.  
**No-go:** the apparent relation is waveform-family, start-time or environment dependent, or no corpus quantity maps to the observable without a fitted dictionary.

### Importance

Kerr is the best route here to a consequential null test of gravity and ultralight fields. It is not the shortest route to proving the corpus's algebra physical. Its value is as a very high-quality falsifier of any claimed mixed-generator bridge.

## UNRUH — cleanest detector-calculus regression

### What it can teach

Unruh–DeWitt theory explicitly joins state, trajectory, switching, coupling and detector gap. It is almost tailor-made to test the claim that a detector has local closure without possessing the global state. It also supports controlled simulator implementations in which each ingredient can be changed independently.

### Next decisive work

Calculate and emulate a factorial design over trajectory, switching and gap, including a literal thermal comparator with the same long-time detailed-balance ratio. Use finite-time coherences or higher correlations as the separating record.

**Go:** one response functional predicts all arms and correctly identifies when accelerated-vacuum and thermal-bath preparations are operationally equivalent.  
**No-go:** the result is only a fitted Planck curve, or the simulator encodes the desired answer without an independent field-correlation measurement.

### Importance

Unruh is the fastest way to improve the corpus's mathematics of detectors and nested closure. It is a weak direct new-physics target. Its correct role is a calibration theorem and experimental-design school for the harder lanes.

## HAWKING — deepest endpoint, weakest next experiment

### What it can teach

Hawking radiation forces every layer to be explicit: causal geometry, quantum state, positive/negative-frequency mode connection, greybody propagation, correlations, detector and backreaction. It is the best conceptual stress test of the complete admissibility stack and the sharpest reminder that a local thermal record need not determine a global pure or mixed state.

### Next decisive work

Implement one end-to-end semiclassical calculation for a declared collapse or stationary state, carrying mode covariance through greybody scattering into a finite detector likelihood. In an analogue platform, measure partner correlations and second-order coherence with state and loss controls, and label the result as a kinematic analogue.

**Go:** the same frozen stack predicts occupation and correlation records across frequency and detector changes.  
**No-go:** a fitted temperature is the only output, partner correlations are noise-equivalent, or an analogue result is used to infer gravitational backreaction.

### Importance

Hawking has the highest possible foundational payoff but the lowest near-term empirical leverage in this set. It should remain an integration endpoint, not the programme's next claim of contact with nature.

## Cross-area insight ladder

The areas form a useful ladder when each is assigned one job:

\[
\boxed{\text{Unruh: define local detector closure}}
\to
\boxed{\text{TH229: factor parent, environment and record}}
\to
\boxed{\text{nuclear open systems: survive many channels}}
\to
\boxed{\text{Kerr: survive strong-field geometry and remote inference}}
\to
\boxed{\text{Hawking: close the state--geometry--detector stack}}.
\]

This is not an ontological derivation. It is a sequence of increasingly hostile tests. Each rung retains a failure product: detector theory, nuclear control, reaction-model discrimination, Kerr constraints or a bounded analogue-gravity result.

## What would change the ranking

- A direct, independently replicated spacetime Unruh observation would move UNRUH above generic nuclear open systems.
- A gravitational Hawking or horizon-partner observation with controlled greybody and state inference would move HAWKING to the top.
- A public (^{229})Th dataset showing irreducible host dependence with no shared nuclear parameter block would lower TH229 as a corpus bridge while retaining its metrological importance.
- A Kerr anomaly consistent across inspiral, ringdown and an independent electromagnetic or population channel would move KERR to the top new-physics position.
- A source-derived Jordan/Lie intertwiner that predicts any one of these records before fitting would reorder the entire list.

## Practical conclusion

The next two pieces of work should run together: a (^{229})Th shared-parent/host-channel factorization design and a public-data Kerr multi-record consistency analysis. Unruh supplies their detector-control language. Generic nuclear resonances stress-test the result. Hawking remains the final integration problem until a gravitational record becomes available.
