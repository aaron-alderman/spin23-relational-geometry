# Exodus QED claim: translation symmetry and whole-boundary audit

## Status

**Admitted as a rigorous adverse assessment of the published theory, not as a
judgment on unpublished data.** The public Exodus material makes a stronger
claim than the earlier momentum-port article tested: ordinary quantum
electrodynamics (QED), without new physics, is said to predict steady thrust
from a closed asymmetric electrostatic device. That claim can be evaluated on
its own mathematical terms.

## 1. The claim family must be separated

The public mini-whitepaper and patent jointly assert that:

1. a force distinct from Coulomb and magnetic forces moves a charged body
   without another ordinary object to react against;
2. the device effectively pushes against a vacuum or quantum background;
3. no new physics is required because QED explains and predicts the effect;
4. electrostatic-pressure and electric-field-divergence components conserve
   energy and momentum;
5. static or time-varying asymmetric fields can produce net force, with the
   direction set by geometry and a leading even-in-voltage scaling;
6. the effect works in vacuum and has been repeatedly and independently
   reproduced.

These are not one claim. Items 1, 5 and 6 are experimental. Items 2--4 are
mechanistic. A force record cannot validate the named QED explanation unless
the explanation supplies a complete conserved momentum ledger and a
prediction unavailable to conventional couplings.

## 2. What QED actually commits to

For electrons and the electromagnetic field, a schematic QED action is

\[
 S=\int d^4x\left[-\frac14F_{\mu\nu}F^{\mu\nu}
 +\bar\psi(i\gamma^\mu D_\mu-m)\psi\right]+S_{\rm material}.
\]

The action has no preferred spatial origin. Translation symmetry therefore
gives a conserved total stress-energy tensor,

\[
 \partial_\mu T^{\mu\nu}_{\rm total}=0,
 \qquad
 \frac{dP^i_{\rm total}}{dt}
 =-\oint_{\partial V}T^{ji}_{\rm total}n_j\,dA.
\]

This survives quantization as the translation Ward identity. Renormalization
changes finite parameters and composite-operator definitions; it does not
license a net force on an isolated state while retaining ordinary QED and a
translation-invariant vacuum.

Consequently an ordinary-QED explanation has only three routes:

- momentum crosses the boundary as real electromagnetic radiation;
- momentum is exchanged with external charges, conductors, cables, chamber,
  plasma, gas, gravitational environment or another prepared body;
- a time-dependent external agent prepares a non-equilibrium field and takes
  the compensating momentum.

If the vacuum itself is assigned a persistent receiving momentum state or a
preferred inhomogeneity, the proposal has added a state, boundary condition or
new sector. It is no longer explained by unmodified QED in the Minkowski
vacuum.

## 3. Classical closure is already decisive for the advertised static term

The spatial Maxwell stress tensor is

\[
 \sigma_{ij}=\epsilon_0\left(E_iE_j-\frac12\delta_{ij}E^2\right)
 +\frac1{\mu_0}\left(B_iB_j-\frac12\delta_{ij}B^2\right).
\]

For a surface enclosing the entire apparatus,

\[
 \mathbf F_{\rm matter}
 =\oint_{\partial V}\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\mathbf n\,dA
 -\frac{d}{dt}\int_V\epsilon_0\mathbf E\times\mathbf B\,d^3x.
\]

In a localized static electrostatic configuration, \(B=0\), the field
momentum term vanishes, and the stress flux through a surface taken to infinity
vanishes. A net charge has \(E=O(r^{-2})\), so stress times area is
\(O(r^{-2})\); neutral multipoles decay faster. Hence

\[
 \boxed{\mathbf F_{\rm complete\ isolated\ static\ device}=0.}
\]

An asymmetric electrode can experience a nonzero local pressure and one
electrode can experience a nonzero force. The rest of the electrodes,
dielectric, supports, voltage source and exterior field carry the balancing
stress. Shape changes the distribution, not the complete integral.

## 4. Where the published derivation fails

The patent's equations 1--11 do not constitute a QED derivation.

### 4.1 The initial energy equation is not general

It begins with \(0=\tfrac12Mv^2+U\). Conserved total energy need not be zero,
and electrostatic field energy \(\epsilon_0\int E^2d^3x/2\) is non-negative.
Choosing an energy zero cannot turn a stored positive field energy into the
negative mechanical potential needed by that equation.

### 4.2 Stationary action does not imply \(d(Ut)=0\)

The variational statement \(\delta S=0\) applies to the action
\(S=\int L\,dt\) under admissible path variations. The product \(Ut\) is not
the action in general, and \(\delta S=0\) does not imply \(d(Ut)=0\). The step
\(Udt=-t\,dU\) is therefore an added assumption, not a consequence of least
action or QED.

### 4.3 Absolute translation is confused with relative displacement

For a closed apparatus translated rigidly by \(X\), translation invariance
requires

\[
 U_{\rm total}(X+a)=U_{\rm total}(X),\qquad
 -\frac{\partial U_{\rm total}}{\partial X}=0.
\]

A derivative of energy can give force when the coordinate changes a separation
from an external body or changes an internal relative coordinate. It cannot
give self-thrust when it merely moves the complete isolated system.

### 4.4 A cross-section difference is not a closed stress integral

The replacement of the volume derivative by a difference resembling
\(\epsilon_0(E_2^2A_2-E_1^2A_1)\) retains selected end faces. It omits side
traction, vector direction, tangential stress, other electrodes, dielectrics,
supports, sources and the exterior field. The correct object is
\(\oint\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\mathbf n\,dA\) on one closed boundary.

### 4.5 Electrostatic pressure is not an additional force

For a conductor with normal exterior field, \(p=\epsilon_0E^2/2\) is a useful
surface form of the same Maxwell traction that encodes Coulomb interaction.
It is not a second, weaker force that can be added after the Coulomb attraction
has been cancelled by a support. The support transmits the complete traction;
splitting it into a cancelled Coulomb part and an uncancelled pressure part
double-counts one electromagnetic interaction.

### 4.6 Time dependence opens radiation and source ports

Switching and phase-dependent drive create magnetic fields, displacement
current, radiation, source recoil, dielectric loss and mechanical transients.
They must be treated with the full time-dependent stress-energy balance.
Multiplying a periodic term by an elapsed time and then averaging it is not a
stationary thrust derivation: the result depends on the arbitrary time origin
and generally represents a boundary/transient term.

## 5. Does quantization rescue the claim?

Not in the advertised form.

### 5.1 Virtual photons do not form an exhaust

Virtual photons are internal elements of a perturbative representation, not
on-shell particles carrying independently measurable momentum to infinity.
The full amplitude obeys the QED conservation laws. Calling electrostatic
interaction “virtual-photon exchange” does not change the closed-system
momentum balance.

### 5.2 Casimir forces are real but internal

QED vacuum correlations and material response can produce geometry-dependent
Casimir stresses. Two bodies may attract or repel and an asymmetric component
may experience force. Translating the complete isolated configuration leaves
its interaction energy unchanged, so the sum of forces on all included bodies
is zero. Dynamical Casimir emission can yield recoil, but then real photons
carry the opposite momentum and the photon-thrust bound applies.

### 5.3 Vacuum momentum proposals require extra structure

Feigel-type proposals use magnetoelectric media, crossed fields or explicitly
time-dependent susceptibilities. The steady version is contentious and has
been shown in realistic analyses to conflict with thermodynamic closure;
transient versions retain an external preparation and recoil ledger. This is a
useful frontier comparator, but it does not derive a static asymmetric
capacitor thruster from ordinary QED.

### 5.4 A prepared or Lorentz-breaking vacuum is new physics/state data

If “engineering vacuum asymmetry” means boundary-conditioned modes, every
boundary and reaction force belongs inside the system definition. If it means
a persistent vacuum momentum reservoir, the theory must specify its state,
dispersion, coupling, noise, backreaction and Lorentz signature. The phrase
alone does not close the model.

## 6. Quantitative consequences

The reported public figure of roughly \(5.2\,\mathrm{mN}\) has a sharp QED
benchmark. If the momentum leaves only as perfectly collimated photons,

\[
 P_{\gamma}=Fc\approx1.56\,\mathrm{MW}.
\]

A millinewton-scale steady force with watt- or kilowatt-scale input therefore
cannot be ordinary unmeasured photon recoil. Static QED predicts zero complete
force; radiative QED predicts a measurable outgoing momentum flux.

The claimed \(0.38g\) vehicle projection is more demanding still: ordinary
QED requires an external momentum flux proportional to vehicle mass. Energy
alone is insufficient; both energy and momentum must close.

## 7. The decisive model tournament

The strongest experiment is not another scale trace. It is a simultaneous
whole-boundary tournament:

1. place thruster, battery, converter and telemetry on one freely moving body;
2. use vacuum, guarding and variable chamber geometry;
3. measure all six mechanical degrees of freedom and the centre of energy;
4. measure closed-surface electromagnetic momentum flux over the relevant
   spectrum, charged and neutral particle flux, temperature and outgassing;
5. preregister the complete sign/scaling table under geometry reversal,
   voltage reversal, apparatus rotation, grounded/floating operation and
   waveform substitution;
6. compare a full Maxwell finite-element solution, including sources,
   dielectrics and supports, with the claimed truncated pressure sum;
7. repeat in free fall or orbit only if the earlier ledger retains a residual.

The discriminator is:

| result | consequence |
|---|---|
| complete Maxwell stress closes the force | ordinary electromagnetism; patent pressure sum used an incomplete boundary |
| force tracks chamber, ground, cable, gas or thermal state | external port or metrology mechanism |
| force equals measured radiation momentum | photon propulsion, not static vacuum thrust |
| reproducible free-body force remains but no QED flux carries momentum | the assertion “ordinary QED explains it” is falsified even if the anomaly is real |
| residual plus a quantitative new-sector signature | candidate new physics; require independent replication and a covariant action |

The important logical point is that a successful anomalous-force experiment
would not vindicate the published QED derivation. It would make that derivation
more urgently inadequate, because standard QED supplies a null theorem for the
closed static device.

## 8. Kenotic disposition

**Admitted:** Exodus has stated a clear, consequential and experimentally
addressable claim; asymmetric electrostatic systems deserve careful
whole-boundary measurement; QED permits local electromagnetic and vacuum
stresses, radiation recoil and externally prepared non-equilibrium effects.

**Not admitted:** the patent's equations derive net static self-thrust; scalar
electrostatic pressure is independent of Coulomb/Maxwell traction; virtual
photons or the Minkowski vacuum provide an unledgered momentum sink; laboratory
force records establish the QED mechanism.

**Current assessment:** the published “ordinary QED” explanation is not merely
incomplete. For a closed static device it conflicts with the translation Ward
identity and whole-boundary Maxwell stress closure. The experimental claim
remains logically open because unpublished apparatus details or data could
reveal either a conventional port or a genuine anomaly.

## 10. Corpus consequence

The admissibility frontier gains a clean fork:

\[
 \boxed{
 \text{standard QED} \Rightarrow \text{closed static thrust}=0;
 \quad
 \text{observed closed thrust}\Rightarrow
 \text{port, error, or physics beyond the stated QED model}.}
\]

This is stronger and more useful than either credulous admission or generic
skepticism. It says exactly which claim fails, why it fails, and what result
would force the corpus to reopen the physics.
