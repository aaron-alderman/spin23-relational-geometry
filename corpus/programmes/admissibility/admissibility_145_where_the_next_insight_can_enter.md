## The shared shape and the important differences

The five areas look related because each turns an inaccessible or incompletely observed state into a finite record. A useful common factorization is

\[
r=D_\eta\!\left[C_\lambda\!\left(U_\theta(s)\right)\right],
\]

where \(s\) is the prepared or inferred state, \(U_\theta\) is its internal or geometric evolution, \(C_\lambda\) supplies the available outgoing channels and \(D_\eta\) is the detector and analysis map. The parameters \(\theta,\lambda,\eta\) must not be collapsed merely because only \(r\) is observed. The next insight comes from an intervention that changes one block while holding the others independently constrained.

That shared diagram is a comparison tool, not a unification. The restriction enters in a different place in every case:

| Area | Where the restriction enters | What can be varied independently | What is inferred rather than controlled | Unique lesson |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unruh | worldline, switching and local field sampling | detector gap, switching, trajectory surrogate and state preparation in a simulator | equivalence to literal acceleration through a relativistic vacuum | local thermal closure need not identify the global state |
| thorium-229 | nuclear transition embedded in an electronic and crystal host | temperature, polarization, site population, illumination, host and feedback | isolated nuclear matrix element and unobserved host channels | a nested physical system can transport a shared invariant through different local closures |
| nuclear open systems | thresholds, selection rules, barriers and continuum coupling | entrance energy and spin/parity, some exit channels and coincidence conditions | microscopic compound state and missed feeding | a structural seam and an escape channel can be separately identifiable in a hostile many-channel system |
| Kerr | causal geometry and horizon scattering boundary conditions | analysis window, waveform model and population assumptions, but not the source preparation | mass, spin, environment and remnant history | one hidden geometry must predict unlike remote records without a fitted dictionary |
| Hawking | geometry, quantum state, positive-frequency continuation and exterior potential | principally state, dispersion and detector settings in analogue systems | gravitational backreaction, interior partner and full evaporation history | a thermal marginal is weaker than closure of the state–geometry–channel–record stack |

The areas are therefore “the same but a little different” in a precise sense: they share the composition \(D\circ C\circ U\), while the controllable block, inaccessible block and admissible claim differ.

## Importance is vector-valued

A single ranking hides the reason each area matters. The present priorities are:

| Kind of next insight | Leading area | Why |
|---|---|---|
| fastest executable discrimination | thorium-229 | the transition is directly driven, feedback-locked and measured across material and environmental interventions |
| strongest test of the corpus vocabulary | nuclear open systems | mature reaction theory can expose whether “seam”, “channel”, “width” and “record” add discrimination or merely rename known quantities |
| cleanest detector theorem | Unruh | the response functional states exactly which field correlations a local trajectory and switching window retain |
| highest-information fundamental null test | Kerr | a common mass-and-spin posterior can be required to predict inspiral, ringdown and absorption or population records |
| deepest integration target | Hawking | no other lane requires geometry, state choice, mode conversion, exterior scattering, correlations, detector and backreaction to be kept distinct |
| shortest path to a corpus-specific bridge | none yet | no source-derived intertwiner presently carries the Jordan response cell into any of these physical carriers and predicts a held-out record |

This separates *importance to the framework* from *probability of discovering new physics*. Unruh may be more important to the detector concept than to direct discovery. Generic nuclear reactions may be more important as a falsifier of the vocabulary than as a beyond-standard-model search. Hawking may be the deepest endpoint while remaining the least executable next experiment.

## The information-gain test

For parameters \(p=(\theta,\lambda,\eta)\), let \(J=\partial r/\partial p\) be the local sensitivity matrix after detector folding. A new measurement is valuable when it adds a row whose component outside the existing nuisance span is nonzero. In ordinary language: it must see a difference that the current records cannot mimic by retuning the environment or apparatus.

This gives each lane a concrete next insight:

### Thorium-229: does a nuclear invariant survive a change of host?

The next record should not merely improve the clock. It should determine whether one shared nuclear parameter block predicts frequency, quadrupole splitting, survival and controlled depopulation across host, temperature and site interventions. Temperature-dependent kilohertz spectroscopy already shows that the crystal is both nuisance and probe; it changes electron density and field gradients at the nucleus rather than acting as a featureless container.

**Insight gained if yes:** local closures can be composed: a nuclear invariant remains transportable while the host supplies typed shifts and channels. This is the most direct physical model currently available for the corpus's claim that a smaller closure can remain meaningful inside a larger one.  
**Insight gained if no:** “the nuclear transition” is not sufficient as a transported canonical value at the required precision; the state must include more of the host and defect environment. That is still a useful correction to the admissibility grammar.  
**New-physics threshold:** a preregistered residual must survive shared-parameter, crystal, laser, temperature, magnetic, defect and detector covariance and then recur in an independent host or clock comparison.

### Nuclear open systems: can geometry and escape be separately recovered?

The decisive experiment changes an entrance or structural coordinate and a continuum coupling separately, then predicts held-out branching, angular correlation and delay records. Effective non-Hermitian poles are useful only if the inferred parent structure and channel amplitudes transport between interventions.

**Insight gained if yes:** the seam/channel distinction is more than language; it is an identifiable factorization with predictive value.  
**Insight gained if no:** either standard reaction theory already contains the whole distinction or the detector has quotiented it away. The W/Z analogy must then be weakened, because a cleaner and more controllable decay system did not support it.  
**New-physics threshold:** not an unusual width, but a replicated operator- or symmetry-specific residual outside modern structure, reaction, feeding and detector models.

### Unruh: when do two globally different preparations become locally identical?

The useful experiment compares accelerated-vacuum and literal thermal preparations with the same asymptotic excitation/de-excitation ratio, then uses finite switching, coherences or higher correlations to seek a separating record. Markov and rotating-wave approximations must be treated as testable reductions, not invisible assumptions.

**Insight gained if equivalent:** the detector defines a genuine local quotient class over the tested scale and observables.  
**Insight gained if distinguishable:** the missing information has been localized to switching, correlation order or memory, giving the detector closure a measured range rather than a universal label.  
**New-physics threshold:** a simulator cannot supply it. A direct claim would require an independently calibrated spacetime acceleration experiment whose complete response defeats conventional QFT and apparatus models.

### Kerr: can one latent geometry close several unlike records?

The strongest test carries an inspiral-derived \((M,a)\) posterior into held-out ringdown tones and damping, while profiling start time, waveform family, calibration, nonlinear merger contamination and environment. Population or absorption constraints are valuable only when their extra astrophysical assumptions remain visible.

**Insight gained if yes:** a remotely inferred carrier can achieve multi-record closure even though it cannot be prepared or intervened on. This is the corpus's strongest example of inference substituting for control without becoming arbitrary.  
**Insight gained if no:** disagreement localizes to geometry, waveform dynamics, environment or detector only if the analysis has kept those blocks separate.  
**New-physics threshold:** a residual consistent across detectors and physically unlike records. Even that would first be a gravity or environment anomaly; a corpus-specific interpretation additionally needs an explicit intertwiner and prior prediction.

### Hawking: can the whole stack predict more than temperature?

The decisive quantity is not a fitted Planck curve. One declared state and geometry must predict frequency-dependent occupation, greybody transmission and partner or higher-order correlations after a calibrated detector map. Analogue experiments can test the kinematic channel and correlation logic while leaving gravitational backreaction unclaimed.

**Insight gained if the stack closes:** local thermality, propagation and correlations can be jointly transported from state to record.  
**Insight gained if only temperature closes:** too much information has been quotiented away to distinguish Hawking production from a thermal or driven source.  
**New-physics threshold:** gravitational evidence with controlled state and propagation inference; analogue agreement alone remains theoretical engineering.

## The next three moves

The most informative sequence is not five parallel literature programmes:

1. **Execute the public thorium and Kerr contracts.** They occupy complementary extremes: highly controllable nested matter and minimally controllable strong-field inference.
2. **Build one common identifiability report.** For each, publish the Jacobian or posterior covariance showing which source, channel and detector directions the actual records separate. A scalar goodness of fit is insufficient.
3. **Use the result to choose the next branch.** If thorium factorization succeeds, move to the hostile many-channel nuclear test. If it fails, deepen the host/site model before exporting the grammar. If Kerr closes, retain it as a strong conventional benchmark. If it fails coherently across records, escalate within gravity before invoking the corpus algebra. Unruh supplies the controlled detector regression throughout; Hawking receives the mature stack only after these lower rungs stop leaking distinctions.

## What would count as a genuinely new insight

The programme should look for one of three increasingly strong outcomes:

- **theoretical engineering:** the factorization exposes a missing nuisance, improves prediction or designs a detector that retains a previously erased distinction;
- **portable physical structure:** the same typed parameter or invariant predicts held-out records across hosts, channels, detectors or events;
- **new physics:** a portable residual survives the complete conventional model union, independent apparatus and replication, and is then predicted by a source-derived physical bridge rather than named after the fact.

The first two outcomes are not consolation prizes. They are the route by which the third becomes credible. The immediate instruction is therefore narrow and hopeful: look first where two nested closures can be independently changed, and require the invariant claimed between them to survive the change.
