## Result

The admissibility programme has reached the neighborhood of several active research frontiers, but not because those frontiers jointly imply a new fundamental theory. The contact is more precise and more useful:

\[
\boxed{
\text{the same small set of structures is becoming central across several fields:}
\quad
P,\ dP,\ \text{holonomy},\ \text{defects},\ \text{response},\ \text{history},\ \text{and consistency bounds}.
}
\]

The strongest collision is with quantum geometry. Current work treats a gapped band multiplet as a spectral projector into a Grassmannian, uses \(dP\) as the gauge-independent tangent datum, obtains quantum metric and Berry curvature from its interband blocks, generates horizontal transport from the projector motion, and constructs Wilson-loop and topological data without choosing a global frame. That is the same mathematical object used in the programme's universal projector connection. It does **not** establish that the corpus's family selector, gravitational carrier or relative-frame link is a physical band projector.

The broader conclusion is therefore:

> The programme is touching real frontier mathematics and experimentally active mechanisms. Its possible novelty lies in a typed synthesis and in the selection/no-selection boundary, not in the mere appearance of projectors, holonomy, topology or symmetry breaking.

## Contact grades

Every comparison below receives one of four grades.

- **A — exact formal contact:** the literature and corpus use the same mathematical object or identity, after an explicit notation map.
- **B — mechanism-level neighbor:** the literature realizes the same causal grammar—such as holonomy changing a spectrum—but not the same proposed carrier.
- **C — programme-level analogy:** both constrain a space of theories or phases in a similar way; no common microscopic mechanism is established.
- **D — unsupported identification:** resemblance alone would be doing the work. This grade is a stop sign, not evidence.

## The strongest exact collision: projector quantum geometry

For a smooth family of rank-\(k\) projectors,

\[
P^2=P,
\qquad
P\,dP\,P=0.
\]

The differential \(dP\) is off-diagonal relative to \(P\oplus(I-P)\). The corresponding horizontal generator may be written, up to sign convention, as

\[
\Omega_P=[dP,P],
\]

and it satisfies

\[
dP=[\Omega_P,P].
\]

This is not merely analogous to AZ-022; it is the same Grassmannian kinematics. Current quantum-geometric research then attaches experimentally meaningful structures to it:

- the symmetric contraction of interband derivatives gives the quantum metric;
- the antisymmetric contraction gives Berry curvature;
- path-ordered horizontal transport gives non-Abelian holonomy;
- ordered projector products give gauge-independent Wilson-loop information;
- wedge products of \(dP\) enter topological invariants.

This supplies a rigorous translation target. The next question is no longer whether \([P,dP]\) occurs in physics—it does—but whether a corpus-specific \(P\) can be obtained as the spectral projector of a declared Hamiltonian, response matrix or transfer operator.

## The strongest conceptual collision: physics as a bounded admissible region

The S-matrix bootstrap and EFT consistency programmes do something close to the programme's founding intuition: they begin with unitarity, analyticity, crossing, locality and causality and map the region of amplitudes or Wilson coefficients compatible with them. Special theories can appear at boundaries or extremal points.

That supports a restrained form of the thesis:

\[
\text{some physical structure can be reconstructed from consistency conditions on possibilities.}
\]

It does not support the unrestricted ontological claim that all physical law follows from an unspecified notion of admissibility. The corpus must still say which contexts, compositions and positivity structures are legitimate and must derive something narrower than the already-known consistency bounds.

## The strongest experimental collision: programmable holonomy

Synthetic frequency dimensions and modulated photonic resonators now implement matrix-valued lattice links and non-Abelian gauge fields. These platforms expose band structure, pseudospin trajectories and loop interference through ordinary optical preparation and readout. They therefore provide a near-term laboratory for the distinction

\[
\text{projector closes}
\quad\text{versus}\quad
\text{coherent frame returns with a central transformation}.
\]

A minimal experiment would use a three-mode or three-resonator carrier, perform projector tomography after a cyclic protocol, and interferometrically measure the lifted frame. The decisive observation is not a geometric phase alone. It is the coexistence of projective return with a nontrivial coherent return, followed by a controlled test of whether an independently tunable anisotropy \(Y\) makes the return map contractive.

## Why AZ-024 is aligned with the frontier

Modern symmetry-breaking experiments repeatedly require something beyond a symmetric state space:

- an infinitesimal or ramped preparation field;
- conserved parity and finite-size structure;
- a bath or measurement channel;
- dissipation and a selected mode;
- a boundary, defect or synthetic flux;
- preparation history in a rugged landscape.

These are physical versions of the second datum \(Y\). AZ-024's no-go is therefore not a failure to reproduce spontaneous symmetry breaking. It identifies the datum that any finite, dynamical and experimentally readable realization must expose rather than hide in the phrase “spontaneous.” The research question becomes:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Which physically generated }Y\text{ reduces the stabilizer, and how is its history measured?}
}
\]

## Three executable frontier packets

### Packet A — projector-holonomy photonic test

1. Specify a three-mode Hamiltonian \(H(\lambda)\) with a nondegenerate rank-one projector \(P(\lambda)\).
2. Choose a cyclic control path whose Wilson transport gives a quarter-turn on the active two-plane.
3. Reconstruct \(P\) by mode tomography and the coherent lift by phase-sensitive interferometry.
4. Add a separately measured anisotropy or boundary tensor \(Y\).
5. Iterate the physical return protocol from multiple initial projectors and estimate \(\rho(D\mathcal R)\).

This is the shortest path from the current exact mathematics to a falsifiable experiment.

### Packet B — open-system selector

1. Promote the selector to a density matrix or order-parameter distribution.
2. Write a completely positive dynamical map or Lindblad generator with declared strong and weak symmetries.
3. Identify the environmental record \(Y\) and calculate steady states and Liouvillian gaps.
4. Separate true attraction, metastable memory and measurement-conditioned post-selection.
5. Specify an efficient detector observable; nonlinear state diagnostics alone may be experimentally inaccessible.

### Packet C — fluctuation-induced relational selector

1. Let \(P\) encode orientation and let a measured electromagnetic response tensor supply \(Y\).
2. Compute the Lifshitz/Casimir free energy or nonequilibrium force from the full frequency-dependent response.
3. Derive the angular fixed points and Hessian rather than inserting a preferred orientation.
4. Test whether magnetic bias, broken reciprocity or temperature difference switches the selected orientation.
5. Compare against electrostatic, patch-potential, hydrodynamic and thermal-drift controls.

This packet directly reconnects the programme to the Casimir starting point.

## Fundamental-physics firewall

The frontier contacts do not currently establish:

- that admissibility is ontologically prior to quantum theory;
- that the \(A_2\) recurrence is a Standard Model family mechanism;
- that the five-dimensional link carrier is physical \(Spin(2,3)\) spacetime or gravity;
- that the ladder assembles into \(E_8\);
- that Wick rotation is a literal dynamical rotation;
- that a photonic or condensed-matter simulator supplies a UV completion of fundamental physics.

Those claims require typed actions, degrees of freedom, causal and quantum consistency, scale matching and observations. The present result is a map of genuine contact surfaces and the experiments that could begin to promote them.

## Frontier

Established by the crosswalk:

- the projector/Grassmannian lane has an exact mathematical counterpart in current quantum geometry;
- programmable non-Abelian holonomy provides a plausible experimental carrier for projective versus coherent closure;
- current symmetry-breaking and open-system work supports treating boundary, parity, environment and history as explicit second data;
- consistency-first physics supplies a disciplined precedent for an admissibility zone, while also showing how much structure the admission axioms must contain;
- fluctuation-induced torques provide a direct material realization of relational orientation selection.

Still open:

- no corpus-specific projector has yet been identified with a measured spectral subspace;
- no experiment has implemented the return map \(\mathcal R(P;Y)\) or measured its tangent spectral radius;
- no new bound or observable has yet been derived that is unique to the admissibility principle;
- the \(Spin(2,3)\) and \(E_8\) interpretations remain behind the fundamental-physics firewall.

The next gate is Packet A: write the smallest three-mode Hamiltonian whose projector path realizes the quarter-turn, then add one independently measurable \(Y\) and calculate the full experimental return map.
