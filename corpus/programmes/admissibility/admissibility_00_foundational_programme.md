## Foundational thesis

> **Admissibility is not merely a constraint imposed on physics, but a more fundamental starting point from which physical structure itself should be derived.**

The usual order of construction begins with a state space, spacetime, fields and an action, and then asks which solutions are physically admissible. This programme reverses that order. It begins with a minimally structured space of possibilities and asks whether the simultaneous requirements for possibilities to coexist, compose, persist, be compared and become empirically accessible can generate the structures ordinarily assumed at the start of physics.

The intended reversal is

\[
\boxed{\text{pre-given physical structures}}
\longrightarrow
\boxed{\text{constraints on their solutions}}
\]

becoming

\[
\boxed{\text{possible distinctions and relations}}
\xrightarrow{\text{admissibility}}
\boxed{\text{states, transformations, causal order, dynamics and observables}}.
\]

This is a strong conjecture. At present the corpus supports the methodological importance of admission gates much more strongly than it supports their ontological priority. The programme exists to close or expose that gap.

## What “admissible” must mean

“Admissible” cannot mean merely “whatever occurs in the actual world.” That would redescribe physics without explaining it. Nor may it mean “whatever satisfies all known physical laws,” because those are among the structures the programme aims to derive. The starting notion must be thinner than the target physics and independently stateable.

The provisional primitive is:

> A possibility is admissible relative to a composition context when it can participate in that context without destroying the distinctions, relations and consistency conditions required to define the context itself.

This makes admissibility relational rather than unary. A state may be locally well formed yet globally incompatible; two individually allowed processes may fail to compose; a formal solution may violate positivity, causality or a boundary condition when embedded in a larger system. Write

\[
\operatorname{Adm}(x\mid C)=1

\]

when candidate \(x\) can be inserted into context \(C\), and

\[
\mathcal A(C)=\{x\in\mathcal P(C):\operatorname{Adm}(x\mid C)=1\}

\]

for the admitted subset of a prior possibility space \(\mathcal P(C)\). The physics cannot reside only in the set \(\mathcal A\); it must also reside in the structure and provenance of the admission map.

## The thinnest proposed starting packet

The programme should spend as little physical structure as possible. Its initial packet contains only candidate notions, each of which remains removable if it can be derived from the others:

1. **Distinction:** at least two alternatives can be told apart.
2. **Relation:** alternatives can stand in typed relations rather than forming an unstructured list.
3. **Composition:** some relations or processes can be joined, with declared domains and codomains.
4. **Compatibility:** not every formal composition is jointly realizable.
5. **Restriction and extension:** a whole can be viewed through parts, and local data may or may not extend globally.
6. **Comparison:** alternative realizations can be identified, ordered or probabilistically distinguished by an internal or external observer.
7. **Record:** some distinctions can persist long enough to constrain later comparisons.

No metric, manifold dimension, Lorentz signature, complex Hilbert space, gauge group, Lagrangian, Born rule or Standard Model representation is contained in this packet by declaration. If one of these is smuggled into “composition,” “record” or “comparison,” the derivation is circular and must be marked as such.

## Admissibility is a stack, not one veto

The corpus repeatedly finds that mathematical existence is cheaper than physical existence. The following stack organizes the difference without assuming that every layer is fundamental:

| Layer | Admission question | Familiar physical contact |
|---|---|---|
| Type | Are the objects and maps being compared of the same declared kind? | fields versus sources, states versus observables, internal versus spacetime indices |
| Logical | Is the candidate free of contradiction under its stated axioms? | consistent equations and boundary data |
| Compositional | Do allowed pieces remain allowed when joined, restricted or coarse-grained? | channels, subsystems, gluing, renormalization |
| Order and positivity | Are states, probabilities, energies or spectral weights nonnegative on the physical domain? | positive cones, Hilbert norms, residues, density operators |
| Causal | Does influence respect an emergent or given causal order and a well-posed propagation problem? | locality, hyperbolicity, finite signalling |
| Dynamical | Is the admitted sector stable under evolution and perturbation? | constraints, vacuum stability, bounded Hamiltonians |
| Quantum-field | Do locality, covariance, unitarity, statistics, anomaly cancellation and radiative closure coexist? | QFT and effective field theory |
| Global | Do local assignments extend around loops and across the whole configuration? | topology, holonomy, modular and anomaly consistency |
| Empirical | Does the construction produce normalized observables that survive calibration and comparison with data? | prediction, falsification and reproducibility |

The central wager is that enough of the upper layers may be forced by demanding coherent realization of thinner lower layers across arbitrary compositions. The central danger is that the table merely imports mature physics under new names.

## The generative programme

Let \(\mathcal P_0\) be a deliberately weak possibility structure. A family of independently justified admission operations \(A_i\) produces a descending sequence

\[
\mathcal P_0\supseteq \mathcal P_1\supseteq\cdots,
\qquad
\mathcal P_{n+1}=A_n(\mathcal P_n).
\]

Simple elimination is not enough. A successful programme must also show that the surviving set acquires new canonical structure:

\[
\mathcal P_n
\xrightarrow{\text{closure under admissible composition}}
(\mathcal S,\mathcal T,\mathcal O,\mathcal C),
\]

where \(\mathcal S\) is a state structure, \(\mathcal T\) a transformation structure, \(\mathcal O\) an observable structure and \(\mathcal C\) a causal or compositional organization. “Canonical” means that the structure is fixed up to a controlled equivalence rather than chosen by an unreported convention.

The programme asks six linked questions.

### A1. Can admissibility generate state space?

Can consistency of mixture, comparison and composition force convex state spaces, ordered vector spaces, positive cones or a narrower class? The existence of Jordan and operator-algebraic state cones is a contact point, not permission to assume that Nature begins with a Jordan algebra.

### A2. Can admissibility generate probability?

Can probabilities arise as normalized measures over mutually exclusive admitted alternatives, and can a noncontextual or compositional requirement force a specific rule? Recovering the Born rule requires more than naming amplitudes or decoherence. The programme must distinguish operational updating, stable record formation and ontological outcome selection.

### A3. Can admissibility generate causal geometry?

Can compatibility of localization, signalling and composition force a partial order, light-cone structure, Lorentzian signature, dimension or equivalence principle? A causal order is not yet a metric, and a cone is not yet spacetime. The observed \(3+1\)-dimensional Lorentzian regime is a target, not an input.

### A4. Can admissibility generate symmetry and particles?

If transformations preserve the admission structure, their automorphisms supply candidate symmetries. Stable irreducible modes of admissible composition may supply candidate particles. This must recover spin-statistics, gauge redundancy, chirality, mass and interaction typing rather than equating a convenient representation with a physical species.

### A5. Can admissibility generate dynamics?

The hardest step is to obtain more than kinematics. A variational principle, Hamiltonian, path weight, channel flow or update rule must be selected rather than inserted. It must preserve the admitted domain, yield a well-posed initial or boundary value problem, and explain why one dynamics among many compatible dynamics is realized.

### A6. Can admissibility generate constants and predictions?

If constants label inequivalent admitted realizations, cumulative global consistency may restrict them. A real result requires a dimensionless relation, interval or discrete value protected against reparameterization and radiative drift. Parameter counting and anthropic compatibility do not constitute a derivation.

## The anti-tautology contract

Every claimed derivation must publish five objects:

1. **Prior space:** what possibilities existed before the gate was applied?
2. **Gate:** what exact condition admits or rejects a candidate?
3. **Provenance:** why is the gate available without assuming the desired result?
4. **Reduction:** which alternatives are actually removed or identified?
5. **Novel consequence:** what structure or number follows that was not encoded in the gate?

A derivation fails the contract if its admission rule contains the target in disguised form, if arbitrary gates can select arbitrary worlds, if the survivor is not unique up to declared equivalence, or if no observable distinguishes the construction from existing physics.

## Relation to the existing corpus

The existing corpus supplies a large library of admission mechanisms and failed promotions:

- positive response measures produce exact cones but do not automatically identify spacetime;
- representation closure and anomaly cancellation remove candidate matter sectors but do not select a microscopic action;
- Ward identities distinguish conserved from broken source directions but do not select which source Nature couples to;
- residue, hyperbolicity and radiative tests distinguish healthy effective sectors from fundamental completions;
- topology and holonomy constrain global transport but do not by themselves provide local dynamics;
- empirical protocols expose when a mathematically real signal is generic, underidentified or uncalibrated;
- no-go and exhaustion results show that repeated failure can locate the missing datum without turning absence into proof of a preferred ontology.

The \(H_2(\mathbb R)_+\), Jordan and \(Spin_0(2,3)\) structures are therefore high-value laboratories for the programme, not its axioms. Their exact correspondences reveal how positivity, two-channel response, symplectic transport and conformal geometry can meet. The unresolved physical intertwiner demonstrates why admissibility must preserve types and provenance at every step.

## What success would look like

The programme has three legitimate levels of success:

1. **Methodological:** a universal, auditable grammar for separating mathematical possibility from physical admission.
2. **Reconstructive:** a small set of prephysical assumptions from which known state, causal or dynamical structures follow with classification theorems.
3. **Predictive:** a surviving structure yields a new protected numerical or qualitative consequence confirmed by experiment.

Level 1 is already strongly represented across the corpus. Level 2 is the purpose of this programme. Level 3 is required before admissibility can be claimed as a new fundamental law of Nature.

## What would defeat the strong thesis

The strong thesis should be weakened or rejected if:

- physically adequate models require irreducible contingent dynamical inputs that no noncircular admissibility principle narrows;
- many inequivalent worlds survive every independently motivated gate, with no measure or selector among them;
- the only successful gates are restatements of already known physical laws;
- probability, time direction, scale or interaction cannot arise without being inserted as primitives;
- the framework cannot recover established physics in a controlled limit;
- or it produces no discriminating observable even after its formal reconstruction is complete.

The fallback conclusion would still be valuable: admissibility would remain a disciplined architecture for theory construction and criticism, but not the ontological source of physical structure.

## Founding research posture

This programme is permitted to ask questions at the edge of physics, but it must keep one hand on established physics at all times. Every speculative object must name its nearest conventional analogue, its additional assumption, its known obstruction, its recovery limit and the calculation or observation capable of changing its status. The space is deliberately open to deep reconstruction and deliberately closed to promotion by metaphor alone.
