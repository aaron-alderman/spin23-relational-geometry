## What is being dimensioned

The nodes in this graph are proposal and review records *about* response families. They are not voltage, current, energy or response values. Their honest carrier at this layer is therefore an SI zero-exponent vector:

\[
(M,L,T,I,\Theta,N,J)=(0,0,0,0,0,0,0).
\]

This says that the record itself is dimensionless. It does not say that the matrix, operator or measurement represented inside the record is dimensionless. That second, quantity-level assignment remains open and must be supplied when the path is connected to a physical calculation or detector.

The distinction prevents two opposite errors. Leaving dimensions absent allows incompatible physical quantities to be joined invisibly. Assigning voltage or energy units to a semantic record confuses the description with the thing described. The present result closes the metadata layer while preserving the physical-units gate.
