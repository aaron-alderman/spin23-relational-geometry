**Date:** 30 October 2026

**Parents:** [experimental capability frontier](admissibility_178_klein_throat_experimental_capability_frontier.md), [throat/nonlinearity/defect map](admissibility_181_throat_nonlinearity_and_defect_capability_map.md), [surface-fluid physical disposition](admissibility_177_klein_throat_surface_fluid_branch_exhaustion_and_physical_disposition.md)

## What can now be built without changing the question

The first hardware should be a phase-coherent programmable graph, preferably
an active electrical network or real-time digital emulator with physical
input/output ports. Its purpose is to demonstrate C1--C4:

1. matched reflection and rotation sewing;
2. signed one/two-lap return;
3. full modal tomography and kernel/gap identification;
4. tunable normalized throat weights;
5. weak nonlinear triads and forbidden-sector sentinels;
6. controlled defect mapping and work-ledger closure.

The [primary-literature comparison](admissibility_183_experimental_literature_collision_and_platform_reprioritization.md)
shows that Klein-bottle photonic and electrical parameter spaces, complex
Hamiltonian reconstruction and efficient impedance spectroscopy already
exist. The proposed apparatus should reuse those capabilities rather than
claim novelty for synthetic nonorientability alone.

Success establishes that the relational mechanism is realizable and
detectable. It does not establish that an ordinary material surface uses the
same effective dissipation.

## Material branch

A later thin-film, interfacial, hydraulic or active-fluid experiment must begin
from its physical stress and boundary laws. For a Newtonian material surface,
the primary candidate remains Boussinesq--Scriven/deformation viscosity:

\[
T_\Sigma=2\mu_s\operatorname{Def}_\Sigma u-\pi P_\Sigma.
\]

Hodge and Bochner operators remain mandatory competing fits. If one of them
fits better, the result demands a transport mechanism or reduction that
explains why cohomological-flux or connection diffusion replaces symmetric
surface strain.

Because a smooth closed Klein bottle does not embed in three-dimensional
Euclidean space, the material implementation must declare one of:

- an orientable double cover with identified sensor/actuator boundaries;
- an open physical surface plus active return map;
- a graph or metamaterial emulator;
- a self-intersecting geometry with a declared crossing law;
- or a higher-dimensional/synthetic coordinate.

Visual resemblance is not an implementation.

## Data package required from hardware

The corpus can ingest a physical result when it includes:

- connection diagram and sewing matrix;
- component-level calibration and uncertainty;
- raw complex transfer matrices;
- time-domain vector ring-downs;
- source, detector and reference phases;
- throat weights and their normalization;
- defect and rotation-control runs;
- work or scattering ledgers;
- analysis code producing the declared observables;
- unsuccessful and excluded runs with reasons.

## Genuine stopping boundary

The remaining choices cannot be settled by adding more algebra to the current
corpus. They require:

1. selection of an actual platform and its bandwidth, scale and budget;
2. engineering of phase-stable ports and switchable return wiring;
3. fabrication or assembly;
4. calibration data replacing the synthetic noise model;
5. measured transfer and ring-down records;
6. for C5, a constitutive or thin-layer derivation for that exact apparatus.

Until one of those inputs exists, larger mode bases and richer synthetic noise
models may improve engineering estimates but do not move the capability claim.

## Verdict

The experimental landscape is defined by a ladder rather than a single heroic
apparatus. The cheapest decisive next act is a coherent programmable
double-cover network with a matched rotation control. Its first non-negotiable
measurement is signed return; its strongest joined measurement is return sign,
kernel dimension, first gap, throat exponent, nonlinear leakage and work
closure. The no-equipment programme is exhausted at the platform-selection
boundary.
