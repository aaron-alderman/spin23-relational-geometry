## Result

The four-resonator quotient bench no longer consists of a Hamiltonian, damping rule and detector map placed beside one another. One Gaussian doubled action now generates them together. In frequency space its retarded operator is

\[
D_R(\omega)=(\omega+i\kappa/2)I-H,
\]

with hub drive \(Bu\), four-port detector \(y=Ca\), amplitude \(a=D_R^{-1}Bu\), dissipated power \((\kappa/2)\lVert a\rVert^2\), and positive noise kernel \(\kappa(2n+1)I\).

The parameters are inherited from AZ-059: 100 MHz common frequency, 2 MHz hub-to-outer couplings and 0.1 MHz linewidth. The exact-fold and resolved-detuning settings are both generated by the same action; only the declared control vector changes. Across 98 frequency/setting cases, the equation residual and input-loss balance residual are below numerical tolerance, while the noise kernel remains positive.

This closes a real gap. Operator, amplitude, irreversible loss, Gaussian fluctuations and calibrated complex detector ports now belong to one generative object on the engineered carrier.

## What it does not close

The hardware was deliberately programmed with the D4-shaped coupling graph. The action therefore does not derive that graph from Claimipedia relations, a material, or a fundamental field theory. It does not assign physical operators to arbitrary typed corpus edges. It also has no spatial deformation or boundary domain and cannot supply OT-046's localized singularity state.

OT-027 remains gated at a narrower point: a relation-to-operator dictionary must predict a held-out physical record rather than encode the desired incidence into the apparatus.

## Implication

The corpus can now distinguish two questions that were previously bundled. “Can one action generate the whole experimental record?” is answered yes for the four-mode engineered bench. “Why does this physical carrier realize this typed relation?” remains open. Building the bench would test the first answer prospectively and improve hidden-mode metrology. Only a source-native carrier that supplies the couplings without being programmed to imitate the graph can address the second and support any fundamental interpretation.
