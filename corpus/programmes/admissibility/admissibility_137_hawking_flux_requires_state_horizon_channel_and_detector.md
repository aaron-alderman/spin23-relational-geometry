# Hawking flux requires a state, a horizon, a channel and a detector

**Date:** 26 October 2026  
**Status:** standard semiclassical structure; information and backreaction remain open  
**Parents:** [Unruh detector closure](admissibility_136_acceleration_changes_detector_closure_not_the_global_vacuum.md), [seam/channel separation](admissibility_135_a_seam_changes_continuations_but_does_not_by_itself_trigger_decay.md)

## The causal chain

Hawking radiation is not obtained from the word *horizon*. A calculation needs at least:

\[
\boxed{\text{geometry and horizon notion}}
+\boxed{\text{quantum state}}
+\boxed{\text{field equation and mode continuation}}
+\boxed{\text{exterior scattering}}
+\boxed{\text{detector}}.
\]

For a stationary black hole the near-horizon mode relation gives

\[
T_H=\frac{\kappa}{2\pi},
\]

but the flux at infinity is filtered by spin-, frequency- and angular-momentum-dependent greybody factors. The Unruh, Hartle--Hawking and Boulware states do not describe the same physical preparation or detector record. Event, apparent, trapping and Killing horizons also answer different questions away from the stationary idealization.

## Local and global closure

Near-horizon short-distance correlations can close into a local thermal relation while the complete exterior record also depends on propagation through the curved potential and on the state at the past boundaries. A distant detector therefore sees neither the bare near-horizon spectrum nor the entire global quantum state. This is nested closure across geometry and scale, not one universal thermostat.

## Firewall

A horizon is not automatically the algebraic seam of AZ-130 or AZ-135. Crossing it does not by itself supply a transition matrix element, and outgoing radiation is not inferred merely by declaring a boundary. Conversely, Hawking mode conversion does not establish objective wavefunction collapse. Information loss, unitary evaporation, islands, remnants and backreaction are additional questions that the semiclassical temperature formula does not settle.

Analogue horizons can test kinematic mode conversion, stimulated emission and partner correlations. Without dynamical gravity and controlled backreaction they do not prove gravitational black-hole evaporation.

## Experiment

Use an analogue horizon with independently measured background flow and dispersion. Prepare vacuum-like and thermal input controls, measure both members of the correlated outgoing/partner pair, vary the horizon gradient, and propagate the measured dispersion through a no-refit scattering calculation.

### Go/no-go

- **Horizon gate:** demonstrate the relevant characteristic crossing in the measured dispersion, not from a fitted temperature alone.
- **State gate:** show that input occupation and technical noise cannot generate the reported pair covariance.
- **Channel gate:** recover the frequency-dependent scattering/greybody map and partner relation.
- **Gravity gate:** keep any gravitational-Hawking claim closed in an analogue system.
- **New-physics gate:** a residual matters only after dispersion, state preparation, finite size, loss and detector response are frozen.

## Implication

Hawking physics turns the admissibility stack into a sequence one can audit. Geometry limits possible continuations; the state populates correlations; dynamics transports them; exterior scattering reshapes them; a detector retains only a quotient. None of those layers may silently stand for all the others.
