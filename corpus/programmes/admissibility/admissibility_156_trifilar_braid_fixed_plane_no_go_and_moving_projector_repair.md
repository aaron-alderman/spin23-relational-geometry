## Result

A symmetric trifilar line contains the right four real canonical coordinates,
but a braid does not acquire geometric holonomy merely because three wires
exchange places.

The exact chain is

\[
\mathbb R^3
=\mathbb R e_c\oplus D,
\qquad
\dim D=2,
\qquad
D\oplus D^*\cong\mathbb R^4.
\]

The cyclic braid acts nontrivially inside \(D\), and its canonical lift
preserves a symplectic form. Yet the projector onto \(D\) is constant. Its
canonical parallel transport therefore has no moving-subspace holonomy. The
visible \(120^\circ\) action is an endpoint permutation or ordinary modal
transfer unless an independent control makes the admitted plane itself move.

The minimal repair is a tunable three-mode network with a moving bright vector
\(n(q)\) and a rank-two dark projector

\[
P(q)=I-n(q)n(q)^T.
\]

A declared latitude loop with \(n\cdot e_c=3/4\) encloses solid angle
\(\pi/2\) and gives the dark doublet a quarter-turn geometric return. This is
an exact target, but positive-definiteness of a proposed capacitance matrix is
not sufficient for realization by ordinary pairwise capacitors. The complete
loop crosses nodal sign constraints and requires a repaired passive synthesis,
transformer/inverter network or active emulator.

## The source-native three-conductor quotient

Choose the orthonormal basis

\[
e_c=\frac1{\sqrt3}(1,1,1),
\qquad
e_1=\frac1{\sqrt2}(1,-1,0),
\qquad
e_2=\frac1{\sqrt6}(1,1,-2).
\]

The common line is \(\mathbb R e_c\). The differential plane is

\[
D=e_c^\perp
=\{x\in\mathbb R^3:x_1+x_2+x_3=0\}
=\operatorname{span}\{e_1,e_2\}.
\]

For a floating balanced line, quotienting the common reference exposes \(D\).
With a shield or declared ground, the common sector is physical rather than
gauge, but the same modal decomposition remains useful and its leakage becomes
a measurable control.

Let \(q=(q_1,q_2)\) be differential flux coordinates and \(p=(p_1,p_2)\)
their conjugate charges. Then

\[
X=(q_1,q_2,p_1,p_2),
\qquad
\Omega=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&I_2\\
-I_2&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

This is a literal four-dimensional symplectic carrier. It is not yet the full
physical \(Sp(4,\mathbb R)\) action: a fixed passive line realizes only the
subgroup generated by its available propagation and coupling controls.

## What the cyclic braid actually does

Let \(C\) cyclically permute the conductor coordinates. It fixes \(e_c\). Its
restriction to \(D\) has determinant one, trace minus one and is therefore a
rotation through \(\pm2\pi/3\):

\[
C|_D=R(\pm 2\pi/3).
\]

The canonical lift

\[
M_C=R(\pm2\pi/3)\oplus R(\pm2\pi/3)
\]

satisfies

\[
M_C^T\Omega M_C=\Omega.
\]

Thus the braid supplies a genuine symplectic transformation of the two-mode
carrier. But the differential projector

\[
P_D=I-e_ce_c^T
\]

obeys

\[
CP_DC^T=P_D.
\]

For the ideal symmetric braid, \(P_D\) does not move at all. Writing a rotating
basis inside the same plane does not change that fact. The universal projector
connection \(P_D\,dP_D\) vanishes, so a claimed non-Abelian return inferred
only from the endpoint wire permutation is gauge or port labelling, not
moving-subspace holonomy.

This does not make twisting uninteresting. It can still produce ordinary
distributed phase, mode conversion under asymmetry, common-mode rejection,
Floquet bands and chirality-dependent coupling. Those records must be named
as such.

## LC loading creates the human-scale branch

For identical series inductors \(L\), shunt capacitances \(C_0\), cell spacing
\(a\), and a differential branch whose coupling has been diagonalized, the
ideal ladder has

\[
\omega(k)=\frac2{\sqrt{LC_0}}\sin\frac{ka}{2}.
\]

At long wavelength,

\[
v_g\approx\frac a{\sqrt{LC_0}},
\qquad
Z_0\approx\sqrt{\frac L{C_0}},
\qquad
f_{\max}=\frac1{\pi\sqrt{LC_0}}.
\]

The executable fixture uses

\[
a=0.1\,\mathrm m,
\quad L=1\,\mathrm H,
\quad C_0=100\,\mu\mathrm F,
\]

and obtains \(v_g\approx10\,\mathrm{m/s}\), \(Z_0\approx100\,\Omega\) and
\(f_{\max}\approx31.8\,\mathrm{Hz}\). A five-hertz differential wave then has
an approximate two-metre wavelength. Real winding resistance, dielectric
loss, mutual coupling and prompt electromagnetic crosstalk must be measured;
the circuit creates a slow guided branch, not a new causal speed limit.

## The moving-projector repair

Use coordinates \((e_1,e_2,e_c)\) and define

\[
n(\phi)
=s\cos\phi\,e_1+s\sin\phi\,e_2+c\,e_c,
\qquad
c=\frac34,
\qquad
s=\frac{\sqrt7}{4}.
\]

Now

\[
P(\phi)=I-n(\phi)n(\phi)^T
\]

is a moving rank-two plane in the three-conductor space. It closes at
\(2\pi\) but is not constant. The solid angle enclosed by \(n(\phi)\) is

\[
\Omega_{S^2}=2\pi(1-c)=\frac\pi2.
\]

Parallel transport in the dark plane therefore returns its frame by a quarter
turn, up to the chosen orientation convention. Classical resonant oscillators
can simulate this degenerate adiabatic geometry, so the target does not
require a quantum interpretation.

A candidate quadratic control is

\[
K(\phi)=k_dP(\phi)+k_b n(\phi)n(\phi)^T,
\qquad k_d,k_b>0.
\]

It has one bright eigenvector and a degenerate dark doublet. A slow control
loop changes the projector while preserving its rank and gap.

## Passive realization is an additional gate

A symmetric positive matrix is a valid quadratic energy, but not every such
matrix is the nodal capacitance matrix of a network made only from positive
pairwise and ground capacitors. In that representation, off-diagonal entries
must be nonpositive, and row sums encode nonnegative ground capacitances.

The evidence fixture tests the simple family with \(k_d=2\), \(k_b=1\). It is
positive definite throughout the loop but violates the direct-capacitor nodal
sign condition at some phases. Consequently

\[
\boxed{\text{positive moving projector}\not\Rightarrow
\text{ordinary capacitor synthesis}.}
\]

The repair options were experimental branches at this stage:

1. test whether a different passive LC embedding with auxiliary nodes can
   realize the required effective family;
2. use transformers or impedance inverters with a complete loss ledger;
3. synthesize the coupling actively and treat supply power/noise as ports;
4. enlarge the carrier so a physically admissible projector loop exists
   without violating nodal signs.

No holonomy claim is admitted until the realized matrix family, not merely the
desired eigenvectors, is measured.

**Successor result.** [AZ-157](admissibility_157_transformer_synthesis_closes_the_trifilar_matrix_but_opens_a_control_work_port.md)
closes the first option negatively: Kron reduction preserves the positive-
capacitor sign class. It closes the second algebraically with an exact ideal
multiport-transformer factorization, while leaving component bandwidth,
parasitics and any time-dependent controller work as physical gates.

## Experimental sequence

### Stage A: characterize the bare braid

Measure the full three-port impedance or scattering matrix and transform it to
the \((e_c,e_1,e_2)\) basis. Test common/differential leakage, degeneracy,
reciprocity, handedness reversal and pitch dependence. The expected ideal
result is the fixed-plane no-go, not a quarter-turn.

### Stage B: calibrate the slow LC carrier

Measure cellwise amplitude and phase, fit the discrete dispersion, and
separate the slow branch from prompt capacitive or radiative feedthrough by
length scaling. Close stored electric energy, stored magnetic energy,
resistive heat and output-port power.

### Stage C: make the projector move

Implement a sequence of calibrated coupling matrices approximating
\(K(\phi)\). At every setting measure poles and the full matrix residues,
reconstruct the bright vector and dark projector, and verify a nonzero gap.
Transport both differential basis inputs around the loop, subtract calibrated
dynamical phase, reverse the loop and test the quarter-turn return without
refitting.

### Stage D: attach stochastic membrane loads

Only after Stage C closes should the nonlinear Markov loads from AZ-155 be
added. Then braid geometry, external ELF forcing, channel noise and resource
recovery can be varied independently. This tests field-mediated phase locking
without identifying the emulator with a brain.

## Corpus implication

The trifilar construction realizes an important correction to “three relations
make a detector.” Three conductors first produce a common line and a real
differential doublet. The doublet and its conjugate variables form a valid
symplectic carrier. But triality of labels, a cyclic permutation and even a
symplectic lift do not force non-Abelian geometry. Holonomy appears only when
the admitted plane changes relative to a larger carrier and the detector can
reconstruct that change.

## Verdict

The LC-loaded trifilar braid remains a human-scale next device, but its
first job is to demonstrate the fixed-plane no-go. The simplest braid gives a
real \(C_3\) action and useful modal physics, not geometric return. A tunable
moving bright direction supplies the exact quarter-turn repair. AZ-157 closes
the ideal passive matrix factorization and moves the remaining construction
gate to finite-band transformer hardware and control-work accounting. This
converts the braid from visual analogy into a falsifiable three-port programme.
