## Why nuclear physics is not a side issue

Nuclei are finite, strongly interacting many-body systems with exceptionally rich and separately measurable ways to remain, rearrange and leave. They are therefore a control laboratory for the corpus before any claim of new electroweak dynamics:

- isomers test selection bottlenecks and delayed records;
- shape coexistence tests several locally stable organizations on one microscopic carrier;
- fission tests barrier passage, collective coordinates and first-passage descriptions;
- compound-nucleus resonances test dense spectra, overlapping poles and channel opening;
- gamma emission, beta decay, particle emission and internal conversion test competing transition operators and detector branches.

None is generically “collapse at a seam.” The parent spectrum, selection rule, coupling, continuum density, branching fraction and detector efficiency remain different entries.

## Open-system form

With parent space (P) and channel space (Q), Feshbach elimination gives

\[
H_{\rm eff}(E)=H_{PP}+H_{PQ}(E-H_{QQ}+i0)^{-1}H_{QP}
=H_{PP}+\Delta(E)-\frac{i}{2}\Gamma(E).
\]

The real shift and imaginary width inherit thresholds and channel structure. In dense resonance regions, widths can redistribute into broad and trapped combinations. This use of *superradiant* concerns open-system resonance segregation and must not be confused with Kerr rotational superradiance merely because the word is shared.

## A realistic three-arm programme

Choose one nucleus with a long-lived isomer, an accessible compound-nucleus resonance region and at least two competing observed exit modes. Combine:

1. energy-resolved neutron or charged-particle entrance spectroscopy;
2. prompt gamma/particle/fission coincidence measurements;
3. delayed isomer and internal-conversion tagging with calibrated dead time and efficiency.

Fit one parent-plus-channel (R)-matrix or effective-Hamiltonian model to the entrance and a subset of exits, then predict held-out branching, angular correlations and delay distributions. Change entrance spin/parity or energy while retaining the same detector model.

### Go/no-go

- **Build gate:** reproduce known energies, spins, parities and conservation laws before interpreting widths.
- **Bridge gate:** separately vary a parent structural coordinate and a channel coupling; if all effects are absorbed into one fitted width, the seam/channel distinction is untested.
- **Record gate:** close the efficiency, missed-channel, feeding and coincidence ledger.
- **Universality gate:** require the same mechanism across at least two nuclei or two channel families without nucleus-specific relabelling.
- **New-physics gate:** promote only a replicated, symmetry-specific residual outside modern nuclear structure, reaction and detector covariance. Otherwise retain the gain as theoretical engineering.

## Implication

Nuclear physics makes the corpus less speculative by giving it hostile controls. If the seam/channel/record grammar cannot clarify isomers, overlapping resonances and competing decay branches, it should not be exported to W/Z physics. If it does clarify them, the immediate payoff is better experimental design and model separation; only a surviving residual earns the name new physics.
