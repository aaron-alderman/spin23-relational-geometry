## Executive verdict

Mazurk's Meaning-Inertia (M-I) proposal is a close conceptual neighbour of the admissibility programme. Both treat lawful continuation as more primitive than a pre-given dynamical description; both emphasize compatibility, refusal, causal order, persistent records or bindings, and the distinction between an ontological constitution and equations of motion.

The overlap is useful but does not close a physics gate. M-I currently postulates the structures that the corpus asks admissibility to derive. Its state space, binding relation, participation scalar, reference mass, causal order, entropy ledger and projection map are not constructed. Its empirical handles do not yet follow from an action, constitutive law or measurement dictionary. The paper should therefore enter the corpus as an **external ontological comparator and source of formalization questions**, not as evidence for Spin(2,3), emergent spacetime, modified inertia, vacuum-energy sequestering or cosmological acceleration.

The strongest import is one proposed invariant:

> a transformation that preserves every binding relation should preserve participation.

The first productive task is to make that sentence mathematical and test whether it has nontrivial consequences not already encoded in the definition of binding.

## What the source actually proposes

The paper introduces a substrate prior to spacetime in the order of constraint rather than in time. A system with internal state (s) is assigned a participation scalar (gamma(s)), with

\[
m_i(s)=m_g(s)=\gamma(s)m_0.
\]

Here (m_0) is a reference mass under full participation. The paper's principal impossibility says that reversible, descriptive or otherwise binding-preserving transformations cannot alter (gamma). Changes in participation must instead accompany creation, destruction or redistribution of binding constraints.

The paper also requires global energy-momentum accounting, substrate causality, binding irreversibility, locality of projection, nondecreasing total entropy and refusal thresholds. It suggests state-dependent free-fall, redshift, inertial-response, vacuum-energy and coherence tests while explicitly declining to specify a mechanism or control scheme.

These are ontological commitments and research prompts. They are not yet a quantitative model.

## Anti-tautology audit

The foundational programme requires a prior space, an exact gate, provenance for the gate, a demonstrated reduction and a novel consequence. On the current M-I presentation:

| Contract item | Current M-I answer | Missing work |
|---|---|---|
| Prior space | systems with internal states and binding relations | define systems, states and bindings without importing mechanics or thermodynamics |
| Gate | binding-preserving changes cannot alter participation | give a decidable preservation criterion and at least one rejected composition |
| Provenance | binding relevance is treated as ontologically primitive | show why this axiom is narrower than the desired inertia law rather than a restatement of it |
| Reduction | some freely rebinding counterfactual worlds are declared inadmissible | exhibit two otherwise coherent models separated by the gate |
| Novel consequence | possible state dependence and vacuum filtering | derive a no-refit observable or protected structural theorem |

The source therefore satisfies the corpus category **ontological conjecture**. It does not yet satisfy **reconstruction result**, **model-local physical result** or **empirically admitted**.

## The participation-scalar observability obstruction

The equation (m_i=m_g=\gamma m_0) does not by itself predict differential free fall. In a prescribed weak external field,

\[
m_i\mathbf a=m_g\mathbf g
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\mathbf a=\frac{m_g}{m_i}\mathbf g=\mathbf g.
\]

If the same (gamma(s)) multiplies inertial and passive gravitational mass, it cancels from the acceleration. A comparison of two internal states therefore remains universal at this level. The paper's first proposed null test needs at least one additional structure:

1. a source law in which active gravitational mass does not cancel;
2. a nongravitational inertial calibration tied to independently measured energy;
3. a transition rule that predicts how (gamma) changes during preparation;
4. or a metric/clock coupling from which a redshift anomaly follows.

The same issue propagates to the other suggested tests. Oscillator sidebands require a dynamical coupling and modulation law. Clock redshift requires a transition-energy and metric dictionary. Coherence dependence requires an operational, basis-independent definition of the relevant binding change. Without these additions, the proposed tests name experimental genres but do not calculate observables.

This obstruction is productive: it identifies the smallest bridge that M-I lacks. Participation must be more than a scalar label; it must enter a typed source-response law.

## Conservation and covariance gate

"Global accounting" is not yet a conservation law. A gravitational realization must supply a covariant stress tensor and field equation whose identity structure enforces compatible conservation. If participation changes locally, the model must state where the compensating energy-momentum resides and how it propagates. The source's claim that local participation changes need not create global geometric back-reaction is acceptable only if constraint equations and causal propagation remain satisfied.

A minimum local model would need objects such as

\[
S[g,\psi,\gamma,\ldots],
\qquad
\nabla_\mu T_{\mathrm{total}}^{\mu\nu}=0,
\qquad
\frac{\delta S}{\delta\gamma}=0,
\]

or a comparably explicit non-action framework. Merely assigning a compensating "binding cost" does not show that the accounting closes.

## Irreversibility and records

The phrase "binding irreversibility" combines at least three distinct claims:

- microscopic transformations may or may not be reversible;
- macroscopic records may be stable under a declared coarse-graining;
- destroying an established correlation may require work or export entropy.

These are not interchangeable. Reversible microscopic dynamics can support effectively irreversible record formation, while physical bindings can be undone with costs computed in ordinary thermodynamics. M-I needs a state space, measure, coarse-graining and cost functional before entropy monotonicity or irreversible binding can be evaluated. Until then, the proposal usefully emphasizes records but does not derive the thermodynamic arrow.

## Vacuum energy and cosmology boundary

The paper's vacuum-energy and de Sitter claims are the point at which conceptual compatibility ends.

The corpus already has an explicit vacuum audit. Its tested radiative portal predicts a large negative vacuum; conditional sequestering can algebraically remove constant loop shifts only by adding global or four-form structure, leaves the small positive residual unset and breaks the direct portal-curvature relation. Saying that vacuum energy "does not gravitate naively" at a participation level supplies none of those missing equations.

The sign boundary also matters. The direct (Spin_0(2,3)) spacetime interpretation is anti-de Sitter, whereas observed late-time acceleration is de Sitter-like. The exact (H_2(\mathbb R)_+) result selects a time-oriented (1+2) Lorentz cone and its (Spin_0(2,3)) conformal carrier; it does not identify that carrier coordinate with a laboratory or cosmological clock. M-I's default de Sitter projection cannot be inferred from this mathematics.

Accordingly:

- M-I does not solve the corpus's vacuum-energy gate;
- the corpus does not supply M-I's de Sitter claim;
- a future bridge would require a covariant background equation, perturbations and a likelihood-level comparison with expansion, recombination, lensing and structure data.

## Formal kernel worth testing

The most charitable precise reading begins with a context (C), a structured binding object (B(C)), and a class of admissible morphisms. Let

\[
f:(C,B(C))\longrightarrow(C',B(C'))
\]

be binding-preserving when it carries every declared binding and composition relation to its counterpart. M-I then proposes an invariant

\[
\gamma(C)=\gamma(C')
\quad\text{for every binding-preserving isomorphism }f.
\]

This is mathematically coherent but initially weak: every isomorphism invariant has the same form. The scientific content lies in answering four harder questions.

1. Which binding structures are physically available without assuming inertia?
2. Does composition force a nontrivial invariant (gamma), or may it remain constant?
3. What change of (B) induces what change of (gamma)?
4. Through which source-response map does (gamma) become observable?

The corpus should not identify (gamma) with determinant, trace, Casimir, entropy, action, mass or a Jordan-cone coordinate by analogy. Each proposed identification must pass typing, dimensional, covariance and observability checks.

## Executable research packet

### MI1 - Binding models and nontrivial quotient

Construct at least two finite binding systems with the same number of states but different composition data. Define binding-preserving morphisms and compute their automorphism groups and quotient invariants.

**Pass:** the preservation rule excludes explicit maps and produces an invariant not inserted as a label.  
**Failure:** "binding" is chosen after the fact so that any desired scalar is invariant.

### MI2 - Composition law for participation

Specify how (gamma) behaves for independent composition, correlated composition, restriction and reversible unbinding. Test multiplicative, additive and intensive alternatives rather than choosing one by rhetoric.

**Pass:** coherence across composition selects or sharply restricts the law.  
**Failure:** every context requires an independent (gamma).

### MI3 - Minimal covariant realization

Build the smallest model in which a state-dependent scalar modifies an observable source or response while total stress is covariantly conserved and no ghost, acausal mode or free-energy loophole appears.

**Pass:** a well-posed model yields a calculable observable and a controlled GR/standard-physics limit.  
**Failure:** (gamma) cancels from all observables or violates conservation, stability or equivalence tests.

### MI4 - Standard-accounting subtraction

For one state-preparation protocol, enumerate ordinary mass-energy, stress, electromagnetic, thermal, chemical, elastic and environmental contributions before assigning an M-I remainder.

**Pass:** the model predicts a residual with fixed sign or scaling that cannot be absorbed into conventional accounting.  
**Failure:** (gamma) is only a relabelling of total energy or an unconstrained nuisance function.

### MI5 - Cosmology only after local closure

Do not use M-I to explain vacuum energy or acceleration until MI3 supplies a covariant local model. Then derive the homogeneous background, linear perturbations and the mapping to observables.

**Pass:** one parameter set survives local, background and perturbation constraints and predicts something beyond a fitted cosmological constant.  
**Failure:** "idle participation" merely renames de Sitter expansion or sequestering without a residual prediction.

## Integration rule

Meaning-Inertia may be cited in this programme for three purposes:

1. as an independent articulation of admissibility, binding and refusal as ontological primitives;
2. as motivation to test whether an invariant of binding-preserving transformations can be reconstructed;
3. as a warning that empirical test lists require a coupling dictionary before they become predictions.

It may not be cited as evidence that inertia varies, that the equivalence principle has been explained, that spacetime emerges, that vacuum energy is sequestered, that de Sitter geometry is selected, or that (Spin_0(2,3)) has acquired a microscopic interpretation.

## Conclusion

In combination with the existing corpus, M-I is most valuable where it is least ambitious physically. It gives the admissibility programme a crisp external formulation of **refusal under binding-preserving continuation**. The corpus, in return, supplies the anti-tautology contract, type discipline, conservation gates, vacuum-energy audit and observable-descent requirements needed to turn that formulation into research.

The combined result is not a new theory of inertia. It is a better-posed question:

> Can a nontrivial participation invariant and its source-response law be derived from independently specified binding and composition data, or does Meaning-Inertia reduce to a philosophical relabelling of ordinary mass-energy and admissibility constraints?

That question is narrow enough to fail, and therefore strong enough to investigate.
