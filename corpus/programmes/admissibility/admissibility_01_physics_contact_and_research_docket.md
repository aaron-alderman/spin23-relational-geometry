# Admissibility foundations: physics contact and research docket

**Date:** 23 October 2026  
**Status:** boundary ledger and staged research plan  
**Parent:** [Foundational programme](admissibility_00_foundational_programme.md)

## Purpose

The foundational thesis is broad enough to drift into metaphysics, relabelling or unconstrained model building. This ledger keeps the programme in contact with the limits of physics by pairing every generative question with an established target, a forbidden shortcut and a decisive test.

## Contact rule

Every work item must maintain the chain

\[
\text{thin primitive}
\longrightarrow
\text{admission gate}
\longrightarrow
\text{derived structure}
\longrightarrow
\text{known-physics limit}
\longrightarrow
\text{discriminating test}.
\]

If any arrow is absent, the item remains a philosophical prompt or mathematical analogy and must not be reported as a physical derivation.

## Staged construction

### Docket 1 — Formalize contextual admissibility

Define a category, relation algebra, operational theory or other minimal setting in which \(\operatorname{Adm}(x\mid C)\) supports restriction, composition and extension. Determine whether admission is a predicate, a weight, an order relation, a closure operator or a family of these.

**Deliverable:** explicit axioms, at least two nonisomorphic models and at least one impossible composition.  
**Stop condition:** the formalism is equivalent to declaring an arbitrary preferred subset and has no invariant content.

### Docket 2 — Prove a first reconstruction theorem

Choose one narrow target: convex state structure, an ordered vector space, a Jordan state cone, a complex Hilbert representation, or a causal partial order. State exactly which primitives and gates force it and classify nearby survivors.

**Deliverable:** theorem or exhaustive finite classification with assumptions exposed.  
**Stop condition:** the target appears only because an isomorphic version was included among the axioms.

### Docket 3 — Composition stress test

Test whether independently admissible subsystems remain admissible under tensoring, coupling, coarse-graining, feedback and restriction. Track local-to-global obstructions and contextual dependence.

**Deliverable:** closure table plus counterexamples.  
**Stop condition:** composition must be changed separately for every desired physical example.

### Docket 4 — Probability and record formation

Build the smallest model in which alternatives, interference, stable records and repeated frequencies coexist. Compare epistemic updating with an ontological admission event without conflating them.

**Deliverable:** an explicit repeated-trial model and a derived probability law.  
**Stop condition:** observed frequencies are inserted as weights or the unique outcome problem is only renamed.

### Docket 5 — Causal and geometric emergence

Ask what restriction on composable influence produces a cone or partial order, what additional data recover intervals, and whether dimension and signature are selected. Begin with finite examples before taking a continuum limit.

**Deliverable:** discrete model, reconstruction map and continuum control statement.  
**Stop condition:** Lorentzian geometry is required to define the supposedly pregeometric gate.

### Docket 6 — Dynamics rather than survivor kinematics

Determine whether an admission-preserving flow is unique, extremal or universal under coarse-graining. Separate reversible microscopic composition from irreversible effective records.

**Deliverable:** a nontrivial flow, its invariant domain, stability analysis and observable response.  
**Stop condition:** infinitely many inequivalent dynamics survive with no selector or measurable universality.

### Docket 7 — Join to the corpus laboratories

Apply the formalism to three already controlled settings:

1. positive two-current spectral response and its \(H_2(\mathbb R)_+\) cone;
2. anomaly-free Furey matter selections and their missing action/intertwiner;
3. the healthy classical/EFT torsion sector and its quantum-closure obstruction.

The objective is not to make all three instances of one object. It is to determine whether one typed notion of admissibility explains why their promotion gates have the same logical form while preserving their distinct physical content.

**Deliverable:** three type-correct translations and a list of structure that does not transfer.  
**Stop condition:** the common language erases the exact distinctions on which the existing no-go results depend.

### Docket 8 — Extract a discriminating consequence

Only after the reconstruction and contact tasks are complete should the programme seek a new prediction. Candidate outputs include a forbidden transition, a protected ratio, a dimension or signature selection, a restricted response cone, or a finite consistency residue.

**Deliverable:** preregistered observable with normalization, nuisance model and no-refit comparator.  
**Stop condition:** the effect is a generic consequence of positivity, passive response, Gaussian elimination or another known mechanism rather than of the new admissibility principle.

## Three scales of “possible”

The programme should never use “possible” without a qualifier:

1. **Formally possible:** expressible without contradiction inside a chosen mathematical language.
2. **Physically admissible:** compatible with the full declared stack of type, positivity, causality, dynamics, global consistency and known empirical limits.
3. **Actually realized:** selected or instantiated in our world and supported by observation.

The first does not imply the second, and the second does not imply the third. A central task is to discover whether the gap between them has lawful structure rather than being an unexplained act of selection.

## Status vocabulary

Every output in this programme must use one of the following labels:

- **definition:** fixes language only;
- **formal consequence:** follows inside stated axioms;
- **reconstruction:** derives a familiar structure from weaker assumptions;
- **model-local physical result:** holds in a typed physical model;
- **empirically admitted:** survives a declared comparison with data;
- **ontological conjecture:** proposes what reality fundamentally is;
- **failed promotion:** a real lower-level result that does not pass the next gate.

No result may move upward because it is elegant, recurrent or compatible with the corpus narrative.

## Immediate first packet

The first executable packet should remain deliberately small:

1. define contextual admission as a closure system over typed composable relations;
2. construct the smallest finite nonclassical example and its nearest classical comparator;
3. ask whether positivity is derived, primitive or contextual in each;
4. enumerate all admission-preserving transformations;
5. identify what extra assumption first creates probabilities, causal order or continuous geometry;
6. translate the result into the two-current response laboratory without calling that laboratory fundamental spacetime;
7. publish the assumption ledger and failed alternatives alongside any positive result.

This packet can fail cleanly. A clean failure would establish which familiar physical ingredient cannot be generated from the proposed thin packet and must either be accepted as primitive or approached through a different reconstruction.

## Governing question

The programme is organized by one question:

> How much of the physical world can be obtained by requiring possibilities to remain jointly realizable under every legitimate change of context, composition and scale—and what irreducible physical input remains when that requirement has done all it can?

That final remainder is not an embarrassment. Locating it precisely is one of the programme's principal scientific outputs.
