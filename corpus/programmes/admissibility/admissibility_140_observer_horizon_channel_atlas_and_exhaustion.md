## Stop rules and exhaustion claim

The current-input analytical tranche is exhausted when every named phenomenon has a typed carrier, boundary, channel, detector, nuisance model and decision rule. That condition is now met at programme level. It does **not** mean the physics is exhausted. Each unfilled item above is a physical or mathematical dependency, and no amount of additional analogy closes it.

### Go/no-go

- If a proposed bridge preserves only verbal shape, retain it as a picture.
- If it reproduces a known equation but adds no held-out record, retain it as theoretical engineering.
- If it survives conventional models and independent detectors with a source-derived map, promote it to a new-physics candidate.

## Endpoint

The shape of this frontier is layered rather than circular: observer access sits inside a field theory; a horizon constrains propagation; a channel carries amplitude; a detector retains a record. Hope becomes a work instruction: look where changing one layer cannot be mimicked by retuning the others.
