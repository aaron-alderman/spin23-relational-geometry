## Result

Claimipedia's research-method lane now supplies four source-native scientific paths that the active Sourcery host admits under `scientific-derivation-composition/1`. They cover a fixed-support local theorem, an operator-range dispatcher, a rank-change approach-arc dispatcher and a boundary-measure dispatcher. Each path has four named operations. Together they retain 16 distinct steps, their assumptions, irreversible losses, evidence references and proposal-only authority.

The test is not merely positive-example playback. Fourteen deliberately overstrong queries return no candidates, and fifteen invalid transformations remain visible in rejection receipts. The live host therefore preserves both the usable route and the reason nearby shortcuts fail.

## Why this does not contradict the zero-of-43 result

[Wave 7.72](../frontier/wave-7/frontier_wave7_72_claimipedia_sourcery_typed_extraction_no_go.md) tested the pinned Claimipedia relationship capture. Its 43 edges say that one claim supports, challenges, qualifies or depends on another. None is authored as a scientific operation with exact endpoints, assumptions, losses, evidence and bounded authority. The direct extraction count remains zero of 43.

This packet tests a different source object: the D02 research-method graphs. Their edges were written as scientific transformations. A relationship such as “qualifies” is not silently relabelled as an operator; the scientific operation is separately named, typed and evidenced. Thus both conclusions are true:

- the generic epistemic graph does not secretly contain a Sourcery algebra;
- a source-native Claimipedia scientific workflow can be made executable without an opaque uniform wrapper.

The difference is the reason attached to each movement. Sourcery can compose a path because the author has said what changes, what must be assumed, what is lost and which evidence licenses the step.

## The open protocol boundary

The active host returned protocol v1. The stricter local contract, `scientific-derivation-composition/common-algebra-2`, additionally checks exact SI base-exponent dimensions on every object and transformation through `fromDimension`, `toDimension`, `dimensionRule` and `dimensionDelta`. [AZ-083](admissibility_83_first_common_algebra2_path_and_legacy_dimension_projection.md) now supplies those fields for the primary local-theorem path and passes the stricter contract. The operator, rank-change and boundary families still lack that migration.

This matters most where a formal transformation is intended to touch physics. A path can be semantically well typed while still failing to say whether it maps voltage to voltage, response to response, energy to action, or a dimensionless claim record to another claim record. The dimension ledger prevents a persuasive path from crossing those boundaries invisibly.

## Open and closed

Closed:

- at least one nontrivial source-native Claimipedia path can pass unchanged live Sourcery admission rules;
- four path families retain assumption, loss, evidence and authority ledgers;
- negative controls and invalid-rule receipts survive composition;
- the bridge is more than generic graph programmability.

Open:

- migration of the remaining operator, rank-change and boundary families to `common-algebra-2` with explicit dimension conventions;
- a product-, norm- or rank-preserving translation that earns the name Jordan rather than borrowing it;
- a physical operator or action that turns a workflow relation into dynamics;
- a held-out detector record that distinguishes the proposed operator from an ordinary programmable encoding.

OT-026 and OT-027 therefore remain open. Their frontier has moved: the question is no longer whether any source-native executable bridge exists, but whether one survives dimensional completion and predicts structure not supplied by its wrapper.

## Implication and look-here instruction

This is a genuine theoretical-engineering improvement. Claimipedia can retain why a step is warranted; Sourcery can test whether a chain of such steps composes without erasing assumptions or losses. That gives the corpus an executable reasoning seam rather than a visual analogy between graphs.

Look next at one path whose quantities already have unambiguous units. Add exact SI exponent vectors and normalization conventions, run it unchanged through `common-algebra-2`, then withhold one composition. If the surviving path predicts that composition, a product identity or a calibrated detector record, the bridge becomes scientifically useful. If it only reproduces metadata written into the wrapper, the physical and Jordan readings remain closed. New physics begins only after conventional typed workflow, dimensions, operator assignment and detector folding have all failed to explain a reproducible residue.
