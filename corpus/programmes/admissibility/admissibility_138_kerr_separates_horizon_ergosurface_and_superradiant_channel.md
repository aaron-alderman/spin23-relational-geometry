## The rotating geometry has several different boundaries

For Kerr mass (M) and spin parameter (a_K),

\[
r_\pm=M\pm\sqrt{M^2-a_K^2},\qquad
\Omega_H=\frac{a_K}{r_+^2+a_K^2},\qquad
\kappa=\frac{r_+-r_-}{2(r_+^2+a_K^2)}.
\]

The event horizon (r_+), stationary-limit surface, ergoregion, light rings and quasinormal-mode boundary conditions are not synonyms. At the equator the stationary-limit surface lies at (2M), outside the horizon for nonzero spin; at the poles they meet. The audit evaluates this separation across four spins.

The horizon generator is

\[
\chi=\partial_t+\Omega_H\partial_\phi.
\]

A neutral bosonic mode has horizon-frame frequency

\[
\widetilde\omega=\omega-m\Omega_H.
\]

For (0<\omega<m\Omega_H), flux conservation permits reflected amplitude greater than the incident amplitude: rotational energy leaves through a superradiant scattering channel. The threshold is a relation among mode labels and horizon rotation, not the location of the ergosurface and not a decay event caused by crossing it.

## Relation to the corpus

Kerr gives a physically exact example in which one generator mixes time translation and rotation, while the sign of a mode frequency changes relative to that generator. That resembles the corpus's interest in mixed generators and changed continuations. It does **not** identify the Kerr generator with the local (T_{\rm e}/T_{\rm h}) cell, derive (Spin_0(2,3)), or turn the superradiant bound into the AZ-135 seam. A source-derived map would have to preserve the metric, representation, symplectic flux and boundary conditions—not merely the two-term shape.

## Experiment and observation

The near-term programme is a joint Kerr consistency fit, not a search for a group name: use inspiral/precession to estimate mass and spin, ringdown to estimate complex mode frequencies, and a scattering or environmental channel to constrain spin-dependent amplification or absorption. Laboratory rotating-fluid, optical or condensate analogues can validate the scattering logic with controllable dispersion.

### Go/no-go

- **GR gate:** mass and spin inferred from independent records must predict the horizon frequency and ringdown spectrum without channel-specific retuning.
- **Boundary gate:** distinguish horizon, stationary limit, light ring and detector photosphere in the model.
- **Channel gate:** require the (m)- and frequency-dependent sign change around (\omega=m\Omega_H); generic gain is insufficient.
- **Environment gate:** profile accretion, plasma, surrounding matter, calibration and waveform systematics.
- **Corpus bridge gate:** no Spin/Jordan promotion without an explicit intertwiner and a held-out prediction.
