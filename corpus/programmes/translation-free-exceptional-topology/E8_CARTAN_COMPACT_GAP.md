\[\Delta_{\rm cell}\simeq0.1019267219\quad(U=u=1),\]

sine-bright; the second is even and cosine-bright. Phase-quadrature drive is
therefore required to measure the actual gap.

This does not establish a rigorous lower bound, thermodynamic coupled-wire
gap, (c_-=8), the 240-current shell or a material realization. The next join
must sweep coupling and perturbations, then retain this cell spectrum while
increasing wire width and measuring the edge/current records.
