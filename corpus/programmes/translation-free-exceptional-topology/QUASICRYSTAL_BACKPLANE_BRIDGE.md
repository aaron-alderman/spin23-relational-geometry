## The admissible join

The geometric-crystals programme supplies a plausible material and fabrication
chassis for the abstract E8 clock array:

\[
\text{quasicrystal backplane}
\longrightarrow
\text{eight addressable wave channels}
\longrightarrow
K_{E_8}\text{ mixer}
\longrightarrow
\text{two-local locks}
\longrightarrow
\text{edge and current tomography}.
\]

The arrows are typed. Long-range quasiperiodic order supplies broken
translation symmetry, phason coordinates and a nonperiodic response medium.
It does not supply E8 by itself. The independently calibrated eight-port mixer
supplies the exceptional integral relation. The clock locks supply a finite
gapped carrier. Only boundary transport and held-out current products can
promote that carrier toward the continuum E8 edge.

This separation rescues the experimentally useful content of the source
proposal while preventing three shortcuts:

- icosahedral diffraction is not E8 current algebra;
- a six-dimensional cut-and-project description is not an eight-dimensional
  physical carrier;
- an (H_4\)-inside-(E_8) mathematical relation is not a material
  Hamiltonian.

## What the apparatus becomes

The first device need not be a gravitational experiment or an exotic alloy.
Use a reconfigurable acoustic, microwave, photonic or electromechanical
quasiperiodic backplane with seven irregular transverse layers and eight
addressable modes per layer. Apply the same source calibration and two-local
locking schedule already certified by the E8 clock protocol. The two exposed
eight-mode boundaries provide the readout surface.

Acoustic electrodeposition remains a fabrication candidate, but it enters at
the lowest claim level. A visible fivefold field pattern proves only field
shaping. Diffraction must establish quasiperiodic order; phason-sensitive
spectroscopy must then distinguish the quasicrystal from its periodic
approximant and amorphous control before exceptional measurements begin.

## Measurement ladder

1. **M0 structure:** diffraction and imaging establish periodic,
   quasiperiodic or amorphous class independently.
2. **M1 phasons:** separate a phason coordinate and linewidth from ordinary
   phonon and mounting modes.
3. **M2 relation:** recover the full complex eight-port identity or E8 transfer
   matrix within frozen calibration error.
4. **M3 carrier:** measure the bulk gap and oriented boundary transfer across
   disorder and finite size.
5. **M4 exceptional detector:** fit eight simple currents, then score all 232
   nonsimple E8 currents as held-out predictions.
6. **M5 material interaction:** compare coherence, gap survival, nonlinear
   mixing, thermal and electrical response using the primary contrast (C).
7. **M6 precision residual:** only after M0--M5 pass, open gravimeter,
   interferometer or clock channels behind electromagnetic, acoustic, thermal,
   vibrational and Newtonian background models.

## Gravity and superconductivity firewall

Standard general relativity already couples to the complete expectation value
of the stress-energy tensor. Material order can change ordinary stresses,
energy flow and electromagnetic fields; low entropy is not an additional
source term by itself. Any proposed organization-dependent coefficient must
therefore be defined covariantly and tested only on residuals left after the
standard tensor and apparatus backgrounds are modeled.

Quasicrystal superconductivity is real but currently occurs at material-
specific temperatures; it does not establish a route to room-temperature
superconductivity. Superconducting transitions remain valuable material and
coherence measurements, not a promised trajectory.
