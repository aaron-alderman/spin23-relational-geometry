## Earliest useful result

The earliest publishable result need not reach Monster. A complete result is:

> A translation-free local Hamiltonian retains a certified real-space
> topological index and bulk--boundary response across a disorder tube, while
> a declared finer edge observable is lost before the bulk topology changes.

That would experimentally and mathematically separate stable topology from
reconstructive boundary memory—the exact distinction needed by the larger
programme.

## Stop rules

- Stop calling a Hall response topological if no real-space invariant or
  protected boundary response can be defined.
- Stop the \(E_8\) promotion if a smaller unimodular edge theory spans every
  measured record.
- Stop the Monster promotion if the data factor through central charge,
  untwined character or a smaller defect quotient.
- Retain a negative result as a quantified enrichment-loss boundary rather
  than weakening detector or provenance requirements.
