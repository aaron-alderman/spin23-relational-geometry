Above $J=0.1$, the data are diagnostic. The product-vacuum one-particle band crosses zero between $J=0.25$ and 0.3, while the exact finite many-body gap remains positive. This is a coordinate failure: the dressed vacuum has reorganized enough that excitation energy relative to the *bare* product vacuum is no longer physical. It is not evidence that a phase transition occurred, and it does not license a strong-coupling claim without renewed local-root and width convergence.

The corridor is sampled, not continuous. Its practical control variables are coupling $J$, odd-drive pole frequency, oscillator strength and excess excitation count. A device leaves the weak-dressing regime when the latter two begin to erode or the one-particle estimate ceases to track the pole, even if a finite-size gap remains visible.
