# Prophetic-admissibility annotation manual v0

## Purpose

This manual prevents an interpreter from changing the target after learning the outcome. Annotators encode source and outcome records independently. They do not decide whether God caused or authorized a statement.

## Source-side record

Annotate only what a pre-outcome reader could recover from the frozen source.

| Field | Values |
|---|---|
| Speech authority | divine first person; attributed divine message; vision; calculation; commentary; institutional forecast |
| Modality | will; must; may; likely; no-earlier-than; no-later-than; conditional warning |
| Agent | named; typed; collective; absent |
| Action | named; bounded class; broad change; absent |
| Object/place | named; bounded class; broad region; absent |
| Time form | exact date; closed interval; lower bound; upper bound; relative duration; sequence only; absent |
| Condition | explicit repentance/obedience condition; implicit covenant condition; unconditional in wording |
| Transformation | literal; day--year; day--millennium; week--year; generation; jubilee; typological recurrence; other |
| Anchor provenance | explicit in source; independently historical; interpreter-selected; outcome-selected |

Record every alternate reading visible before the outcome. Do not collapse alternatives into the one later judged successful.

## Outcome-side record

Outcome annotators receive the fulfilment predicate but not the preferred theology or interpretive lineage. Record:

- event start and end interval;
- agents, action, object and place;
- independent sources and their dependencies;
- whether the event is ordinary at the declared breadth;
- whether the forecast caused or materially shaped the outcome;
- missing components and contrary components; and
- whether observation is censored because the horizon remains open.

## Match vector

Do not begin with one binary `fulfilled` field. Use

\[
M=(m_A,m_X,m_O,m_L,m_T,m_C),
\]

for agent, action, object, location, time and condition. Each component is `match`, `partial`, `mismatch`, `unresolved` or `not-specified`.

A composite prediction is not a complete success when only its easiest component matches. A broad geopolitical statement does not receive exact-date credit. A conditional warning is not scored as failed merely because its stated condition changes the outcome; the condition itself must have been source-visible before the event.

## Repair ledger

After the outcome, record each change to:

- source translation or textual witness;
- time conversion;
- start anchor;
- calendar;
- event identity;
- physical versus spiritual realization;
- exact date versus interval or lower bound;
- single versus repeated fulfilment; and
- conditionality.

Repairs may be theologically or historically reasonable. They cannot validate the original forecast. A repaired model begins a new forecast lineage and must face a new held-out outcome.

## Independence classes

- **I0 — same sentence or narrator:** no independent outcome.
- **I1 — dependent reception:** later source reuses the prophetic text.
- **I2 — independent literary source:** separate source, historical dependence possible.
- **I3 — independent material/administrative record:** inscription, archive, archaeology or securely external chronicle.
- **I4 — prospectively registered public forecast and independently recorded outcome.**

Only I3 and I4 can close the strongest predictive lane. I2 can support a sensitivity analysis.

## Dating classes

- **D0:** surviving witness securely predates outcome.
- **D1:** source layer is strongly inferred to predate outcome, but the witness is later.
- **D2:** defensible date interval overlaps outcome.
- **D3:** source or redaction postdates outcome.
- **D4:** date materially inferred from the apparent fulfilment itself.

The primary ranking reports D0 separately. D1 enters only sensitivity analysis. D2--D4 cannot establish foresight.

## Blinding and adjudication

1. Two source annotators encode independently.
2. Two outcome annotators encode independently.
3. An adjudicator sees disagreements but not model scores.
4. Believing and skeptical teams exchange anonymized records.
5. The held-out partition stays encrypted or access-controlled until the operator family and score are frozen.

## Stop rules

Do not rank major prophecy when:

- fewer than six independent D0/I3-or-I4 cases exist;
- one book, author or reception lineage supplies more than half the admitted cases;
- the preferred model has been changed after held-out inspection;
- candidate intervals cover more than a declared fraction of the evaluation horizon; or
- negative-control texts score within the preregistered equivalence margin.
