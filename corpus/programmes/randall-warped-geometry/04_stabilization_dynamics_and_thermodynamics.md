# Stabilization, Dynamics and Thermodynamics

## 1. The radion is the missing dynamical coordinate

In a two-brane warped model, the separation \(L\) cannot simply be declared.
Fluctuations of the inter-brane distance appear in four dimensions as the
radion. Since the hierarchy is

\[
m_{\rm IR}/m_{\rm UV}=e^{-kL},
\]

a dynamical \(L\) makes the hierarchy itself dynamical. The static model is
therefore incomplete until the radion has a stable potential and acceptable
mass and couplings.

Goldberger--Wise stabilization introduces a bulk scalar \(\Phi(y)\) with
brane-localized potentials. Its boundary-preferred values generate an
effective potential \(V_{\rm eff}(L)\). In the corpus grammar:

\[
\text{bulk field}
\xrightarrow{\text{two boundary selectors}}
\text{profile}
\xrightarrow{\text{on-shell action}}
V_{\rm eff}(L)
\xrightarrow{\min}
\text{stable local hierarchy}.
\]

This is a concrete example in which boundary VEV-like data stabilize a
geometric relation rather than merely assign a particle mass.

## 2. Local energy causes a global constrained response

The stabilized bulk cannot respond independently at each value of \(y\). The
higher-dimensional Einstein equations include constraint equations linking
slices. In the recent effective-theory derivation, adding energy to a brane
requires the stabilizing profile to adjust so that the same four-dimensional
curvature is realized throughout the bulk.

This gives a strong version of the corpus intuition:

\[
\boxed{\text{a locally observed invariant may be maintained by a distributed parent response}.}
\]

It also supplies a warning. Integrating out the stabilizer while freezing its
profile can give the wrong four-dimensional effective potential. The hidden
carrier must be eliminated subject to its constraint, not deleted.

## 3. Thermal phases

Warped geometry admits different finite-temperature saddles. Schematically,

\[
\text{deconfined black-brane phase}
\longrightarrow
\text{stabilized warped/confining phase}.
\]

The transition can involve bubble nucleation, latent heat, metastability and
gravitational radiation. But the recent strongly backreacted analysis shows
that a large supercooled first-order transition is not universal. The outcome
depends on the stabilizing potential and backreaction.

Consequently:

- a static warped endpoint does not determine its formation history;
- the same apparent low-energy hierarchy may arise through different thermal
  paths;
- gravitational-wave absence need not refute every warped model;
- gravitational-wave presence would still require reconstruction of the
  transition parameters and competing sources.

## 4. Negative effects and failure modes

| Effect | What it changes | Required detector or calculation |
|---|---|---|
| unstabilized radion | hierarchy drifts; fifth-force scalar remains | radion potential, mass and trace coupling |
| scalar backreaction | warp profile and phase thermodynamics | coupled Einstein--scalar solution |
| detuned brane tension | induced four-dimensional curvature | junction conditions and cosmological constant |
| KK contamination | deviations from local four-dimensional gravity | full brane-to-brane propagator |
| radion--Higgs mixing | masses, widths and collider channels | scalar mixing matrix and branching ratios |
| thermal metastability | delayed or failed transition | bounce action and nucleation rate |
| strong supercooling | reheating and cosmological history | free energy, entropy and percolation tests |
| EFT breakdown | loss of predictive control | curvature and energy relative to cutoff |

These are not all “failed RS.” Some are implementation failures, some exclude
parameter regions, and some change the detector signature. The negative-result
classification must be applied case by case.

## 5. Relation to the corpus's thermodynamic channel

The work supports a limited proposition: temperature and entropy can select
between geometries whose low-energy field content and spectra differ. It does
not show that temperature is a second time coordinate or that thermodynamics
is itself gravity. The honest chain is

\[
\text{thermal state}
\to
\text{dominant bulk saddle}
\to
\text{warp/stabilizer profile}
\to
\text{effective four-dimensional spectrum and gravity}.
\]

Thermodynamics changes which geometric carrier is realized; the gravitational
field equations then determine the response inside that phase.
