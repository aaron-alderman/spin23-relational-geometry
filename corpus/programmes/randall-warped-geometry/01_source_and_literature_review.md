# Source and Literature Review

**Method:** primary papers and official institutional or experimental sources
are used for scientific claims. The 29 October 2026 Matheorems post is retained
only as the retrieval prompt.

## 2. Supersymmetry and geometric sequestering

Randall and Sundrum's [*Out of This World Supersymmetry Breaking*](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9810155)
places visible fields and supersymmetry breaking in separated higher-dimensional
locations. Local direct couplings are suppressed, while the super-Weyl anomaly
communicates breaking through renormalization-group functions. This work is one
of the origins of anomaly mediation and is conceptually important here because
it distinguishes:

1. geometric separation of sectors;
2. the channels that survive that separation;
3. an observable low-energy spectrum.

It is a precise precedent for the rule that a boundary or quotient removes
some relations but does not remove every globally permitted channel.

## 3. Recent warped effective theory and cosmology

Mishra and Randall's [*Phase Transition to RS: Cool, not Supercool*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.09633)
uses strong Goldberger--Wise backreaction to study the black-brane/high-
temperature phase and transition to the confining warped phase. It finds that
large supercooling and an associated first-order gravitational-wave signal are
not universal consequences of an RS endpoint.

Lüst, Nee and Randall's [*More Effective RS Field Theory*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.11771)
derives a stabilized low-energy theory allowing nonzero four-dimensional
cosmological constant and both brane constraints. The higher-dimensional
Einstein equation requires the same four-dimensional curvature throughout the
bulk; stabilizing fields adjust globally when energy is added locally to a
brane. At leading order, the induced cosmological constant is insensitive to
the details of stabilization even though the stabilizer profile must respond.

Together these works replace the static slogan “shape determines scale” with
a dynamical statement: local perturbation, global constraint, stabilizer
response and thermal history must close simultaneously.

## 4. Dark-sector programme

Fan, Katz, Randall and Reece, [*A Dark-Disk Universe*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.3271),
observes that data require most dark matter to be cold and effectively
collisionless but allow a subdominant interacting component. If that component
can radiate dark energy, it may cool into a rotationally supported disk. The
proposal yields stellar-dynamics and possibly gamma-ray signatures.

Randall and Reece's [comet-impact analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.0576)
was a conditional downstream application, not evidence that a dark disk exists.
Kramer and Randall later found that interstellar-gas constraints were dominated
by discordant gas and pressure measurements; the available data were inadequate
to decide the disk's existence. This branch exemplifies a disciplined move from
allowed microphysics to morphology to a detector with explicit nuisance limits.

## 5. Gravity, information and nonfactorization

Geng, Karch, Perez-Pardavila, Raju, Randall, Riojas and Shashi,
[*Information Transfer with a Gravitating Bath*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.04671),
shows that when the bath gravitates, spatial location need not define an
independent radiation subsystem. A sector decomposition of Hilbert space can
retain nontrivial Page-like behavior where a naive spatial split does not.

Their [*Inconsistency of Islands in Theories with Long-Range Gravity*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.03390)
uses gravitational dressing and the Gauss law: energy nominally localized in a
disconnected island remains detectable asymptotically. Known island examples
avoid the direct contradiction in conjunction with a massive graviton. This is
a valuable no-go pattern for the corpus: a proposed quotient is inadmissible if
a conserved long-range relation still crosses it.

## 6. Experimental status

Warped extra dimensions remain model frameworks, not observations. Collider
searches test particular couplings, widths, localizations and decay channels;
null results constrain those parameter slices rather than refuting every warped
geometry. An [ATLAS full-Run-2 heavy-resonance analysis](https://atlas.cern/Updates/Briefing/Search-High-Mass-Particles)
reported no excess and constrained radion and KK-graviton benchmarks. No
confirmed RS radion, KK graviton or warped fifth dimension is presently part of
established particle physics.

## 7. Source verdict on the prompt post

The post is useful as an intuition prompt and inaccurate as a status statement.

- “The hierarchy becomes geometry” is a fair description of RS1.
- “Gravity leaks and is diluted” blurs RS1 redshift, RS2 localization and
  large-volume extra-dimensional language.
- “Found a reason why” should read “proposed a falsifiable geometric
  mechanism for why.”
- The factor \(10^{32}\) is naturally a squared-scale comparison; the warp
  required for Planck-to-weak masses is of order \(10^{-16}\).
