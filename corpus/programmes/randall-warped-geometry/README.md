## Purpose

Lisa Randall's work supplies one of the clearest existing demonstrations that
locally measured physical structure can be selected by a higher-dimensional
geometry. The programme is therefore relevant to the corpus's language of
carrier, face, selector, relation and detector. Relevance is not identity:
Randall--Sundrum uses a spacelike fifth dimension, five-dimensional Einstein
gravity and brane boundary conditions. It does not derive the corpus's Jordan
carrier, family structure or proposed configuration-space readings.

The most direct intersection is Karch--Randall localized gravity:

\[
AdS_4\ \text{brane}\subset AdS_5\ \text{bulk},
\qquad
SO(2,3)\subset SO(2,4).
\]

Thus the corpus's \(Spin(2,3)\) group occurs as the spin cover of the isometry
group of a selected four-dimensional face inside a \(Spin(2,4)\) parent. This
is an exact group and geometry statement. It does not show that the corpus's
independently constructed \(Spin(2,3)\) carrier is that brane.

## Central correction to the prompt post

The phrase “gravity leaks into the extra dimension and is exponentially
diluted” conflates mechanisms. In the compact two-brane model, the hierarchy
is chiefly the exponential redshift of local mass scales across a warped
metric. In the noncompact one-brane model, the gravitational zero mode is
localized near the brane. In Karch--Randall, effective four-dimensional
gravity is mediated by a light massive bound graviton on an \(AdS_4\) brane.

## Authority boundary

- **Established mathematics within the models:** the metric solutions, warp
  redshift, mode equations, group inclusions and conditional effective actions.
- **Established physics framework:** general relativity, effective field
  theory, collider and cosmological methods used to constrain the models.
- **Not experimentally established:** a physical warped fifth dimension,
  brane localization of the Standard Model, an RS radion or KK-graviton tower.
- **Not supplied:** a derivation of the corpus's Jordan algebra, triality,
  three-family structure, timelike configuration channel or absolute frame.
