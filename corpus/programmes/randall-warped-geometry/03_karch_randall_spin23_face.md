# Karch--Randall and the \(Spin(2,3)\) Face

## 1. Exact symmetry ladder

Four-dimensional anti-de Sitter space is the homogeneous space

\[
AdS_4\cong SO(2,3)/SO(1,3),
\]

whereas

\[
AdS_5\cong SO(2,4)/SO(1,4).
\]

Their connected spin covers give the exact ladder

\[
Spin_0(2,4)\supset Spin_0(2,3)\supset Spin_0(1,3),
\]

with the familiar low-dimensional identifications

\[
Spin_0(2,4)\cong SU(2,2),
\qquad
Spin_0(2,3)\cong Sp(4,\mathbb R).
\]

Karch--Randall realizes the middle group geometrically: an \(AdS_4\) brane is
embedded in an \(AdS_5\) bulk. The corpus had already derived the same abstract
group descent from the complex Hermitian Jordan carrier

\[
H_2(\mathbb C)\longrightarrow H_2(\mathbb R).
\]

The coincidence is exact at the group level. The carrier map is not yet known.

## 2. Why this is more than a shared group name

The Karch--Randall construction contains all four pieces needed for a physical
face:

1. a higher-dimensional ambient metric;
2. an embedded hypersurface with induced \(AdS_4\) geometry;
3. a fluctuation operator whose spectrum contains a localized light spin-2
   bound state;
4. brane sources that detect the bound state as approximate four-dimensional
   gravity.

This is stronger than observing that \(SO(2,3)\) is an available symmetry. It
shows how a face carrying that symmetry can control an effective detector
record while remaining coupled to a larger carrier.

## 3. The light graviton is a localization result

Linearized tensor perturbations reduce, after an appropriate field
redefinition, to a one-dimensional spectral problem in the transverse
coordinate. The warp factor creates an effective potential. A light bound
spin-2 state can then dominate brane-to-brane propagation even though the
five-dimensional space has infinite volume.

The important corpus lesson is

\[
\boxed{\text{effective dimensionality is a spectral statement, not merely a coordinate count}.}
\]

The detector experiences four-dimensional gravity because its coupling is
dominated by a localized mode over a regime of scales. The remaining spectrum
is not deleted; it produces corrections and defines the limit of the effective
description.

## 5. A sharper interpretation of “locally invariant”

Karch--Randall gives the phrase a physical meaning:

- observers and sources confined to the brane transform under its induced
  \(SO(2,3)\) symmetry;
- the full state and boundary conditions live in the \(SO(2,4)\) bulk;
- the local gravitational law is controlled by a mode localized near the face;
- global bulk structure determines where that local description fails.

Local invariance is therefore not reduced or approximate algebra. It is the
exact covariance of the selected face together with a scale-limited effective
dynamics inherited from the parent.

## 6. Research consequence

The corpus should test a new fork:

1. **ambient reading:** \(Spin(2,3)\) is the fundamental gravity group;
2. **face reading:** \(Spin(2,3)\) is the exact symmetry of a selected phase or
   defect inside a \(Spin(2,4)\) parent;
3. **response reading:** \(Spin(2,3)\) is only the conformal completion of a
   local real Jordan response carrier.

Karch--Randall makes the second reading a serious established model class. It
does not choose among the three readings for this programme.
