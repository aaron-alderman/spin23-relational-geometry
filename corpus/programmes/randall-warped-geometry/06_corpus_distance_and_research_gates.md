## 2. Exact gains

This review adds four pieces the corpus did not previously hold together:

1. **face symmetry:** \(Spin(2,3)\) can be the exact isometry spin group of an
   \(AdS_4\) face rather than the symmetry of the ambient carrier;
2. **spectral dimensionality:** effective four-dimensional gravity can be a
   localized-mode statement rather than the deletion of the fifth coordinate;
3. **distributed stabilization:** a local brane perturbation can require a
   global bulk-profile response to preserve common four-curvature;
4. **Gauss-law refusal:** long-range relational data can invalidate a quotient
   that looks spatially well separated.

## 3. Missing bridges

### Gate R1: carrier intertwiner

Construct or rule out a typed map between the corpus's
\(H_2(\mathbb C)\to H_2(\mathbb R)\) real-form selection and the geometric
\(AdS_5\to AdS_4\) embedding. Matching groups is insufficient.

**Success:** a map preserving actions, positivity, sources and detector
records.
**Failure:** a dimension, representation, locality or source-type mismatch.

### Gate R2: localized spectrum

Write the transverse spin-2 fluctuation operator for a declared Karch--Randall
background, compute its light bound state, continuum/tower and brane residues,
and compare the spectral logic with the corpus's domain-wall reader.

**Success:** an explicit common Sturm--Liouville localization functor with
typed differences.
**Failure:** similarity limited to generic exponential localization.

### Gate R3: selector dynamics

Determine whether any corpus selector has a genuine modulus whose effective
potential can be derived by integrating a parent field subject to boundary
data, as in Goldberger--Wise stabilization.

**Success:** a stable selected face and predicted fluctuation mode.
**Failure:** selector remains an external declaration with no dynamics.

### Gate R4: detector and phenomenology

Select one non-generic observable that distinguishes an ambient
\(Spin(2,3)\) gravity theory from a \(Spin(2,3)\) face inside a
\(Spin(2,4)\) parent.

Candidate records include a radion-like scalar, a massive/gravitational tower,
scale-dependent gravitational propagator or a thermal phase-transition
signature. Each is generic to model classes until a corpus-specific relation
fixes a mass, residue or ratio.

### Gate R5: long-range quotient test

For every proposed face or detector quotient, identify the analogue of the
gravitational Gauss-law record. A quotient is invalid if a conserved charge or
asymptotic dressing remains measurable across the supposedly removed relation.

## 4. Fastest finite calculation

The most useful immediate calculation is not a full cosmology. It is a
three-column comparison of one declared background:

1. the \(AdS_5\) bulk metric and junction data;
2. the induced \(AdS_4\) face and its \(Spin(2,3)\) action;
3. the brane-to-brane spin-2 spectral measure.

Then ask whether the corpus's five-dimensional primitive-two-form carrier maps
to any of those three columns. A negative answer would be valuable: it would
retain Karch--Randall as a model of “local face inside parent” while closing a
false Jordan/metric identification.

## 5. Stop conditions

- Do not infer an actual fifth dimension from an effective warped emulator.
- Do not identify equal Lie groups acting on different carriers.
- Do not call a massive bound graviton a mass-generation derivation for matter.
- Do not infer three families, triality or \(E_8\) from two branes or an orbifold.
- Do not claim experimental support from the absence of excluded parameter
  regions.
- Do not use the dark-disk branch as evidence for warped geometry; they are
  independent programmes.
