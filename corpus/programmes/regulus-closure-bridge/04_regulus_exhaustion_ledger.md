## Closure criterion

A branch is exhausted here when it has reached one of four states:

1. exact and independently verified;
2. joined to an existing corpus carrier by an explicit intertwiner;
3. rejected by a representation, incidence or orbit obstruction;
4. dependent on new physical data, a new source or a genuinely new theorem.

## What would reopen the programme

The current regulus lane should reopen only if one of the following appears:

1. a canonical integral lift relating the mod-three carrier to a measured real
   or complex response lattice;
2. a source-derived physical observable whose channels form the required
   Plücker line geometry;
3. an explicit intertwiner changing the failed 240-object orbit comparison;
4. a nonstandard \(PSp_4(3)\subset W(E_8)\) embedding with the required
   40-object and 240-object actions;

   regulus closure into genuine dynamics;
6. new literature showing an additional classical \(E_6\) configuration not
   generated by the present \(27/36/40/45/72/120/240/432\) hierarchy;
7. a source-derived selector, device or noise law that distinguishes one of
   the 36 complete two-qutrit stabilizer-MUB frames.

Until then, further iteration inside the present data would duplicate known
incidences or turn exact proximity into unsupported physical identity.
