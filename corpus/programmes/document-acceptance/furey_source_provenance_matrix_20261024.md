## Purpose

This matrix prevents three layers from being blended:

1. what Furey's cited publications explicitly construct or claim;
2. what the foundational review infers or uses as physical interpretation; and
3. what the later Spin(2,3)/admissibility corpus independently derives, tests or rules out.

The links below identify the primary source and the corresponding review section. They do not certify every displayed derivation in the source. Equation-, proposition- and page-level reproduction remains a specialist verification task wherever the final column says **reproduce**.

## Maintenance rule

When a document makes a Furey-related claim, it should cite either a primary source row above or a numbered corpus claim that explicitly owns the later deduction. If neither exists, label the statement as a question or analogy. A future page-level audit should add exact equation, proposition or page locators without strengthening the source's physical claims.
