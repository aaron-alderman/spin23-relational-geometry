# Worldwright identity-proxy discrimination design

The functional-form experiment established a real synthetic ambiguity: every normalized monotone identity law changes the same 87 of 640 endpoint classifications, while their intermediate paths differ. Endpoint-only validation therefore cannot select a response law.

This design treats the ambiguity as a falsification problem. Across the three tested interior levels, salience 0.75 is the only single level at which all six aggregate signatures—including the null control—are distinct. The predicted changed-classification counts are 0 for the null control, 49 for the quadratic law, 64 for linear, 70 for square-root, 77 for normalized logistic and 87 for the half-threshold law. The closest pair differs by only 6 of 640 classifications.

The recommended design therefore uses 0 and 1 as controls, 0.75 as the primary discrimination level, and 0.25 as a curvature replication level. The lower level deliberately exposes the threshold law's alias with the null control while separating the other four non-null curves. A staged test should first ask whether identity adds held-out predictive information at all, then distinguish broad curve families, and only then attempt close law selection.

The resolution calculation makes the cost asymmetry explicit. Under a deliberately conservative independent-binomial approximation, the synthetic null-versus-quadratic gap at salience 0.75 requires roughly 328 observations per arm, while the linear-versus-square-root gap requires roughly 21,855. These are planning bounds, not a final power calculation: a paired design may be substantially more efficient, but its discordance and empirical variance are not available yet.

Four outcomes are preregistered as non-confirmatory: null superiority removes or demotes identity as a causal coordinate; an absent, reversed or non-monotone held-out response rejects the tested monotone family; overlapping uncertainty leaves the curve unidentified; and an invalid or post-treatment proxy invalidates the test itself. The required data include a pre-intervention identity proxy, frozen salience mapping, consistent intervention choices, action time, locality, channel, baseline state, outcome window, proxy reliability, missingness and an untouched holdout.

This is an executable design audit, not empirical validation.
