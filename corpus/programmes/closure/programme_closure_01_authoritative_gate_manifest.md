## First irreducible break

The earliest open gate is G01, immediately after the established matter carrier:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Which two gauge-invariant composite operators are the physical ports, and why?}
}
\]

The corpus has shown that acceptable candidates exist. It has not shown that the Standard Model or Furey algebra selects a unique pair, their relative normalization, or their contact prescription.

Everything downstream is therefore best read as a family of conditional consequences indexed by that unresolved source choice.

## Why the parent programmes do not close the gap

- The raw Furey TRO retains order but fails the second Kantor identity.
- Its structurable repair is a valid Kantor system but closes on classical linking algebras.
- The real lepton slice reaches (mathfrak{sp}(4,\mathbb R)) in a contact five-grading, but the established CP pairing does not preserve it.
- Freudenthal geometry recognizes inverse-matched source/dual pairs but does not select the face, orientation or scale.
- The Albert/Freudenthal homogenization recovers the determinant while losing the required physical pairing on the tested slice.

These are valuable classification and no-go results. None selects the physical ports in G01.
