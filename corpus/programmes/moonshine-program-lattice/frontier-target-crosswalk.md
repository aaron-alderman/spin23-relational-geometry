## Result

The Moonshine programme now touches seven maintained frontier targets, but it
does so at three different distances.  FPT-002 and FPT-019 can move immediately
by computation: the scalar/genus-one quotient is already known, and the cost
of each proposed refinement can be audited.  FPT-007, FPT-009 and FPT-022 have
exact finite carriers but still require either a physical source or an
intertwiner.  FPT-010 and FPT-017 remain comparisons only: the current chain
does not produce a new positivity bound or a renormalization-group flow.

This prevents two opposite errors.  Moonshine is no longer isolated from the
frontier lane, but neither is its mathematical closure counted as experimental
or four-dimensional physical closure.

## The closest no-new-experiment tranche

The next work should stay entirely on the mathematical side until it either
produces a discriminator or fails:

1. enumerate the four oriented order-46 cases and their power-map labels;
2. modularly transport the known order-46 Hauptmoduln to
   \(Z(h^2,h^{23};\tau)\) and normalize them compatibly;
3. group the 46A/B and 46C/D results by their Conway and Baby-Monster
   centralizer lifts;
4. record the resulting no-go: fifth power fixes the involution and leaves
   each orientation pair scalar-degenerate;
5. identify the complete \(C_{23}\times S_4\) centralizer and place the
   \(Q_{46AB}\cong C_2^2\), \(Q_{46CD}\cong C_2\) quotient labels inside its
   five insertion classes;
6. close every single-insertion scalar route: the product orders
   \(23,46,46,69,92\) are cyclic and all A/B scalar series pairs agree;
7. construct the smallest matrix kernel from the surviving order-23 branches
   to the ten even genus-two characteristics;
8. reject the join if the modular representations or anomaly lines cannot
   intertwine.

Steps 1--6 close the scalar part of FPT-002. The projective-centralizer audit
then closes the remaining genus-one \(S_4\) representation route: the
transgressed cocycle is trivial and the five class traces determine the
module. Step 7 is therefore the FPT-007 higher-genus junction. Step 8 is useful
even when negative: it converts a speculative bridge into a typed no-go.

## The finite emulator tranche

The defect plane already supplies a compact synthetic-gauge target:

\[
A=\mathbb F_{23}^2,
\qquad
q(a,b)=ab/23,
\qquad
S(a,b)=(b,-a),
\qquad
D_5(a,b)=(5a,14b).
\]

An emulator need not realize a Monster VOA.  It must realize these two
operations with independently calibrated controls, preserve the declared
quadratic data, and demonstrate the predicted 44-element closure before
projective readout and the 22-element closure after it.  The decisive record
is the central sign: coherent label readout must see it; projective readout
must not.  That is a source-restricted FPT-009/FPT-022 experiment.  A generic
qudit compiler reproducing the matrices on demand would verify software, not
the proposed physical provenance.

## Where the physical path stops

No present arrow maps the VOA grading variable to laboratory time, the
Monster defect to a material defect, or the genus-two sewing modulus to a
spacetime metric component.  The Spin adjacency also remains finite and
arithmetic:

\[
\mathrm{Dic}_{11}<SL(2,23)<Sp(4,23)\cong Spin(5,23),
\]

whereas the corpus's physical carrier uses the real form
\(Sp(4,\mathbb R)\cong Spin_0(2,3)\).  A physical promotion requires a lift
choosing real signature, positive energy, time orientation, dynamics and a
detector map.  None follows from reduction modulo 23.

## Ranked continuation

1. **Mixed-level genus-two intertwiner:** use the exact
   \(Sp(4,2)\times Sp(4,23)\) CRT carrier; a direct order-11 action on the ten
   characteristics is impossible. The unique projective \(C_2\) sign
   coupling survives, while the scalar metaplectic \(C_4\) lift is ruled out
   for the one-block embedding. The genuine eta line instead gives
   \(v_\eta(K)=i\), while \(K\) permutes the ten even characteristics with
   cycle type \(3^3\,1\). The eta-normalized free-Majorana action is now
   complete: its three cycle products are \(1\), its fixed odd--odd eigenvalue
   is \(-1\), and its determinant is \(-1\). Compute the fermionic
   Baby-Monster action on the same cycles and compare the combined monomial
   operator with the sewn defect action.
2. **Cross-grade VOA/defect composition:** test fusion or intertwining data
   not determined by the now-exhausted graded \(S_4\) characters.
3. **Finite \(\mathrm{Dic}_{11}/D_{11}\) emulator:** conventional but sharp
   test of quotient scars and detector equivalence.
4. **Scale/RG and Spin lift:** hold until a physical carrier and action exist.

This ranking makes the programme useful either way.  A successful intertwiner
would be new mathematical structure close to the corpus's central seam.  A
failed intertwiner would remove a large speculative branch.  The emulator
would still demonstrate, in an ordinary platform, the exact information-loss
geometry that motivated the admissibility language.
