## Result

The corpus's order-23, 2A-fermionic and 2B-Leech branches are not merely
adjacent.  The Monster power map supplies an exact common junction at order
46.  If \(h\) has order 46, then

\[
g=h^2,\qquad z=h^{23}
\]

have orders 23 and 2 and commute.  The four order-46 Monster classes divide
as follows:

| order-46 class of \(h\) | \(h^2\) | \(h^{23}\) | corpus branch selected by the involution |
|---|---|---|---|
| \(46A,46B\) | \(23A\) or \(23B\) | \(2B\) | Leech orbifold / pair-Gram seam |
| \(46C,46D\) | \(23A\) or \(23B\) | \(2A\) | Majorana-Weyl x Baby-Monster fermionization |

The precise A/B output depends on the chosen generator, as it must: fifth
power and inversion exchange the two oriented order-23 classes.  What is
invariant is the class-level diamond

```text
                 23AB
                /    \
          46A/B        46C/D
             |            |
             2B           2A
       Leech branch   fermionic branch
```

This closes the former existence question: the 2A and 2B channels both meet
the order-23 channel inside cyclic order-46 subgroups of the Monster.  It does
not identify their seam variables.  The correct next object is a
twisted--twining/generalized-moonshine function for the commuting pair
\((g,z)\), together with its genus-two sewing, not an asserted equality
between two unrelated binary labels.

## 1. Why the order-46 bridge is correctly typed

The identities above are elementary inside the cyclic group generated by
\(h\):

\[
|h^2|=\frac{46}{\gcd(46,2)}=23,
\qquad
|h^{23}|=\frac{46}{\gcd(46,23)}=2,
\qquad
[h^2,h^{23}]=1.
\]

ATLAS power-up data records \(46A,46B\) above 2B and \(46C,46D\) above 2A,
while all four occur above 23A and 23B.  This is an exact statement about
Monster conjugacy classes.  It is weaker than claiming that every chosen
representative of 23A/B has every kind of root, and stronger than observing
that 2 and 23 both divide the Monster order.

For a commuting pair, generalized moonshine supplies the natural observable

\[
Z(g,z;\tau)=\operatorname{Tr}_{V^\natural_g}
\!\left(zq^{L_0-1}\right),
\]

where \(V^\natural_g\) is the \(g\)-twisted module.  The ordinary order-46
McKay--Thompson series \(T_h\) and the two-variable function \(Z(g,z;\tau)\)
are related data, but they are not interchangeable.  Computing \(T_h\) alone
does not establish how the 2A/2B insertion acts in the order-23 twisted
sector.

## 2. The joined compiler

Before passing to the compiler, the same diamond can be resolved through the
two involution centralizers.  These are the two canonical Monster local
geometries already used by the corpus:

\[
C_{\mathbb M}(2A)=2.B,
\qquad
C_{\mathbb M}(2B)=2^{1+24}.Co_1.
\]

For \(h\in46C,46D\), the central involution is 2A.  Hence \(h\) lies in
\(2.B\), and quotienting by its central involution sends it to an order-23
element of the Baby Monster.  The centralizer arithmetic closes exactly:

\[
|C_{\mathbb M}(h)|=92=2\cdot46,
\]

while the two Baby-Monster order-23 classes have centralizer order 46.  Thus
the 46C/D rail is literally the central double-cover lift of the Baby-Monster
23A/B rail selected by 2A fermionization.

For \(h\in46A,46B\), the central involution is 2B, so \(h\) lies in
\(2^{1+24}.Co_1\).  Projection through the normal 2-group sends it to an
order-23 element of \(Co_1\), whose two order-23 classes have centralizer order
23.  Here

\[
|C_{\mathbb M}(h)|=184=8\cdot23.
\]

The kernel of the centralizer projection therefore has order eight.  This is
an exact residue in the Leech-side lift, but the corpus does not yet identify
that kernel with the Rule-60 affine deck bit or with a physical seam.  The
asymmetry \(2\cdot46\) versus \(8\cdot23\) is a new discriminator for the
twisted--twining calculation, not permission to force the two rails into the
same finite carrier.

## 3. Admissibility as retained information

Across the joined chain, admissibility can be made operational: a proposed
transition is admissible only if its target retains the datum needed by the
next operation, or declares that datum irrecoverably quotiented out.

| carrier before quotient | compressed output | datum lost | consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| two cyclotomic primes / inverse ideals | ordinary binary theta series | orientation and ideal-class inverse | the two rails become equal |
| 23A and 23B generators | scalar \(T_{23}\) | generator orientation | A/B cannot be recovered from genus one |
| \(\mathbb F_{23}^2\setminus\{0\}\) | \(\mathbb P^1(\mathbb F_{23})\) | common scale and central sign | \(\mathrm{Dic}_{11}\) falls to \(D_{11}\) |
| 22 nonzero charge labels | untwisted Fourier projection | individual nonzero charge | only neutral versus charged remains |
| ten even spin-sector amplitudes | bosonization sum | determinant/anomaly line | \(\chi_5\) cannot be the additive sum |
| oriented seam family | scalar \(\chi_{10}=\chi_5^2\) | character/sign of \(\chi_5\) | scalar closure forgets the marked seam |

This is the useful content of the admissibility viewpoint here.  It does not
say that Nature optimizes an unspecified functional.  It supplies a sequence
of explicit quotient maps and asks, at each stage, whether the observable
still carries enough information to compose with the next map.

## 4. The carriers are adjacent, not identical

Several nearby counts must remain separated:

- the order-23 fixed-point VOA has \(23^2=529\) twist-charge modules;
- its nonzero projective labels form 24 directions, split into orbits of 2
  axes and 22 mixed directions under the finite transition skeleton;
- the 22 nonidentity powers of a generator give 11 inverse pairs, and adding
  the identity produces the count 12;
- the conditional \(\operatorname{Rep}(\mathfrak{so}(3)_{23})\) defect
  category has rank 12, as does the genus of \(X_1(25)\);
- the genus-two fermionic carrier has 10 even spin structures and 6 odd
  structures that vanish in the vacuum amplitude;
- the live ten-sector permutation carrier has a nine-dimensional sum-zero
  representation whose determinant is the finite sign character.

These are different sets with different actions.  In particular, \(12\ne10\),
so no labelwise identification can join the order-23 defect basis to the
even-spin basis.  A bridge must be a restriction, induction, Fourier kernel,
defect junction or sewing map between carriers.  The order-46 diamond tells
us which insertion labels that map; it does not construct the map.

## 5. What “program” now means

The combined branch has six levels which should not be conflated:

1. **Update:** \(1+\sigma\) is a finite cellular/group-algebra operation.
2. **Symmetry:** \(\sigma\), the order-46 roots and the Monster classes are
   reversible group elements.
3. **State space:** VOA modules and twist-charge sectors are the typed memory.
4. **Observable:** ordinary and generalized McKay--Thompson functions are
   graded traces.
5. **Composition:** defects, vertex operations and sewing say how interfaces
   can be joined.
6. **Integrity law:** denominator and Hecke relations constrain the whole
   graded construction.

This deserves the word program in the algebraic sense: states, typed
operations, compositional interfaces and global consistency checks coexist.
It is not yet a physical time evolution.  A Hamiltonian, real-time carrier,
locality statement and detector coupling remain absent.

## 6. Executable next tests

The order-46 junction replaces a vague search with a small comparison table.
For each \(h\in46A,46B,46C,46D\):

1. identify \(g=h^2\in23AB\) and \(z=h^{23}\in2A/2B\) with a declared
   generator orientation;
2. use \(h=g^{12}z\) and transport the known order-46 Hauptmodul by the
   explicit matrix \(\left(\begin{smallmatrix}1&11\\2&23\end{smallmatrix}\right)\),
   retaining compatible normalization rather than only scalar coefficients;
3. compare the 2A pair with the Majorana-Weyl x Baby-Monster decomposition and
   the 2B pair with the Leech orbifold decomposition;
4. use the fifth-power Galois pairing to verify that the normalized cyclic
   trace still collapses A/B and C/D, then add the exactly bounded noncyclic
   insertions \(Q_{46AB}\cong C_2^2\) and \(Q_{46CD}\cong C_2\);
5. test degeneration to the already verified common scalar \(T_{23}\);
6. sew the resulting functions through two handles and ask whether their
   determinant/anomaly lines land on the ten-even-characteristic carrier;
7. only after that, test whether the off-diagonal \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) coordinate
   is supplied by the sewing parameter or a defect-junction modulus.

Three outcomes are useful.  Equality after orientation-forgetting would
identify the common scalar shadow.  A controlled phase or character mismatch
would identify the missing anomaly line.  Incompatibility of modular
multipliers would rule out the proposed seam junction without any new physical
experiment.

## 7. Boundary of the result

The joined map establishes an exact finite-group junction and a sequence of
typed information-loss gates.  It does **not** establish a Monster action on
\(Spin_0(2,3)\), identify 2A fermion parity with the Rule-60 deck bit, derive
four-dimensional dynamics, or turn the numerical 12/12 coincidence into a
bijection.  Those promotions remain inadmissible until the explicit
twisted--twining and sewn intertwiners exist.

The [mixed-level 46 seam](mixed-level-46-genus-two-seam.md) sharpens the
sewn-intertwiner gate. The order-11 orientation rotation cannot act on the
ten level-2 characteristic labels or lift as finite order in
\(Sp(4,\mathbb Z)\). The exact common finite ambient carrier is instead
\(Sp(4,\mathbb Z/46)\cong Sp(4,2)\times Sp(4,23)\); the missing object is a
non-factorized vector-valued kernel coupling its two prime factors.
Its scalar channels are now classified: a \(C_2\) sign can couple the
projective seam. The finite dicyclic \(C_4\) character has quarter-turn value
\(i\), but its genuine scalar metaplectic promotion is impossible in the
declared one-block embedding because a lifted quarter-turn has fourth power
equal to the metaplectic kernel. A genuine scalar eta line survives with
quarter-turn value \(\zeta_8\) and relation-defect value \(v_\eta(K)=i\);
the unresolved gate is equality with the sewn defect/Pfaffian phase.
The order-23 projective-centralizer audit also closes the remaining
genus-one representation escape: coprimality forces the transgressed cocycle
to be trivial, and the five class traces determine the full ordinary
\(S_4\) module. The kernel must therefore carry cross-grade or higher-genus
composition data.
