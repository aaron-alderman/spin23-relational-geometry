## Distance definition

“Close to the corpus” can mean several incompatible things. A topic can resemble corpus language while having no shared physical carrier; it can share an experiment but not a mechanism; or it can inherit a rigorous method while remaining evidentially absent. Each mystery is therefore assigned

\[
\Delta_m=(d_X,d_L,d_D,d_E,d_P),
\qquad
d_i\in\{0,1,2,3,4\},
\]

where:

| Coordinate | Question |
|---|---|
| \(d_X\) | Is the mystery's physical carrier already represented and typed in the corpus? |
| \(d_L\) | Does the corpus contain its actual dynamics or only an analogy/method? |
| \(d_D\) | Are its observable and detector maps already specified? |
| \(d_E\) | Does the corpus hold direct evidence, rather than references or prompts? |
| \(d_P\) | How many genuinely new assumptions are needed for physical promotion? |

The scale is: 0 exact/native closure; 1 explicit corpus object with a finite remaining task; 2 partial typed bridge; 3 generic method plus missing physical join; 4 absent, conflicted or requiring a new ontology. The unweighted total ranges from 0 to 20:

| Total | Band |
|---:|---|
| 0--4 | embedded |
| 5--8 | adjacent |
| 9--12 | bridged but unjoined |
| 13--16 | method-only or remote |
| 17--20 | currently disconnected |

Distance is not plausibility. A true phenomenon can be distant because the corpus has not studied it; a false identification can be close because the corpus contains a precise no-go theorem for it.

**Stop rule.** If “consciousness” only renames integration, report, memory, attention, recurrence or metacognition, the fundamental branch closes while the organizational programme remains.

## Cross-target structure

The 21 targets reduce to five recurring missing arrows:

1. **Carrier debt:** what physical state actually bears the proposed distinction?
2. **Transduction debt:** how does that state alter the next scale rather than merely correlate with it?
3. **Detector debt:** which record distinguishes it after nuisance and response folding?
4. **Selection debt:** what chooses one interpretation from the many models fitting finite data?
5. **Scale debt:** why does the effect survive from molecule, monument, atmosphere or cosmos to the claimed observation?

The corpus is strongest on detector and selection debt, moderately strong on generic response/transduction form, and weakest when a target requires a new biological, archaeological or atmospheric carrier. This is why detector-rich measurement is closest, while content-rich metaphysics is farthest.
