## Result

The first primary-source inspection both strengthens and constrains the muqarnas--octonion branch.

Topkapı Scroll catalogue drawing 108 is not a single planar adjacency graph. It superposes at least four data layers:

\[
\boxed{
\text{construction scaffold}
\oplus
\text{cell boundaries}
\oplus
\text{spatial-level marks}
\oplus
\text{boundary/repeat data}.
}
\]

Any transcription that collapses these layers into one untyped graph will create false incidences and destroy the plan-to-geometry information. The correct starting object is a typed multiplex planar complex.

At the same time, the drawing supplies something the abstract octonion branch lacked: a non-arbitrarily selected frame. A radial origin lies at the southeast corner of the square plan; principal rays and concentric quarter circles organize the construction; centre axes and an asymmetric half-vault boundary supply further orientation data. Architecture therefore has native directions against which a signed construction defect could eventually be measured.

No map from that architectural frame to the imaginary octonions is established. No privileged sevenfold motif is documented in the plate. Those negative observations protect the branch against retrofitting.

## 4. Why the boundary is part of the algebra

The source says explicitly that asymmetric bottom-edge units identify a half-vault repeat. If those units are mirrored immediately to make a visually satisfying full vault, the transcription erases evidence of the intended gluing operation.

Let \(B\) be the ordered boundary word of exposed cell-edge types. The full assembly is not obtained from an unconditioned symmetry operation, but from a typed sewing

\[
M\;\mathop{\#}_{B,\tau(B)}\;\tau(M),
\]

where \(\tau\) is the appropriate reflected or rotated mate. Whether orientation reverses, which edges pair and which tier marks must agree are part of the operation. The asymmetric boundary is therefore a prospective quotient scar and compiler interface, not noise around the central ornament.

## 6. The absence of seven is informative

The catalogue foregrounds four-, five-, six-, eleven- and twenty-fourfold data, not a native sevenfold incidence system. The correct response is not to search the dense drawing until seven convenient objects can be selected. It is to freeze the native grammar and ask later whether its multiplication or junction tensor contains a seven-dimensional quotient or subsystem that predicts withheld data.

This gives a hard anti-overfitting rule:

> Seven labels discovered only after unrestricted selection from a dense plan have no evidential force.

A strong Fano result must arise from the full native cell table, with the search multiplicity reported, and outperform randomized relabellings.

## 7. Revised data architecture

The vector transcription must keep four synchronized objects:

1. **scaffold graph** -- radial, circular and centre-line construction relations;
2. **cell complex** -- polygon boundaries, shared faces and named unit types;
3. **level field** -- stippling and any other source marks associated with spatial depth;
4. **boundary word** -- ordered exposed interfaces and intended repeat operation.

The realization map then becomes

\[
R:(G_{\rm scaffold},K_{\rm cell},h_{\rm level},B_{\rm boundary})
\longrightarrow M_{3D}.
\]

This is richer than the graph model initially proposed and much closer to the corpus's typed-carrier discipline.
