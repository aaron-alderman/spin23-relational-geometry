## The main corpus journey

The current research path can be read as:

\[
\begin{array}{rcl}
X&:&\text{exceptional/Jordan/Lie ambient architecture}\\
\downarrow A&&\text{positive, causal, gauge and representation constraints}\\
\downarrow\rho&&H_2(\mathbb R),\ Sp(4,\mathbb R)\simeq Spin_0(2,3)\text{ response throat}\\
\downarrow L&&\text{source--response, projector and channel dynamics}\\
\downarrow D&&\text{resolved, summed, oriented and boundary detectors}\\
\downarrow I&&\text{spectral, nonlinear, integral, geometric and memory scars}\\
\downarrow R&&\text{composition, scale and frontier experiments}.
\end{array}
\]

This is a navigation path, not a proof that \(E_8\) is the physical background or that every intermediate structure is uniquely selected.

## Representative instantiations

### Triality quotient

- \(X\): \(D_4\) roots, singularity, magic-square sheets or four resolved channels.
- \(A\): declared cyclic action and compatible metric/dynamics.
- \(\rho\): orbit quotient or unresolved detector block.
- \(I\): \(G_2\) long/short ratio, hidden modes, cubic orientation, \(\mathbb Z_3\) lattice scar and monodromy.
- Open gate: a physical operator over the singularity or the four-mode experiment.

### Moving projector and holonomy

- \(X\): projectors and their frame lifts.
- \(A\): gapped control paths and positive state geometry.
- \(T\): the universal connection \([P,dP]\).
- \(J\): independently measured selector datum when contraction is claimed.
- \(I\): quarter-turn holonomy and orientation-sensitive records.
- Open gate: source-native platform implementation with conditioned and unconditional maps.

### Freudenthal inverse response

- \(X\): paired Jordan source and dual-response slots.
- \(A\): positive \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) face and dual congruence.
- \(J\): Gaussian/log-determinant variational functional.
- \(\rho\): restriction from the exceptional parent to the response face.
- \(I\): quartic discriminant and critical-null inverse ray.
- Boundary: the action derives inverse matching but not the exceptional embedding or triality scar.

### Casimir--thermoelectric response

- \(X\): causal material-response tensors across frequency, field and geometry.
- \(A\): passivity, Kramers--Kronig, Onsager, boundary and material constraints.
- \(D\): Hall, Seebeck, Nernst and differential force/torque records.
- \(R\): common carrier and no-refit propagation between experiments.
- Open gate: one broadband tensor and nuisance-complete force prediction.

### Measurement-conditioned selection

- \(X\): system plus apparatus plus record.
- \(\rho\): conditioning on a trajectory versus tracing over outcomes.
- \(L\): Kraus/instrument dynamics.
- \(D\): complete record and ensemble channel tomography.
- \(I\): branch purification may survive in records while unconditional uniqueness does not.
- Boundary: conditioned selection is not unconditional physical selection.

## Priority signposts

1. **Objective provenance:** derive rather than choose \(J\).
2. **Viability and reachability:** compute the difference among admissible, viable and reachable sets.
3. **Composition:** measure when serial admissibility and direct admissibility disagree.
4. **Scale:** test which scars commute with coarse-graining and renormalization.
5. **Detector-relative actuality:** classify distinctions that exist physically but not in a declared record algebra.
6. **Experimental closure:** execute the four-mode quotient-scar and projector-return protocols.
