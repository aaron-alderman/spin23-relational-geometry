# Mystery Atlas 01: Consciousness and Brain--Environment Coupling

**Date:** 24 October 2026  
**Status:** literature-grounded research map; no consciousness-specific new physics admitted

## Result

Consciousness is a real explanandum and a productive frontier, but the present evidence does not select a fundamental consciousness field, Orch OR, an electromagnetic theory, or Schumann driving. The corpus can keep all of these branches visible by requiring each to name a carrier, coupling, perturbation and record that differs from ordinary neural dynamics.

## 1. Competing organizational theories

Global neuronal workspace, integrated information, higher-order, recurrent-processing and predictive-processing families currently make partially overlapping claims at different explanatory levels. The 2025 Cogitate adversarial collaboration tested preregistered predictions of GNWT and IIT across six laboratories and 256 participants; results supported parts of each while challenging key commitments of both. This is a mature model for the atlas: theories earn contact through mutually agreed discriminators, not retrospective accommodation. See the [primary adversarial study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-08888-1).

The corpus's strongest local contribution remains the [compile-out trilemma](../../claims/1100-1199/1144_consciousness_is_a_live_organizational_spike_but_not_yet_the_missing_physical_coupling.md). If a proposed conscious variable is fully derived from admitted physical state and can be substituted away without changing predictions, it is an interpretation or useful macrovariable, not an additional physical degree of freedom. A radical branch reaches M3 only when it specifies independently variable state, lawful preparation and a distinct future record distribution.

## 2. Orch OR and microtubule branches

The established base is that neuronal microtubules are biological structures with electrical, optical, mechanical and pharmacological properties. Experiments reporting energy migration in microtubules and modulation of anesthetic response justify studying them as possible neural interfaces. They do not yet establish long-lived in-vivo quantum computation, objective gravitational reduction, or a consciousness-specific causal role. The existing [neural response slice](../../claims/1200-1299/1224_rapid_seen_suppressed_neural_alternation_defines_a_testable_spin24_to_spin23_response_slice_not_an_orch_or_derivation.md) records the relevant sources and already states this firewall.

The decisive programme is multiscale and simultaneous:

1. identify a microtubule observable with calibrated spatial and temporal support;
2. measure coherence time, temperature dependence and environmental decoherence in the biological setting;
3. perturb that observable selectively while matching membrane, synaptic, arousal and drug effects;
4. predict a consciousness record beyond ordinary network models;
5. test the objective-reduction timescale rather than fitting it after the event.

The branch closes as a consciousness mechanism if network-level controls reproduce the record or if the proposed quantum variable compiles out. It remains valuable as cell biology.

## 3. Other quantum-consciousness programmes

Quantum-consciousness is not one hypothesis. At least three materially different branches must not borrow evidence from one another:

- **Phosphorus nuclear spins and Posner molecules.** Fisher proposed that phosphorus-31 spins protected in calcium-phosphate clusters could store quantum information and influence synaptic calcium release. This is a concrete biochemical proposal with intermediate gates: the relevant clusters must exist under physiological conditions, exhibit the required coherence or entanglement, reach neural compartments, and have a readout that changes firing. See the [original proposal](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aop.2015.08.020). Subsequent spin-dynamics analysis found no protected long-lived intermolecular singlet and bounded one idealized shared singlet lifetime, illustrating how chemistry can prune the mechanism before any consciousness claim; see the [critical model study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6228494/).
- **Xenon isotope and radical-pair proposals.** A mouse study reported lower anesthetic potency for xenon isotopes carrying nuclear spin than for spin-zero isotopes. This is an interesting isotope-dependent anesthesia result, not a direct consciousness measurement, and it needs independent replication plus chemical alternatives. See the [primary isotope study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29642079/). A radical-pair model can reproduce the direction of that isotope dependence, but model compatibility does not establish that the proposed radical pair exists or mediates anesthesia; see the [mechanism paper](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33737599/).
- **Generic superposition or entanglement proposals.** Claims that any superposition produces experience have a vast scope and therefore need a psychophysical mapping, a rule for combination, and a discriminating experiment—not only the fact that quantum mechanics applies to biology.

These branches share a staged test order: physical state existence, coherence under biological conditions, biochemical transduction, neural mediation, and finally consciousness specificity. Failure at an earlier gate prevents later vocabulary from rescuing the mechanism. “Quantum cognition” also names quantum-probability models of human decisions; mathematical use of Hilbert-space probability does not imply a quantum brain.

## 4. Electromagnetic and field theories

Brains generate and respond to electromagnetic fields; EEG and MEG are established detector channels. The open question is whether endogenous fields are merely records and local influences already contained in electrophysiology, or whether a field-level invariant provides indispensable integration. A serious field theory must supply Maxwell-compatible boundary conditions, source strengths, spatial resolution, perturbation specificity and an observation not reproduced by ephaptic coupling or recurrent neural networks.

Frequency match is not enough. Entrainment requires phase-sensitive transfer, and causal relevance requires a coupling magnitude consistent with noise, tissue filtering and competing endogenous fields.

## 5. Schumann resonance and brain-wave frequencies

Schumann resonances are real, lightning-driven modes of the Earth--ionosphere cavity. Their approximate peaks near 7.8, 14.3, 20.8, 27.3 and 33.8 Hz vary with ionospheric conditions; representative ground magnetic amplitudes are around the picotesla scale. See the [NASA review of measured Earth-cavity characteristics](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20120008699/downloads/20120008699.pdf) and [NASA visualization/mission account](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/10985/).

Human EEG bands occupy overlapping frequencies, but named EEG bands are broad empirical ranges, not narrow external receivers. Spectral overlap is therefore M1: a coincidence between two oscillatory systems with no causal direction established.

There are suggestive studies. A 2016 QEEG analysis reported spectral similarities and periods of coherence, but its aggregation and multiple-comparison structure do not establish environmental driving. A small randomized double-blind insomnia study using an artificial “Schumann resonance” device reported changes in sleep measures, while also carrying a device-patent interest and not testing natural Earth-to-brain coupling. See the [QEEG study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26785376/) and [randomized device study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35707548/). These are hypothesis-generating, not a basis for health claims.

### The decisive Schumann experiment

Use multiple shielded and unshielded sites; co-record calibrated local ELF electric and magnetic fields, EEG/MEG, physiology and environmental interference; preregister cross-spectral phase, lag, directionality and harmonics; include synthetic frequency-shifted, phase-scrambled and amplitude-matched exposures; blind participants and analysts; and replicate across independent laboratories.

Promotion requires all of the following:

- local environmental field predicts neural phase beyond shared time-of-day, power-line, motion, weather and instrumentation effects;
- controlled attenuation or phase manipulation changes the neural record dose-dependently;
- the transfer function is physiologically plausible at the measured amplitude;
- effects track the environmental signal rather than the label “7.83 Hz”;
- held-out replication succeeds.

A frequency-shifted synthetic field producing the same effect would support generic ELF entrainment, not Schumann-specific coupling. No effect under adequate sensitivity would close the direct-coupling branch at that dose while leaving indirect geomagnetic or circadian questions open.

## 6. Altered states, meditation and psychedelics

Dreaming, anesthesia, meditation, seizure, sensory deprivation and psychedelics change conscious contents and organization. They are high-value perturbations because they move the system through distinct metastable regimes. The research target is a transition invariant that generalizes across report, no-report physiology and intervention—not the importation of visionary geometry as a microscopic map.

Nested wheels, lattices, entities, unity and timelessness are admissible phenomenological data when recorded systematically. They become physics only through prospective predictors of neural dynamics, behaviour or independently observed environment. The [altered-state signpost protocol](../../claims/1400-1499/1468_theology_altered_state_and_beauty_signposts_generate_eight_typed_spin23_missing_middle_experiments.md) correctly assigns such motifs zero evidential weight at intake.

## 7. Near-death experiences and terminal lucidity

The established base is that some people report structured experiences around life-threatening events and that striking cognitive episodes near death are reported. The measurement problem is severe: timing of experience relative to physiology is often reconstructed after recovery; drug, hypoxia, sleep, memory consolidation, expectation and selection effects are difficult to separate; apparent absence of brain activity depends on electrode coverage and sensitivity.

The useful experiment is prospective, multisite and time-locked: dense physiological monitoring, hidden targets with audited exposure, immediate structured interviews, complete denominator reporting, medication and resuscitation timelines, and independent adjudication. A verified perception containing information unavailable through normal channels would be an M4 event. Rich experience alone, however important, does not establish perception without brain function or survival of consciousness.

## 8. Psi, anomalous cognition and mind--matter claims

Telepathy, precognition, remote viewing and psychokinesis belong in the atlas because they make empirical claims and have long report histories. They remain M0--M1 as physical mechanisms: the literature is disputed by small effects, flexible protocols, sensory leakage, optional stopping, publication selection and replication instability.

The minimum promotion design uses adversarially agreed protocols, automated randomization and time stamping, tamper-evident raw data, preregistered stopping, complete trial publication, independent replication, and effect survival under increasingly strict leakage controls. The outcome must predict a channel capacity or intervention response, not merely a small aggregate departure whose location changes after reanalysis.

## 9. Panpsychism, idealism and consciousness-first ontologies

These positions may alter what a physical theory is taken to describe, but ontology alone does not supply a distinct record law. They belong at M0 until a version specifies a mathematical carrier, composition rule, dynamics and observation that differs from physicalism under some feasible intervention. Their philosophical value does not depend on reaching that gate; their admission as physics does.

## Corpus implication

The most useful shared question is not “which frequency is consciousness?” but:

\[
\boxed{\text{which proposed variable remains causally necessary after the physical state, detector and nuisance model are made complete?}}
\]

That question keeps the frontier genuinely open while making it progressively harder for coincidence, metaphor or missing measurements to impersonate a new force.
