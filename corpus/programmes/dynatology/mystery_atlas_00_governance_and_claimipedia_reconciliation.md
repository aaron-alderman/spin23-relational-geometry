# Mystery Atlas 00: Governance and Claimipedia Reconciliation

**Date:** 24 October 2026  
**Status:** canonical intake and evidence-separation protocol; not endorsement of any listed interpretation

## Purpose

The corpus needs somewhere for questions that are scientifically live, culturally persistent, weakly evidenced, or conventionally labelled esoteric. Excluding them hides assumptions; admitting them without types corrupts the evidence ledger. This atlas therefore treats a mystery as a **question with an unresolved coordinate**, not as evidence for an extraordinary answer.

Every entry is split into five layers:

1. **Established base** -- observations or records that survive ordinary source criticism.
2. **Conventional account** -- the strongest available explanation, including its actual limits.
3. **Unresolved residue** -- what remains after nuisance, selection and detector effects are modelled.
4. **Speculative branches** -- testable mechanisms, including unconventional ones.
5. **Cultural or metaphysical interpretation** -- historically important meaning claims that do not become physical evidence merely by resembling corpus mathematics.

The governing asymmetry is deliberate: an extraordinary branch is not assigned zero prior probability, but it inherits a larger burden because it must explain the base data, outperform ordinary comparators, and survive independent replication.

That is a **coverage result**, not proof that no differently named passage exists in every historical projection. The reader exposes a fixed captured corpus and paginated claims rather than a semantic full-text search.

Claimipedia nevertheless supplies three useful governance patterns already present as programmes: `independentSource`, `equalEvidence` and `credentialReview`. This atlas imports their discipline, not their topical content:

- evaluate the same evidential defect the same way whether it supports or opposes the preferred mystery;
- separate witness or author credentials from the evidential content of a record;
- prefer independent instruments, teams and analysis paths;
- preserve source identity and version rather than circulating an orphaned claim.

No Claimipedia record was changed. The repository is the present authoring surface; a future projection may export promoted cards into Claimipedia.

## Evidence zones

| Zone | Meaning | Permitted language |
|---|---|---|
| M0 | motif, report or mathematical possibility only | imagined, reported, represented |
| M1 | established object or phenomenon; target mystery may be absent | measured base, ordinary comparator |
| M2 | reproducible anomaly or incomplete conventional mechanism | unresolved residue |
| M3 | testable speculative mechanism with discriminating prediction | candidate mechanism |
| M4 | independently replicated deviation defeating declared conventional comparators | frontier anomaly |
| M5 | new law or ontology required by convergent records | new-physics evidence |

The zones are not a ladder of excitement. Most good investigations terminate at M1 by explaining an apparent anomaly. That is success. A cultural interpretation may remain meaningful while its physics branch closes.

## Dynatology card for mysteries

For each entry define

\[
\mathfrak D_m=(X,A,T,J,\rho,L,D,I,R; B,N,K,Z),
\]

where the first nine coordinates retain their canonical meanings and:

- \(B\) is the established base and best conventional comparator;
- \(N\) is the nuisance and alternative-explanation model;
- \(K\) is the kill test for the extraordinary interpretation;
- \(Z\) is the current evidence zone.

The map must answer four additional questions:

1. Is the claimed effect a property of the object, the environment, the detector, the analyst, or their coupling?
2. Was the numerical pattern predicted before data selection?
3. Does the proposed mechanism transfer enough energy or information to produce the claimed effect?
4. Which result would make proponents lower confidence rather than rename the effect?

## Promotion and firewall rules

- A shared number, symmetry, rhythm or shape is a search pointer, not a mechanism.
- A simulated resonance proves a boundary-value property of the model, not historical intent, biological action or useful power extraction.
- Correlation with a physiological record does not establish environmental driving; simultaneous environmental measurement, phase-sensitive analysis and sham controls are required.
- A neural or microtubule effect under anesthesia does not by itself distinguish quantum consciousness from ordinary pharmacology.
- Unidentified means not yet identified; it does not name an origin.
- Absence of a complete conventional account is not positive evidence for a preferred alternative.
- A clean null closes the tested coupling while preserving the broader cultural or phenomenological question.

## Immediate operating rule

Preserve every mystery that can be stated as a measurement or inference problem. Quarantine only the unsupported identification. The repository can therefore say both:

\[
\boxed{\text{the question is admitted}}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\boxed{\text{the extraordinary conclusion is not yet admitted}}.
\]
