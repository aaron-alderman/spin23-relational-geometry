# Mystery Atlas 10: Muqarnas Mathematics Literature Review and Novelty Boundary

**Date:** 24 October 2026  
**Status:** targeted literature review; novelty assessed conservatively and separated into established mathematics, transferred formalism, new application and possible new theorem  
**Machine record:** [mathematics novelty map](muqarnas_mathematics_literature_map_v1.json)

## Executive answer

The proposed programme is mostly **existing mathematics assembled across fields**, with a smaller core that appears to be a **new formalization and application**. It becomes genuinely new mathematics only if the source-derived muqarnas grammar supports a theorem not already guaranteed by graph rewriting, operads, cell complexes or known nonassociative algebra.

The current novelty boundary is:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{l}
\text{existing: plan geometry, shape grammar, graph reconstruction, typed gluing,}\\
\qquad\text{operadic composition, octonions, Moufang identities, Jordan determinants;}\\[2mm]
\text{apparently new: their source-faithful combination as a muqarnas compiler audit;}\\[2mm]
\text{potentially new mathematics: a native nonassociative grammar, closure invariant,}\\
\qquad\text{or classification theorem proved from the transcribed corpus.}
\end{array}}
\]

A targeted search found no direct scholarly treatment joining muqarnas with octonions, Fano-plane multiplication, Moufang structure or Jordan algebras. Absence from this search is not proof of global bibliographic absence; a publication claim would require database-complete searches, citation chaining and full-text review in architectural history, computational design and nonassociative algebra.

## 1. Directly established muqarnas mathematics

### Practical and projective construction geometry

Historical and modern studies already establish that muqarnas plans use radial grids, circles, polygons, stars, cell vocabularies, tiers and projection rules. Necipoğlu and Al-Asad's geometric analysis of the *Topkapı Scroll* explicitly demonstrates plan-to-three-dimensional transformation and notes that different tier heights can produce equally valid interpretations. This already contains the central idea of a multivalued lift.

The [Topkapı Scroll source](https://www.getty.edu/publications-reports/item/248TH5) therefore precedes our terminology but not our observation that a plan can underdetermine spatial realization.

### Shape grammars

Shape grammar is a mature formalism. Stiny and Gips introduced algorithmic generation of spatial designs, and Stiny formalized languages of two- and three-dimensional shapes. The modern [systematic mapping of fifty years of shape grammars](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/full/10.1177/14780771221089882) documents extensive architectural and engineering applications. The foundational formalism is reviewed in Stiny's [Introduction to Shape and Shape Grammars](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.1068/b070343).

Calling a muqarnas rule system a generative or shape grammar is therefore not new mathematics.

### Computational muqarnas reconstruction

Several papers already move from planar representation to three-dimensional form:

- Gherardini and Leali combine two-dimensional drawings with photogrammetric three-dimensional models and modular hierarchy in their [Persian architectural reconstruction framework](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00004-015-0287-z).
- Computational interpretations generate alternative spatial forms from one planar muqarnas projection.
- Senhaji and Benslimane perform [automatic 3D reconstruction](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1296207416304022) by matching planar shapes to possible spatial elements and composing them through neighbourhood relations.
- Razzaghmanesh and Gürer provide [node- and surface-based graph generation](https://research.itu.edu.tr/en/publications/exploring-muqarnas-generation-through-graph-theory-node-and-surfa/) from the Hakim Mosque mihrab plan.
- Arena, Hajismail and Mercurio's [2026 review and generative study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00004-026-00878-8) explicitly describes two-dimensional tessellation, projection rules and shape grammar.
- Recent [Sahip Ata portal decomposition](https://www.nature.com/articles/s40494-024-01530-9) supplies another grid, cell and projection case.

The claims that muqarnas are modular, rule governed, graph representable and computationally generable are established.

## 2. Existing mathematics ready to transfer

### Typed and attributed graph rewriting

Double-pushout graph transformation models a rule as a typed replacement with a preserved gluing interface. Its concurrency and commutativity theorems state when independent rule applications can be reordered. Habel, Müller and Plump's [double-pushout analysis](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/mathematical-structures-in-computer-science/article/abs/doublepushout-graph-transformation-revisited/AF02050525390437E1DF746DE4459926) and Lack and Sobociński's [adhesive categories](https://www.brics.dk/RS/03/31/index.html) already provide the relevant foundation.

This is a strong candidate for cell replacement and boundary sewing. Applying it to a four-layer historical muqarnas transcription would be new domain work, not a new theory of graph rewriting.

### Operads and wiring diagrams

Operads encode many-input composition and hierarchical substitution. Wiring-diagram algebras explicitly distinguish syntactic interconnection from semantic realization; see Vagner, Spivak and Lerman's [open dynamical systems](https://www.tac.mta.ca/tac/volumes/30/51/30-51abs.html) and the Royal Society review of [operads for system design](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.2021.0099).

This is almost exactly the formal home for the proposed compiler square:

\[
\text{syntactic cell assembly}
\xrightarrow{\text{semantics}}
\text{geometric realization}.
\]

If realization respects operadic composition, the square commutes. A measured failure means either the semantics is incomplete, hidden state is missing, or the proposed macro-operation is not an operation in the frozen operad. This reframes “nonassociativity” more carefully than treating any order dependence as octonionic.

### Cell complexes, boundary words and topology

CW/simplicial complexes, combinatorial maps, rotation systems, covering spaces, monodromy and cellular homology already describe incidence, oriented boundary and gluing. They are appropriate for the cell complex and half-vault boundary word. None needs to be reinvented.

### Constraint satisfaction and parametric CAD

Enumerating height, tier, bend and cell-type assignments is a finite constraint-satisfaction or mixed discrete-continuous geometry problem. Existing SAT/SMT, integer programming and parametric CAD methods can implement it. The novelty would be the source-faithful constraints and complete lift enumeration.

### Partial algebras and multicategories

Cell attachment is naturally partial: two objects compose only when typed boundaries match. Partial algebras, categories and multicategories already formalize this. A failed intermediate attachment is not automatically a nonassociative associator; it may merely be undefined.

## 3. Existing exceptional mathematics

### Octonions and the Fano plane

The oriented Fano plane, octonion multiplication, alternativity, associators, \(G_2\), triality and projective/Jordan geometry are classical. Baez's [review](https://people.sissa.it/~cecotti/octonions.pdf) remains a central entry point. Incidence-based octonion reconstruction is also active mathematical literature.

No new octonion mathematics follows from labelling seven architectural cells. A valid result would require the complete native multiplication or junction table to satisfy octonion identities and predict withheld relations.

### Moufang structures

Moufang identities, loops and planes are established nonassociative geometry. In particular, Moufang projective planes are tied to alternative division algebras and the octonions. Testing a muqarnas partial grammar against Moufang identities is novel as an application, but the identities and their theory are not new.

### Jordan geometry and determinants

Exceptional Jordan algebras, rank, determinant, idempotents and the Cayley plane are established. Modern determinant work includes [determinants in Jordan matrix algebras](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/03081087.2022.2049187). What is not established is that a muqarnas compatibility object naturally forms a Jordan algebra or that a Jordan determinant predicts architectural closure.

## 4. What appears new in this programme

### A. The four-layer source object

Representing one historical plan simultaneously as

\[
(G_{\rm scaffold},K_{\rm cell},h_{\rm level},B_{\rm boundary})
\]

with evidence status and uncertainty attached to every relation appears to be a new synthesis. Individual layers are familiar; their governed separation is the contribution.

This is a **new data model and research method**, not yet new pure mathematics.

### B. The muqarnas compiler square

Comparing macro-cell collapse followed by spatial realization against direct realization followed by assembly appears novel in the muqarnas literature reviewed:

\[
R\circ C\stackrel{?}{=}\operatorname{Compose}\circ R^*.
\]

Categorically, this asks whether realization is an algebra morphism or functor for the chosen grammar. The abstract question is existing mathematics; the historically grounded object, residual classification and experiment appear new.

### C. Quotient-scar classification

Classifying noncommutation residuals as exterior geometry, internal seam, tier, orientation, support or erased history appears to be a new application of the corpus's quotient-scar ontology.

### D. Source-selected scalarization

The corpus's octonion associator lacks a preferred imaginary direction. Muqarnas supply independently selected radial, boundary-normal and vertical directions. Testing whether a native compiler residual can be scalarized by those architectural directions without circular fitting appears new.

It remains only an experiment until a common carrier or intertwiner is constructed.

### E. Predictive exceptional-algebra audit

No direct literature was found testing a muqarnas grammar for alternativity, Moufang identities, signed Fano incidence or Jordan closure. Running these tests with label-permutation corrections and withheld-junction prediction appears new.

## 5. What would qualify as genuinely new mathematics

The following would cross from new application into a mathematical contribution.

### Theorem class 1 -- grammar classification

Prove that a frozen family of muqarnas grammars belongs to a classified algebraic type: associative operad algebra, inverse semigroup, groupoid, alternative partial algebra, Moufang loopoid or another precisely defined structure.

### Theorem class 2 -- lift-fibre classification

Characterize

\[
\mathcal L(P)=\{M:\pi(M)=P\}/\!\sim
\]

for a family of plans: prove finiteness, parameter dimension, connected components, obstruction classes or completeness of an enumeration algorithm.

### Theorem class 3 -- compiler-defect invariant

Construct a defect \(\Delta\) independent of transcription coordinates and reduction presentation; prove its transformation law under reflection, refinement and boundary sewing; show that it detects inequivalent lift histories.

### Theorem class 4 -- closure determinant

Construct a source-native symmetric or Jordan object \(J(P)\) and prove

\[
\det J(P)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{specified failure or singular closure condition}.
\]

A fitted matrix that happens to correlate with known valid plans is not enough.

### Theorem class 5 -- universality across styles

Prove that regional muqarnas grammars are functorially related, share a universal core, or occupy distinct equivalence classes under declared local transformations.

### Theorem class 6 -- exceptional positive or no-go

Either exhibit an explicit predictive Fano/Moufang subgrammar, or prove that the native grammar cannot contain one under the allowed composition and orientation rules. A clean no-go would also be new and valuable.

## 6. Novelty risk

The largest risk is terminological novelty: redescribing established shape grammar, graph rewriting or operadic semantics with new names. The programme should therefore use existing machinery wherever possible:

| Programme term | Existing mathematical home |
|---|---|
| typed cell attachment | attributed graph grammar / partial algebra |
| macro-cell collapse | graph rewriting / operadic substitution |
| boundary word | combinatorial map / open graph interface |
| lift fibre | inverse problem / moduli fibre / constraint solution set |
| construction-history scar | noncommuting abstraction/realization square |
| sewing independence | DPO concurrency or coherence theorem |
| tier monodromy | permutation representation / covering monodromy |
| closure matrix | compatibility, rigidity or response matrix |

The new vocabulary is justified only where it distinguishes evidence and physical interpretation not captured by the standard formalism.

## Verdict

At present this is best described as:

> a new source-governed mathematical synthesis and experimental programme built mostly from existing mathematics.

It is already more than an analogy because the radial generator has been recovered from a primary source and the compiler boundary has an exact certificate. It is not yet a new algebra or theorem because the native cell grammar and three-dimensional realization map have not been extracted.

The fastest route to new mathematics is not to start with octonions. It is to prove something about the lift fibre, boundary sewing or compiler defect. If that object then satisfies alternative, Moufang or Jordan identities, the exceptional connection will be earned rather than imposed.
