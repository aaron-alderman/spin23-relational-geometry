# Mystery Atlas 21: Aether, substrate and the universal-cone problem

**Date:** 25 October 2026
**Status:** governed frontier map; established constraints joined to testable substrate branches; no evidence for a preferred frame admitted

## Result

The corpus may keep **aether** as the intuitive historical name for an underlying carrier, but it must not use the word as though it named one theory. At least six inequivalent propositions sit beneath it:

1. the quantum vacuum has nontrivial state and response;
2. a dynamical timelike field selects a preferred local frame;
3. Lorentz kinematics emerges in the infrared from non-Lorentz microscopic dynamics;
4. spacetime and matter both emerge from a relational or discrete substrate;
5. a material medium supplies analogue relativistic propagation without being the vacuum of our universe; and
6. a dark material sector behaves as a condensate or superfluid on some scales.

Only the first is part of ordinary quantum field theory without further ontology. The others require distinct actions, modes, couplings and observations. The old mechanical luminiferous aether is not restored merely because the vacuum has structure, a Higgs expectation value, Casimir response or quantum fluctuations.

The productive question is therefore not

> Does the aether exist?

but

> Is there an independently variable carrier state whose dynamics and ports explain the observed universal causal cone while predicting a record not already contained in Lorentz-invariant effective field theory or general relativity?

This is presently an **M1/M3 hypothesis family**: strong null constraints and complete candidate theories exist, but no preferred-frame or microscopic-substrate signal has been admitted.

## 1. Vocabulary firewall

| Name | Minimal content | What it does not imply |
|---|---|---|
| structured vacuum | QFT ground state, condensates, susceptibilities and boundary-dependent correlators | material rest frame or drag |
| vacuum order parameter | a field has a nonzero expectation value | observable Lorentz violation unless the expectation value carries spacetime indices or produces nonuniversal propagation |
| Einstein--aether | a generally covariant metric theory with a unit timelike vector field | mechanical fluid, electromagnetic aether or microscopic explanation of that vector |
| khronon/preferred foliation | a scalar defines preferred spacelike leaves | detectable absolute motion in every sector |
| emergent Lorentz symmetry | low-energy excitations share approximately relativistic dispersion | exact universality at all scales or emergent gravity dynamics |
| analogue spacetime | excitations in a known medium obey an effective curved metric | ontological identity between the laboratory medium and spacetime |
| relational/discrete substrate | continuum geometry is an effective description of more primitive relations | a preferred frame; some discrete constructions preserve Lorentz symmetry statistically |
| dark condensate | a new material sector develops collective modes | identification with the electromagnetic vacuum or corpus carrier |

“No aether” is thus too coarse. What observations strongly exclude is a broad class of **accessible preferred-frame, dispersive, birefringent, drag-producing or equivalence-violating couplings**. A perfectly decoupled substrate is empirically empty; a Lorentz-invariant substrate may be physically meaningful but is not an absolute frame.

+O\!\left(\frac{k^3}{\Lambda},\frac{k^4}{\Lambda^2}\right)

for every observed species, with one effective metric (g_{\mathrm{eff}}). A preferred timelike direction (u^\mu) permits additional invariants such as

(u\!\cdot\!k)^2,\qquad

\frac{(h^{\mu\nu}k_\mu k_\nu)^2}{\Lambda^2},

Their coefficients control anisotropy, species-dependent limiting speeds, dispersion and birefringence. This makes the central missing theorem precise:

\[
\boxed{

\longrightarrow
\text{one radiatively stable universal cone}

\]

It is insufficient to tune equal speeds at one scale. Matter loops, symmetry breaking, temperature, density and curvature must not split them beyond observation.

The full substrate packet is

where (S_B) is the carrier action, (Pi_i) are physical ports to each species, (mathcal N) is its noise/dissipation kernel, (D) is the detector map and (R) is the recovery limit yielding established physics. This extends the corpus generator--state--port discipline: naming a carrier without its state, action, ports and detector is not yet a model.

## 3. Goldstone modes and the missing fifth force

If a continuous global symmetry is spontaneously broken, flat-space reasoning suggests gapless Nambu--Goldstone directions. For broken spacetime symmetry the counting is subtler, and with diffeomorphism or gauge redundancy some apparent modes are constrained, mixed or absorbed. A substrate proposal must nevertheless perform the mode audit rather than saying that the carrier is hidden.

For each fluctuation about (B_0), record:

- spin or helicity;
- kinetic sign and characteristic speed;
- mass or gap;
- coupling to conserved matter sources;
- screening or decoupling mechanism;
- radiative stability; and
- cosmological and compact-object production.

An unscreened light scalar or vector generally mediates an additional long-range interaction, modifies radiation loss, or violates universality of free fall. This is the “massless fifth-force phonon” problem. It is not fatal by definition, but it demands either sufficiently weak coupling, a mass gap, symmetry-protected derivative coupling, environmental screening, or a demonstration that the would-be mode is not physical.

Einstein--aether theory makes this audit explicit: besides the two tensor polarizations, its linearized metric--aether system contains additional spin-1 and spin-0 modes with coupling-dependent speeds. Binary pulsars, cosmology, stability, vacuum-Cherenkov considerations and gravitational waves then constrain the same parameters. This is a model of how the corpus should handle every substrate branch: count first, constrain second, interpret last.

## 4. Corpus translation

### 4.1 Higher relational space and the local carrier

The higher relational space may be represented by (B), while a local response face carries a bilinear or symplectic invariant

\[
M^{T}\Omega M=\Omega .
\]

This equation says that (M) preserves (Omega) on the declared carrier. It does **not** establish that (Omega) is the vacuum substrate, that (M) is a spacetime transformation, or that a preferred frame exists. A physical bridge requires a map

\[
(B,S_B,\Pi)\longrightarrow(\Omega,M,D)
\]

derived before the detector record is fitted. The locally invariant carrier language is therefore compatible with a substrate hypothesis while remaining neutral about it.

### 4.2 T1/T2

A useful conditional realization is:

- **T1:** fast excitation propagation on the effective cone;
- **T2:** slower background configuration, order parameter, foliation or mixing channel that changes the admissible T1 dynamics.

This is a research ansatz, not a consequence of (Spin(2,3)). Its decisive question is whether eliminating T2 leaves a measurable memory, dispersion, hysteresis, extra pole or noise kernel. If T2 compiles out exactly, it is either redundant description or inaccessible ontology.

### 4.3 Higgs and condensates

The Higgs vacuum expectation value demonstrates that a vacuum can contain an order parameter without behaving like a nineteenth-century medium. The Standard Model Higgs expectation value is a Lorentz scalar: it changes masses and mixing while selecting no spacetime direction. A vector, tensor or derivative expectation value could instead select a frame or foliation and must face Lorentz tests. “There is a VEV” and “there is an absolute frame” are therefore different claims.

### 4.4 Gravity and thermodynamics

General relativity already couples clock rates, acceleration and equilibrium temperature through geometry. A substrate model earns contact only if it derives the metric response, stress tensor and conservation laws from (B), or predicts a controlled departure. Merely relabelling the gravitational field as density, pressure or refractive index adds no record law.

The high-value join is a fluctuation--dissipation question: if gravity or inertia is a collective response of (B), what noise accompanies its susceptibility, and why has that noise not appeared in clocks, free fall, interferometers or compact objects? A lossless zero-temperature sector is possible, but must be shown rather than assumed.

### 4.5 Casimir effects

Casimir and Casimir--Polder forces are excellent probes of boundary-conditioned field response and material dispersion. They do not by themselves detect a medium rest frame or microscopic cutoff. The same force can be organized as a change in zero-point modes, a stress-tensor calculation, or interacting matter--field response; only differences in records arbitrate ontology.

The corpus's same-carrier Casimir--thermoelectric programme becomes substrate-sensitive only if a declared (B) predicts an additional distance, orientation, boost, sidereal, temperature or nonequilibrium dependence that survives a full Lifshitz/material/noise model. “A force exists in vacuum” is not that prediction.

## 5. Literature-facing branches

### A. Preferred-frame effective field theory

The Standard-Model Extension supplies a sector-by-sector coefficient language for Lorentz and CPT violation and a continually maintained constraint ledger. Einstein--aether and khronometric/Hořava models provide explicit gravitational actions rather than a generic medium metaphor. Their value to this programme is methodological: they translate a preferred direction into modes, speeds, radiation and post-Newtonian observables.

The joint GW170817/GRB 170817A observation bounded the fractional gravity--light speed difference to the (10^{-15}) scale for that event. This does not exclude every preferred-frame theory, but it kills broad untuned regions and makes “different cones that later converge” a quantitative radiative-stability problem.

### B. Emergent Lorentz symmetry

Condensed-matter systems show that relativistic quasiparticles and effective metrics can arise from nonrelativistic constituents. The frontier is not whether one Dirac cone can emerge, but whether **all** matter, gauge and gravitational excitations share one cone, one equivalence principle and sufficiently suppressed higher-order dispersion. Renormalization-group attraction toward a common speed is a candidate mechanism; the corpus should ask for its basin of attraction and stability across symmetry breaking.

### C. Analogue gravity and backreaction

Bose--Einstein condensates and other quantum fluids permit controlled horizons, effective curved metrics, dispersion above a healing scale and, increasingly, backreaction calculations. They cannot establish that spacetime is a fluid. They can test the logical machinery needed by a substrate theory: mode conversion, horizon robustness, ultraviolet dispersion, state preparation, detector folding and backreaction on the carrier.

### D. Discrete and relational microstructure

A microscopic cutoff need not select a lattice rest frame. Causal-set-style random orders, quantum graphs, tensor networks and other relational constructions ask how continuum dimension, locality and Lorentz invariance are recovered. The corpus contribution should be a recovery audit: which invariant survives coarse-graining, what defect remains, and what detector can see it?

### E. Dark condensates and superfluids

Dark-sector condensates can possess phonons, vortices and environment-dependent phases while leaving ordinary electromagnetism approximately Lorentz invariant. This is a materially different proposal from vacuum aether. Its discriminators are galactic dynamics, lensing, structure formation, merger behaviour, phonon-mediated forces and phase-transition scales—not Michelson--Morley alone.

## 7. Minimal executable programme

### Gate S0: classify the claim

Declare whether the proposal contains a scalar condensate, timelike vector, foliation scalar, discrete order, material dark medium or only standard QFT vacuum structure. Mixed labels fail intake.

### Gate S1: freeze one action and state

Supply (S_B), background (B_0), stress tensor, conservation laws and stable fluctuation spectrum. Reject ghosts, gradient instabilities and undeclared strong coupling before phenomenology.

### Gate S2: derive the ports

For photons, fermions, neutrinos and gravity, derive rather than name (Pi_i). Record which sectors are exactly decoupled and whether that decoupling is radiatively stable.

### Gate S3: recover the known limit

Show one universal metric, ordinary gauge response, general-relativistic weak fields and established thermodynamics within declared tolerances. A verbal “emergent Lorentz symmetry” is not recovery.

### Gate S4: choose one residual

Preselect one of: sidereal anisotropy, species-cone splitting, higher-order dispersion, extra polarization, dipole radiation, equivalence violation, excess noise, hysteresis or a topological defect signature.

### Gate S5: detector and nuisance closure

Supply raw observables, calibration, conventional forward model, material/environmental nuisances, analysis provenance and a result that would lower confidence. Cross-sector combination occurs only after each detector has a valid local model.

## 8. Stop rules

The branch stops or changes status when:

- the substrate has no independently variable state and changes no record distribution;
- all couplings are set to zero solely to evade constraints;
- the universal cone is imposed separately for each species rather than derived;
- a Casimir, Higgs, superconducting or vacuum-polarization effect is treated as proof of a preferred frame;
- the proposal imports gravity by naming an effective metric but derives neither Einstein dynamics nor a controlled alternative;
- a frequency, group or matrix resemblance substitutes for a microscopic port; or
- every null result is absorbed by moving the substrate beyond the tested scale without a prior scaling law.
