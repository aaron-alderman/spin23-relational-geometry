## 2. Gauge equation-of-motion quotient

The hypercharge phase current is gauged. In the gauge-invariant local operator quotient it is related by the Abelian gauge-field equation of motion to

\[
\partial_\nu B^{\nu\mu}.
\]

It therefore does not supply an independent global source direction of the same kind as baryon or lepton number. Modulo that gauged/EOM direction, the classical global symmetry space is two-dimensional:

\[
\operatorname{span}\{B,L\}.
\]

The electroweak anomaly breaks \(B+L\) while preserving \(B-L\) when the right-handed-neutrino completion is present and no Majorana term is inserted. The exact continuous protected global space is therefore one-dimensional.

This is compatible with the earlier Ward analysis: one axis is genuinely protected; a second physical response channel can exist, but it cannot acquire equal status merely by being placed beside \(B-L\) in a matrix.

## 3. Higgs dressing of incidence is dynamical, not canonical

Extend incidence by an arbitrary Higgs phase weight:

\[
\lambda_h=(5,5,1,1,1,0,h).
\]

Its four Yukawa violations are

\[
V_H\lambda_h=(10+h,\,6-h,\,2-h,\,1+h).
\]

No value of \(h\) makes all four entries vanish. More strongly, the value that minimizes a positive weighted breaking norm

\[
\mathcal B(h)=
w_u(10+h)^2+w_d(6-h)^2+w_e(2-h)^2+w_\nu(1+h)^2

\[
\boxed{
h_*= -\frac{10w_u-6w_d-2w_e+w_\nu}
{w_u+w_d+w_e+w_\nu}.}

The preferred Higgs dressing therefore depends on the four interaction weights. For equal weights, \(h_*=-3/4\). In a top-Yukawa-dominated regime it approaches \(-10\), cancelling the up-type violation while enhancing others.

This supplies a plausible continuation rather than a terminal obstruction: the interaction hierarchy can define a running best-fit incidence direction.
