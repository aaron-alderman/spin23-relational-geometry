## Interpretation now ruled out

The following shortcuts are closed:

- a triple bond universally predicts a physical \(3{:}1\) coefficient;
- three representatives automatically imply three physical vacua or generations;
- a one-dimensional fixed vector range implies a one-dimensional observable algebra;
- a clean two-state exponential fit excludes hidden slower modes;
- an unlabeled scalar detector can recover the orientation of triality breaking;
- an exceptional parent follows merely because the same integer appears in a fold, index or family count.

## Surviving general theorem schema

For each proposed quotient, declare a tuple

\[
\mathcal Q=(X,G,\pi,\mathcal I,\mathcal L,\mathcal D),
\]

where:

- \(X\) is the parent carrier;
- \(G\) is the identified symmetry;
- \(\pi\) is the quotient, expectation or coarse-graining map;
- \(\mathcal I\) is the invariant scar;
- \(\mathcal L\) is the induced dynamics; and
- \(\mathcal D\) is the detector/readout.

A physical quotient-scar claim is admissible only if the following diagram is typed and tested:

\[
X\xrightarrow{\mathcal L}X
\quad\overset{\pi}{\longrightarrow}\quad
X/G\xrightarrow{\mathcal L_{\rm eff}}X/G
\xrightarrow{\mathcal D}\text{records}.
\]

Failure of \(\pi\mathcal L=\mathcal L_{\rm eff}\pi\) is not automatically fatal; it means the quotient dynamics has memory and requires a non-Markov effective kernel.

## Remaining nonredundant branches

### R1 — carrier-specific conditional-expectation index

Calculate range, bimodule, finite-index status and dissipative gap for the actual expectations in claims 704 and 706. This can determine whether the exceptional and Clifford selectors differ by more than range.

### R2 — oriented detector for \(\operatorname{sgn}\Delta\)

Construct a pair of physically reversed detector faces whose exchange is a transposition of the three microscopic channels. Verify that their antisymmetric record flips \(\Delta\) while leaving \(q,c,\Delta^2\) fixed.

### R3 — triality action on the existing magic-square modules

Apply invariant/coinvariant descent to the actual modules \(A,B,C\) of claims 322--327, rather than to scalar surrogates. Determine the descended bilinear, cubic and fermionic structures and whether the overlapping-generation obstruction changes. It is expected not to change, but must be proved.

### R4 — singularity and monodromy realization

Construct the \(D_4\) deformation, order-three action, quotient discriminant and vanishing-cycle monodromy. Decide whether a localized mode carries the discarded triality doublet.

### R5 — Freudenthal action comparison

Compare the doubled-field Freudenthal action with the source--dual-response carrier. A valid bridge must select the same corner and normalization and produce the critical-null equation variationally.

### R6 — physical platform map

Search the frontier target set for a carrier with three resolvable channels, tunable exact/weak cyclic symmetry, time-resolved records and two independent contrast detectors. Phonon/magnon, Floquet, synthetic gauge and open-system platforms are candidates; selection requires an explicit cost and nuisance model.

## Priority

Proceed in order \(R3\to R1\to R2\). These can be decided internally or by finite computation. Treat \(R4\) and \(R5\) as literature-and-construction branches. Enter \(R6\) only with the exact invariants from the earlier branches, so the experiment tests a quotient scar rather than generic three-mode dynamics.
