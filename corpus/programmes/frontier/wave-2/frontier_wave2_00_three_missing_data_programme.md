## W2-A — normalized response projection

Determine whether the action-derived dressed electron Dirac response admits a canonical projection to a positive scalar

\[
\rho_e(s)\in H_2(\mathbb R)_+
\]

with fixed residue normalization. The calculation must distinguish:

- Lorentz and helicity projection;
- broken-phase Higgs-VEV normalization;
- Ward/EOM terms;
- local contacts versus separated spectral support;
- source-index positivity versus spinor positivity.

**Promotion:** one action-derived map with no free face rotation or relative residue.  
**No-go:** every admissible map retains a continuous projection/residue family.  
**Pin:** a named loop integral or experimental normalization is the only missing input.

## W2-B — physical source realization

Classify the smallest local unitary actions coupling a physical field to the incidence and \(B-L\) source quotient. Determine whether shift symmetry, anomaly freedom, RG flow, Furey incidence, or another declared principle selects one of the two incidence rays.

**Promotion:** a symmetry or dynamics fixes the ray and makes the factor-four electron/up discriminator a no-refit prediction.  
**No-go:** the most general action contains an unconstrained source vector spanning the same quotient.  
**Pin:** UV matching data are irreducible but experimentally addressable.

## W2-C — protected exceptional selector

Test whether the tuned \(10_H\oplus\overline{126}_H\) diquark-cancelling kernel can be symmetry protected. The target must enforce both:

\[
F\propto H
\]

and the fixed mediator mixing vectors while preventing orthogonal heavy states from regenerating dangerous exchange.

**Promotion:** a complete symmetry/finite-calculus action makes the kernel invariant under allowed counterterms.  
**No-go:** any protecting symmetry either restores the dangerous channels, removes a required Furey/Yukawa link, or leaves an allowed misalignment counterterm.  
**Pin:** the finite representation or one-form data needed to decide the invariant counterterm ring are absent.

## Cross-branch strike

\[

\longrightarrow

\]
