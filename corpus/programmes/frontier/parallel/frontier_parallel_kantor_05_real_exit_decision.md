## Decision

The phase-doubled local product cannot carry contact (Spin(2,3)). A real two-plane can, but only if its reality is physically supplied before the Kantor product is applied.

Among the four proposed selectors:

- Higgs dressing solves gauge typing but not antiunitary selection;
- a CP-odd spurion supplies orientation but not an invariant real form;
- Majorana/Nambu reality supplies a gauge-covariant real plane, with a no-half-projection caveat;
- a neutral real boundary supplies a complete effective real plane, with a microscopic-provenance caveat.

This initially left one focused bridge:

\[
\boxed{

\xrightarrow{\ S\ }

\qquad
S^T\omega_{\rm boundary}S=\omega_{\rm Nambu}.

The linear classification has now been executed. If the target boundary plane is strictly gauge neutral, the only linear equivariant (S) is zero. If it retains the same rotating connection, reflection matching fixes (S) to (pm I) or (pm D). The strictly neutral bridge must therefore be quadratic and use at least two equal-charge faces; see [the completed intertwiner classification](frontier_parallel_kantor_06_nambu_boundary_intertwiner_classification.md).

## Required properties of the bridge

The intertwiner must:

1. descend from the gauge-covariant Nambu module without treating its redundant half as mirror matter;
2. land in neutral, gauge-invariant response variables;
3. preserve the Euclidean metric used by (V_K);
4. preserve the area form up to the physically declared boundary orientation sign;
5. intertwine CAR conjugation with Fourier/boundary reality;
6. be unique up to at most the already known global sign;
7. arise from an action or conditional expectation rather than a basis fit.

If such an (S) exists, functoriality immediately transports the real Kantor product and its contact (mathfrak{sp}(4,\mathbb R)) grading to the observable boundary carrier.
