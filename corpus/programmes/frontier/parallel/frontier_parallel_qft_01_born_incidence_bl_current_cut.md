1. recover the corpus UV Gram matrices from physical left/right Dirac couplings;

## 1. Scheme, branch and operator basis

Work after electroweak symmetry breaking in dimensional regularization and \(\overline{\mathrm{MS}}\). At the order calculated here there is one fermion loop and no internal gauge or Higgs line. An anticommuting \(\gamma_5\) is therefore sufficient; beyond this order the axial-current prescription and finite Ward-restoring renormalization must be declared explicitly.

The underlying unbroken-phase dimension-three vector basis is

\[
(j_Q,j_{u^c},j_{d^c},j_L,j_{e^c},j_{\nu^c},j_H),
\]

with \(\partial_\nu B^{\nu\mu}\) retained or eliminated according to a declared hypercharge equation-of-motion convention and \(\partial^\mu(H^\dagger H)\) restored for longitudinal/off-shell contacts. Modulo hypercharge and the total-gradient convention, the external global-source quotient has dimension six. The present \((\lambda,q)\) plane is a chosen two-dimensional slice of that larger mixing problem.

The current coefficients in the all-left-handed corpus convention are

\[
\lambda_d=(5,5,1,1,1,0),\qquad
\lambda_m=(5,5,1,1,3,0),\qquad
q=3(B-L)=(1,-1,-1,-3,3,3).

Here \(d\) denotes the deduplicated and \(m\) the multiplicity-preserving incidence assignment. For a physical Dirac fermion, an all-left-handed coefficient \(x_{f^c}\) becomes the right-handed charge \(-x_{f^c}\). Thus the physical chiral charges are:

The crucial structural fact is visible immediately: \(q\) is purely vectorial, whereas incidence has axial components. Mass thresholds can therefore leak incidence into a longitudinal spectral channel while leaving \(B-L\) exactly transverse.

## 2. Renormalized correlator and contacts

Define

\[
i\!\int d^4x\,e^{iqx}\langle T J_i^\mu(x)J_j^\nu(0)\rangle_R
=(q^\mu q^\nu-q^2\eta^{\mu\nu})\Pi^T_{ij}(q^2)
+q^\mu q^\nu\Pi^L_{ij}(q^2)
+P^{\mu\nu}_{ij}(q),
\]

where \(i,j\in\{\lambda,q\}\) and \(P^{\mu\nu}_{ij}\) is a real local polynomial fixed by source counterterms. We use

\[
\rho^{T,L}_{ij}(s)=\frac1\pi\operatorname{Im}\Pi^{T,L}_{ij}(s+i0).
\]

Minimal subtraction removes the ultraviolet pole. Any finite change of the quadratic source counterterms changes \(P_{ij}^{\mu\nu}\) but has zero discontinuity for \(s>0\). Hence the Born spectral formula below is scheme independent at separated timelike momentum, even though the reconstructed real correlator and its subtraction constants are not.

For a Dirac species \(f\), write

\[
v_i^f=\frac{g_{L,i}^f+g_{R,i}^f}{2},\qquad
a_i^f=\frac{g_{R,i}^f-g_{L,i}^f}{2},\qquad
\beta_f(s)=\sqrt{1-\frac{4m_f^2}{s}}\,\Theta(s-4m_f^2).
\]

Direct evaluation of the cut fermion bubble gives

\[
\boxed{
\rho^T_{ij}(s)=
\sum_f\frac{N_c^f}{12\pi^2}
\left[
v_i^fv_j^f\,\beta_f\!\left(1+\frac{2m_f^2}{s}\right)
+a_i^fa_j^f\,\beta_f^3
\right].}
\]

In the tensor convention above, the longitudinal cut is

\[
\boxed{
\rho^L_{ij}(s)=
\sum_f\frac{N_c^f}{2\pi^2}
a_i^fa_j^f\frac{m_f^2}{s}\beta_f.}
\]

The second formula is also the two-point Ward check: \(q_\mu J_i^\mu=2m_fa_i^f\bar f\gamma_5f\), apart from anomalous terms. Since \(a_q^f=0\) for every completed Dirac pair,

\[
\boxed{\rho^L_{qq}=\rho^L_{\lambda q}=0,\qquad \rho^L_{\lambda\lambda}\ge0.}
\]

This is the exact Born-level \(B-L\) Ward constraint. It fails if a Majorana mass is introduced, which is a genuinely different programme branch.

## 3. Explicit two-current matrix

For the deduplicated incidence assignment, the mixed transverse cut receives contributions only from down-type fermions and neutrinos:

\[
\rho^T_{\lambda_dq}(s)
=\frac1{12\pi^2}\sum_{g=1}^{N_g}
\left[

where

\[
F_V(s,m)=\beta(s,m)\left(1+\frac{2m^2}{s}\right).

The top/up sector and charged-lepton sector are pure axial-vector pairings against a vector \(B-L\) source and therefore contribute no mixed two-point cut at this order. This is not a fitted zero; it follows from the chiral charge table.

## 4. A contact-free signed interference observable

Couple two weak, calibrated external vector probes \(A_\mu,B_\mu\) to \(J_\lambda,J_q\). At fixed timelike \(s\), prepare coherent source combinations

\[
J_\pm=\frac{J_\lambda}{N_\lambda}\pm\frac{J_q}{N_q}.
\]

By the optical theorem, the difference of inclusive absorptive rates is

\[
\boxed{
\Delta\mathcal R(s)
\equiv\mathcal R_+(s)-\mathcal R_-(s)
=\frac{4\rho^T_{\lambda q}(s)}{N_\lambda N_q}}
\]

up to the common, known probe kinematic factor. The sum determines the two diagonal cuts. Therefore the signed coherence

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal C_T(s)=
\frac{\rho^T_{\lambda q}(s)}
{\sqrt{\rho^T_{\lambda\lambda}(s)\rho^T_{qq}(s)}}}
}
\]

is measurable by two-port inclusive tomography, is independent of local contact counterterms, and is invariant under independent positive rescalings of the two calibrated sources.

At the massless Born checkpoint,

\[
\mathcal C_d^{UV}=\frac9{\sqrt{231\cdot48}}
\simeq0.08547,
\qquad
\mathcal C_m^{UV}=\frac{15}{\sqrt{239\cdot48}}
\simeq0.14005.
\]

The two incidence normalizations therefore make distinct, signed, scale-free predictions **if** their integer source definitions are operationally fixed. The certificate also verifies \(|\mathcal C_T|\le1\) across representative separated thresholds.

## 5. What this advances—and what it does not

### Advance

- The source-first branch now has an explicit renormalized nonlocal cut, not merely a free charge Gram matrix.
- The conserved and broken axes are distinguished dynamically by \(\rho^L\): \(B-L\) is exactly null while incidence is mass-sensitive.
- The deduplicated/multiplicity incidence fork can be read out by a signed mixed cut.
- The formulas give direct lattice, dispersive, or collider-source targets, including threshold functions and Ward checks.

### No-go boundary

Under the allowed finite source redefinition

\[
J_\lambda\longmapsto aJ_\lambda+bJ_q,
\]

the spectral matrix transforms by congruence and \(\mathcal C_T\) changes with the shear \(b\). Indeed the UV-orthogonal representative has \(\rho_{\lambda_\perp q}^{UV}=0\) by construction. Consequently no one-energy number extracted from the \(2\times2\) matrix can select the incidence ray while the source frame remains an affine torsor.

This is a sharp result, not a failure of the calculation:

\[
\boxed{
\text{the absorptive cut supplies a ruler readout, but it does not select the ruler.}}
\]

The observable becomes physical when an apparatus, UV mediator, lattice operator definition, or source-matching condition fixes the coupling vectors. Without that datum, only congruence invariants formed from two or more response matrices are frame independent.
