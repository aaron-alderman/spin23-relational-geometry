## Executive result

The shortest mediated seam can be completed to an explicit real-even first-order finite geometry, but it does not survive as an ordinary Connes three-form.

Start from

\[
PN\longrightarrow PS\longrightarrow NS\longrightarrow NP.
\]

Closure under the real structure adds

\[
NP\longrightarrow SP\longrightarrow SN\longrightarrow PN.
\]

Together these six sectors form the connected cycle

\[
\boxed{PN-PS-NS-NP-SP-SN-PN\cong C_6.}
\]

There is an explicit \(6\times6\) Dirac matrix on this cycle with

\[
J^2=1,
\qquad JD=DJ,
\qquad J\gamma=-\gamma J,
\qquad D\gamma=-\gamma D,
\]

and

\[
[[D,L(a)],R(b)]=0.
\]

Thus the enlarged mediator graph really does realize the desired six-cycle at label level. This is stronger than the previous graph-existence result.

However, the forward seam factors as

\[
E_{PN,PS}\,E_{PS,NS}\,E_{NS,NP}=E_{PN,NP},
\]

with calculus types

\[
\boxed{\text{right}\;\times\;\text{left}\;\times\;\text{right}.}
\]

It is nonzero as a matrix product, but it belongs to neither

\[
\Omega_D^3(\mathcal A)
\quad\text{nor}\quad
\Omega_D^3(\mathcal A^\circ).
\]

If one replaces the separate left/opposite calculi by the maximal joint diagonal envelope, the seam enters the represented degree-three space—but the entire represented degree-three image is junk in the tested six-cycle, including \(E_{PN,NP}\).

Therefore the current verdict is:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{l}
\text{ordinary real spectral-triple calculus: seam is not a three-form;}\\
\text{maximal joint diagonal calculus: seam is junk;}\\
\text{undeclared mixed/superconnection calculus: indeterminate.}
\end{array}}
\]

The Möbius branch remains open only if the programme explicitly adopts and derives a mixed bimodule differential calculus whose quotient retains the alternating product.

## 1. The real-even six-sector witness

Order the Hilbert basis as

\[
(PN,PS,NS,NP,SP,SN).
\]

Let \(D\) be the adjacency matrix of the six-cycle, with equal nonzero edge weights for the minimal witness. Let

\[
\gamma=\operatorname{diag}(+,-,+,-,+,-).
\]

Define \(J\) by complex conjugation followed by

\[
PN\leftrightarrow NP,
\qquad
PS\leftrightarrow SP,
\qquad
NS\leftrightarrow SN.
\]

The certificate verifies the KO-6 label-level signs and \(JD=DJ\). Every edge joins opposite grading.

The left and opposite actions are

\[
L(a)e_{ij}=a_i e_{ij},
\qquad
R(b)e_{ij}=b_j e_{ij}.
\]

Every cycle edge preserves one coordinate:

| Edge | Preserved coordinate | First-order type |
|---|---|---|
| \(PN-PS\) | left \(P\) | right-changing |
| \(PS-NS\) | right \(S\) | left-changing |
| \(NS-NP\) | left \(N\) | right-changing |
| \(NP-SP\) | right \(P\) | left-changing |
| \(SP-SN\) | left \(S\) | right-changing |
| \(SN-PN\) | right \(N\) | left-changing |

Hence

\[
[[D,L(a)],R(b)]=0
\]

on every block. The construction is a genuine strict-first-order enlargement, not the forbidden direct reversal of the six-sector model.

## 2. Why the seam is not an ordinary three-form

A represented left one-form is generated by

\[
L(a)[D,L(b)].
\]

It sees precisely the edges on which the **left** character changes. A represented opposite one-form sees precisely the edges on which the **right** character changes.

Along the forward half-cycle the types alternate:

\[
PN\overset{R}{\longrightarrow}PS
\overset{L}{\longrightarrow}NS
\overset{R}{\longrightarrow}NP.
\]

Therefore the matrix unit \(E_{PN,NP}\) is generated by an alternating product

\[
\omega_R^{(1)}\omega_L^{(2)}\omega_R^{(3)}.
\]

The certificate constructs each directed matrix unit inside its correct represented one-form space and verifies their product is nonzero.

It also constructs the complete represented third-degree images of the left and opposite universal differential envelopes and verifies

\[
E_{PN,NP}\notin\pi_D(\Omega^3_{\rm univ}(\mathcal A)),
\]

\[
E_{PN,NP}\notin\pi_D(\Omega^3_{\rm univ}(\mathcal A^\circ)).
\]

This is a typing obstruction, not yet a junk calculation. In the ordinary calculus the candidate is not present to be retained or quotiented.

## 3. The maximal joint diagonal benchmark

One possible repair is to treat the joint left/right character as a single coordinate. On the six selected sectors, the represented image of

\[
\mathcal A\otimes\mathcal A^\circ
\]

separates all six basis states and is therefore the full diagonal algebra \(\mathbb C^6\).

For this maximal envelope, the certificate computes the universal represented spaces and the Connes junk ideal directly. It finds

\[
\operatorname{rank}\pi_D(\Omega^2)=18,
\]

\[
\operatorname{rank}\pi_D(\Omega^3)=18,
\]

and

\[
\operatorname{rank}\pi_D(d\ker\pi_2)=18.
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{\Omega_D^3(\mathbb C^6)=0}
\]

for this six-cycle witness. In particular,

\[
\boxed{[E_{PN,NP}]=0.}
\]

The executable result includes an explicit universal two-form \(\eta\) satisfying

\[
\pi_D(\eta)=0,
\qquad
\pi_D(d\eta)=E_{PN,NP}.
\]

So the seam is not merely absent by a rank count; it has a concrete junk preimage.

## 4. Why the joint benchmark is not automatically the physical answer

The original first-order geometry distinguishes \(L(\mathcal A)\) from \(R(\mathcal A)\). Its physical fluctuations are built from left one-forms together with their real partners. Replacing this structure by one diagonal algebra on joint sector labels changes the differential calculus.

In particular, the joint algebra treats the bimodule label \((i,j)\) as a new point. That is precisely the move the earlier two-slot and nested-commutant audits warned must not be made silently.

The maximal-envelope junk result is therefore a strong no-go for one natural completion, not a theorem about every possible mixed calculus.

## 5. The exact remaining fork

### Branch M1: ordinary Connes calculus

**Verdict:** closed for the seam.

The alternating product is not a class in either ordinary degree-three calculus.

### Branch M2: maximal joint diagonal calculus

**Verdict:** exact no-go.

The seam is represented but is explicitly junk; indeed the full represented degree-three image is junk.

### Branch M3: bimodule differential calculus

**Verdict:** indeterminate.

One may define a bigraded or two-sided calculus with components such as

\[
\Omega_R^1\otimes\Omega_L^1\otimes\Omega_R^1.
\]

Its differential, product, involution, junk ideal and gauge covariance must be specified. No such structure is fixed by the current corpus.

### Branch M4: Quillen-type superconnection

**Verdict:** live, conditional.

The alternating factors may occur as different graded pieces of a superconnection and contribute through its curvature or holonomy rather than as an ordinary Connes three-form. This requires an action and a proof that the resulting seam is not an arbitrary coupling.

### Branch M5: structural groupoid transport

**Verdict:** live but non-dynamical.

The six-cycle can remain a transport/groupoid statement without claiming a propagating scalar or differential form. A physical observable must then be constructed separately.

## 6. Consequence for the Möbius claim

The programme can now state more than “the represented calculus is missing.” It has an explicit decision tree:

\[
\boxed{
\text{one-seam real closure gives }C_6,
\quad
\text{but its half-turn is an alternating mixed product.}
}
\]

The standard single-algebra route does not contain it, and the maximal joint-algebra route kills it. Therefore any physical Möbius claim must name the nonstandard mixed calculus or superconnection that retains it.

This is close to a stop-everything criterion:

> If no independently motivated mixed differential calculus exists, the Möbius branch terminates as a structural groupoid analogy rather than a physical finite-geometry effect.
