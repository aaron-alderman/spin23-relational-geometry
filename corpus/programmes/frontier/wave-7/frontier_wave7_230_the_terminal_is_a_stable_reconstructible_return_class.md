## Answer

The corpus does not converge toward one final microscopic state. It converges
toward a rule for returning:

\[
\boxed{
\text{one carrier}
\longrightarrow
\text{two faces}
\longrightarrow
\text{three relations}
\longrightarrow
\text{the carrier again, with a residue}.
}
\]

The fourth move is not a fourth object. It is \(1'\): the original carrier
after one relational circuit. Usually \(C'\sim C\) under a declared gauge,
basis, label or detector quotient, but the traversal has left a phase,
orientation, holonomy, memory, entropy production, defect class or predictive
record. Repetition therefore draws a helix rather than a circle. Its projection
may look like a fixed point; the lifted process retains depth.

The best name supported by the corpus is:

\[
\boxed{\textbf{stable reconstructible return class}.}
\]

It is stable because transverse perturbations contract or remain inside a
certified invariant set. It is reconstructible because a declared detector
and decoder recover the same equivalence class and predict held-out records.
It is a return class because exact equality of microscopic states is neither
necessary nor generally meaningful.

## Why a fixed point is too small

The original reflexive-closure target

\[
 P=\Pi_{\rm dist}(\mathcal R[P])
\]

remains a valid special case. But the later corpus proves that a fixed point
cannot be the whole terminal.

AZ-057 constructs three mechanisms with the same fixed population \(0.8\),
the same contraction \(1/3\), and the same duration. One is equilibrium with
zero stationary entropy production; one is driven with hidden-cycle entropy
production \(1.7993904355\); one is monitored and carries a reset lower bound
\(0.6268694576\,k_BT\) per record. State-level convergence erases their
physical difference.

Similarly:

- the scalar Moonshine trace identifies \(23A\) and \(23B\);
- curl identifies \(\mathbf A\) and \(\mathbf A+\nabla\chi\) and misses flat
  Wilson periods;
- the distributed \(A_2\) metric identifies a relational plane but does not
  select its physical labels, orientation or basepoint;
- an unoriented triangle closes combinatorially but cannot decide clockwise
  from anticlockwise traversal.

Terminality must therefore include the path, detector quotient, provenance and
resource ledger—not merely the endpoint.

## One, two, three, return

### One: persistence

There must be one typed carrier on which every claimed face, operation and
record lives. Combining results from different emulators does not close the
loop.

### Two: distinction

Two faces are the minimum comparison: diagonal blocks, bound/free sectors,
inside/outside, two source directions, Jordan/Lie presentations or two
experimental alternatives. They are complementary views or sectors of one
carrier, not arbitrary objects placed beside each other.

### Three: consistency and detection

A third relation closes the first loop. It can expose a return defect that no
single pair sees. Operationally, the “third” often appears as an independent
detector or intervention: reverse traversal, coherent phase, singular values,
a marked boundary or a held-out channel.

Three does not automatically choose orientation. It supplies the first place
where orientation can be tested.

### Return: residue

After composition the carrier returns only up to the admitted equivalence:

\[
 \Phi^m(C)\sim C.
\]

The residue \(\Omega(\gamma)\) records what the endpoint forgot. If
\(\Omega=0\) and \(m=1\), the terminal is an ordinary fixed point. If \(m>1\),
it may be a limit cycle. With nontrivial fiber transport it is holonomy. Under
coarse-graining it is a universality class or renormalization fixed shape.
With long-lived history dependence it is a metastable attractor.

## “Spin deeper and deeper”

The recurring spin is the repeated application of the same grammar at
successive depths:

\[
 C_n
\to(F_n^+,F_n^-)
\to R_n^{(1)}R_n^{(2)}R_n^{(3)}
\to C_{n+1}.
\]

Microscopic detail may change with \(n\), while the induced response law,
quotient and residue converge. Jordan/Schur recursion supplies the local
algebraic version: eliminate an interior variable, update the effective
response, repeat. Renormalization supplies the scale version. Holonomy supplies
the path version. Detector refinement supplies the epistemic version.

What stabilizes is therefore not necessarily \(C_n\). It is the type shape of
admissible transport:

\[
 \text{persist}
\to
\text{differentiate}
\to
\text{relate}
\to
\text{return}
\to
\text{reconstruct}.
\]

## Where Spin(2,3), \(E_8\) and the Monster sit

None is the terminal by name alone.

- \(Spin_0(2,3)\cong Sp(4,\mathbb R)\) is the natural continuous closure
  envelope of the two-face positive response when sufficient independent
  generators are physically supplied. It describes the allowed motion of the
  relational carrier; it does not choose the carrier, ports or orientation.
- \(E_8\) is a maximal exceptional organization containing many compatible
  substructures. In the corpus it remains an atlas or zoo until one action and
  low-energy intertwiner derive the observed labels and couplings.
- The Monster and \(V^\natural\) provide an unusually complete, self-contained
  operator/defect structure and an exact physical CFT existence proof. They
  still do not make a laboratory carrier select itself, and finite traces do
  not reconstruct every orientation.

These structures are increasingly rich candidate realizations of the return
grammar. They are not substitutes for the return test.

## The terminal certificate

For a declared context, a candidate terminal is admitted only when:

1. **carrier closure:** every face, relation and detector acts on the same
   typed carrier;
2. **dynamical closure:** the essential split and relations arise from one
   action or independently calibrated controls;
3. **return closure:** \(\Phi^m(C)\sim C\) with a stable transverse response;
4. **observational closure:** the detector is injective on every distinction
   the claim intends to retain;
5. **reflexive closure:** reconstruction returns the same class and predicts
   held-out data;
6. **provenance closure:** labels, orientation and normalization are not fitted
   from the claimed output;
7. **ledger closure:** energetic, entropic, calibration and uncertainty costs
   accompany the state-level description;
8. **scale closure:** the statement survives every limit or coarse-graining
   direction it claims.

This is relative terminality, not the end of physics. New detectors or a wider
context can refine the quotient and reopen the class.

## Finite model

The certificate uses

\[
 F(s,r)=(s+1\bmod3,r/3),
\qquad s\in\mathbb Z_3.
\]

There is no one-step fixed point. Yet

\[
 F^3(s,r)=(s,r/27),
\]

so all trajectories approach the stable three-cycle
\(\{(0,0),(1,0),(2,0)\}\). A coarse detector retaining only \(r\) reports an
ordinary fixed point and erases the spin phase. A phase-sensitive detector
recovers the return orbit.

This is the smallest exact model of the user's intuition: spinning deeper can
converge without stopping.
