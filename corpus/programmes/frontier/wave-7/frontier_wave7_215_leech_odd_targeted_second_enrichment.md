# Frontier Wave 7.215: targeted second odd-sector enrichment

## Result

The first block-Lanczos layer left six of the required 24 odd Ritz directions
above the exact separator. This packet constructs their residuals in the full
cutoff-two product basis, retains the 50,000 configurations with greatest
aggregate six-direction weight, re-orthogonalizes that finite correction
against the complete 48-dimensional first-layer space, and solves the valid
54-dimensional Rayleigh--Ritz problem.

The correction is collective. The selected residuals occupy 2,002,387 product
configurations before truncation; after re-orthogonalization the six-vector
correction has support on 57,610 configurations. The calculation evaluates
15,058,434 charging transitions for the first residual layer and 60,990,469
for the targeted correction. Orthogonality to the old space is below
\(3.0\times10^{-16}\), and the correction Gram error is below
\(4.2\times10^{-14}\).

The number of Ritz values below
\(\beta_{25}^{(-)}=4.313466520526788\) rises from 18 to 20. The twentieth is
4.311288365976402, while the twenty-first is 4.364703049260699 and the
twenty-fourth is 4.567522699577261. The finite correction therefore makes
measurable progress but does not satisfy the 24-vector separator condition.

## Interpretation

This is a variational theorem about the declared finite subspace, not a gap
certificate. It rules out the idea that one small residual correction of the
six lagging first-layer Ritz vectors is sufficient. It also shows that the
odd dressing is not a negligible local perturbation: the useful correction is
spread through millions of product configurations generated by the joined
Leech charging metric.

The next proof step should not simply enlarge this truncation blindly. It
should either build a tensor-network representation of the second residual
block and its exterior residual, or count eigenvalues below a shift by an
independent inertia method. Only after 24 trial values lie below the separator
and a validated cluster residual/Lehmann solve is available can this lane
contribute a rigorous odd-sector lower bound.
