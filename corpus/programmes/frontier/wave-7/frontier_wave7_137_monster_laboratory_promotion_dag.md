## Result

The corpus now has one falsifiable route from a candidate material or quantum
simulator to the statement “this carrier realizes Monster physics.” It is not
a list of numerological resemblances. It is a dependency graph in which every
downstream measurement must remain attached to the same physical carrier:

\[
\begin{array}{c}
A\;\text{Leech edge}\to B\;\text{crossed inversion}\to
C\;\text{orbifold sectors}\to D\;m\text{-condensation}\to
E\;J\text{ character}\\
E\to\{F\;2A,\;G\;23AB\}\to H\;46\text{-root}\to
I\;\text{Griess/Jordan/Norton--Sakuma}.
\end{array}
\]

Changing carrier, phase, boundary termination or defect implementation resets
the downstream chain unless an experimentally verified isometry transports
the measured observables. Consequently, a surface-code twist, an XXZ
Tambara--Yamagami sector and a classical evaluation of \(J\) cannot be stacked
on paper and called a Monster realization.

## The gates

1. **A — Leech edge.** Realize the even unimodular rank-24 edge, with
   \(c_-=24\), trivial bulk anyon group and primitive null-vector gapping.
2. **B — enrichment.** Show that the actual branch cut acts
   \(V_\ell\mapsto V_{-\ell}\). The preregistered clock-process surrogate uses
   \(W\simeq0\), while the factorized control must remain \(W\simeq1\).
3. **C — parent.** Recover the four \(V_\Lambda^+\) sectors, their
   \(\mathbb Z_2^2\) fusion, spins and toric-code modular matrix using that same
   flux.
4. **D — condensation.** Condense the twisted boson \(m\), not the Leech boson
   \(e\), and observe the current ladder \(24\to0\) without changing
   \(c_-=24\).
5. **E — character.** Resolve the zero weight-one term, the first Moonshine
   degeneracies, and the weight-two split
   \(196884=98580+98304\) across sizes and aspect ratios.
6. **F/G — independent marked defects.** Measure the \(2A\) interface and the
   marked \(23AB\) Fourier recoupling law. Generic Ising or
   \(\mathrm{TY}(\mathbb Z_{23})\) behavior is explicitly insufficient. The
   \(2A\) preflight now requires the three Ising weights and the paired
   \(2A\cdot2A=2B\) product from Wave 7.156 on the same sector grading.
7. **H — common root.** Construct one order-46 process whose powers return the
   already tested order-23 and order-2 operations. At \(y=1\), its connected
   ratios have frozen magnitude targets \(0.00373794542221053\) and
   \(0.0784643601781287\); \(y=0.8,1.2\) are held out.
8. **I — operator closure.** Reconstruct a selected Jordan corner from six
   two-point and ten three-point coefficients, test the Jordan identity on
   held-out data, and predict untrained Norton--Sakuma pair types. The
   cross-sector preflight now adds eight blind \(-7/8\) minimal-vector
   transitions and the anomaly-sensitive \(1/8\) orthogonal oscillator
   response before attempting those larger closures.

## What each stopping point means

Passing A establishes a laboratory Leech-edge candidate. Passing through E
establishes a Moonshine-character candidate, not yet the Monster operator
algebra. Only passing A–I on one carrier warrants “laboratory Monster
realization,” and even then only at the measured CFT, defect and operator
scope. Failure is informative: B rejects a factorized topological impostor; D
separates the Leech and Moonshine extensions; F/G reject generic defect
categories; H rejects independently assembled layers; I rejects scalar-output
emulation.

## Present frontier

The theoretical physics claim is no longer open: the Leech inversion orbifold
and heterotic constructions realize the Moonshine theory at their declared
scopes. The laboratory claim remains open at A. Wave 7.131 supplies a local
continuum coupled-wire architecture, not a microscopic material Hamiltonian.
Wave 7.138 fixes its full 24-channel integer interaction matrix, but its
12–22-field vertex terms still require a microscopic compilation. Wave 7.139
shows that an exact integral basis change diagonalizes those terms, trading
vertex order for a dense channel-mixing matrix rather than leaving a formal
many-body obstruction. Wave 7.157 implements that mixer exactly on 24
853-level registers using 555 modular linear gates per chiral block. This
closes the coefficient-faithful digital instruction set. Wave 7.158 shows
that 853 nevertheless aliases a marked unit-cube input \(2579\mapsto20\);
the first worst-case wrap-free prime is 5167, and the sufficient cutoff ladder
is now explicit. Wave 7.159 then proves that a fixed-strength direct clock
penalty loses its locking gap as \(p^{-2}\), while fixed-gap rescaling needs
control range up to \(5.52\times10^9\) by \(N=64\). The naïve clock route is
therefore rejected in favor of a rotor-native or multiscale design.
Wave 7.160 supplies the first rotor-native mode: its fixed-strength gap
converges to 0.8784249807 and reaches \(1.21\times10^{-7}\) relative accuracy
by cutoff \(N=4\). Wave 7.161 joins these modes into ideal strips through 32
wires without gap collapse and leaves exactly 24 modes per open edge, while
periodic closure removes them. Local locking and ideal finite-strip mode
counting are therefore viable; thermodynamic local-perturbation stability,
spatial hardware locality and the infrared Leech edge remain unproved.
Wave 7.162 freezes the missing edge measurements: spatially resolved
chirality, \(1/L\) onsets, Casimir ratio, thermal Hall slope, exactly 24
weight-one currents, no norm-two roots and the periodic-closure control.
Wave 7.163 adds a sourced thermodynamic statement at fixed cutoff: some
nonzero bounded local-perturbation interval preserves the periodic bulk gap
uniformly in volume. Its numerical radius and uniformity as \(N\to\infty\)
remain a 72-setting experimental scan, and the theorem does not cover the
open chiral edge.
Wave 7.164 fixes one quantitative stress axis inside that scan: projected
nearest-neighbor excitation hopping closes the bulk band exactly at
\(|t|/\Delta=1/2\), with three stronger-coupling points held out from the
low-strength dispersion fit.
Wave 7.165 then audits the physical projection: a natural static
\(\sin\theta\sin\theta\) coupler is leakage-limited to
\(t_{\rm eff}/\Delta\approx0.1956\) under the declared one-percent
first-order proxy. Stronger held-out points require engineered selective

Wave 7.166 closes the logical selective-exchange primitive at finite cutoff:
an eigenbasis rotation followed by a two-label Givens gate has zero ideal
leakage. Its platform decomposition, hardware fidelity and overlapping-bond

one-excitation sector: short-time first-order runs need 9–21 steps, whereas
the strongest long-time 744-cell point exceeds 1.6 million logical gates and

Wave 7.168 closes the named matchgate branch for the ideal selected-subspace
chain: an open-boundary sine transform, diagonal mode phases and its inverse
give zero Trotter error with a \(M^2+144M\) logical upper bound. At 744 cells
the 660672-gate count beats first-order brickwork from 746 steps onward. This
is an exact free-fermion null model for dynamic calibration, not progress
beyond Gate A and not a Monster discriminator.
Wave 7.169 then audits the most literal superconducting implementation and
finds a three-channel sign-frustration certificate: no local polarity choice
can make the required (C\propto K) into an ordinary positive-capacitor node
matrix. Gate A therefore requires mediated signed mixing, the original

24-node capacitive schematic is excluded.
Wave 7.170 supplies the ideal passive recovery: an exact rational
(K=LDL^T) factorization uses 24 positive capacitors behind a signed linear

has magnitude at most (1/2), but 242 of 276 possible mixing entries are
nonzero. The next Gate-A object is therefore a measured 24-port admittance
matrix, followed by a parasitic and finite-bandwidth stability analysis.
Wave 7.171 performs the first such spectral audit in the recorded basis:
(\kappa_2(K)=31816.43), so a uniform one-percent worst-case inverse guarantee
has the sufficient threshold (\delta\leq3.11\times10^{-7}). This adversarial

Wave 7.172 executes the first frozen co-ordinate search. Among 3000 integral
candidates, a certified determinant-(-1) basis reduces the condition number

Wave 7.173 freezes that Pareto choice as four sub-1000-condition candidates.
The selection rule now consumes measured admittance covariance, mixer loss,

deterministic coordinate-selection procedure.
Wave 7.142 then uses one shared dynamical sign per wire bond to gauge the
diagonal inversion of all 24 channels and verifies all 1152 mutual null

Waves 7.133–136 supply executable commissioning operations and discriminators,

not move the single-carrier frontier past A.

## Claim boundary

Completing the DAG would prove that a particular laboratory carrier realizes
the tested Moonshine-CFT structure. It would not show that the Monster is a
four-dimensional spacetime symmetry, that it is uniquely fundamental, or
that nature selects it outside the engineered system. Those are separate
P4 questions.
