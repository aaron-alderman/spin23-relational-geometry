## What each stopping point means

Passing A establishes a laboratory Leech-edge candidate. Passing through E
establishes a Moonshine-character candidate, not yet the Monster operator
algebra. Only passing A–I on one carrier warrants “laboratory Monster
realization,” and even then only at the measured CFT, defect and operator
scope. Failure is informative: B rejects a factorized topological impostor; D
separates the Leech and Moonshine extensions; F/G reject generic defect
categories; H rejects independently assembled layers; I rejects scalar-output
