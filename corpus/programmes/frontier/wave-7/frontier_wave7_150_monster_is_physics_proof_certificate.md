   V^\natural=V_\Lambda^+\oplus(V_\Lambda^T)^+.

   The character certificate recovers \(J\), removes all weight-one currents

3. \(V^\natural\) is a unitary holomorphic \(c=24\) chiral conformal field

   Therefore it has unitary time evolution, a finite-size spectrum, thermal
