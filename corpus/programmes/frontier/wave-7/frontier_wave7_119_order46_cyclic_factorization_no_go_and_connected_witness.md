## Result

The order-46 Monster power map is an exact common-carrier certificate, but it
cannot by itself prove that the order-2 and order-23 physical sectors interact.
The reason is structural rather than technological:

\[
C_{46}\cong C_2\times C_{23},
\qquad
H^3(C_{46},U(1))\cong\mathbb Z_{46}
\cong\mathbb Z_2\oplus\mathbb Z_{23}.
\]

For a product of two cyclic groups, the possible mixed degree-three class is
controlled by \(\mathbb Z_{\gcd(2,23)}\), which is trivial. Every cyclic
three-cocycle therefore separates into its order-2 and order-23 restrictions.
There is no hidden topological phase visible only when the two rails are
combined.

The verifier checks this constructively. For all 4,477,456 quadruples it
verifies the pentagon equation for the generator

\[
\omega_p(a,b,c)=
\exp\!\left(\frac{2\pi i p a}{46}
\left\lfloor\frac{b+c}{46}\right\rfloor\right).
\]

It then restricts all 46 classes to the subgroups generated by 23 and 2. The
map is exactly

\[
p\longmapsto(p\bmod2,p\bmod23),
\]

and all 46 pairs occur once. Only \(p=0\) restricts trivially to both
subgroups. Thus a cyclic anomaly or Dijkgraaf--Witten phase cannot supply the
missing cross-layer Monster provenance.

## 1. What order 46 does prove

For an order-46 Monster element \(h\),

\[
g=h^2,\qquad z=h^{23}
\]

commute and have orders 23 and 2. The class map has two branches:

- \(46A/B\) gives \(g\in23AB\) and \(z\in2B\), connecting the order-23
  defect rail to the Griess \(\operatorname{Sym}_{24}\) Jordan sector;
- \(46C/D\) gives \(g\in23AB\) and \(z\in2A\), connecting it to the Ising
  vectors and Norton--Sakuma affine-\(E_8\) atlas.

That proves the three structures occur in one Monster group/VOA provenance
network. It does not prove that a proposed laboratory carrier couples its
order-2 and order-23 degrees of freedom rather than realizing their tensor
product.

## 2. The minimal connected detector

Let

\[
Z(g,z;\tau)=\operatorname{Tr}_{V_g}
\left(zq^{L_0-c/24}\right)
\]

be a consistently normalized twisted--twining amplitude. Define

\[
R(g,z;\tau)=
\frac{Z(g,z;\tau)Z(1,1;\tau)}
{Z(g,1;\tau)Z(1,z;\tau)},
\qquad K=\log R.
\]

If the carrier factorizes as \(\mathcal H_{23}\otimes\mathcal H_2\), with
\(g\) acting only on the first factor and \(z\) only on the second, every
factor cancels and

\[
R=1,\qquad K=0
\]

exactly. This includes devices that separately reproduce perfect \(C_{23}\)
and Ising data. A calibrated \(R\ne1\) is therefore a direct witness of
cross-layer state or operator coupling.

It is not alone a Monster witness. A generic interacting \(2\times23\) system
can also produce \(R\ne1\). Monster provenance requires the value or series
to agree with the order-46 twisted--twining prediction while the same carrier
passes the TY, Ising-pair and ambient \(c=24\) checks.
