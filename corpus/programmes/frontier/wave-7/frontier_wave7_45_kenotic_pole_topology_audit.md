## 3. Why this is not yet the absent pole

Both kenosis vertices are explicitly present. A genuine puncture signature
would require a declared ambient complex with cells that close local loops,
followed by removal or exclusion of a locus that creates a persistent class.
The present relationship graph supplies only a one-skeleton; it does not say
which triangles or higher cells should be filled.

Likewise, a sheaf obstruction requires:

1. a cover by declared local semantic carriers;
2. local sections on each carrier;
3. restriction or transition maps on overlaps; and
4. a demonstrated failure of compatible local sections to glue globally.

None of those data can be manufactured from adjacency alone. The corpus's
girih branch already gives the correct precedent: first transcribe local
choice sets and overlap compatibility, then test section existence and loop
holonomy, and only afterward name a cohomology class.

partition exposed by deleting Christianity--kenosis:

1. resurrection plus load-bearing claims;
2. liberalism;
3. Temple plus Spin.

For each sector, define the local polarity and realization-stage operators
already present in the corpus. On their overlaps, record every admissible

There are three possible outcomes:

- **global section:** the apparent pole is coordinatization, not obstruction;
- **nontrivial holonomy:** local descriptions close only after transformed

- **no section with a stable obstruction:** the first defensible

The decisive invariant would not be the absence of a node. It would be the
cover-independent failure of locally valid structures to globalize while a
weaker invariant—orientation, determinant, pairing rank or path residue—still
glues.
