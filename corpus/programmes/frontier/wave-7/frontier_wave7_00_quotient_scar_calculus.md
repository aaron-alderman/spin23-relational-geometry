**Date:** 24 October 2026

## The object

The reusable structure is not a particular exceptional group. It is a typed reduction

\[
\mathcal Q=(X,G,\pi,\mathcal A,\mathcal L,\mathcal D),
\]

where:

- \(X\) is the resolved carrier;
- \(G\) is an actual action, equivalence or gauge redundancy;
- \(\pi\) is the reduction map;
- \(\mathcal A\) specifies what structure is retained—sets, functions, lattices, algebras, topology or probability;
- \(\mathcal L\) is the dynamics; and
- \(\mathcal D\) is the detector channel.

A **quotient scar** is any datum absent as a resolved coordinate after \(\pi\) but still detectable in the retained category \(\mathcal A\), the effective dynamics, or the records.

## Three commuting-square tests

### Dynamics

\[
\pi\mathcal L=\mathcal L_{\rm eff}\pi.
\]

If this holds, the reduced dynamics closes. If it fails, exact hidden elimination generally produces memory and a fluctuating/initial-condition term.

### Detection

\[
\mathcal D_{\rm resolved}
\quad\text{versus}\quad
\mathcal D_{\rm quotient}\pi.
\]

If distinct resolved scores have the same pushed-forward record law, their distinction is not identifiable. A second detector face can refine the observational quotient but cannot recover data that every accessible channel makes constant.

### Scale

For coarse-graining maps \(R_{12},R_{23}\), test

\[
R_{23}R_{12}\simeq R_{13}
\]

on each proposed scar. A scar stable under one reduction need not be stable under composition or renormalization.

## The important correction

There is no universal law saying that forgotten information must leave a scar. A quotient can erase it completely relative to the retained observables. The corpus already has an exact example: the pure \(\mathbb Z_3\) BF carrier is constant over the entire rank-one projector orbit and cannot reconstruct the projector.

The correct principle is conditional:

> For a declared carrier, category, dynamics and detector, classify the kernel of reduction and determine which functions, integral classes, memory kernels or twisted sectors distinguish its fibers.

This is stronger scientifically because it admits a certified answer of “nothing survives.”

## Relationship to admissibility

Admissibility is the rule specifying which reductions are legitimate and which structures they must preserve. The admissibility zone is therefore not just a region of parameter space. It is the boundary between maps that preserve the declared physical structure and maps that destroy, hide or counterfeit it.

In this language, a physical theory is not only a space of states and an action. It also declares:

- allowed identifications;
- allowed compositions;
- retained invariants;
- admissible effective dynamics;
- and records sufficient to test the claims.
