# Frontier Wave 7.252: Intercell coupling splits collective and relative E8 modes

**Date:** 26 October 2026

**Status:** explicit projected two-cell Hamiltonian promoted

Projecting two resolved compact cells onto four local states each turns the
phase-lock term \(-J\sum_i\cos(\theta_{A,i}-\theta_{B,i})\) into a signed
16-dimensional Hamiltonian. For \(0<J\le0.1\), the first product doublet
splits into a lower swap-even collective branch and an upper swap-odd
relative-cell branch while the projected gap remains open and cutoff-stable.

At \(J=0.1\), the cutoff-10 gap is 0.1822513372 and the branch splitting is
0.0051948553. This establishes dynamics across a cell boundary, not a bulk
phase. Larger retained local manifolds and widths are now the decisive gates.
