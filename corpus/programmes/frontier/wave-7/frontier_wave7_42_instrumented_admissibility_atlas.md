Its sixteen node-level classes include exact lattice and representation
calculations, Markov and qutrit simulators, process tomography, programmable

This is deliberate. A mathematical carrier is tested by exact computation; an
effective dynamical carrier by an emulator or material platform; a claim of
fundamental provenance by a held-out prediction after every conventional
interaction has been calibrated. Calling all three “experiments” does not make
their authority equal.

## How to read an entry

An entry is a decision surface, not a shopping list:

1. **Input and output** state the proposed passage.
2. **Invariant and information loss** say what must survive and what the passage
   is allowed to forget.
3. **Platform, protocol and detector** make the passage observable.
4. **Nuisance model** states the nearest conventional explanation.
5. **Decision rule and falsifier** prevent an interesting trace from becoming
   an automatic success.
6. **Reopen datum** says what new fact would justify another attempt after a
   guardrail or null result.

The target entry supplies the concrete detector requirements and scars; the
relation supplies the hypothesis being tested. Fundamental returns additionally
list every edge in their QR-ordered cycle.

\[

The route can yield theoretical engineering improvements even if every signal
is conventional: better tomography, explicit loss accounting, calibrated
memory discrimination and no-refit response prediction. New physics becomes a
live interpretation only at the final boundary, when the registered nuisance
model fails while the predicted reversal, frequency, geometry and detector
relations survive.

not to invent more entries; it is to choose a small platform family, lower its
records into Claimipedia and Sourcery, and execute the preregistered decision
