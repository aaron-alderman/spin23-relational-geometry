# Frontier Wave 7.253: The E8 dimer gap converges before its dispersion

**Date:** 26 October 2026

**Status:** root-count gap promoted; splitting coefficient withheld

Enlarging each cell from four to six to eight retained states leaves the
projected dimer gap stable below 0.15% at \(J=0.05\) and 0.1. The existence
and swap ordering of collective and relative-cell modes therefore survive the
first projection audit.

Their numerical splitting does not yet pass: the final six-to-eight-root
changes are 1.16% and 2.15%. Gap stability arrives before quantitative
dispersion. Longer chains may use the stable gap and symmetry labels, but not
yet advertise a converged velocity or bandwidth.
