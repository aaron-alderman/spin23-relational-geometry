## The result in ordinary language

The programme now has a definite answer to “what is the shape we have actually
built?” The science map contains twelve constructive moves, fifteen critical
boundaries and twelve independent returns. Where constructive and critical
flows can compensate one another, their integral interface is exactly the
eight-dimensional `A8` transfer lattice on nine jointly reachable science
states. That is a theorem about the present typed map. It is not a claim that
matter, spacetime or nature is made from `A8`.

This closes the finite cartographic task. Every named move and return has an
input, output, preserved distinction, allowed loss, platform, detector,
nuisance model, decision rule, falsifier and reopening datum. Sourcery can carry
an admitted route without forgetting its assumptions, losses or evidence, and
must retain a refused step as a refusal. Claimipedia remains the authored claim
and challenge carrier; it does not become the physical state space merely
because some of its relation operators also have recognizable algebra.

## Closed, open and why

### Closed

- **The names and counts are closed.** `K12`, `G15`, `F12`, the Mixed Core Nine
  and the Mixed Interface have fixed definitions and executable certificates.
- **The mixed shape is closed.** Its lattice is `A8`, with rank eight,
  determinant nine and 72 shortest roots.
- **The route discipline is closed.** Ordered steps and the
  assumption–loss–evidence ledger compose by declared laws; a failed type gate
  cannot be laundered into a path.
- **The instrumentation schema is closed.** All 39 objects have the same
  16-field decision contract.
- **The pinned Claimipedia operator search is closed.** Its semisimple content is
  exactly `sl2 + sl3`, not `sp4`; reopening requires a new evidence-bound
  cross-operator rather than another path through the existing graph.
- **Two end-to-end controls are closed.** F05 closes inside its declared
  effective model; F01 refuses an oriented quarter-turn for an exact reason.
- **The refinement question is closed at this level.** Bookkeeping refinements
  preserve `A8`; a genuinely new joint bridge changes it predictably to `A9`.

### Open

- **One physical carrier is open.** The registered relations presently span
  exact calculations, simulators and proposed devices. They have not all been
  derived from one microscopic action.
- **Orientation is open where it should be.** A projector chooses an axis, not
  a direction around it. F01 needs a separately calibrated phase, loop
  direction, polarization or wall orientation.
- **Scale is open.** A finite relation can be erased by bandwidth, noise,
  coarse-graining, thermodynamic limits or detector reduction.
- **Execution is open.** A complete experimental contract is not apparatus,
  data or independent replication.
- **Ontology is open.** The exact formal shape does not establish that
  admissibility generates all physical law, nor does it identify an exceptional
  or lattice-theoretic microscopic parent.

The difference matters. “Closed” means no unnamed step remains in the finite
argument. “Open” means a new physical datum, calculation or record is required;
more rearrangement of the same formal objects cannot supply it.

\[

\longrightarrow\text{wall}

\longrightarrow\text{return slope conditioned on memory}.
\]

In the declared model, integrating the positive fluctuation gives

\[
\Gamma(\theta)=\log(m^2+g\sin^2\theta),
\qquad \Gamma''(0)=2g/m^2>0,

and the chosen gradient update contracts when
`0 < alpha g / m^2 < 1`. The experiment is not “look for A8.” It is: prepare

effective operator. Only beyond that gate does “new physics” become an honest
reading.

## How to picture the frontier

Picture nine stations joined by transfers whose total inflow equals total
outflow. Eight independent differences remain because a common shift changes
nothing. Constructive routes say how a transfer might be made; guardrails say
what additional information or separation the transfer must respect. Their
overlap is not a place where every move is allowed. It is the balance sheet on
which an allowed move and its cost can be recorded together.

This explains both the shape and the limit. Adding another description of an
existing road does not enlarge the frontier. Opening a genuinely new road to a
previously isolated station does. The frontier is therefore a testable property
of typed reachability, not a sacred diagram.

## Implications

The strongest implication is methodological. A speculative programme can now
end a route in three different, useful ways:

1. an exact passage with a bounded claim and observable;
2. an exact refusal that identifies the missing datum; or
3. an experimental decision surface on which conventional improvement and new
   physics share the same apparatus up to a predeclared escalation gate.

That is the practical content of the Jordan–Lie middle. Jordan admission asks
whether the states and responses can coexist and compose. Lie transport asks
how admitted possibilities can be moved while retaining their relations. The
mixed interface records where those two jobs can be performed together without
hiding the information paid along the way. Dynamics and detectors decide
whether a formal transfer becomes a physical history and a retained record.
