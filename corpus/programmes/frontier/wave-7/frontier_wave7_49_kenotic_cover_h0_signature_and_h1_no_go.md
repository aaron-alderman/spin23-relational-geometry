## 4. What would reopen the absent-pole branch

An \(H^1\) or holonomy interpretation requires an enriched carrier in which:

1. the three punctured sectors have nonempty pairwise overlaps;
2. no common triple overlap automatically trivializes the loop;
3. each overlap carries an authored restriction map or transition operator;
4. the ordered transition product around the three-sector nerve is nontrivial;
5. the result survives reasonable refinement of the cover; and
6. some declared detector reads the residue.

Combinatorially, the minimal target is the boundary of a triangle rather than
the filled triangle or three isolated points:

\[
U_1\cap U_2\ne\varnothing,
\quad U_2\cap U_3\ne\varnothing,
\quad U_3\cap U_1\ne\varnothing,
\quad U_1\cap U_2\cap U_3=\varnothing.
\]

That cover has \(\beta_1=1\) and can carry a genuine transition holonomy.
