## Result

The sentence **“Monster is physics”** has four inequivalent meanings.  The
first two no longer need to be argued from numerical resemblance:

| level | proposition | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | the Moonshine VOA is an internally consistent chiral quantum field theory | established |
| P2 | Monster data occur as states, indices, dualities and broken gauge symmetries in a constructed physical theory | established within heterotic string theory |
| P3 | a controllable laboratory carrier realizes a provenance-sensitive Monster subtheory | open |
| P4 | Monster structure is selected by the fundamental physics of our universe | open |

Paquette, Persson and Volpato give the decisive missing literature bridge for
P2.  In their heterotic models, the McKay--Thompson series are supersymmetric
indices counting spacetime BPS states, their modular groups arise as spacetime
T-duality groups, and the Monstrous Lie algebras act as algebras of
spontaneously broken gauge symmetries.  This is a physical interpretation of
Moonshine data inside a specified theory, not a post-hoc match of integer
sequences.

Their sharpened index relation is

\[
Z_{g,1}(T,U)=
\bigl(T_{1,g}(T)-T_{g,1}(U)\bigr)^{24}.
\]

Here \(T,U\) are compactification moduli, while the root labels become string
winding and momentum quantum numbers.  The exchange of the two root rails is
therefore realized as a T-duality/Weyl operation in this model, rather than
being inferred from the bare matrix shape of \(II_{1,1}\).

It does **not** prove P3 or P4.  A consistent string compactification is not a
measurement of nature, and an emulator of a finite permutation is not a
Monster realization.  The corpus should therefore stop asking whether the
Monster can occur in physics and start asking for the smallest
**provenance-sensitive physical witness** that distinguishes a Monster
realization from a generic device programmed to reproduce the same scalar
trace.

The scope also has a known firewall: the compactified construction is
effectively \(0+1\)-dimensional and is treated at zero string coupling to avoid
the gravitational-anomaly subtleties identified by the authors.  It is a
controlled theoretical realization, not yet a complete finite-coupling
cosmology.

## 1. The realization functor

Let \(\mathcal C_{\mathbb M}\) denote a declared finite presentation of the
Moonshine process theory: selected sectors, defect lines, junctions,
intertwiners and sewing operations.  Let \(\mathcal P\) be the process
category of a proposed carrier: preparable subspaces as objects and physical
operations as morphisms.  A physical realization is a monoidal map

\[
\mathfrak R:\mathcal C_{\mathbb M}\longrightarrow\mathcal P
\]

with five obligations:

1. **preparation:** every tested source object maps to a reproducible state or
   sector;
2. **composition:** fusion, junction and sewing compositions are preserved;
3. **dynamics:** the image operations have a Hamiltonian, channel or path
   integral, not only a lookup table;
4. **detection:** declared traces and phases map to calibrated measurements;
5. **provenance:** \(\mathfrak R\) is faithful on a witness set that separates
   the Monster construction from its rivals.

The fifth condition is the firewall.  A device can reproduce
\(T_{23A}=T_{23B}\) while erasing the very orientation the corpus seeks.  Such
a device emulates an output; it does not establish Monster provenance.

## 3. A witness stronger than the partition function

The scalar genus-one partition function is a checksum, not an identifying
measurement.  The corpus has already proved the order-23 version of this
problem: \(23A\) and \(23B\) have the same scalar McKay--Thompson series.
More generally, low-genus untwined amplitudes can agree for distinct
constructions.

The minimum credible witness should combine independent structural layers:

\[
W_{\mathbb M}=
\left(
c=24,
V_1=0,
\text{frame/Griess data},
2A\text{ fermionization},
23AB\text{ defect},
46\text{ junction},
\text{sewing}
\right).
\]

The qualifier “frame/Griess data” matters.  The unrestricted FLM uniqueness
conjecture is not available as a theorem.  What is proved is a framed
characterization: a holomorphic framed rank-24 VOA with \(V_1=0\) is
\(V^\natural\).  Other partial uniqueness results use the Griess algebra.
Consequently, observing \(c=24\) and no weight-one current is suggestive but
not by itself a proof of Monster provenance.

The corpus can use a finite operational substitute for the whole VOA:

1. certify the \(2A\) Ising/Majorana split
   \(\tfrac12+\tfrac{47}{2}=24\);
2. reconstruct the invertible sixteen-component Arf/Fourier transform, with
   six odd-spin zeros and ten even-spin components;
3. retain the \(23A/23B\) orientation in a defect-labelled or cross-grade
   observable;
4. verify the order-46 junction connecting the \(2A\) and \(23AB\) rails;
5. check one nontrivial sewing/coherence relation not fixed by scalar
   characters.

Passing this finite suite would realize a provenance-sensitive Monster
**subtheory**.  It would not yet reconstruct all \(196883\) nontrivial
weight-two directions or prove a fundamental role for the complete Monster.

## 4. Ranked physical routes

### Route A: heterotic BPS laboratory in theory

This is the strongest existing physics route.  Its next useful calculation is
to align the corpus's order-23/order-46 defect junction with the BPS charge
lattice and identify which indexed amplitude retains the A/B lift.  Success
would join the new defect results to a carrier that already has a physical
action and observable.

### Route B: a chiral \(c=24\) boundary

A holomorphic \(c=24\) theory has the correct gravitational anomaly to serve
as a chiral boundary theory.  The Leech theory provides the pre-orbifold
carrier and the Moonshine module is its \(\mathbb Z_2\) orbifold extension.
The missing result is a local, stable microscopic Hamiltonian or circuit whose
edge scaling limit is demonstrably \(V^\natural\), together with access to
defect-labelled correlations.  Thermal Hall response can certify \(c\), but
cannot by itself distinguish Monster from another \(c=24\) edge.

### Route C: a quantum simulator

Quantum algorithms can approximate CFT dynamics, and a finite simulator can
implement the selected defect/fusion presentation.  This is the nearest route
to P3, but it must derive its gates from one Hamiltonian family and pass blind
tomography.  Programming the expected matrices directly proves only
emulatability.

### Route D: gravity

Monster CFT symmetric products and proposed AdS\(_3\) duals provide useful
spectral and factorization benchmarks.  They remain conditional until a
complete bulk theory, dictionary and independent observable are supplied.
They do not automatically imply an AdS\(_4\) or real \(Spin_0(2,3)\) lift.

## 5. The first falsifiable experiment

The first experiment should test the inference architecture rather than claim
that a tabletop device contains the entire Monster.

1. Construct a controlled carrier with the Ising types
   \(1,\psi,\sigma\) and an independently addressable order-23 clock sector.
2. Implement two candidate interface families: a factorized
   Ising\(\times C_{23}\) model and a non-factorized order-46 junction.
3. Conceal the implemented family from the inference code.
4. Provide only noisy preparation, composition and interference records.
5. Require the code to infer whether the junction is factorized, select an
   orientation-sensitive closed history, and predict its phase before the
   held-out run.
6. Reject the Monster-provenance claim if scalar traces alone perform equally
   well or if the predicted phase is removable by a basis gauge.

This does not prove P4.  It validates the method needed to recognize a Monster
subtheory without being told its labels.

## 6. Promotion and falsification gates

P3 is earned only if all of the following pass:

- one source-derived carrier and action;
- preparation of the declared sectors;
- composition tomography for at least one fusion or defect junction;
- an orientation-sensitive complex amplitude;
- a held-out quantitative prediction;
- robustness under allowed perturbations;
- rejection of smaller rival type systems;
- a public data and analysis record fixed before unblinding.

P4 additionally requires a naturally occurring carrier and evidence that the
Monster structure is selected rather than engineered.  A failure is useful:
if every accessible response factors through a smaller quotient, then the
Monster is not experimentally identifiable on that carrier even if it acts in
the ultraviolet description.
