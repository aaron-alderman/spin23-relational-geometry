# Frontier Wave 7.263: The gap-proof route is perturbative, not Knabe

**Date:** 26 October 2026

**Status:** theorem applicability audited; certificate open

The projected E8 chain has not been shown frustration free, so Knabe-type finite-size thresholds do not presently apply. Exact two-site norms instead place the model in the structural setting of a weak local perturbation of a gapped product system: the centered bond norm is 0.129 of the local gap, vacuum-to-two mixing is 0.0264, and one-to-two mixing is 0.0801 of the bare separation.

These numbers make a Yarotsky-style or blocked linked-cluster proof plausible, not complete. The next gate is an explicit line-by-line theorem-hypothesis certificate with rigorous norms; failure of its constants must remain a recorded negative result.
