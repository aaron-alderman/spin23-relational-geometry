## Result

The relation corpus supplies a source-native five-dimensional quotient with a quadratic form of signature \((2,3)\), together with a canonical triangle cubic. The quadratic form is nondegenerate. The cubic has a one-dimensional radical and destroys every nonzero infinitesimal symmetry of the quadratic form.

This is the strongest same-carrier algebraic structure presently derived from the 24 relations without importing a Leech basis, Golay coordinate action or exceptional-Jordan multiplication.

\[
\boxed{

The quotient is canonical relative to the declared relation taxonomy. It is not a quotient by graph topology and does not identify distinct statements physically; it retains only their relation modality.

## 3. The canonical triangle cubic

The 24-edge backbone contains eleven undirected triangles. Define

\[
N_\triangle(x)=\sum_{\{e,f,g\}\in\Delta}x_ex_fx_g.
\]

This is invariant under graph relabelling and requires no cycle basis. Its polarized middle action on the full backbone has rank 17. Hence

\[
\boxed{
B_{24}=B_{17}^{\triangle}\oplus R_7^{\triangle}
}
\]

at the level of dimension, where the seven-dimensional radical is spanned by

\[
\mathrm{QR\!-!001},\mathrm{QR\!-!003},\mathrm{QR\!-!005},
\mathrm{QR\!-!007},\mathrm{QR\!-!010},\mathrm{QR\!-!020},
\mathrm{QR\!-!026}.
\]

All seven `extends_to` edges participate in at least one triangle. The radical instead contains isolated instantiation, enrichment, separation and both rules-out relations.

The tensor-contraction Gram \(\Phi_\triangle^*\Phi_\triangle\) is diagonal in the edge basis with entry twice the number of triangles containing that edge. It is positive semidefinite but not nonlocal and not nondegenerate.

## 5. The Hodge pairing recovers the silent directions but leaves a scale

Let \(B\) now denote the backbone incidence matrix and \(L=BB^T\) its graph Laplacian. The canonical nonlocal Hodge projectors are

\[
P_{\rm cut}=B^TL^+B,
\qquad
P_{\rm cyc}=I-P_{\rm cut}.
\]

They have ranks 14 and 10. The large backbone component has 10,881 spanning trees, and the exact projector denominators divide

\[
10881=3^3\cdot13\cdot31.
\]

The most general positive metric that weights only the canonical Hodge sectors is

\[
G_{a,b}=aP_{\rm cut}+bP_{\rm cyc},
\qquad a,b>0.
\]

It is nonlocal and nondegenerate, with

\[
\det G_{a,b}=a^{14}b^{10}.
\]

But the graph supplies no value of \(a/b\). The symmetric choice \(a=b\) gives \(G_{a,a}=aI\), collapsing back to the local metric. Thus Hodge theory fills the triangle radical only by reopening the familiar relative-scale ambiguity.

## 6. The cubic is not compatible with continuous \(Spin(2,3)\)

Let \(\mathfrak{so}(\eta)\cong\mathfrak{so}(2,3)\) act on \(W_{\rm type}\). A generator \(A\) preserves the cubic tensor \(T\) only if

\[
T(Au,v,w)+T(u,Av,w)+T(u,v,Aw)=0
\]

for all \(u,v,w\).

The ten-dimensional \(\mathfrak{so}(2,3)\) generator space gives a linear constraint matrix of rank ten. Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak{so}(2,3)\cap\mathfrak{stab}(T)=\{0\}.
}

The type cubic preserves no continuous subgroup of the full \((2,3)\) isometry algebra. It acts as a complete continuous-symmetry breaker on this quotient, not as an invariant cubic of \(Spin(2,3)\).

This also blocks an exceptional promotion. The cubic has a radical, while the middle action of a unital simple Jordan cubic is nondegenerate. No \(E_6\) action or exceptional determinant has been produced.
