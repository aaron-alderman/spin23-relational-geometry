## 1. What the decomposition means

The algebra separates into three roles:

1. a scalar channel acting uniformly on the acyclic singleton sector;
2. one full \(M_2(\mathbb Q)\) transformation channel on Spin--Temple;
3. a 30-dimensional radical of directed paths feeding between the component
   layers.

The radical is not noise added to the calculation.  It records precisely the
one-way qualifications, dependencies, supports and challenges that do not lie
on reversible cycles.  Products retain finite path history; sufficiently long
strictly component-moving products vanish.  This is an exact finite model of a
semisimple transformation core with transient extension memory.

The center of the full algebra is only the scalars even though the semisimple
quotient has two central blocks.  The radical links the scalar and matrix
sectors strongly enough that their central projectors do not remain independent
central elements before quotienting.  Thus “core plus environment” is visible
only after removing the directed extension layer.
