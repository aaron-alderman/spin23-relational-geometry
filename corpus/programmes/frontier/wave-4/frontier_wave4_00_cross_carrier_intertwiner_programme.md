# Frontier wave 4: construct or exclude the cross-carrier intertwiner

**Opened:** 18 October 2026  
**Parent:** [Wave 3 synthesis](../wave-3/frontier_wave3_04_synthesis_the_cross_carrier_intertwiner_is_the_common_gate.md)

Wave 3 reduced the remaining internal selector problem to one typed object:

\[
Y_F:\mathcal H_{\rm source}\longrightarrow W_{\rm mediator},
\qquad
K_W=Y_F^\dagger G_FY_F\not\propto I_3.
\]

The programme now tests three independent possible provenances. No branch may use the exceptional (QQ) row as an input and then call the output Furey-, Jordan- or (Spin(2,3))-derived.

## W4-A — Furey/Jordan representation classification

Classify every gauge-, TRO-, grading- and reality-compatible map from the established Furey matter/arrow carriers into the three-copy mediator space. Compute the relevant Hom space and its commutant action. Determine whether every positive Gram operator is forced to be scalar, or exhibit a non-scalar operator whose coefficients are fixed without importing the Clebsch table.

## W4-B — exceptional/action provenance

Starting from complete unified invariant tensors and the declared broken-phase components, test whether the action, finite Dirac support, or radiative self-energy matrix produces a canonical non-scalar (K_W). Track which Clebsches, Yukawa matrices, masses and counterterms enter. A (QQ)-kernel is a derivation only if its orientation survives arbitrary allowed parameter changes and is not inserted through the same row it is supposed to explain.

## W4-C — code/topology/(Spin(2,3)) orientation transport

Test whether the existing Peirce, ribbon, Golay, lattice or (Spin(2,3)) structures contain a canonical three-dimensional module and an equivariant map to the mediator multiplicity space. Compute stabilizers and orbit dimensions. A dimension match or analogy does not count; the map must preserve the named actions and produce a normalized non-scalar Gram operator.

## Common pass conditions

A positive result must simultaneously establish:

1. a typed domain and codomain already present in the corpus;
2. a nonzero Hom space under all required symmetries;
3. coefficient normalization not chosen from the target (QQ) row;
4. (K_W\not\propto I_3) with a simple, protected danger eigenspace;
5. a relation to one incidence ray rather than paired admission;
6. stability under every symmetry-allowed counterterm and heavy threshold.

If all three provenances fail or remain dependent on imported orientation data, the internal selector programme is indeterminate at this rung and the calibrated operational programme becomes the only live continuation.
