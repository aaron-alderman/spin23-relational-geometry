## Use rule

A zero unresolved count proves lexical ownership completeness in the declared filesystem scope. It does **not** prove that every scientific problem is solved, that the keyword pattern captures unstated tacit questions, or that semantic topic mappings establish the proposed physics. The live frontier remains the open-topic register; this census proves that explicit corpus gate language has nowhere else to hide.
