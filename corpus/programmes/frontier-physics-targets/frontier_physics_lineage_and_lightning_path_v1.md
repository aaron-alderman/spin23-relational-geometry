## Why this path can conduct

1. **One shared object already exists.** Projector quantum geometry uses the same Grassmannian kinematics and universal connection \([P,dP]\) as the exact corpus construction.
2. **One minimal dynamics already exists.** The three-mode model separates projective closure from coherent quarter-turn return and gives an analytic contraction slope after adding a second datum.
3. **Candidate hardware already contains the pieces.** Integrated photonics is the closest protected-doublet carrier; atomic holonomy supplies the best reversal/process-tomography precedent; acoustic and photon--magnon--phonon platforms offer programmable three-component alternatives.
4. **The principal false positives are named.** Loss, postselection, detector folding, metastability, ordinary anisotropy and feedback can each imitate selection, and the current map contains tests for them.
5. **Failure remains useful.** A null result yields calibrated holonomy/process tomography, resource bounds and a measured detector-equivalence class rather than an uninterpretable absence.

## The three junctions that are not yet joined

### Junction J1 — primitive to projector

The corpus does not yet derive complex quantum state space or its spectral projectors from thin contextual admissibility. For the lightning experiment, standard quantum mechanics may supply this layer explicitly. That permits a mechanism test but not a foundational derivation.

**Closure requirement:** a reconstruction theorem with nonisomorphic competitors and an impossible composition, or a clear declaration that the projector is imported standard physics.

### Junction J2 — corpus projector to physical projector

Abstract isomorphism is insufficient. The physical Hamiltonian or response matrix must generate the measured \(P\), its connection and the independently calibrated \(Y\) without a fitted basis identification.

**Closure requirement:** a commuting square from control parameters to Hamiltonian, spectral subspace, measured modes and return map; reversal must invert the holonomy while leaving the selector calibration independently checkable.

### Junction J3 — laboratory selection to fundamental interpretation

Even a successful selector experiment would establish a physical selection grammar, not Spin(2,3) gravity, three generations or E8 unification.

**Closure requirement:** a typed action, degree-of-freedom dictionary, anomaly and quantum-consistency audit, scale matching and at least one no-refit observable that differs from the nearest conventional model.

J2 is experimentally attackable now. J1 is a foundational mathematics programme. J3 remains the fundamental-physics firewall.

## Minimum viable lightning experiment

Use a programmable three-mode photonic or acoustic network with phase-sensitive output tomography.

1. Calibrate a cyclic gapped control path and reconstruct its instantaneous spectral projector.
2. Demonstrate projective return while the lifted two-frame returns by a quarter turn; reverse the path and reverse the coherent holonomy.
3. Add a physically separate, directly measured anisotropy or monitored-feedback datum \(Y\).
4. Iterate from many initial projectors and estimate the fixed point and tangent spectral radius.
5. Retain complete measurement records and reconstruct both conditioned trajectories and the unconditional channel.
6. Measure activity, dissipated heat or optical/electrical work, and record-reset cost at matched contraction.
7. Train the nuisance model on part of the control space and require held-out prediction under reversal, rate, loss, disorder and detector changes.

The decisive result is not “a phase was observed.” It is the simultaneous closure of four independently calibrated statements:

\[
\boxed{
P(T)=P(0),\qquad
U_{\rm frame}(T)\neq I,\qquad
\rho(D\mathcal R)<1,\qquad
\mathcal E_{\rm uncond}\neq\text{postselected collapse fiction}.
}
\]

## Branches off the conductor

- **Casimir/material branch (FPT-003):** use a measured response tensor as \(Y\) and predict a no-refit angular force or torque. This reconnects the experiment to the original Casimir starting point, but has a harder nuisance floor.
- **Two-current branch (FPT-004):** test whether a multichannel positive response cone collapses to a robust ray. This is computationally nearer but does not alone realize selector dynamics.
- **Scale and fundamental branch (FPT-011--FPT-014, FPT-017):** pursue only after a source-native carrier or a reconstruction result exists; otherwise the join is lexical rather than physical.

## Promotion rule

The lightning path promotes in stages:

1. **Exact mechanism:** the physical device realizes the declared projector/holonomy/return-map equations.
2. **Robust phenomenon:** the result survives detector, loss, rate, disorder and conditioning audits.
3. **Admissibility-specific result:** a prediction or exclusion follows that generic quantum control, feedback or positivity does not already imply.
4. **Fundamental relevance:** a typed action and scale-matched dictionary connect the result to flavour, gravity or cosmology.

Stopping at any earlier stage is a legitimate scientific result. Skipping a stage is not a join.
