## Operating rule

An open topic stays on this list until one of four things happens:

- its closure condition is met and linked;
- its kill test fires and the topic becomes a retained no-go;
- a dependency proves it unreachable and it becomes blocked;
- it is split into sharper child topics, with the parent marked superseded.

“Interesting” is not a status. Every topic has a next action, detector bundle, closure condition and kill test in the machine register.

## Cross-cutting unknowns

The queue exposes eight recurring unknowns that should be reported for every new arm:

1. **carrier:** what physical degrees of freedom hold the state?
2. **admission:** which constraints define allowed states and transformations?
3. **dynamics:** what generates motion, attraction, switching or decay?
4. **orientation:** what physical reference retains a sign or phase lift?
5. **memory:** where is history stored and how long does it persist?
6. **detector:** which records separate the leading mechanisms after nuisance folding?
7. **scale:** which conclusions survive size, time, frequency and coarse-graining limits?
8. **translation:** what typed map connects formal structures to physical operators and observations?
