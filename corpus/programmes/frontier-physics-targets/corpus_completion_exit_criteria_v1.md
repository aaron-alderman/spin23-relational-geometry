## The durable target

The corpus is complete only as a **dated saturation claim under a declared search protocol**. Every generated physics–mathematics adjacency must have a typed disposition; every exhausted branch must have its nearest reopening neighbourhood searched; every experimental frontier must have an executable programme with build, bridge and physics go/no-go decisions; and theory, literature, implications, narrative, documentation, linking, retrieval, provenance and reproducible release must close together.

This replaces the weaker reading that a zero count of finite tasks executable from current repository inputs is enough. That count remains useful: it says when another calculation would merely add freedom. It does not show that all relevant connections were generated, that dead ends were searched around, or that every experiment can decide what it claims to decide.

Completion never means that nature or mathematics has no unknown structure. It means that the corpus has saturated its declared and reproducible search space, classified every residue, and named the event or datum that would reopen each externally blocked branch.

No gate can compensate for another. A beautiful narrative cannot close an untyped physical map. A passing experiment cannot repair missing provenance. Complete linking cannot turn PageRank into evidence. A true theorem cannot supply a detector, and a detector cannot choose an ontology by itself.

## The adjacency universe

For every normalized target, claim family or physical object, the saturation pass asks which of the following relations applies:

1. same mathematics on another physical carrier;
2. the same carrier in another representation;
3. reduction, quotient, lift or extension;
4. boundary, bulk, defect or topology;
5. static, driven, dissipative or measurement-conditioned dynamics;
6. weak/strong coupling and low/high-frequency limits;
7. finite/infinite size and competing orders of limits;
8. duality, intertwiner, shared action or cross-grade map;
9. conservation law, anomaly, index or response invariant;
10. detector pushforward and information loss;
11. thermodynamic, causal, positivity and KMS compatibility;
12. an alternative mechanism producing the same record.

Each generated edge must become established, conditional on a named datum, experimentally testable, mathematically obstructed, physically inequivalent, redundant, exhausted with a neighbourhood receipt, or speculative and quarantined. “Interesting,” “suggestive,” and “perhaps related” are not terminal dispositions.

## Three experimental decisions

Every experimental programme must make three different decisions.

- **Build gate:** can the required carrier, preparation, sensitivity, bandwidth, coherence, calibration and controls actually be achieved?
- **Bridge gate:** does the resulting record distinguish the proposed corpus mechanism from the strongest conventional alternatives and nuisance models?
- **Physics gate:** does the surviving outcome support improved theoretical engineering, a revised conventional model, or escalation toward new physics?

The programme must predeclare positive, null and ambiguous branches and say which claims and topics each branch promotes, kills, narrows or leaves untouched. A null result without a scientific consequence is not a completed experiment.

## External blockers

An external blocker is admissible at the endpoint only if it records the exact missing datum or event, why no in-scope finite task can supply it, the neighbouring alternatives already searched, the affected claims and topics, its reopening trigger, and a review date or event. “Needs experiment,” “needs more mathematics,” and “future work” are not sufficient.
