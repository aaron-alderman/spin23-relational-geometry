## What “mapped” means

A crosswalk says that two entries address the same research object or gate. It does **not** say that their formalisms are equivalent or that one supplies evidence for the other. The canonical targets normalize navigation; the source documents retain authority over their own claims and status.

## Frontier shape

The filled region consists of exact local maps, established conventional neighbours, explicit no-go results, calibrated observables and executable next tests. The unfilled region consists of missing translations, unclosed response tensors, unbuilt apparatus, uncontrolled limits and joined models not yet calculated. The boundary between them is the **admissibility zone**: each target records what may enter, which invariant or observable survives, what conventional explanation must be defeated, and what result would stop the route.

The result is neither a single grand-theory claim nor a loose list of anomalies. It is a portfolio in which mathematics, simulation, materials, detectors, dynamics, geometry and new-physics gates can advance independently while remaining cross-referenced.
