## Implication

The hopeful statement is now operational: **look here, with this missing thing**. A successful result may yield a better engineered selector, detector, capture channel, transport definition or no-refit force prediction. A failed result is also useful because each branch has a kill test and a typed fallback. New physics is not promised by the map. It becomes plausible only where a conventional, fully sourced model fails after the named detector and nuisance controls have closed.

The stopping rule is therefore part of the result. Until a reopening datum arrives, autonomous elaboration should not promote these branches. It should maintain provenance, improve access and prepare the exact experiment or calculation that the datum will make admissible.
