# Corpus assessments for the frontier physics targets v1

## Assessment vocabulary

- **Within reach:** the corpus contains an exact object and a finite calculation, implementation or experiment that could move the target.
- **Near vicinity:** the corpus and current physics share a carrier or mechanism, but one typed join or decisive measurement is absent.
- **Mapped vicinity:** relevant structures and tests exist, but they do not yet share a physical carrier or forward model.
- **Gated:** the present route ends at a named no-go, missing theorem, uncontrolled limit or new-physics requirement.
- **Audit column:** the target must be measured or reported across other experiments rather than pursued as an isolated mechanism.

These are reach assessments, not truth probabilities. “Within reach” means that the next gate is finite and executable.
