## The current story

The corpus is organized around an invariant relation rather than a single
symmetry slogan. Its non-negotiable explanatory order is **Jordan admission and composition → typed admissibility region** (the living carrier and its admissible relations) **→ locally invariant carrier → Lie transformation and transport** of thing and mirror face **→ holonomy, dynamics, selection and records**. In the central exact model,
\(M^T\Omega M=\Omega\): \(M\) moves the living carrier, \(M^{-T}\) is its
mirror face, and \(\Omega\) carries their persistent local comparison. The
exterior-square relation space then separates as
\(\Lambda^2V^*=\mathbb R\Omega\oplus\Lambda^2_0V^*\); its five-dimensional
primitive remainder carries the signature-\((2,3)\) geometry behind
\(Sp(4,\mathbb R)\cong Spin_0(2,3)\). This is an exact theorem about a declared
carrier and its relations, not a declaration that either space is physical
spacetime. Read the [canonical reading contract](READING_CONTRACT.md), the
[locally invariant carrier spine](programmes/admissibility/admissibility_123_locally_invariant_carrier_and_mirror_face_spine.md)
and the [explanatory standard](EXPLANATORY_STANDARD.md) before interpreting
any group-centred branch.

The admissibility programme turns that question into an atlas. It tracks what is mathematically filled, conditionally constructed, excluded, bounded, underidentified, experimentally inaccessible and still uncharted. Dynamics remains essential, but no longer enters as an unexplained sovereign: it must preserve or transform an admitted domain and leave records by which competing mechanisms can be separated.

Start with [The admissibility frontier: the current story of the corpus](syntheses/the_admissibility_frontier_current_synthesis.md). It is the reader-facing synthesis of what has been found, what it means, why the programme remains hopeful, where the live edges are and how to join without surrendering the corpus's kenotic authority boundaries.
