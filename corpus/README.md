# Canonical research corpus

This directory is the source-of-truth research record. It is intentionally distinct from the curated books and generated website.

## Contents

- [`claims/`](claims/README.md) — the numbered, append-only research ledger;
- [`syntheses/`](syntheses/) — large interpretive and thesis-scale documents;
- [`programmes/`](programmes/) — coordinated branches, waves and closure exercises;
- [`governance/`](governance/) — repository history and publication state;
- [`registers/`](registers/) — manifests, audits and machine-readable programme state.

The numbered filename is the stable historical identity of a claim. Folder placement is only storage sharding and does not alter that identity or its epistemic status.
