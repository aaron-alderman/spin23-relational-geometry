# Correspondence

Correspondence preserves contact with people, papers and outside programmes.
Letters record what was asked or answered at a particular time. Conversations
record how understanding changed through clarification, correction and reply.

This lane does not turn agreement into evidence or disagreement into defeat.
Statements by participants remain theirs; editorial summaries remain
interpretations; mathematical and physical claims retain their own evidence,
verification and admission requirements.

## Letters

The first indexed collection is the [Spin(2,3) open-letter answer
programme](../programmes/open-letters/spin23_open_letter_answer_programme.md).
Its eight standalone answers sharpened four executable packets but are not
replies from the named addressees and do not constitute peer review.

## Conversations

The first indexed conversation is [**OPH and the Admissibility
Frontier**](conversations/oph-and-admissibility-frontier/README.md). It preserves
the corpus's current response first and the initial correspondence second, so a
reader can move backwards through the exchange without the later clarification
silently rewriting the earlier audit. No joint endorsement is inferred.

## Authority boundary

A correspondence page may correct the corpus's representation of another
programme, preserve a reply, expose a surviving disagreement or open bounded
work. It cannot by itself prove a theorem, identify a physical theory with
nature, promote a claim or multiply one exchange into independent evidence.
