This conversation tracks a correction rather than replacing its earlier state.
The initial OPH audit asked what the programme had established and which
bridges remained supplied. Bernhard Mueller's public reply then exposed a
symmetrical omission: a corpus that asks OPH to locate the passage from an
observer formalism to consciousness must also say where its own account stops.

The current response accepts that burden and proposes a shared consciousness
protocol. It also records a best-case OPH--corpus reading and a deliberately
speculative Peirce-field extension. These are proposals from this corpus unless
explicitly endorsed elsewhere.
