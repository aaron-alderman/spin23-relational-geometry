## Why the corpus responded

The public claim presented OPH as a single process from which observation,
quantum mechanics, four-dimensional spacetime, gravity, time, entropy and the
remaining problems of physics could be recovered. The initial correspondence
did not answer that breadth with dismissal or automatic assent. It decomposed
the claim into typed questions: what object is defined, what theorem or
execution follows, which premises are supplied, what gives the construction
physical meaning and which independent record could test that meaning.

The word **admits** was the central distinction. A model may admit an effective
description without deriving it, uniquely selecting it or showing that nature
uses it.

## What the audit found

The audit found a serious and unusually inspectable foundations programme. In
the finite total-two carrier, OPH supplies a classified family of completely
positive record laws sharing one classical repair rule while retaining
different quantum coherences. A phase-referenced detector separates laws that
the classical current interface identifies. Repeated repair contracts named
laws toward record completions, while equilibrium alone does not select among
them.

Those are exact and useful results on the declared carrier. The audit also
found that the source selection of the carrier, the physical choice of record
law, the relativistic interacting limit, matter parameters, responsive
geometry, a unique total entropy and experimental identification remained open
or conditional. Its verdict was therefore neither “OPH is false” nor “OPH has
derived nature.” It was that OPH gives the corpus an executable example of the
middle between allowed possibilities and durable records.

## What the initial reading left too quiet

The audit treated “observer” as a term requiring a bridge to physical records,
but it did not give the consciousness question its own interface contract. It
could therefore sound as though the burden belonged to OPH alone. The corpus
itself also has a remainder: positive states, transformations, detectors and
local closures do not derive first-person experience.

That omission is retained here because it matters. The later response does not
retroactively place a consciousness protocol in the original audit. It records
why the author's reply changed the conversation and why the corpus must accept
the same standard it applies outward.
