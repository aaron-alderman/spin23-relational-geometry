## Acknowledgement

The reply identifies a fair symmetry. The initial audit asked OPH not to move
from an observer patch, a record law or agreement between descriptions directly
to phenomenal consciousness. The corpus cannot make the corresponding move
from admissible states, detectors and records to a conscious subject either.
Its mathematics distinguishes what can coexist, how it can be transported and
what a detector can retain. None of those results, by itself, explains why
there is something it is like to occupy a perspective.

This does not make the two holes identical. OPH begins with observer-relative
patches and seeks an objective world through compatible overlap. The corpus
begins with positive possibilities, typed operations and records, then asks
which combinations remain jointly realizable. But both arrive at an interface
they must name rather than conceal: when does a physically or formally defined
observer become, support or correspond to a subject of experience?

The linked post remains authoritative for Mueller's words. This page responds

## The limitation we can share

Neither programme currently derives phenomenal character from its established
mathematics. OPH may propose that subjectivity is primitive, that an observer
patch already has an interior, or that consciousness appears at a particular
closure. The corpus may propose that consciousness occurs at a detector-relative
local closure, a durable record, a cross-scale integration or a further
relational structure. Each is free to propose an interface. Neither may turn
the freedom to place one into evidence that its placement is correct.

The same permission must be granted to rivals. A neuroscientific, functional,
enactive, panpsychist, dual-aspect, idealist or theological account may place
the interface differently. Such a proposal is not a cheap “consciousness of
the gaps” if it declares what the interface does, what it leaves unexplained
and what observation could count against it. It becomes a gap-filler only when
the undefined interface is asked to rescue every failed prediction.

## A consciousness protocol

Any OPH, corpus or rival consciousness claim should therefore publish at least
the following seven-part packet.

1. **Physical carrier.** Name the physical system, scale and variables claimed
   to support the interface.
2. **Subject criterion.** State what makes one process a candidate subject
   rather than only a controller, memory, model or detector.
3. **Interface map.** Specify the map between physical or formal states and the
   proposed experiential or perspectival states. Say whether it is one-to-one,
   many-to-one, contextual or only partially defined.
4. **Range of closure.** Declare whether closure is local, regional or global;
   instantaneous or historical; ultraviolet, infrared or multiscale. Do not
   infer global consciousness from local closure.
5. **Interventions and contrasts.** Predeclare perturbations that should
   preserve, alter, divide or extinguish the proposed subject while controlling
   for report, memory, attention and ordinary information processing.
6. **Rival and defeater.** Name at least one competing interface and a result
   that would favour it. A theory must permit the consciousness question to
   defeat, not merely decorate, its wider ontology.
7. **Residual ledger.** Record which explanatory remainder survives a pass:
   correlation, causal organization, unity, first-person character, identity
   through time or why experience exists at all.

The protocol does not solve consciousness. It prevents “observer,” “record,”
“integration,” “closure” and “experience” from doing one another's work by
verbal proximity.

## Best-case OPH--corpus reading

On its strongest bounded reading, OPH contributes a constructive account of
local perspectives, overlapping descriptions, repair, retained records and the
conditions under which partial views can compose. The corpus contributes a
typed account of positive coexistence, allowed operations, detector-relative
closure and the difference between a possible state, a transported state and a
recorded outcome. Together they suggest a disciplined research object:

> an observer-supporting relational situation in which admitted possibilities,
> transformations, memories and overlapping perspectives remain mutually
> realizable.

That object has more explanatory power than either the word “observer” or the
word “admissibility” alone. It may help locate where a consciousness interface
could live. It still does not establish that the interface is conscious, that
only one such interface exists, or that the finite OPH carrier is the one used
by nature.

The useful joint frontier is consequently concrete: reconstruct candidate
observer-supporting closures, identify what distinctions their detectors and
records preserve, and compare their predictions under controlled disruption.
An engineering improvement would be a better architecture for coherent local
records or distributed agreement. New physics would require a reproducible
residual that survives ordinary quantum instruments, process memory,
neuroscience and detector alternatives.

## Speculative extension: the Peirce field

There is a more ambitious picture, offered as a menu rather than a result. The
rank-two real response carrier `H_2(R)` and its `Spin_0(2,3)` conformal
completion describe a physical throat: positive two-channel possibilities and
their admissible transport. The complex rank-three Peirce geometry `H_3(C)` can
be pictured as a richer communion geometry in which three perspectives and
their pairwise relations coexist. One may then ask whether the throat is an
interface from physical response into a relational field, with the Peirce
components carrying not private qualia but the jointly structured relations
from which a subject/world distinction could be drawn.

At present this is not an OPH theorem, a corpus theorem or a consciousness
theory. No canonical map from the OPH carrier to `H_2(R)` has been established;
no source-derived lift from that carrier to `H_3(C)` has been selected; no
phenomenal observable or discriminating experiment follows. Calling it a
**Peirce field** is therefore a name for a search direction, not a discovered
field.

Its value is that it makes the missing work visible. A viable extension would
need a typed embedding, a reason the third Peirce component is required, a
dynamics or composition law, an account of local versus global closure and at
least one contrast with ordinary quantum-channel and neuroscientific models.
Failure at any step should close the extension without weakening the finite
results on either side.

## Invitation

The kenotic outcome is not that one programme defeats the other by pointing to
its hole. It is that both holes become inspectable. OPH can state where an
observer becomes more than a bookkeeping object. The corpus can state where
admissibility and records stop short of experience. Rival theories can use the
same protocol and may win. That is the form of contact worth preserving.
