# Reading contract: admission before symmetry

This corpus must be read from the admissible domain outward, not from a preferred symmetry group inward.

The governing order is:

\[
\boxed{\text{Jordan admission and composition}}
\longrightarrow
\boxed{\text{typed admissibility region}}
\longrightarrow
\boxed{\text{Lie transformation and transport}}
\longrightarrow
\boxed{\text{dynamics, selection and records}}.
\]

The arrows do not claim that one mathematical structure temporally manufactures the next. They specify the order of explanation and the type of question assigned to each layer.

The [explanatory standard](EXPLANATORY_STANDARD.md) governs each arrow: begin with the things being related, state why they are alike and different, identify the reason for the difference, report mathematical and physical closure separately, and place implications beside non-implications. Pictures and speculative readings may be offered as a menu, but none is required to accept the core result.

1. **Jordan asks what can coexist.** Positive states, responses, observables, compositions and eliminations delimit a cone or domain of mutually admissible possibilities.
2. **The typed middle is the live object.** A carrier, its allowed compositions, its histories, its selectors and its records must remain jointly realizable. This compatibility region—not a group name—is the centre of the programme.
3. **Lie asks how admitted possibilities can be transported.** Infinitesimal transformations and their closure describe motion or relation that preserves declared structure. They do not decide which states are admissible, which carrier nature realizes or which member of an orbit is selected.
4. **Dynamics lives inside, on or across the boundary.** An action, flow or update proposes histories. The admissibility gates determine whether those histories preserve positivity, type, causality, stability, global consistency and observable records.
5. **Detectors return the construction to evidence.** A detector folds histories into records and thereby reveals some distinctions while quotienting away others.

For the rank-two positive response cone, the Jordan triple/TKK construction has the exact conformal completion

\[
H_2(\mathbb R)_+
\xrightarrow{\mathrm{TKK}}
\mathfrak{sp}(4,\mathbb R)
\cong
\mathfrak{so}(2,3).
\]

This is an important theorem about the admitted space. It is not the starting premise, the centre of interpretation or a declaration that nature fundamentally uses \(\operatorname{Spin}_0(2,3)\). The group is the symmetry of a space of possibilities already delimited on the Jordan side.

## Summary test

A faithful summary must still make sense if every exceptional or Lie-group name is temporarily hidden. It should first identify:

- what is admitted;
- on which carrier;
- under which composition and positivity rules;
- which transformations preserve or cross the domain;
- what selects a realized history; and
- what detector record could distinguish the proposal.

Only then should it name the resulting Lie closure. A summary that begins with `Spin(2,3)`, treats the group as the programme's prior container, or describes the work primarily as a search for that symmetry has reversed the explanatory order.

## Authority boundary

The Jordan-first order is the corpus's interpretive architecture. The exact Jordan/TKK closure is established on its declared carrier. The claim that admissibility is ontologically prior to all physics, or that this particular carrier is fundamental in nature, remains open.
