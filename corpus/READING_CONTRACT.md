# Reading contract: preserve the distinction

This corpus must be read as a change in where physics looks. The route to new physics is not assumed to be higher energy alone. It may be greater experimental finesse: preserving phase, delay, boundary order, memory, orientation or correlation that a coarser detector erases. The first question is therefore not “which large symmetry contains reality?” but “which distinction separates the candidate mechanisms, what admits it, how does it move and can an apparatus retain it?”

The algebraic rule—admission before symmetry—serves that experimental rule.

The governing explanatory order is:

\[
\boxed{\text{Jordan admission and composition}}
\longrightarrow
\boxed{\text{living carrier and admissible relations}}
\longrightarrow
\boxed{\text{locally invariant carrier}}
\longrightarrow
\boxed{\text{Lie transport of thing and mirror face}}
\longrightarrow
\boxed{\text{holonomy, dynamics, selection and records}}.
\]

The arrows do not claim that one mathematical structure temporally manufactures the next. They specify the order of explanation and the type of question assigned to each layer. Once the carrier and types are declared, the operative relation is balanced rather than one-way: Jordan composition constrains admissible Lie transport, while transport tests which Jordan relations remain dynamically stable.

Every reader-facing account must also complete the return path:

\[
\boxed{\text{candidate mechanisms}}
\longrightarrow
\boxed{\text{distinguishing invariant}}
\longrightarrow
\boxed{\text{detector and reversal}}
\longrightarrow
\boxed{\text{record}}
\longrightarrow
\boxed{\text{updated admissible region}}.
\]

Without that return, the corpus is only a classification of possible structures. With it, admissibility becomes an experimental programme.

The [explanatory standard](EXPLANATORY_STANDARD.md) governs each arrow: begin with the things being related, state why they are alike and different, identify the reason for the difference, report mathematical and physical closure separately, and place implications beside non-implications. Pictures and speculative readings may be offered as a menu, but none is required to accept the core result.

1. **Jordan asks what can coexist.** Positive states, responses, observables, compositions and eliminations delimit a cone or domain of mutually admissible possibilities.
2. **The typed admissibility region—the typed middle—is the live object.** A carrier, its allowed compositions, its histories, its selectors and its records must remain jointly realizable. This compatibility region—not a group name—is the centre of the programme.
   For a directed typed relation graph, the default pre-quotient object is now
   the source/target-and-type two-rail carrier of AZ-126. A scalar adjacency or
   Laplacian is a declared quotient of that carrier, not a neutral synonym for
   the complete relation.
3. **The locally invariant carrier keeps perspectives comparable.** In the central symplectic instance, \(M^T\Omega M=\Omega\) joins the direct action \(M\) to its mirror face \(M^{-T}\). Pointwise invariance, compatible transport \(\nabla\Omega=0\), and globally trivial return are three different claims.
4. **Lie transformation and transport ask how admitted possibilities can move.** Infinitesimal transformations and their closure describe motion or relation that preserves the locally declared comparison. They do not decide which states are admissible, which carrier nature realizes or which member of an orbit is selected.
5. **Dynamics lives inside, on or across the boundary.** An action, flow or update proposes histories. The admissibility gates determine whether those histories preserve positivity, type, causality, stability, global consistency and observable records.
6. **Detectors return the construction to evidence.** A detector folds histories into records and thereby reveals some distinctions while quotienting away others.

7. **Ternary structure records mediated influence.** A Jordan triple, ternary operator product or typed three-way compatibility law may encode how two terms relate through a third context. This is a mathematical form of mediated dependence, not permission to rename a cubic expression as a new physical force.

## Originating language and the third-order arc

The phrase “hope, kenotically, is third-order influence with a soul” may be retained as originating language under four firewalls:

- “third-order” denotes an irreducibly three-place relation unless a physical perturbative order is separately derived;
- “kenotic” governs the relinquishment of excess authority and the preservation of alternatives;
- “soul” points to retained identity across admissible transformations and projections;
- none of these words names a physical field, coupling or observable without an independently typed model and detector.

The admissible scientific translation is to study context-mediated influence, identity-retaining transport, memory and detector-visible invariants. The metaphysical or theological interpretation remains optional and cannot serve as a premise in a physical derivation.

For the rank-two positive response cone, the Jordan triple/TKK construction has the exact conformal completion

\[
H_2(\mathbb R)_+
\xrightarrow{\mathrm{TKK}}
\mathfrak{sp}(4,\mathbb R)
\cong
\mathfrak{so}(2,3).
\]

This is an important theorem about the admitted space. It is not the starting premise, the centre of interpretation or a declaration that nature fundamentally uses \(\operatorname{Spin}_0(2,3)\). The group is the symmetry of a space of possibilities already delimited on the Jordan side.

## Summary test

A faithful summary must begin with the finesse reversal and must still make sense if every exceptional or Lie-group name is temporarily hidden. It should first identify:

- which presently erased distinction is worth recovering;
- what is admitted;
- on which carrier;
- under which composition and positivity rules;
- which transformations preserve or cross the domain;
- what selects a realized history; and
- what detector, reversal and independent replication could distinguish the proposal.

Only then should it name the resulting Lie closure. A summary that begins with `Spin(2,3)`, treats the group as the programme's prior container, describes the work primarily as a search for that symmetry or relegates the finesse pathway to its conclusion has preserved the old narrative.

## Authority boundary

The Jordan-first order is the corpus's interpretive architecture. The exact Jordan/TKK closure is established on its declared carrier. The claim that admissibility is ontologically prior to all physics, or that this particular carrier is fundamental in nature, remains open.
