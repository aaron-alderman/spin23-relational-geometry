Let

\[

\qquad i=0,1,2.

\]

These are the three color-conjugate handle reflections. They form a covariant orbit:

\[
RH_iR^{-1}=H_{i+1}.

Their linear color average vanishes exactly:

\[
\boxed{

}

\]

So there is no nonzero color-singlet linear handle operator in this orbit. This confirms the direct-intertwiner obstruction.

But the quadratic congruence channel

\[
\boxed{

\[

\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{
\text{color twirl of individual handle relations}
\Longrightarrow
\text{one gauge-singlet quadratic symplectic relationship}.
}

\]

This is a concrete realization of the programme's recurring idea that the physical invariant may live in the **relationship square** even when no constituent linear arrow survives.

## 2. Why the linear selector disappears

The certificate computes the exact matrix sum and obtains

\[

Any color-invariant linear quantity obtained by averaging this orbit is therefore zero. One cannot restore a microscopic handle-exchange operator simply by summing its three color orientations.

This gives a sharper meaning to the earlier full-rank commutator obstruction. The noncommuting handle is a nontrivial (C_3)-charged object; it contains no trivial representation at linear order.

\[

This is the correct observable-level replacement for a forbidden linear intertwiner.
