## 1. Group-ring calculation

Let \(g\) cyclically permute the three color sheets. The norm element is

\[
N=1+g+g^2.
\]

On the augmentation ideal,

\[
N|_I=0.
\]

Because \(g^{-1}=g^2\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
\Delta_{C_3}
&=(1-g)(1-g^{-1})\\
&=2-g-g^2\\
&=3-(1+g+g^2).
\end{aligned}
\]

Hence

\[
\Delta_{C_3}|_I=3\,1_I.
\]

In the three-sheet permutation basis,

\[
\Delta_{C_3}=
\begin{pmatrix}
2&-1&-1\\
-1&2&-1\\
-1&-1&2
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Its zero mode is the color singlet \((1,1,1)\); both color-standard directions have eigenvalue three. This is exactly the familiar singlet/augmentation split of the three-cycle permutation module.
