# The two-bit glue obstruction is exactly the binary Heisenberg commutator

## Status

**Exact symplectic and central-extension identification at the parity-quotient level. Physical identification of the two bit labels remains open.**

The binary Heisenberg sector previously had to be adjoined as a dual-character completion. It now arises intrinsically from the two Hadamard faces.

After quotienting each \(C_4\oplus C_6\) face glue by its order-six mutual radical,

\[
\overline H_M
=H_M/\operatorname{Rad}_L(H_M)
\cong\mathbb F_2^2,
\]

\[
\overline H_L
=H_L/\operatorname{Rad}_M(H_L)
\cong\mathbb F_2^2.
\]

The exponentiated discriminant pairing between them is

\[
\boxed{
\exp(2\pi i\,b(x,z))
=(-1)^{x\cdot z}.}
\]

This is exactly the commutator pairing of the two-qubit Heisenberg group.

## 1. Radical quotients

Write

\[
H_M=\langle h_4,h_6\rangle
\cong C_4\oplus C_6
\]

and

\[
H_L=\langle k_4,k_6\rangle
\cong C_4\oplus C_6.
\]

Their mutual radicals are

\[
\langle2h_4,2h_6\rangle
\cong C_2\oplus C_3
\]

and

\[
\langle2k_4,2k_6\rangle
\cong C_2\oplus C_3.
\]

Therefore

\[
\overline H_M
\cong
(C_4/2C_4)\oplus(C_6/2C_6)
\cong\mathbb F_2^2,
\]

and similarly for \(\overline H_L\).

The first bit records the odd/even class of the quartic generator; the second records the parity of the sextic generator after its order-three part is removed.

## 2. Perfect duality

For

\[
x=(a\bmod2,b\bmod2)\in\overline H_M
\]

and

\[
z=(c\bmod2,d\bmod2)\in\overline H_L,
\]

the mutual pairing is

\[
b(x,z)=\frac{ac+bd}{2}\pmod{\mathbb Z}.
\]

Exponentiating gives

\[
\exp(2\pi i\,b(x,z))
=(-1)^{ac+bd}.
\]

Every nonzero \(x\) pairs nontrivially with some \(z\), and conversely. Thus the pairing is perfect:

\[
\boxed{
\overline H_L\cong\overline H_M^*.}
\]

The two integral faces supply the primal and dual binary label spaces.

## 3. The four-dimensional symplectic quotient

Set

\[
\overline V
=\overline H_M\oplus\overline H_L.
\]

Define

\[
\omega((x,z),(y,w))
=x\cdot w+z\cdot y.
\]

This is alternating and nondegenerate over \(\mathbb F_2\). Hence

\[
\boxed{
\overline V\cong\mathbb F_2^4}
\]

with its standard symplectic form.

Moreover,

\[
\overline H_M
\quad\text{and}\quad
\overline H_L
\]

are complementary Lagrangians. The \(X/Z\) polarization is therefore not imposed externally: it is the parity shadow of the two transverse integral faces.

## 4. Central extension

The sign-central Heisenberg group associated with \(\overline V\) has multiplication

\[
(\epsilon,x,z)(\eta,y,w)
=
(\epsilon+\eta+z\cdot y,x+y,z+w),
\]

with centre \(C_2\). It has order

\[
2\cdot|\overline V|
=2\cdot16
=32.
\]

Its commutator is

\[
[(x,0),(0,z)]
=(-1)^{x\cdot z},
\]

exactly the exponentiated glue pairing.

Adjoining the compatible fourth-root central refinement gives

\[
P(k,x,z)=i^kX^xZ^z
\]

and the order-64 binary Pauli/Heisenberg group constructed earlier:

\[
|H_2|=4\cdot16=64.
\]

The discriminant gluing canonically supplies the sign commutator. The choice of \(\mu_4\) refinement is compatible with the independent quartic phase construction, but identifying the two fourth-root lines canonically still requires a map between the corresponding quartic quotients.

## 5. What has been unlocked

Earlier, the order-eight quartic extension was correctly found to be abelian. A separate dual-character space had to be added to obtain Heisenberg noncommutativity.

The new lattice tower explains where that dual sector comes from:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\text{first integral face}/\text{radical}
&\longrightarrow X\text{-labels},\\
\text{second integral face}/\text{radical}
&\longrightarrow Z\text{-characters},\\
\text{mutual discriminant pairing}
&\longrightarrow\text{Heisenberg commutator}.
\end{aligned}}
\]

The dual sector is the parity quotient of the other integral face.

## 6. Relation to “one carrier, two faces”

The slogan now has an exact finite meaning:

1. **one carrier:** the rational symplectic/primitive-wedge space;
2. **two faces:** the transverse integral glues \(M\) and \(W_HM\);
3. **relationship:** their perfect parity pairing;
4. **central residue:** the sign generated by noncommuting \(X/Z\) lifts.

This is a mathematically precise instance of relational composition producing noncommutativity only when both faces are retained.

## 8. Stop-everything and falsification gates

### Stop-everything

1. The two parity coordinates are derived as named Standard-Model field invariants.
2. The quartic \(M/I\) character maps canonically to the \(\mu_4\) centre of this glue-derived Heisenberg group.
3. A Jordan-triple product yields the same perfect pairing between its two faces.
4. The code's logical Pauli operators are identified with the two radical quotients without a fitted symplectic basis.

### Boundaries

- the sign-central extension is canonical; the fourth-root refinement still needs a canonical line identification;
- symplectic isomorphism does not by itself label the two bits physically;
- the construction does not restore the falsified raw puncture/support equivariance;
- the Heisenberg commutator is a parity shadow, not the full integral discriminant form.
