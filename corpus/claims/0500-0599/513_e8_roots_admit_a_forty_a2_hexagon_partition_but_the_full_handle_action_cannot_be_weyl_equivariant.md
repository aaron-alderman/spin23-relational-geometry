# \(E_8\) Roots Admit a Forty-\(A_2\)-Hexagon Partition, but the Full Handle Action Cannot Be Weyl-Equivariant

**Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Branches:** B07, B09, B10, B12, B13 and B14  
**Claim status:** exact root enumeration and exact-cover construction; the order obstruction applies to the ambient wedge action but is removed by the natural global \(C_3\) kernel of the fiber permutation action

## 1. Executive result

The new factorization

\[
240=40\cdot2\cdot3
\]

survives its first nontrivial \(E_8\) test. The full ambient wedge action fails faithful Weyl-equivariance, but the natural faithful quotient on the fibers passes the group-order gate.

### Positive result

The 240 roots of \(E_8\) can be partitioned into

\[
\boxed{40\text{ pairwise disjoint }A_2\text{ root hexagons}.}
\]

Thus it is combinatorially possible to associate one six-root \(A_2\) fiber to each of the 40 genus-two Lagrangian polarizations.

### Ambient obstruction and quotient repair

The handle action faithful on the full primitive-wedge carrier has order

\[
466{,}560=2^7\,3^6\,5.
\]

The \(E_8\) Weyl group has order

\[
|W(E_8)|
=696{,}729{,}600
=2^{14}3^5 5^2 7.
\]

Because the former contains \(3^6\) while the latter contains only \(3^5\),

\[
466{,}560\nmid |W(E_8)|.
\]

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{the full ambient wedge action cannot embed faithfully in }W(E_8).}
\]

However, an anti-diagonal binary \(C_3\) fixes every one of the 240 fibers. The faithful fiber-permutation quotient therefore has order \(466{,}560/3=155{,}520\), which does divide \(|W(E_8)|\). The order obstruction forces this natural quotient but does not rule out a Weyl-equivariant fiber action.

## 2. Exact \(E_8\) root model

The certificate uses the standard 240-root realization:

- 112 integer roots obtained by permuting
  \[
  (\pm1,\pm1,0,0,0,0,0,0);
  \]
- 128 half-integer roots
  \[
  \frac12(\pm1,\ldots,\pm1)
  \]
  with an even number of minus signs.

Coordinates are doubled in the computation, so root norm two becomes squared norm eight.

For every root \(\alpha\), the exact inner-product distribution is:

| relation to \(\alpha\) | number of roots |
|---|---:|
| \(\alpha\) | 1 |
| \(-\alpha\) | 1 |
| inner product \(+1\) | 56 |
| inner product \(-1\) | 56 |
| orthogonal | 126 |

Hence the \(+1\) root graph is regular of degree 56.

## 3. The forty \(A_2\) hexagons

An \(A_2\) subsystem is generated by roots \(\alpha,\beta\) with

\[
(\alpha,\beta)=-1.
\]

Its six roots are

\[
\{\pm\alpha,\pm\beta,\pm(\alpha+\beta)\}.
\]

After quotienting by antipodes, an \(A_2\) subsystem is a triple of root lines. Exhaustive enumeration finds:

\[
120\text{ root lines},
\qquad
1{,}120\text{ distinct }A_2\text{ triples}.
\]

An exact-cover search finds 40 disjoint triples covering all 120 root lines, equivalently 40 disjoint hexagons covering all 240 roots.

This proves existence, not canonicity. Many covers may exist, and the certificate does not identify one selected by the handle group, Furey algebra or Jordan structure.

## 4. The naive graph lifts fail

The intersection graph on the 40 Lagrangian polarizations is the strongly regular graph

\[
\operatorname{srg}(40,12,2,4).
\]

Giving each base vertex a six-cycle or six-element complete fiber yields the most direct untwisted graph products. Their degrees are:

| lift | degree |
|---|---:|
| Cartesian with \(C_6\) | 14 |
| tensor with \(C_6\) | 24 |
| strong product with \(C_6\) | 38 |
| lexicographic with \(K_6\) | 77 |
| complete bipartite links over adjacent fibers | 72 |

None has degree 56.

Therefore the \(E_8\) root graph is not any of these canonical untwisted sixfold lifts. A surviving construction requires a nontrivial cocycle telling each polarization adjacency which decorated roots to connect.

## 5. The Weyl-group obstruction and its precise boundary

An equivariant bijection

\[
\mathcal F\longrightarrow\Phi(E_8)
\]

that carries the ambient order-\(466{,}560\) wedge action faithfully to root-system automorphisms would embed that group into \(\operatorname{Aut}\Phi(E_8)=W(E_8)\).

The order-divisibility failure proves that no such embedding exists.

This is an exact obstruction to retaining the relative binary order-three rotation faithfully.

The first escape route occurs naturally: the binary action already has a global anti-diagonal \(C_3\) kernel. After quotienting it, the faithful permutation group is

\[
S_3\times PSp_4(3),
\]

of order \(155{,}520\). This order divides \(|W(E_8)|\).

The remaining routes are:

1. realize the natural \(C_3\) quotient inside \(W(E_8)\);
2. restrict to a subgroup whose order divides \(W(E_8)\);
3. map fibers to \(E_8\) objects without preserving the root metric;
4. use \(E_8\) only as a local \(A_2\)-fiber organizer rather than the global carrier symmetry.

## 6. What remains genuinely open

The exact-cover result means the \(40\times A_2\) picture is not impossible. A nontrivial relation could still exist if:

- a source-derived cover selects 40 particular \(A_2\) subsystems;
- the natural \(C_3\) quotient or a proper handle subgroup acts Weyl-equivariantly;
- the missing dynamics is represented by transitions between inequivalent covers;
- the \(E_8\) lattice appears one rung above the fixed root-system action.

These are materially narrower claims than “the 240 fibers are the \(E_8\) roots.”

## 7. Stop-everything criteria

Promote the \(E_8\) branch only if a construction supplies:

1. a canonical exact cover of the roots by 40 \(A_2\) hexagons;
2. a map from the 40 Lagrangians to those hexagons;
3. an explicit nontrivial decoration cocycle reproducing all root inner products;
4. the natural \(S_3\times PSp_4(3)\) quotient, or a smaller source-selected subgroup, acting inside \(W(E_8)\);
5. compatibility with the Furey/Jordan field labels.

## 8. Falsification criteria

- Cardinality 240 alone is not an \(E_8\) bridge.
- The full ambient wedge group cannot act faithfully through \(W(E_8)\).
- The faithful fiber-permutation quotient is smaller by \(C_3\) and is not excluded by group order.
- No untwisted standard graph product reproduces the \(E_8\) degree.
- An arbitrary exact cover is not a canonical physical construction.
- A proposed quotient must identify the anti-diagonal order-three rotation that becomes invisible on fibers.
