\[

\[
\boxed{

\]

Thus the coefficient-two structure of the integral word is already encoded in the unique charge relation on the Furey spanning tree.

\[

\[

The left kernel is one-dimensional and generated by

\[

\[

\[

Solving for the charged-lepton weight gives

This is not an arbitrary dependence introduced to fit \(K_+\); it follows from the source sign weights.
