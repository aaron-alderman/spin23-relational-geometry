# The two Hadamard faces are transverse isotropic glues with a two-bit obstruction

## Status

**Exact discriminant-gluing theorem for the two distinguished index-24 overlattices.**

Inside the discriminant group of

\[
I_H\cong U(8)\oplus\langle-2\rangle\oplus U(-18),
\]

the two integral faces \(M\) and \(W_HM\) correspond to transverse isotropic subgroups

\[
H_M\cong H_L\cong C_4\oplus C_6.
\]

Their intersection is zero, but their mutual discriminant pairing is nonzero. It factors through exactly two parity bits:

\[
\boxed{
b((a,b),(c,d))
=\frac{ac+bd}{2}\pmod{\mathbb Z}.}
\]

The obstruction to combining the two integral faces is therefore binary even though each face contains quartic and ternary data.

## 1. Glue coordinates

Use the common-core basis

\[
(p,q,u,z,y)
\]

with Gram lattice

\[
U(8)\oplus\langle-2\rangle\oplus U(-18).
\]

The first face is generated over \(I_H\) by

\[
h_4=v=\frac12p+\frac14q,
\qquad
h_6=s=\frac16y.
\]

Their orders in \(A_{I_H}\) are four and six. Thus

\[
H_M=\langle h_4,h_6\rangle
\cong C_4\oplus C_6.
\]

The Hadamard-transformed face is generated by

\[
k_4=W_Hv=-\frac14p-\frac12q,
\qquad
k_6=W_Hs=\frac16z,
\]

giving

\[
H_L=\langle k_4,k_6\rangle
\cong C_4\oplus C_6.
\]

Both groups are isotropic for the discriminant quadratic form, as required for even integral overlattices.

## 2. Transversality

Direct enumeration gives

\[
\boxed{H_M\cap H_L=0.}
\]

Hence

\[
|H_M+H_L|=24^2=576.
\]

This recovers the fact that the two faces intersect exactly in \(I_H\). It also shows that their gluing data are genuinely independent as finite subgroups.

## 3. Mutual pairing

The only nonzero generator pairings are

\[
b(h_4,k_4)=\frac12,
\qquad
b(h_6,k_6)=\frac12.
\]

The crossed pairings vanish:

\[
b(h_4,k_6)=b(h_6,k_4)=0.
\]

Therefore

\[
b(ah_4+bh_6,ck_4+dk_6)
=\frac{ac+bd}{2}\pmod{\mathbb Z}.
\]

Although the generators have orders four and six, the mutual pairing remembers only

\[
(a\bmod2,b\bmod2)
\in\mathbb F_2^2.
\]

It is the ordinary dot product on the two parity vectors.

## 4. The mutual radicals

The subgroup of \(H_M\) pairing trivially with all of \(H_L\) is

\[
\operatorname{Rad}_L(H_M)
=\langle2h_4,2h_6\rangle
\cong C_2\oplus C_3.
\]

It has order six. Symmetrically,

\[
\operatorname{Rad}_M(H_L)
=\langle2k_4,2k_6\rangle
\cong C_2\oplus C_3.
\]

Thus all order-three information and the even halves of the quartic data are mutually compatible. Only the two odd parity bits obstruct simultaneous integral gluing.

## 5. Reconstruction of the ambient primitive-wedge lattice

The opposite-face radical has an exact interpretation. Its generators are

\[
2k_4=-\frac12p
=-F_1\pmod{I_H},
\]

and

\[
2k_6=\frac13z
=R-E_2-F_2\pmod{I_H}.
\]

These are precisely the missing order-two and order-three generators that enlarge

\[
M
\]

to the full primitive-wedge lattice

\[
N\cong2U\oplus\langle-2\rangle.
\]

Therefore the ambient glue subgroup is

\[
\boxed{
H_N=H_M\oplus\operatorname{Rad}_M(H_L),}
\]

of order

\[
24\cdot6=144.
\]

Since

\[
|\det I_H|=41472,
\]

the resulting overlattice has determinant

\[
\frac{41472}{144^2}=2,
\]

exactly the determinant of \(2U\oplus\langle-2\rangle\).

By symmetry,

\[
H_{W_HN}
=H_L\oplus\operatorname{Rad}_L(H_M)
\]

reconstructs the Hadamard-transformed ambient primitive-wedge lattice.

## 6. The two ambient lattices

The two order-144 ambient glue subgroups are isotropic. Their intersection has order 36:

\[
|H_N\cap H_{W_HN}|=36.
\]

Their sum is the full 576-element group \(H_M+H_L\), but that sum is not isotropic. In fact, 360 of its elements are individually isotropic, yet the set of isotropic elements is not a subgroup.

Consequently there is no even integral overlattice obtained by simply adjoining both complete face glues. The parity pairing is the exact obstruction.

## 7. Structural interpretation

The arithmetic hierarchy is now:

\[
\begin{array}{ccccc}
&&I_H&&\\
&\swarrow&&\searrow&\\
M&&&&W_HM\\
&\searrow&&\swarrow&\\
N&&&&W_HN
\end{array}
\]

where:

- \(I_H\) is the shared arithmetic core;
- \(M\) and \(W_HM\) are transverse index-24 face glues;
- the opposite mutual radicals complete each face to its determinant-two ambient lattice;
- the two-bit pairing prevents a single integral lattice from containing both complete faces in this common rational realization.

This is a precise version of “one carrier, two faces, relational obstruction.”

## 8. Consequences for the programme

### Strengthened

- both distinguished index-24 overlattices are explicitly classified;
- their gluing groups and mutual pairing are known;
- the obstruction is exactly \(\mathbb F_2^2\);
- the order-six radicals reconstruct the known ambient primitive-wedge lattice;
- the repeated \(2\), \(3\), \(4\), \(6\), \(12\), \(24\), and \(144\) scales now belong to one lattice tower.

### Pinned

- the code group has not been shown to act on these glue subgroups;
- the two parity bits have not been identified with physical quantum numbers;
- no dynamics yet selects one face or an allowed transition;
- the nonintegral union is an obstruction, not evidence for superposition of lattices.

## 9. Stop-everything and falsification gates

### Stop-everything

1. The two parity bits are identified canonically with chirality, weak orientation, or the two puncture labels.
2. The order-six radical is derived from the Standard-Model code or Furey algebra as the unique ambient completion.
3. A Jordan-triple composition law reproduces the discriminant pairing \((ac+bd)/2\).
4. A physical transition law moves between the two faces while preserving precisely their common radical data.

### Boundaries

- transverse isotropic subgroups need not have an isotropic sum;
- the order-24 face quotients are abelian and are not automorphism groups;
- the binary obstruction survives even though the ambient odd-prime actions are conjugate;
- reconstruction of \(N\) is arithmetic, not yet dynamical.
