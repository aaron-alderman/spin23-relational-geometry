# The Standard Even-\(E_6\) Subgroup Has No Invariant Forty-\(A_2\) Cover of the \(E_8\) Roots

**Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Branches:** B07, B09, B10, B12, B13 and B14  
**Claim status:** exhaustive orbit computation for the explicit standard \(W(E_6)^+\) embedding; other abstract \(PSp_4(3)\) embeddings or groupoid constructions remain open

## 1. Result

The natural subgroup

\[
W(E_6)^+\cong PSp_4(3)
\subset W(E_8)
\]

coming from the index-three \(E_6\oplus A_2\) root sublattice does not preserve any forty-\(A_2\) exact cover of the \(E_8\) roots.

Its action on all 1,120 \(A_2\) root subsystems has orbit distribution

\[
\boxed{
1^1,\qquad
27^3,\qquad
120^1,\qquad
216^3,\qquad
270^1.}
\]

The total is

\[
1+3(27)+120+3(216)+270=1{,}120.
\]

There is no orbit of size 40. There is also no union of complete orbits of total size 40.

Therefore the standard \(PSp_4(3)\) embedding cannot act transitively on forty disjoint \(A_2\) fibers and cannot stabilize a forty-hexagon cover setwise.

## 2. Computation

The certificate begins with the explicit \(E_6\oplus A_2\) simple-root system inside the standard 240-root realization of \(E_8\).

The even Weyl subgroup is generated by products

\[
s_{\alpha_0}s_{\alpha_i},
\qquad i=1,\ldots,5,
\]

of pairs of \(E_6\) simple reflections. These generate the kernel of the Weyl sign character:

\[
W(E_6)^+.
\]

Every generator is applied to:

- all 240 roots;
- all 120 antipodal root lines;
- all 1,120 \(A_2\) triples of root lines.

Breadth-first orbit enumeration then produces the distribution above.

## 3. Why orbit size forty is necessary

The qutrit polarization space is a transitive \(PSp_4(3)\)-set of size 40:

\[
\operatorname{Lag}(2,\mathbb F_3^4).
\]

An equivariant association assigning one \(A_2\) hexagon to each polarization would require a 40-element orbit of \(A_2\) subsystems.

No such orbit exists in the standard embedding.

Even weakening transitivity to setwise preservation cannot help: an invariant 40-element set would have to be a union of the listed orbits, and no subset of their multiplicities sums to 40.

## 4. Combined obstruction

The standard \(E_6\oplus A_2\) construction now fails in two independent ways:

1. on individual roots it has orbits
   \[
   72+6+162,
   \]
   not a transitive 240-point action;
2. on \(A_2\) subsystems it has no invariant 40-element cover candidate.

Thus the exact abstract group identity

\[
S_3\times PSp_4(3)
\cong
W(A_2)\times W(E_6)^+
\]

does not extend to the required permutation geometry in the standard root-subsystem embedding.

## 5. What remains open

This computation does not classify every subgroup of \(W(E_8)\) abstractly isomorphic to \(PSp_4(3)\). A surviving route could involve:

1. a nonstandard conjugacy class of \(PSp_4(3)\) subgroups;
2. a larger object on which several \(E_6\oplus A_2\) decompositions form one orbit;
3. a groupoid whose objects are exact covers and whose morphisms are Weyl transformations;
4. an \(E_8\) lattice or theta relation not based on individual roots or \(A_2\) covers.

These routes require new data. The standard root-level route is pinned.

The [regulus closure bridge](../../programmes/regulus-closure-bridge/README.md)
now supplies one such non-root object: the same 40-polarization carrier
constructs transitive \(PSp_4(3)\) orbits of 45 regulus-pair sections and 27
maximal closure cliques, while its 36 elliptic sections reconstruct the 36
double-sixes. This realizes the finite \(E_6\) cubic incidence but
does not alter the no-go proved here for a 40-element invariant set of
\(A_2\) root subsystems.

## 6. Consequence for the research narrative

The mathematically secure chain now stops at

\[
\text{faithful fiber group}
\cong
W(A_2)\times W(E_6)^+
\subset W(E_8)
\]

as an abstract subgroup relation.

It does not continue to

\[
\text{240 fibers}
\cong
\text{240 \(E_8\) roots}
\]

equivariantly under the standard embedding.

This is exactly the distinction between “the same group occurs” and “the same representation or geometry occurs.”

## 7. Stop-everything criteria

Reopen the root-level \(E_8\) identification only if:

1. another \(PSp_4(3)\) conjugacy class in \(W(E_8)\) has a 40-orbit of \(A_2\) subsystems;
2. an invariant forty-cover is constructed by a different subgroup;
3. a groupoid of covers produces the 40-polarization action;
4. Furey/Jordan data supplies a canonical nonstandard embedding.

## 8. Falsification criteria

- The standard even-\(E_6\) subgroup has no 40-orbit on \(A_2\) subsystems.
- No invariant forty-cover can be assembled from its orbit decomposition.
- Abstract equality of group orders or isomorphism types is insufficient.
- The arbitrary exact cover from Cycle 429 is not rescued by the standard subgroup.
- This result does not exclude non-root or nonstandard-embedding \(E_8\) relations.
