## Executive result

The Jordan route passes the charge-three test exactly but fails the faithful residual-\(\mathbb Z_3\) test unless it is centrally lifted to the fermion module.

Let

\[
J_f=\operatorname{Herm}_3(\mathbb C),
\qquad
X=I-3P=\operatorname{diag}(-2,1,1).
\]

The traceless Hermitian \(X\) generates the Jordan automorphism flow

\[
A\longmapsto e^{itX}Ae^{-itX}
\]

with derivation

\[
\delta_X(A)=i[X,A].
\]

On matrix units,

\[
\delta_X(E_{ij})=i(q_i-q_j)E_{ij},
\qquad
q=(-2,1,1).
\]

Therefore the two cross-Peirce orientations have charges \(\mp3\), while both population-diagonal blocks are neutral. This is precisely the desired relational-carrier action:

\[
\boxed{
\delta_X(PJ_fQ)=-3i\,PJ_fQ,
\qquad
\delta_X(QJ_fP)=+3i\,QJ_fP.
}
\]

But at the would-be residual transformation,

\[
e^{2\pi iX/3}=\omega I_3,
\qquad \omega=e^{2\pi i/3}.
\]

Conjugation by a scalar is trivial:

\[
A\mapsto\omega A\omega^{-1}=A.
\]

Thus the Jordan automorphism group sees the continuous charge-three rotation but divides out its central \(\mathbb Z_3\). In group language, \(\operatorname{Aut}(\operatorname{Herm}_3(\mathbb C))\) is projective: the central cube roots act trivially on observables.

The same problem appears in the plain commutant-normalizer route. If the real structure sends \(W=e^{itX}\) to \(W^{-1}\), then the standard inner-gauge-style lift gives

\[
WJWJ^{-1}=1.
\]

Hence a family commutant unitary is not automatically a nontrivial ordinary NCG inner gauge transformation.

The surviving construction must therefore include a central lift:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Jordan observables carry the adjoint/projective action, while fermions or line operators carry the fundamental action retaining }\mathbb Z_3.
}
\]

This is mathematically coherent and familiar in gauge theory, but it is an extra piece of geometry. Without it, the Jordan route cannot produce faithful \(\mathbb Z_3\) topological order or level-three BF.

## 1. Exact Peirce action

The projector \(P\) gives

\[
J_f=PJ_fP\oplus QJ_fQ\oplus(PJ_fQ\oplus QJ_fP).
\]

Because \(X=-2P+Q\), it commutes with both diagonal Peirce blocks. For \(K=PKQ\),

\[
[X,K]=(-2-1)K=-3K,
\]

and for \(K^\dagger=QK^\dagger P\),

\[
[X,K^\dagger]=(1-(-2))K^\dagger=3K^\dagger.
\]

Exponentiation rotates the real two-plane \(PJ_fQ\oplus QJ_fP\) as a complex charged relation. The charge is derived from the population ranks and unimodularity; no tensor-product coordinate algebra is needed to state the Jordan derivation.

## 2. Why the centre disappears

The automorphism acts by conjugation, so every scalar unitary is in its kernel. In particular,

\[
e^{2\pi iX/3}
=
\operatorname{diag}(e^{-4\pi i/3},e^{2\pi i/3},e^{2\pi i/3})
=\omega I_3.
\]

The flow generated by \(X\) on \(J_f\) therefore has effective period \(2\pi/3\), not \(2\pi\). The effective Jordan automorphism circle is the original \(U(1)\) modulo its \(\mathbb Z_3\) centre.

That quotient is harmless if only adjoint observables matter. It is fatal if the centre is supposed to be:

- a faithful discrete gauge remnant;
- a charge acting on elementary matter;
- the source of nontrivial Wilson lines;
- the dynamical group described by level-three BF theory.

BF theory detects the global form and line operators, not merely the adjoint Lie algebra.

## 3. The required module lift

The family vector space \(\mathbb C^3\) carries the fundamental action

\[
\psi\mapsto e^{itX}\psi.
\]

At \(t=2\pi/3\), this is

\[
\psi\mapsto\omega\psi,
\]

which is nontrivial. Thus the existing family fermion module can in principle retain the centre even though the Jordan observable algebra cannot.

A complete structure would have:

1. Jordan observables transforming by conjugation;
2. fermions transforming in a central extension/fundamental module;
3. a connection whose adjoint action differentiates to \(\delta_X\);
4. a charge-three relational scalar in the cross-Peirce module;
5. a compact global lift whose centre acts on Wilson lines and matter.

The distinction is analogous to the difference between a \(PSU(3)\) adjoint theory and an \(SU(3)\) theory with fundamental matter. The Lie algebra is the same; the global gauge group and line spectrum are not.

## 4. Compatibility with \(J_F\) and \(\gamma_F\)

The family action is diagonal and does not mix finite chiral grading, so

\[
[W,\gamma_F]=0
\]

in the replicated geometry.

If \(J_F\) complex-conjugates family coefficients, then

\[
J_FWJ_F^{-1}=\bar W=W^{-1}.
\]

This provides the correct conjugate action on antiparticles. But it also means that inserting \(W\) into the usual expression \(uJuJ^{-1}\), as if it were an algebra unitary, cancels it.

Therefore:

- \(W\) is kinematically compatible with reality and grading as a module symmetry;
- it is not thereby an ordinary inner gauge transformation of the generation-blind algebra;
- promoting \(W(x)\) to a local symmetry requires an outer/module connection or extended gauge datum.
