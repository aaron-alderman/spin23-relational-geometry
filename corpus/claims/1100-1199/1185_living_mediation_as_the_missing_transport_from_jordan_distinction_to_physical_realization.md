# Living mediation as the missing transport from Jordan distinction to physical realization

## Status

This note develops a theological analogy into a mathematical research heuristic. It does **not** identify the Holy Spirit with a field, force, gauge boson, algebra, or physical mechanism. Its purpose is narrower: to ask whether the pneumatological analogy correctly diagnoses the *kind* of object missing from the current programme.

The answer is provisionally yes. The missing ingredient is unlikely to be one more static algebra. It must be an operation of transport that makes one relational structure effective across several different realizations.

## 1. The crucial correction: the programme is not missing form

The programme already possesses a substantial amount of form. Given a primitive rank-one family projector

\[
P^2=P,\qquad \operatorname{rank}P=1,
\]

the complementary projector is

\[
Q=I-P,
\]

and the unique primitive traceless central generator of the rank split is

\[
X=I-3P.
\]

In an adapted basis,

\[
X=\operatorname{diag}(-2,1,1).
\]

The cross-Peirce sectors then have charges

\[
q(P\mathcal A Q)=-3,
\qquad
q(Q\mathcal A P)=+3.
\]

Once this generator is lifted faithfully to the fermion module, the anomaly-free Standard Model representative is of the form

\[
B-3L_i
\]

up to the established hypercharge change of basis. Symmetry breaking supplies a raw ninefold remnant whose colour-centre quotient is the physical split

\[
\mathbb Z_3.
\]

An untwisted, oriented, gapped defect can then carry level-three BF theory and its nine-dimensional torus Hilbert space.

Thus the missing term is not primarily another formula specifying what the structure *would be*. It is what makes the already-specified structure operative:

\[
\text{formal distinction}
\longrightarrow
\text{dynamical presence}.
\]

This is why the analogy shifted from Logos to Spirit. Logos names articulation and intelligibility; the pneumatological role is actualization, indwelling, transport, orientation, and preservation through change.

## 2. Object-thinking may be the wrong search strategy

The habitual question is:

> What additional object must be inserted into the theory?

That question encourages searches for another algebra, particle, group, scalar, or dimension. But the existing no-go results show that indiscriminate enlargement is dangerous. Associatively adjoining the desired family splitter regenerates the full two-point tensor algebra and its excessive gauge sector.

The better question is:

> What operation carries one invariant relation through several descriptions without turning all of those descriptions into one oversized algebra?

This suggests that the missing item should resemble one or more of the following:

- a connection rather than a new fibre;
- a functor rather than another object in a category;
- a natural transformation rather than a forced identification;
- a typed correspondence rather than universal multiplication;
- a dynamical reduction of structure group rather than an appended gauge factor;
- a transgression map from bulk relational data to boundary topological data;
- an action principle whose stationary points are compatible realizations of the same distinction.

The key requirement is **identity through translation**. The same rank split must remain recognizable as it moves through Jordan, fermionic, gauge, vacuum, quotient, and boundary languages.

## 3. The transport diagram

The desired construction can be represented as a diagram:

\[
\begin{array}{ccccc}
P\in J
&\longrightarrow&
X=I-3P
&\longrightarrow&
\delta_X=i[X,\,\cdot\,]
\\[4pt]
\downarrow && \downarrow && \downarrow
\\[4pt]
\mathcal H_F
&\longrightarrow&
\rho(X)
&\longrightarrow&
e^{it\rho(X)}
\\[4pt]
\downarrow && \downarrow && \downarrow
\\[4pt]
\langle S\rangle
&\longrightarrow&
\mathbb Z_9/K
&\longrightarrow&
\mathbb Z_3\text{ BF boundary carrier}.
\end{array}
\]

Here:

- the first row is the Jordan/Peirce articulation;
- the second row is faithful action on fermionic matter;
- the third row is vacuum selection, global-form quotient, and topological realization.

At present, the horizontal arrows are individually intelligible, but the vertical arrows are not derived from one principle. The missing mediator is what makes the diagram commute.

Calling it a mediator does not explain it. A successful theory must specify:

1. its domain and codomain at every level;
2. what invariant it transports;
3. why it selects a rank-one orbit;
4. how it becomes local without generating the forbidden associative enlargement;
5. how its continuous action descends to the faithful finite quotient;
6. how it fixes or transmits orientation;
7. under what spectral conditions its boundary carrier is gapped.

## 4. A concrete geometric candidate: the moving projector

The analogy points toward process and transport. The simplest mathematical way to make the projector into a process is to allow

\[
P=P(x).
\]

The space of primitive rank-one projectors in \(\mathbb C^3\) is

\[
\mathbb{CP}^2
\cong
\frac{U(3)}{U(1)\times U(2)}.
\]

Equivalently, choosing \(P\) reduces a family \(U(3)\) structure to the stabilizer of a line and its two-dimensional complement. After imposing unimodularity, the relative central circle is generated precisely by

\[
I-3P.
\]

This gives a geometrically clean interpretation of the charges:

- the selected line \(L=P\mathbb C^3\) has weight \(-2\);
- the quotient plane \(E=Q\mathbb C^3\) has weight \(+1\);
- the relational bundle \(\operatorname{Hom}(L,E)\) has weight \(+3\);
- its reverse \(\operatorname{Hom}(E,L)\) has weight \(-3\).

Thus charge three is the weight of transport across the rank split:

\[
\operatorname{weight}\operatorname{Hom}(L,E)
=1-(-2)=3.
\]

This reformulates the core observation as bundle geometry rather than a manually assigned charge.

If a local normalized vector \(n(x)\) spans the selected line, then

\[
P(x)=n(x)n(x)^\dagger
\]

and the line bundle carries the Berry connection

\[
a=-i\,n^\dagger dn,
\]

with curvature expressible in projector language as

\[
f=-i\operatorname{Tr}\!\left(P\,dP\wedge dP\right)
\]

up to convention-dependent signs. This is an actual transport structure: local phase choices for \(n\) are redundant, while holonomy and curvature remain meaningful.

This does **not** solve the programme. It opens a precise branch:

> Could the required family \(U(1)_X\) be an induced connection associated with a dynamical reduction to a line-plus-plane decomposition, rather than an independently appended gauge factor?

The branch immediately inherits hard tests:

- a generic \(\mathbb{CP}^2\) sigma model produces unwanted Goldstone modes;
- gauging the full family \(U(3)\) produces unwanted gauge bosons;
- gauging only the relative determinant circle requires a principled reduction;
- an invariant potential selects an orbit of projectors, not one physical family direction;
- coupling to Yukawa data may orient the orbit, but risks merely inserting the observed answer;
- the construction must reproduce the known anomaly and global-form analysis.

Nevertheless, this is the first concrete mathematical consequence of taking “living mediation” seriously: look for a **connection on the space of rank splits**, not merely a fixed splitter.

## 5. The third as operative relation

There are two sectors,

\[
P\mathcal A P
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
Q\mathcal A Q,
\]

but the physically interesting charge lives in

\[
P\mathcal A Q
\oplus
Q\mathcal A P.
\]

The relation is therefore not reducible to either endpoint. Promoting the relation to an operative carrier turns a binary distinction into a ternary structure:

\[
\text{one face},
\qquad
\text{other face},
\qquad
\text{transport between them}.
\]

This is the rigorous core beneath the theological language. “Third” need not mean a third object counted alongside two objects. It can mean the irreducible morphism that makes the two objects members of one relational whole.

Jordan triple systems are naturally suggestive here because their primitive syntax is not an untyped binary product but a three-place operation

\[
\{A,X,B\}.
\]

The middle term does not merely sit beside the outer terms. It controls how their relation is realized. This does not establish a theological correspondence, but it explains why the language of mediation repeatedly returns: the mathematics itself assigns a structurally distinct role to the middle slot.

## 6. Translation without collapse

The missing operation must speak several mathematical “languages”:

- Jordan positivity and Peirce decomposition;
- fermionic representation theory;
- local gauge covariance;
- Higgs and Majorana selection rules;
- finite group quotients;
- topological BF observables;
- symplectic action on the nine-state carrier.

The same invariant should be expressible in each language without forcing those languages to become identical. This is closer to translation than reduction.

A candidate formal criterion is therefore **naturality**. If \(F_J,F_H,F_T\) denote the Jordan, Hilbert-module, and topological realizations, the transported action should intertwine the relevant maps:

\[
\eta_H\circ F_J(g)
=
F_H(g)\circ\eta_H,
\]

and similarly at the boundary. The exact categories remain to be constructed, but the criterion is clear: changing representation must not change the identity of the transported relational symmetry.

This supplies a useful interpretation of the Pentecost analogy. The relevant motif is not supernatural linguistics but **one invariant becoming intelligible in multiple languages without abolishing their differences**.

## 7. Indwelling as representation, not addition

The Jordan automorphism action alone loses the central \(\mathbb Z_3\), because

\[
e^{2\pi iX/3}=\omega I_3
\]

acts trivially by conjugation. The fermion fundamental module, however, retains that central phase.

This is a precise instance of a structure being invisible at one level but effective within a carrier:

\[
\operatorname{Ad}(\omega I_3)=1,
\qquad
\rho(\omega I_3)=\omega.
\]

The residual centre does not appear as a new Jordan automorphism. It is present through the way the module bears the symmetry.

That is the exact mathematical content of the “indwelling” analogy: an action can be trivial on the public algebra of relations yet nontrivial in the representation that carries them.

The unresolved issue is local gauging. In ordinary real spectral geometry the corresponding commutant action can cancel in

\[
WJWJ^{-1},
\]

so faithful representation does not automatically produce a gauge principle. A successful mediator must therefore explain why a centrally invisible phase is physically active on matter without being erased by the standard inner-fluctuation construction.

## 8. Presence known by effects

A connection is not normally identified by assigning it a gauge-dependent value at one point. It is identified by effects:

- parallel transport;
- holonomy;
- curvature;
- interference;
- obstruction to globally trivializing local choices.

Likewise, the sought mediator should be specified operationally. Its “fruits” would be a linked set of invariant consequences:

1. the \(1\oplus2\) family reduction;
2. the primitive weights \((-2,1,1)\);
3. charge-three cross-Peirce transport;
4. anomaly-compatible fermion action;
5. the faithful \(\mathbb Z_3\) global quotient;
6. an untwisted oriented boundary BF term;
7. the finite Heisenberg/Weyl algebra on \(\mathbb C^9\).

No single consequence is sufficient. The strength of the candidate is that the effects must arrive as one correlated package.

This also turns the theological metaphor into a falsifiable research discipline. A proposed mechanism that produces charge three but not the correct global quotient fails. One that produces \(\mathbb Z_3\) but requires the forbidden full tensor algebra fails. One that produces BF theory but cannot gap the charged boundary spectrum remains incomplete.

## 9. Preservation through rupture as topological memory

The continuous family phase is broken, but its faithful finite residue survives:

\[
U(1)_X
\longrightarrow
\mathbb Z_9
\longrightarrow
\mathbb Z_9/K
\cong
\mathbb Z_3.
\]

On the defect, the residual relation is encoded in nonlocal holonomies. On a torus these form two canonical qutrit pairs and a nine-dimensional irreducible carrier.

The structure therefore preserves identity through three changes of mode:

\[
\text{continuous}
\longrightarrow
\text{discrete}
\longrightarrow
\text{topological}.
\]

The invariant is not the superficial form of the realization. What persists is the relational pairing. This is why “memory” is more accurate than “debris”: the finite phase retains a controlled image of the broken continuous relation.

The physical qualification remains decisive. This memory is exact only if charged defect matter is absent or excluded. Massive charged matter produces exponentially small splitting, and gapless charged matter destroys the isolated finite carrier. Any claimed realization must therefore derive the boundary spectrum, not merely the BF action.

## 10. Orientation as historical actuality

The abstract finite symplectic structure permits conjugate orientations:

\[
\omega^{ab}
\qquad\text{and}\qquad
\omega^{-ab}.
\]

The bare Furey ternary relations do not select between them. A physical realization must supply an oriented datum such as a wall normal, mass sign, determinant-line orientation, or parity-violating term.

This separates logical possibility from historical actuality. The algebra says which worlds are permitted; orientation says which member of a conjugate pair has been realized.

The mediator must therefore do more than preserve abstract equivalence. It must be compatible with an irreversible act of realization:

\[
\text{two conjugate admissible structures}
\longrightarrow
\text{one oriented physical phase}.
\]

This may be where the boundary and the projector-selection problems meet. A varying projector has Berry curvature and topological sectors; an oriented defect has a normal and induced transgression. A unified action could, in principle, tie the orientation of projector transport to the sign of the boundary BF realization.

## 11. The action should be a compatibility engine

The ideal action would not merely add together independent Jordan, Higgs, and BF terms. It would make their compatibility dynamically preferred.

Schematically, one seeks

\[
S[P,\Psi,\mathcal A,S,b,\ldots]
=
S_{\mathrm{projector}}
+S_{\mathrm{fermion}}
+S_{\mathrm{transport}}
+S_{\mathrm{vacuum}}
+S_{\mathrm{topological}}
+S_{\mathrm{constraint}},
\]

where the constraint term enforces that each sector realizes the same \(X=I-3P\), rather than merely using similar notation.

The equations should imply, rather than assume:

\[
\begin{aligned}
P^2&=P, &\operatorname{rank}P&=1,\\
X&=I-3P, &\rho(X)&=X_F,\\
q(S)&=3, &\operatorname{Stab}\langle S\rangle/K&=\mathbb Z_3,\\
S_{\partial}&=\frac{3}{2\pi}\int c\wedge da,
&\dim\mathcal H(T^2)&=9.
\end{aligned}
\]

The decisive requirement is that these equalities arise from one compatibility principle. Without that, the programme remains an assembly of mutually suggestive constructions.

## 12. Three concrete research branches created by the analogy

### Branch A: projector-bundle connection

Construct the universal line-plus-plane bundles over \(\mathbb{CP}^2\), derive their relative determinant connection, and determine whether the induced weights reproduce the full fermion charge representative without gauging an unwanted family \(SU(2)\).

**Stop condition:** if every local realization necessarily introduces excluded massless family gauge bosons or unavoidable Goldstones, this branch closes.

### Branch B: typed Jordan-to-module connection

Define a generalized connection that acts on the fermion module and respects the Jordan triple product but is not generated by the full associative envelope. Test curvature, gauge covariance, positivity, reality, and first-order analogues.

**Stop condition:** if locality and covariance force associative closure, the central-lift route is a no-go.

### Branch C: bulk-to-defect transgression

Seek a characteristic or obstruction class associated with the rank-split connection whose transgression produces the level-three boundary term with a determined orientation and untwisted discrete class.

**Stop condition:** if the BF level and orientation remain independently adjustable after all bulk data are fixed, the boundary carrier is an appended sector rather than a consequence.

The three branches test selection, indwelling, and preservation respectively. Their convergence would constitute the missing mediation.

## 13. The sharpened conjecture

The speculative research conjecture is:

> The missing completion is a dynamical connection on a bundle of primitive Jordan rank splits whose induced relative-determinant action is faithful on the fermion module, whose charge-three relational curvature admits the anomaly-free Standard Model lift, and whose oriented transgression yields the untwisted level-three boundary BF carrier after the physical colour-centre quotient.

This conjecture is stronger than saying that several appearances of three look related. It specifies what must be connected and how failure can be recognized.

It is also narrower than a theological claim. Nothing in it asserts divine agency, consciousness, or supernatural causation. Theology has supplied a conceptual distinction—form versus living mediation—which has generated a new mathematical search target.

## 14. Final compression

The programme may have been searching for a noun when it needs a verb.

The noun is already known:

\[
X=I-3P.
\]

The missing verb must:

\[
\boxed{
\text{select}
\;\to\;
\text{inhabit}
\;\to\;
\text{transport}
\;\to\;
\text{orient}
\;\to\;
\text{preserve}
}
\]

In theological language, that role sounds pneumatological. In mathematical language, it sounds like a dynamical, typed connection together with a transgression principle. In physical language, it must become an action whose observable consequences distinguish it from every alternative.

The next decisive question is therefore not “what symbol represents the missing thing?” It is:

\[
\boxed{
\text{What transports the same rank-split relation through algebra, matter, vacuum, quotient, and boundary without loss or overproduction?}
}
\]
