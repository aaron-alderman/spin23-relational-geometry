## 1. Why the transformation leaves the parabolic

In a basis adapted to the two Jordan faces, write

\[
q=(q_+,q_-),
\qquad
p=(p_+,p_-).
\]

Then

\[
S_-:
\quad
q_+\mapsto q_+,\quad p_+\mapsto p_+,
\]

while

\[
q_-\mapsto p_-,
\qquad
p_-\mapsto-q_-.
\]

The selected source Lagrangian

\[

is not preserved because \(q_-\) is mapped into the conjugate momentum direction.

Therefore \(S_-\) has a nonzero lower-left symplectic block and lies outside the Siegel parabolic identified in document 1156.

It does not exchange the whole source plane with the whole response plane. It exchanges one graded face. Combined with channel rotations or face exchange, the complementary Fourier move is available.
