## Executive result

The odd-weight issue found in document 1162 is not a no-go. It is a boundary between two formulations.

A current metaplectic formulation defines vector-valued Siegel Eisenstein series for any weight compatible with the Weil central character. In genus two, the \(A_2\) discriminant carrier therefore admits the exact nine-component coset sum

\[
E^{3}_{2,A_2}(Z)
=
\sum_{\gamma\in\widetilde P_S(\mathbb Z)\backslash Mp(4,\mathbb Z)}
\phi_\gamma(Z)^{-6}
\rho_{A_2,2}(\gamma)^{-1}\mathfrak e_{(0,0)}.
\]

It is well defined because the weight-three automorphy phase exactly cancels the action of the lifted Siegel parabolic on the zero discriminant vector.

The new obstruction is analytic rather than algebraic:

\[
\boxed{k=3=g+1.}
\]

The standard absolute-convergence result for the holomorphic genus-\(g\) Siegel Eisenstein series requires

\[
k>g+1.
\]

Thus weight three lies precisely on the boundary. The first compatible odd weight with unconditional holomorphic absolute convergence is

\[
\boxed{k=5.}
\]

The weight-three nonholomorphic spectral deformation exists in an initial half-plane, but its meromorphic continuation and functional equation must be proved in the metaplectic odd-weight setting rather than borrowed from the even-weight theorem.

## 1. Correction to the previous frontier

Document 1162 correctly found that the theorem of Stein used there assumes even weight and therefore cannot be specialized verbatim to \(A_2\). But the stronger interpretation—“the odd-weight series may not exist”—is unnecessary.

The general metaplectic framework uses

\[
Mp(4,\mathbb Z)
\]

rather than forcing the Weil representation to descend to \(Sp(4,\mathbb Z)\). For a lattice of signature \(\sigma\), the modular weight is required to obey the central-character condition

\[
2k\equiv\sigma\pmod4

\]

up to the sign convention for the chosen representation or its dual.

For \(A_2\),

\[
\sigma=2,
\]

so

\[
k\equiv1\pmod2.
\]

Weight three is therefore not anomalous. It is the smallest compatible weight meeting the genus-two lower bound \(k\ge3\).

## 2. The nine-component coefficient space remains exact

The discriminant module is

\[
D=A_2^*/A_2\cong\mathbb Z/3.
\]

At genus two, the Weil representation acts on

\[
\mathbb C[D^2]
=\mathbb C[(\mathbb Z/3)^2],
\]

whose basis is

\[
\mathfrak e_{(a,b)},
\qquad a,b\in\mathbb Z/3.

Therefore

\[
\dim\mathbb C[D^2]=9.
\]

No lattice doubling or 81-component enlargement is needed to define the correct metaplectic object.

## 3. Generator formulas

The integral metaplectic group is generated by lifted Fourier transformations, symmetric translations and Levi changes of basis.

For a symmetric integral matrix \(B\),

\[
T_B=
\left(
\begin{pmatrix}I&B\\0&I\end{pmatrix},1
\right)
\]

acts by

\[
\rho(T_B)\mathfrak e_\alpha
=e\!\left(\operatorname{tr}(q(\alpha)B)\right)
\mathfrak e_\alpha.
\]

The full Fourier generator acts by a finite Fourier transform on \(D^2\), multiplied by the signature-dependent eighth root.

For

\[
R_A=
\left(
\begin{pmatrix}A&0\\0&A^{-T}\end{pmatrix},
(\det A)^{1/2}
\right),
\qquad A\in GL(2,\mathbb Z),
\]

the zero vector is an eigenvector:

\[
\rho(R_A)\mathfrak e_0
=(\det A)^{-\sigma/2}\mathfrak e_0.
\]

For \(\sigma=2\),

\[
\rho(R_A)\mathfrak e_0
=(\det A)^{-1}\mathfrak e_0.
\]

## 4. Exact parabolic cancellation at weight three

The Siegel parabolic is generated by the translations \(T_B\) and the Levi elements \(R_A\).

### Translations

Since

\[
q(0)=0,
\]

we have

\[
\rho(T_B)\mathfrak e_0=\mathfrak e_0.
\]

The seed is translation invariant.

### Orientation-preserving Levi transformations

If \(\det A=1\), both the weight and Weil phases are one on \(\mathfrak e_0\).

### Orientation-reversing Levi transformations

If \(\det A=-1\), choose \(\phi_A^2=-1\). For \(k=3\),

\[
\phi_A^{2k}
=\phi_A^6
=-1.
\]

Meanwhile,

\[
(\det A)^{-\sigma/2}
=(-1)^{-1}
=-1.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{
\phi_A^6
=
\rho(R_A)|_{\mathfrak e_0}.
}
\]

The two signs cancel in the slash action. Changing the metaplectic sheet sends \(\phi_A\mapsto-\phi_A\), but

\[
(-\phi_A)^6=\phi_A^6,
\]

so the coset summand is sheet-independent at this stage.

This proves that the weight-three zero seed descends through the lifted Siegel parabolic.

## 6. The convergence boundary

For a genus-\(g\) Siegel Eisenstein series arising from the zero-dimensional cusp, the standard holomorphic absolute-convergence condition is

\[
k>g+1.
\]

Here

\[
g=2,
\qquad k=3,
\]

so equality holds rather than strict inequality.

Therefore:

- the weight-three coset expression is well defined algebraically;
- absolute convergence of the holomorphic series is not supplied by the standard theorem;
- a nonholomorphic spectral parameter provides an initial convergence region;
- reaching the holomorphic value requires analytic continuation or regularization.

The next compatible odd weight is \(k=5\), and

\[
5>3.
\]

Thus

\[
E^{5}_{2,A_2}(Z)
\]

is the first unambiguously absolutely convergent holomorphic witness in the same nine-dimensional Weil representation.

Weight five proves that the metaplectic carrier and its parabolic sum are not empty. Weight three remains distinguished as the minimal—and analytically critical—case.

## 7. A canonical invariant scalar already exists, but it is quadratic

Suppose a vector-valued section transforms as

\[
E(\gamma Z)
=\phi_\gamma(Z)^{2k}
\rho(\gamma)E(Z),
\]

with \(\rho\) unitary. Then

\[
\|E(\gamma Z)\|^2
=|\det(CZ+D)|^{2k}\|E(Z)\|^2.
\]

Also,

\[
\det(\operatorname{Im}(\gamma Z))
=\frac{\det(Y)}{|\det(CZ+D)|^2}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal I_k(Z)
=
\det(Y)^k\|E(Z)\|^2
}
\]

is invariant.

For the minimal \(A_2\) series,

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal I_3(Z,s)
=
\det(Y)^3
\left\|E^{3}_{2,A_2}(Z,s)\right\|^2.
}
\]

This gives the programme a canonical scalar intensity or power-like observable without choosing a preferred component or an external dual section.

But it is quadratic. It loses the phase information required for a linear scattering amplitude and is not automatically Herglotz. A linear physical response still requires a covariantly transforming dual probe.

## 8. Interpretation of weight three

Weight three should not yet be assigned a physical meaning merely because it is minimal. Mathematically it compensates the metaplectic central character of the \(A_2\) discriminant module.

Possible physical interpretations to test include:

- three units of determinant-line charge;
- a cubic density or three-form-like response weight;
- the transformation weight of a three-relationship composite;
- a Jacobian carried by the measure converting the nine-vector into a scalar observable.

None is presently derived. The safe statement is:

\[
\boxed{
k=3\text{ is forced by automorphic compatibility at the minimal genus-two weight, not yet by dynamics.}
}
\]
