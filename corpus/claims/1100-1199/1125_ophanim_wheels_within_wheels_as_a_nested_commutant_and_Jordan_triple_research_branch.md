## Executive result

“Wheels within wheels” is a better mathematical metaphor for the surviving branch than “add another point.”

The [direct-sum construction](1124_the_exact_four_direct_sum_has_a_two_sided_slot_obstruction_to_decorating_physical_SM_fermions.md) placed the family structure beside the Standard-Model algebra. That created new Krajewski rows and columns but could not attach an independent family label to a physical bimodule whose left and right gauge supports were already occupied.

The nested alternative is

\[
H_{ij}^{\rm phys}
\longmapsto
H_{ij}^{\rm phys}\otimes F,
\qquad F\cong\mathbb C^3,
\]

with the internal algebra acting on the physical carrier and the family algebra acting inside its multiplicity commutant:

\[
\pi_L(a)=\pi_i(a)\otimes I_F,
\qquad
\pi_R(b^\circ)=\pi_j^\circ(b)\otimes I_F,
\qquad
\rho_F(f)=I_{ij}\otimes f.
\]

Then

\[
[\rho_F(f),\pi_L(a)]=0,
\qquad
[\rho_F(f),\pi_R(b^\circ)]=0,
\]

while a family-sensitive finite Dirac operator can satisfy

\[
[D_F,\rho_F(f)]\ne0.
\]

The family wheel therefore turns *inside* the physical gauge correspondence without consuming either of its two support slots.

This is an exact finite-dimensional escape from the two-sided-slot obstruction. It is not yet an ordinary spectral-triple solution, because a choice remains: either promote the family action into the coordinate algebra and accept the tensor-product gauge/character consequences, or retain it as a commutant/Jordan relational algebra and state the generalized axioms explicitly.

## 1. Translation of the image

The Ophanim phrase is used here as a heuristic, not as evidence or historical identification. Its useful mathematical content is nested action:

- the **outer wheel** is the physical internal bimodule carrying left and opposite Standard-Model actions;
- the **inner wheel** is the family/multiplicity algebra acting in the commutant of both;
- the **axle** is the finite Dirac operator, which couples the two layers because it need not commute with the family action;
- the **turning relation** is the represented one-form \([D_F,\rho_F(f)]\);
- “eyes” can be read cautiously as observable projections or states, but this part is metaphor only and carries no theorem.

The key change is ontological. Family is not another location beside weak and color. It is an internal relational action on the multiplicity of a gauge correspondence.

## 2. Exact tensor-factor witness

Take a physical bimodule carrier

\[
H_{ij}=V_i\otimes V_j^*,
\]

and introduce a three-dimensional family space \(F\). On

\[
\widetilde H_{ij}=V_i\otimes V_j^*\otimes F,
\]

define

\[
L(a)=\pi_i(a)\otimes I\otimes I_F,
\]

\[
R(b)=I\otimes\pi_j(b)^T\otimes I_F,
\]

\[
\rho_F(f)=I\otimes I\otimes\operatorname{diag}(f_P,f_I,f_N).
\]

These actions obey

\[
[L(a),R(b)]=[L(a),\rho_F(f)]=[R(b),\rho_F(f)]=0.
\]

Both physical algebra slots remain intact. Nevertheless, for

\[
D_F=T_{ij}\otimes K_F,
\]

one obtains

\[
[D_F,\rho_F(f)]
=T_{ij}\otimes[K_F,f],
\]

which is nonzero whenever the family kernel \(K_F\) connects distinct character eigenspaces.

This is the smallest precise meaning of a wheel turning within another wheel: mutually commuting carrier actions joined dynamically by a noncommuting relational operator.

## 3. Why this evades the direct-sum obstruction

The direct-sum model tried to encode a physical state by three central supports \((i,j;f)\), which an ordinary bimodule cannot carry.

The nested model instead uses

\[
(i,j)\otimes f.
\]

Here \((i,j)\) remains the internal bimodule support, while \(f\) is an endomorphism of its multiplicity space. It is not a third direct-sum support. Consequently:

- weak and color actions are retained;
- the same family projector can act on every chosen physical sector;
- Dirac and Majorana blocks may use different family kernels or different opposite anchors;
- family one-forms can be nonzero without adding scalar-scalar matter cells.

This is exactly the structural capacity the null-mask problem needs.

## 4. The unavoidable fork

### 4.1 Promote the inner wheel to an associative coordinate algebra

If \(\rho_F(\mathbb C^3)\) is declared part of the spectral-triple algebra, the combined action is effectively tensorial. Then the real-algebra decomposition, character doubling, gauge fields, unimodularity and anomalies must be calculated. The six-character result in [1123](1123_only_the_direct_sum_reduced_SM_plus_C3_has_the_four_characters_but_it_makes_S_a_genuine_algebra_extension.md) cannot be bypassed by notation.

### 4.2 Retain the inner wheel as relational commutant data

If the family algebra is instead an auxiliary represented algebra in

\[
\operatorname{End}_{\mathcal A_{\rm int}-\mathcal A_{\rm int}^\circ}(H_F),
\]

it does not automatically generate a new gauge factor. But the construction is no longer an ordinary single-algebra finite spectral triple. One must specify:

- which axioms the auxiliary action obeys;
- how reality and grading act on it;
- whether its one-forms enter the spectral action;
- what replaces or generalizes first order;
- how junk is defined for mixed internal/family forms;
- why radiative corrections preserve the distinction.

This is the honest boundary between an associative extension and a relational/Jordan extension.

## 5. Jordan-triple interpretation

Spaces of operators between bimodule sectors naturally carry a ternary product

\[
\{x,y,z\}
=\frac12\left(xy^*z+zy^*x\right).
\]

Unlike an ordinary binary product, this composition remembers:

1. a source carrier;
2. a mediating relation;
3. a target carrier.

That is the algebraic content of the project’s phrase **relational-carrier and Jordan-triple composition**. A relation is not forced to become another point of the coordinate algebra. It may live as an operator space between carrier sectors, closed under ternary composition.

The nested picture is therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{internal bimodule carrier}
\supset
\text{family commutant wheel}
\supset
\text{Jordan-triple relation space}.
}
\]

This is a plausible categorical home for the earlier “\(A\,X\,B\)” intuition: \(A\) and \(B\) specify carrier faces, while \(X\) is a composable relation whose closure is ternary rather than an indiscriminate outer product.

## 6. Relation to \(Spin(2,3)\)

The project already has an exact local result:

\[
H_2(\mathbb R)
\quad\text{has conformal algebra}\quad
\mathfrak{sp}_4(\mathbb R)\cong\mathfrak{spin}(2,3).
\]

The new nested-carrier result does not prove that every commutant wheel closes to \(H_2(\mathbb R)\). It identifies where such a Jordan carrier could live: inside the multiplicity endomorphisms of a physical gauge bimodule, rather than as an extra direct-sum spacetime point.

The next constructive test is therefore precise:

\[
\text{sector-dependent null mask}
\stackrel{?}{\subset}
\text{a real }H_2(\mathbb R)\text{ Jordan slice of the family commutant}.
\]

If this inclusion exists and is stable under the mixed one-form/junk calculus, it would connect the physical null-mask problem to \(Sp(4,\mathbb R)\cong Spin_0(2,3)\) without pretending that \(Spin(2,3)\) is another gauge summand.

## 7. Stop-everything criteria

**Metaphor stop:** Ophanim language is a mnemonic for nested actions, not evidence for a physical or theological claim.

**Hidden-tensor stop:** if the family commutant is promoted to the coordinate algebra, restore the full real-tensor character and gauge analysis.

**Generation-blind stop:** a multiplicity space with no represented family action and no family-sensitive \(D_F\) does not solve the null-mask problem.

**Axiom stop:** do not call the auxiliary commutant construction a spectral triple until reality, grading, first order or its replacement, orientability and junk are defined.

**Spin stop:** do not infer \(Spin(2,3)\) from nesting alone; exhibit the real Jordan slice and its conformal action.

**Positive trigger:** construct a real-even nested representation whose Jordan-triple relation space realizes the Majorana/Dirac mask, survives junk reduction and contains the established \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) carrier.

## 8. Verdict

The phrase “wheels within wheels” names the correct surviving architecture:

\[
\boxed{
\text{not more points beside the physical algebra,}
\quad
\text{but another action inside its relational commutant.}
}
\]

This converts the direct-sum stop into a constructive programme. The next step is to build the sector-dependent null mask inside the multiplicity commutant and test whether its self-adjoint relation space is the already identified \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) Jordan carrier.
