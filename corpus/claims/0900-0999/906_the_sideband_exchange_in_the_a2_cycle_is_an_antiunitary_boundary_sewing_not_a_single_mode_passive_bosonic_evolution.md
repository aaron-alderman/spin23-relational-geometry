## 1. Bosonic Nambu no-go

Let

\[

\]

Its canonical commutators are encoded by

\[
[\Psi_B,\Psi_B^\dagger]=\eta_B.
\]

A bosonic Bogoliubov matrix $M$ must satisfy

\[
M^\dagger\eta_BM=\eta_B.
\]

The pure exchange instead reverses the metric. It maps annihilation directly to creation without the amplification required by a legitimate bosonic squeeze transformation.

Finite squeezing gives

\[
u=\cosh r,
\qquad
v=e^{i\phi}\sinh r,

and never reaches $u=0$. Consequently the exact exchange is not a finite stable limit of one-mode squeezing.
