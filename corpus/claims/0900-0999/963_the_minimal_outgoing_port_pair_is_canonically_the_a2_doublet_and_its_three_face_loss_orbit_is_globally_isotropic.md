\[

\[

## 3. Local anisotropy and global consistency

Every symmetric two-dimensional form decomposes as scalar plus trace-free spin-two part. Averaging a trace-free form over the three (120^\circ) rotations annihilates it. Hence

\[
\sum_{k=0}^2
\left(B_k-\frac{\operatorname{tr}B_0}{2}I\right)=0.

- a face locally breaks (S_3) to one reflection and distinguishes two channels;

This statement is exact linear algebra. It is not yet a statistical-mechanical derivation or a cubic-norm theorem.

- the positive coupling square root is reflection covariant;

## 5. Stop-everything and falsification criteria

**Jordan trigger:** a quadratic representation (P(x)) or triple product produces (B_0) and transports it by the same (C_3) orbit.

**Carrier trigger:** one microscopic field supplies both the placement amplitudes and outgoing coupling (G_k(\omega)) with the established (S_3) covariance.

**Cubic trigger:** the orbit-sum scalar is recovered as a Hessian or polarization of the candidate cubic norm.

**Typing falsifier:** the proposed two-port environment has trivial (C_3) while being claimed to realize three distinct face placements.

**Ontology warning:** equality of (S_3) representations does not prove equality of physical degrees of freedom.
