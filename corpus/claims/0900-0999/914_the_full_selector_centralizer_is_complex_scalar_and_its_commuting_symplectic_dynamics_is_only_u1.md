## 2. Symplectic part of the commutant

The compatibility identities imply

\[
\mathcal I^TJ\mathcal I=J,
\qquad
\mathcal I^TJ+J\mathcal I=0.

\[
(aI+b\mathcal I)^TJ(aI+b\mathcal I)
=(a^2+b^2)J.
