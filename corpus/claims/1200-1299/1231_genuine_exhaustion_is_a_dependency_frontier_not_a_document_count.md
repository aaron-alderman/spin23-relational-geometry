## Result

The seven current blockers are not seven independent questions. For the present candidate action they form this workflow graph:

\[
A2\longrightarrow A4\longrightarrow A7,
\qquad
(A4,A5)\longrightarrow A6,
\qquad
(A6,A7)\longrightarrow A9,
\qquad

These edges mix logical prerequisites, present-model dependencies and research-prioritization choices. They are not a theorem that every analogue experiment or alternative chiral construction must follow the same order.

The programme should therefore not allocate equal effort to every missing gate. Its present dependency roots are the compact defect descent (A2), physical-state construction (A4), and physical selector (A5). The stress operator A7 can be developed conditionally while A4 is open. The Standard-Model limit A6, physical response A9 and branch experiment A11 cannot be completed by additional response geometry alone.

This gives a finite meaning to **genuine exhaustion**. A lane is exhausted only when every currently admissible route has reached one of four endpoints:

1. a derivation with declared assumptions and executable checks;
2. a falsification or no-go theorem with its assumption boundary;
3. a unique smallest resolving calculation;
4. an external-input boundary naming the measurement, coefficient, action or expert judgment unavailable inside the corpus.

Document count, repeated analogy and additional group embeddings are not exhaustion criteria.

## Three layers of blockers

### Layer I — construction roots

- **A2:** derive the compactification, defect orientation and gapped charged spectrum rather than assuming the BF reduction.
- **A4:** choose a vacuum and coefficients, construct the gauge-fixed quadratic theory, identify the physical cohomology and prove positive residues on specified domains.
- **A5:** supply a transforming asymmetry or history-bearing selector and show that the selected chiral module retains anomaly cancellation and nonzero CP violation.

These gates require new physical structure. Downstream calculations cannot decide them retroactively.

### Layer II — matching and observable definition

- **A7:** vary the completed renormalized action with respect to the metric, including improvement and contact prescriptions, and verify the Ward identities.
- **A6:** integrate out the declared heavy sector and match the surviving fields and couplings to the observed chiral Standard Model.
- **A9:** calculate the renormalized spin-two and spin-zero spectral densities for fixed physical probes in that matched theory.

These gates are large but conventional calculations once Layer I supplies a definite theory.

### Layer III — discrimination

- **A11:** prepare controlled sectors and test for a reproducible response outside the conventional rank-two plane. The newly identified charged-matter bridge is necessary but not sufficient: it must be derived from A2, inherit a positive physical state from A4, use the A7/A9 operator definition, and produce a powered effect.

A11 is therefore the visible end of the chain, not its independent foundation.

## Exhaustion rules

For each gate, the machine-readable frontier records:

- the first missing datum;
- the smallest resolving calculation;
- a decisive falsifier;
- which work can proceed conditionally;
- which downstream gates must not be promoted from it.

A gate may be marked **internally exhausted** when every route that uses only present corpus inputs has reached a derivation or bounded no-go. It remains **externally open** when a named experiment, complete action, numerical coefficient or specialist judgment could change the verdict. This distinction prevents both endless internal elaboration and premature claims of impossibility.
