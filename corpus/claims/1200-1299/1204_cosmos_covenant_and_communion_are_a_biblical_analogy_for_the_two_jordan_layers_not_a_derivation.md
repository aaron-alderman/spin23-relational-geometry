# Cosmos, covenant and communion are a biblical analogy for the two Jordan layers, not a derivation

## Classification

This document records an interpretive analogy. It is not a scientific derivation, a theological proof, or evidence for the physical programme.

The proposed correspondence is:

\[
\begin{aligned}
H_2(\mathbb R)/Spin_0(2,3)
&\longleftrightarrow
\text{cosmos: order, manifestation and consequence},\\
H_3(\mathbb C)/\text{Peirce geometry}
&\longleftrightarrow
\text{communion: name, election and relationship},\\
\text{typed mediation between them}
&\longleftrightarrow
\text{covenant or Logos: relational identity embodied in action}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The analogy is attractive because the mathematical frontier and the theological motif have the same abstract shape: universal order does not by itself select a particular person or family direction. In the mathematical construction, a rank-one projector cannot orient itself, and the protected \(\mathbb Z_3\) quotient forgets which projector produced it. In the analogy, law preserves form while calling or election supplies orientation.

No arrow in the scientific programme is justified by this analogy. In particular, the terms *Logos*, *covenant*, *election* and *recognition by fruits* do not provide a family selector, a Lagrangian term, a spectrum, or empirical evidence.

The valid use is explanatory:

\[
\boxed{
\text{creation supplies order; election supplies orientation; covenant joins them.}
}
\]
