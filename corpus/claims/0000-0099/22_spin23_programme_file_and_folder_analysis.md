# Spin(2,3) programme analysis

**Scope:** `candidate papers/spin2_3`, 365 files and approximately 304,000 words of text/code.  
**Conclusion:** this is the corpus's operational centre. It contains a legitimate reduced-model research programme, but not a completed fundamental theory.

## Root files

### `README.md`

The correct entry point. It explains that core, kernels, research, papers, checks and archive have different authority. Its most important contribution is its reading rule: mathematical facts, branch choices and physical interpretations must not be merged.

### `CLAIM_LEDGER.md`

The main prose claim ledger. It is more useful than the compendium for scientific assessment because it makes claims atomisable and status-bearing. It should become generated from structured facts rather than remain a parallel manually maintained authority.

### `ROADMAP.md`

An execution-oriented list of missing tasks. It is useful operationally but ages quickly. Any roadmap item that later received an SRP disposition should link to that successor rather than remain apparently open.

### `SCIENCE_RESEARCH_PROGRAMME_DESIGN.md`

The constitution for fact governance, branch worlds, method portfolios, adversarial review and human admission. This is among the strongest files in the corpus. It correctly states that tool reproducibility, document polish and internal coherence do not amount to scientific truth.

### `ASSOC_SPIN23_OVERLAP.md`

An association/overlap note. It presently contains three broken file references. It should be treated as a navigation/relationship document, not a physics result.

## `core`

### `core/master-framework.md`

Organises the programme into statics, dynamics, epistemics, consistency, interpretation, phenomenology and open problems. It explicitly admits that direct experimental predictions are missing. This is the best architectural summary of the reduced model.

The central objects are a four-component carrier, a grading/branch split, a preferred octonionic direction and projected observables. The framework's main burden is not writing these objects down; it is deriving why this carrier, selector, orientation and readout are physically selected.

### `core/ambient-reduction-scaffold.md`

The largest core workbench. It proposes parent-to-effective, bulk-to-transport and observable-bridge stages. It contains useful explicit intertwiner and basis calculations, but also documents the decisive limitation: the toy parent operators are chosen so their reduced image has the desired matrices. The parent-side canonical origin remains missing.

This file should be split eventually into:

1. admitted exact matrix/intertwiner results;
2. candidate parent realisations;
3. unresolved physical selector/readout requirements.

### `core/parent-inquiry-map.md`

Ranks candidate parents and ideas by plausibility and beauty. Its ranking is a research heuristic, not evidence. Claims involving the 225-dimensional relation space, generation ladders or a toric resolution of colour should inherit the later SRP14 “no parent winner” status.

### `core/spin23-compendium.md`

A wide application guide. It is helpful as a vocabulary map but contains some of the older layer's most dangerous verbs: “derives internally” can mean “is realised inside the chosen reduced model,” not “is derived from a physical action.” Its mass, uncertainty, generation and topological sections must be read through the later claim ledger and SRP results.

The compendium is best retained as historical/interpretive documentation and not used as the canonical statement of current warrant.

## `kernels`

The 33 kernel files are the right modularisation strategy. They fall into six groups.

### Foundational kernels

- `statics.md`: fixes carrier, generators, grading and representation data.
- `dynamics.md`: introduces effective evolution laws; these are conditional models unless derived from an action.
- `epistemics.md`: defines projected/readout observables; the observable map remains an independent physical burden.
- `consistency.md`: checks algebraic and probabilistic compatibility.
- `interpretation.md`: assigns physical meaning and therefore carries a lower authority ceiling.
- `phenomenology.md`: collects possible external consequences but presently lacks a calibrated empirical map.
- `open-problems.md`: should be preferred over optimistic conclusions elsewhere.

### Topology and discrete symmetry

- `topological.md`, `diii-anomaly-bridge.md` and `discrete-symmetries.md` establish classifying data for chosen Hamiltonians and symmetry operations.
- `orientation-rule-upgrades.md`, `orientation-d1-bulk-stability.md` and `w3-kappa-sign-correlation.md` investigate the unresolved sign/orientation problem.
- `kappa_u-moment-map-lemma.md` identifies an exchange-odd coupling slot inside the model. This is a structural uniqueness statement, not proof that nature uses the slot.

### Observable/selector descent

- `u-selector-bracketing.md` correctly separates fixed, dynamical and mixed readings of the selected octonionic direction.
- `upstream-selector-program.md` defines the central missing-middle research programme.
- `even-sector-observable-projector-descent.md` and `odd-sector-epsilon-channel.md` test specific descent routes.

The later SRP8 and SRP17 results supersede any suggestion here that the selector or sign has been uniquely obtained.

### Auxiliary and generation closure

- `conditional-static-spectrum-closure.md` obtains a one-generation-like spectrum only after auxiliary assumptions.
- `auxiliary-vacuum-doublet-candidate.md`, `auxiliary-projector-casimir-rewrite.md`, `branch-casimir-superselection-candidate.md` and `quaternionic-auxiliary-block-screening.md` attempt to justify those assumptions.
- `full-fock-auxiliary-obstruction.md` and `even-line-exotic-branch-obstruction.md` are valuable negative results: naive completions add unwanted states or fail closure.
- `minimal-physical-subcarrier-candidate.md` identifies a smaller conditional survivor.

These files show real progress by exclusion, but the word “generation” should remain representation-level until chirality, anomaly cancellation, dynamics and observed couplings are supplied.

### Right-handed and hypercharge completion

- `minimal-right-handed-singlet-candidate.md` identifies a possible singlet and its tension with the chosen branch generator.
- `right-handed-completion-screening.md` correctly concludes that simply duplicating the T2 sector does not solve the problem.
- `unified-carrier-hypercharge-test.md` produces a no-go for a neutral auxiliary block.
- `unified-carrier-projector-fix.md` supplies a minimally enlarged projector operator.
- `t1-3plus1-branching.md` studies product symmetry and a possible hypercharge slot.

These are representation-engineering studies, not yet the Standard Model. The full state table and anomaly polynomial remain the decisive audit.

### Group intersections

`g2-spin23-intersection.md` computes an algebraic intersection and compact irrep content. This is an exact finite-dimensional question. It does not establish that colour is spacetime, that a G2 automorphism acts as a gauge connection, or that the intersection is dynamically selected.

## `research`

### Rounds 1–5

The round directories implement portfolios of independent methods, cross-arm checks and branch tournaments. They are more scientifically valuable than a single “best derivation” because they record where methods agree, where they merely share assumptions and where negative results occur.

The repeated `in`, `out`, `lat` and cross-arm documents should eventually be compressed into generated matrices. Their content is important; their current navigation cost is high.

### SRP7 and SRP8

These establish the programme's honest baseline:

- parent lift is multiple;
- origin is unfixed;
- empirical bridge is analogue;
- inverse reconstruction is non-identifying;
- orientation needs Z2-odd input;
- symmetric dynamics does not produce a globally unique sign;
- sixteen analysed world combinations remain live.

Any earlier assertion of a unique parent, orientation or observer mechanism is superseded by this result.

### SRP10–SRP12

These import external mathematical patterns under non-propagation rules. The result is mainly negative/disciplinary: a boundary field or limiting direction can sharpen a selector theorem, but does not supply the missing physical source; a robustness theorem must specify topology, perturbation class and invariant.

### SRP14

The common-gate parent tournament. It finds no scientifically justified winner. A reduced/null model remains the burden baseline. SO(2,4) and Spin(3,3) survive as exact-lift candidates; J3(O), E8, Furey and Geometric Unity remain more remote or structurally different branches.

This should be treated as the canonical upstream status.

### SRP15

Constructs charitable versions of the programme. Its recommendation—a minimal effective synthesis—should guide future work. It correctly places downstream dynamics ahead of additional ambient enlargement.

### SRP16

Proves a mathematical operator-selection result on the 225-dimensional endomorphism space and counts excluded directions. The physical origin of the selecting operator remains open. This is an example of a good exact result with a deliberately limited interpretation.

### SRP17–SRP18

The current mathematical high point:

- exact selector spectra and partial-selection lattices;
- fixed-background and random-unitary/Lindblad realisations;
- a spontaneous-breaking audit;
- an exact null comparator;
- cross-validation that caught a normalization incident;
- classification of low-dimensional Spin embeddings;
- a noncanonicity/naturality result.

The synthesis admits two conditional mechanisms plus the null, does not derive the selector, and does not establish phenomenology. Spontaneous selection remains below admission because it encodes the target orbit or lacks the needed physical completion.

### SRP19

The Furey/Weinstein bridge audit. It admits a Furey structural equality at the algebra level, finds no controlled Geometric Unity bridge, and finds no new observable from either integration.

### SRP20

Construction-level observable contracts are admitted. Empirical contracts are not. This is the programme's cleanest stopping point: the mathematics can distinguish model branches if a measurement map is supplied, but no present experiment supplies that map.

### Specialist tracks

- `spectral-transition`: useful technical language for branch changes; model-independent only at the abstract operator level.
- `topological-dissipation`: viable analogue/paper-local models, not a derivation of fundamental time.
- `faddeev-efimov`: an exploratory scaling bridge; should remain quarantined until the numerical and representation maps are locked.
- `observer-settlement`: attempts to replace observer language with explicit patches/selectors. High conceptual value, still open.
- `relational-field-settlement`: proposes relational phase and measurements. Strong as an analogue research plan.
- `consciousness-bridge`: highly speculative. Charge, connection, Orch OR and “third-order influence” do not presently have a common derived model. Keep separate from the core.

## `programme`

This directory contains 72 design-contract, JSON and REL artifacts. They encode branch registries, ledgers, source facts and work packs. Their role is governance and reproducibility.

Strengths:

- exact machine-readable propositions;
- separation of candidate and admitted facts;
- explicit work contracts and result receipts;
- traceability between prose and checks.

Risks:

- schema validity can create a false feeling of scientific validity;
- the same claim may exist in Markdown, JSON and REL with unclear precedence;
- 72 governance artifacts impose a maintenance burden disproportionate to the number of experimentally live claims.

Recommendation: define one canonical fact snapshot and generate the Markdown views.

## `checks`

The directory contains 72 Python checks, eight MJS checks, one PowerShell corpus check, four bytecode caches and one figure. All source scripts pass syntax parsing.

The checks validate declared finite propositions such as:

- matrix commutators and centralisers;
- projector ranks and spectra;
- branch enumeration;
- orientation/sign behavior;
- independent reproductions;
- parent tournament scoring;
- observable-contract consistency.

They do **not** validate:

- that the chosen matrices are physically canonical;
- that a model is unitary as a relativistic QFT;
- that the Standard Model is recovered;
- that an analogue result applies to fundamental spacetime;
- that a fit or finite enumeration covers unknown physical mechanisms.

The main corpus checker presently fails on two absolute paths and three broken references. Generated `.pyc` files should be removed from the research corpus or ignored.

## `papers`

### Paper 1 — Octonionic two-branch transport

This is a mathematically legitimate conditional dynamical-systems paper. Given a conjugate-paired two-branch ansatz, it derives adapted variables, phase-locking and persistence boundaries, and four transport classes. The manuscript itself states that the dynamical equations are not derived from a bulk variational principle.

Best publication posture: a reduced nonassociative-inspired transport model.  
Not justified: evidence that elementary particles physically traverse a second time.

### Paper 2 — DIII winding number

Constructs a chosen chiral Clifford Hamiltonian and computes a nonzero degree/winding number. The topological calculation belongs to the gapped family; the massless sector is an equatorial slice. The sign is convention dependent while nontriviality is stable within the chosen construction.

Best publication posture: explicit topological classification of a Spin(2,3)-motivated Hamiltonian.  
Required before stronger claims: independent audit of antiunitary conventions, class assignment, regularisation and physical boundary spectrum.

### Paper 3 — Dissipative softening of Chern plateaus

The most conventional physics-facing manuscript. It studies how dissipation/temperature soften topological plateaus and includes figures for bands, phase maps, line cuts, Berry curvature and thermal comparison.

Best publication posture: open-system/topological-matter model with reproducible numerics.  
Not justified: direct evidence for the ambient Spin(2,3) theory.

The DOCX/PDF pairs are rendered outputs, not independent evidence. LaTeX and code are the auditable sources.

## `archive`

Historical overflow and a former complete framework. Useful for provenance only. Any claim that still matters should point to its successor in core, kernels or research. Archive files should never be cited as current authority without checking for later supersession.

## Scientific verdict

The programme has achieved:

- a concrete reduced carrier and explicit finite-dimensional algebra;
- several exact spectra, projectors, intersections and topological invariants;
- paper-local dynamical and open-system models;
- an unusually honest map of non-uniqueness and no-go results.

It has not achieved:

- a unique parent or selector;
- a derivation of one physical time from two physical times;
- a positive, unitary field theory with the Standard Model spectrum;
- a derivation of masses or three generations;
- a measurement map connecting the selector to data;
- a new confirmed empirical prediction.

The highest-value next step is exactly what the later corpus concludes: stop enlarging the architecture, construct the smallest generally covariant action and complete state/anomaly table, and make one surviving portal face existing bounds.
