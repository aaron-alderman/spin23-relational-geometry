## 1. Restoring the coordinate that was omitted

The original lock used only the two-dimensional traceless part of the external carrier:

\[
K:\operatorname{Sym}_2^0(\mathbb R)\longrightarrow\mathfrak{su}(2)_{\rm adj}.
\]

But the full Jordan algebra is

\[
H_2(\mathbb R)
=\mathbb R\,I\oplus\operatorname{Sym}_2^0(\mathbb R),
\qquad
\dim_{\mathbb R}=1+2=3.
\]

The smallest full-carrier extension is therefore

\[
\boxed{
L:\ H_2(\mathbb R)\longrightarrow\mathfrak{su}(2)_{\rm adj},
\qquad
L\in\operatorname{Mat}_{3\times3}(\mathbb R).
}
\]

Under the Jordan-native external \(SO(2)\), the trace coordinate is neutral and the two traceless coordinates rotate. Consequently

\[
\mathfrak{su}(2)_{\rm adj}\otimes H_2(\mathbb R)
\cong
\mathbf3_0\oplus\mathbf3_{\rm complex,,|q|=1}.
\]

The missing column is exactly one neutral real weak triplet. Together with the earlier charged complex triplet, it gives nine real components.

## 2. Custodial vacuum

Temporarily enlarge the right action to \(SO(3)_R\) and choose

\[
L_0=kI_3.
\]

\[
SO(3)_L\times SO(3)_R

Only the \(SO(2)\subset SO(3)_R\) generated by \(T_{R3}\) is gauged as the Abelian factor. With couplings \(g,g_E\), the gauge mass matrix is

\[

\begin{pmatrix}
g^2&0&0&0\\
0&g^2&0&0\\
0&0&g^2&-gg_E\\
0&0&-gg_E&g_E^2

\]

Thus

\[
m_W^2=2g^2k^2,
\qquad
m_Z^2=2k^2(g^2+g_E^2),

\[
\boxed{

=\frac{m_W^2}{m_Z^2\cos^2\theta_K}

}
\]

Adding the trace-axis triplet exactly repairs the factor-of-two custodial failure of the two-column lock.

## 3. Scalar decomposition

Under the unbroken diagonal group,

\[
\mathbf3\otimes\mathbf3
=\mathbf1\oplus\mathbf3\oplus\mathbf5.
\]

The original source is H. Georgi and M. Machacek, [*Doubly charged Higgs bosons*](https://doi.org/10.1016/0550-3213(85)90325-6), *Nuclear Physics B* **262** (1985) 463–477.

\[
\boxed{
\text{complex triplet}\oplus\text{real triplet}
=\mathfrak{su}(2)_{\rm adj}\otimes
\bigl(\operatorname{Sym}_2^0(\mathbb R)\oplus\mathbb RI\bigr).
}
\]

The custodial repair is obtained by coupling the weak adjoint to the **full** Jordan carrier rather than only its relational/traceless face.

## 5. Furey interface reopened in a larger category

Furey's natural endomorphism algebra contains a neutral weak adjoint but no charged weak triplet. The full-carrier tensor product changes the category:

\[
(\mathbf1,\mathbf3)_0\otimes H_2(\mathbb R).

Thus the earlier absence theorem is respected rather than evaded: the charge resides on a genuinely external index, not inside \(\operatorname{End}(\mathbb V)\).

This is the smallest explicit external-index construction found so far. Its existence does not explain why the tensor product is dynamical or how chirality is selected.
