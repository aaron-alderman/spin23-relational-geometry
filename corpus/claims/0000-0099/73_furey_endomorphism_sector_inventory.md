## 1. Moving to the correct category

The direct comparison

\[
\Lambda^\bullet\mathbb C^3\stackrel?{\cong}\mathbb V
\]

failed because the two eight-complex-dimensional spaces have different gauge representation content. Furey’s 2026 programme is endomorphic, so the next object is

\[
\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb C}(\mathbb V)
\cong\mathbb V\otimes\mathbb V^*.
\]

Using the natural block decomposition, take

\[
\mathbb V=
O_1\oplus O_2\oplus H\oplus C\oplus R
\]

with

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
\text{block}&SU(3)&SU(2)&Y\\ \hline
O_1&\mathbf1&\mathbf1&0\\
O_2&\mathbf3&\mathbf1&1/3\\
H&\mathbf1&\mathbf2&1/2\\
C&\mathbf1&\mathbf1&1\\
R&\mathbf1&\mathbf1&0
\end{array}
\]

and complex dimensions \(1+3+2+1+1=8\).

For a block map \(X:\text{source}\to\text{target}\), the commutator action gives a charge difference. A global sign convention is free at this stage; the convention aligning the quark-doublet type with Standard-Model left-handed hypercharge is

\[
Y_X=Y_{\rm source}-Y_{\rm target}.
\]

## 4. The mirror theorem for the full endomorphism algebra

If

\[
\operatorname{Hom}(V_a,V_b)
\]

contains \((R_a\otimes R_b^*)_{Y_a-Y_b}\), then the reverse block contains

\[
(R_b\otimes R_a^*)_{Y_b-Y_a},
\]

its conjugate representation. Hence the complete endomorphism algebra is closed under

\[
(R,Y)\longleftrightarrow(\overline R,-Y).
\]

The full inventory is therefore vectorlike. Its anomalies cancel for the uninteresting reason that every chiral candidate has a mirror.

This is not a defect of an endomorphism algebra; it is a warning about particle interpretation. A chiral Standard Model can arise only after a physically justified complex polarization, grading, ideal, projector or asymmetric dynamical selection.

## 5. Where selection could enter

The source construction contains several possible selectors:

- Hermitian versus anti-Hermitian splitting under \(\dagger\);
- highest-grade octonionic and quaternionic stabilizers;
- Clifford parity or chirality;
- the nested volume elements;
- positivity and trace conditions;
- a particle/antiparticle polarization;
- a Higgs vacuum or mass operator.

The next calculation must apply these explicitly to the block maps and ask whether they retain exactly one anomaly-free chiral set. Merely choosing the desired six rows from the table would be an arbitrary projector.

## 6. Cross-branch meaning

The external \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) carrier cannot by itself solve this multiplicity problem. A gauge-singlet external operator commutes with the internal group and acts inside each multiplicity space; unless the shared involution or boundary condition distinguishes copies, it adds couplings rather than selecting a generation.

Conversely, if the same real/antiunitary selector that defines the external carrier also chooses one orientation of the endomorphism blocks while preserving the anomaly-free set, the internal/external bridge would become genuinely explanatory.

## 7. Stop-everything and falsification tests

**Positive:** a source-derived grading or involution selects exactly one \(Q,u^c,d^c,L,e^c,\nu^c\) set, removes mirrors, and passes the existing anomaly certificate without hand-picked block deletion.

**Negative:** every anomaly-free selection requires arbitrary choices among the two down, three lepton, two charged-singlet and seven neutral-singlet candidates.

**Current result:** all required representation types occur, but the full algebra is mirror-complete and multiplicity-rich.
