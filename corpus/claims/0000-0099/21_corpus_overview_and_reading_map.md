**Source:** `C:\Users\aaron\sourcery\other\random-thoughts\physics`  
**Inventory:** 525 files, about 15.5 MB, including roughly 570,000 whitespace-delimited words of text and code.  
**Purpose:** explain what the corpus is, how its divisions relate, and which files should be treated as authoritative, exploratory, executable, rendered, or archival.

## Overall assessment

This is not one theory and should not be read from top to bottom as if it were a single proof. It is a research portfolio with four distinct functions:

1. **Educational and idea-generation atlases:** the large `symmetry` and `phonons` surveys.
2. **Speculative theory branches:** Spin(2,3), Spin(3,3), SO(2,4), exceptional Jordan, E8, Furey and Geometric Unity comparisons.
3. **Research governance and adversarial checking:** the meta framework, claim ledgers, source ledgers, work packs, SRP rounds and executable checks.
4. **Analogue and operational work:** topological/dissipative paper models, Navier–Stokes comparisons and the local audio-response laboratory.

The early prose often reads like an older LLM: it moves rapidly from a correct mathematical resemblance to a sweeping physical interpretation, uses confident connective language where a derivation is missing, and repeats a pattern at increasing levels of abstraction. The later programme corrects much of this. In particular, the SRP7–SRP20 material openly records non-uniqueness, failed selector origins, retained null models, missing empirical maps and absence of a parent-model winner.

The corpus's most valuable product is therefore not a completed Spin(2,3) theory. It is the combination of:

- a well-developed set of candidate structures;
- an unusually explicit record of their missing middles;
- executable finite-dimensional checks;
- a research-governance method that prevents paper-local mathematics from becoming universal physical authority.

## Historical layers

### Layer A: generative synthesis

Characteristic files include:

- `symmetry/0 - overview.md`;
- the early Spin(2,3) compendium and parent inquiry map;
- `speculation/0a - 225 relational base.md` and `0b - 120 and quaternionic 4D geometry.md`;
- early E8/Jordan/Spin bridge narratives;
- the Navier–Stokes Word handoffs.

This layer is excellent at producing connections. It is weak at policing whether the connection is an isomorphism, embedding, analogy, chosen representation, dynamical reduction or empirical identification.

### Layer B: modularisation

The large ideas are split into core, kernels, branch-specific papers, checks and research tracks. Logical roles such as axiom, choice, derived result, interpretation and missing item become explicit.

### Layer C: governed adversarial programme

The SRP rounds add source snapshots, typed relations, world enumeration, independent checks, null comparators and admission gates. This is the strongest layer. It often reaches a negative conclusion:

- multiple parent lifts survive;
- orientation is not uniquely derived;
- symmetric dynamics do not pick a sign without extra data;
- the Furey equality is algebraic, not yet physical;
- no controlled Geometric Unity bridge is present;
- exact construction-level observables exist, but empirical maps do not.

These negative conclusions should supersede earlier suggestive wording.

## Authority hierarchy

Use the following hierarchy when two files sound inconsistent:

1. **Exact standard mathematics with a source or executable proof.**
2. **Latest admitted SRP result with declared scope and a reproducible check.**
3. **Conditional theorem inside an explicitly stated toy/paper model.**
4. **Core or kernel postulate.**
5. **Interpretation or bridge proposal.**
6. **Early overview rhetoric, numerical resemblance or dimensional analogy.**

Date alone is not sufficient. A later narrative cannot overturn an exact earlier calculation without identifying the changed assumption.

## Strongest parts of the corpus

- The programme now distinguishes discovery from scientific warrant.
- The branch registry prevents one chosen parent or orientation from silently becoming “derived.”
- The executable checks cover finite-dimensional spectra, centralisers, projectors, signs, branch tournaments and reproducibility.
- Null models are retained rather than treated as uninteresting.
- The Furey intake carefully separates the 15-dimensional carrier, 225-dimensional endomorphism space, `Cl(0,8)`, `H16(C)` and `J3(O)`.
- The paper-local topological models are potentially publishable as mathematical/condensed-matter models even if the fundamental interpretation is removed.
- The phonon and audio branches provide realistic systems in which response, phase, topology and dissipation can be studied without claiming fundamental validation.

## Weakest parts of the corpus

- The early material repeatedly turns “can be represented as” into “is physically caused by.”
- Several numbers—15, 16, 27, 120, 225—are treated as suggestive before a unique functor, action or observable is supplied.
- The same selected octonionic direction can appear as a convenient basis choice, a physical time orientation, a colour stabiliser and an observer rule. These are not equivalent roles.
- Proposed chains mix subgroup restriction, stabilisers, representation spaces, Jordan algebras and heuristic reductions under one arrow notation.
- Dynamical ansätze are often mathematically tractable but are not derived from the claimed ambient algebra.
- Some older files use “confirmed,” “exact” or “closed” for computations whose physical relevance remains conditional.
- The huge symmetry survey interleaves textbook material with incorrect or overly broad theses, making source authority hard to see.
- The Word Navier–Stokes handoffs contain very strong claims whose crucial analytic steps are explicitly still gaps.

## Bottom line

The correct high-level summary is:

> The corpus began as a broad, older-LLM-assisted theory-generation exercise and evolved into a serious research-management system around a reduced Spin(2,3) laboratory. The internal mathematics now includes several exact and reproducible results, but no file establishes a unique ambient origin, a ghost-free fundamental two-time theory, the Standard Model, or a new empirical prediction. The corpus is scientifically useful when read as a portfolio of conditional models and negative-selection results, not as one cumulative derivation.
