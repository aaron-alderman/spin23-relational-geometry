# Charged Real-Slice No-Go: Electroweak Breaking Does Not Rescue the Fixed \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) Slice

**Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Branches:** B01, B03, B08 and B11

## 1. The issue

The unique \(M_2(\mathbb C)\) linking factor contains

\[
H_2(\mathbb R)
\subset H_2(\mathbb C)
\]

as the matrices whose off-diagonal relation \(z\) is real. But that relation is the \(e^c\)-like block map and carries nonzero Abelian charge.

One possible escape was that the real slice might become physical only after electroweak symmetry breaking. The surviving electromagnetic action must therefore be checked.

## 2. General theorem

A complex one-dimensional representation of \(U(1)\) with charge \(q\ne0\) is, as a real representation, the plane rotation

\[
R(q\theta)=
\begin{pmatrix}
\cos q\theta&-\sin q\theta\\
\sin q\theta&\cos q\theta
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Let

\[
v_\phi=(\cos\phi,\sin\phi)
\]

span an arbitrary real line. Then

\[
v_\phi\wedge R(q\theta)v_\phi
=\sin(q\theta),
\]

independent of \(\phi\).

Therefore no real line is invariant under every \(U(1)\) rotation when \(q\ne0\). The stabilizer of an unoriented real line is only

\[
q\theta\in\pi\mathbb Z.
\]

For unit charge this is the \(\mathbb Z_2\) image generated by \(z\mapsto-z\).

## 3. Before and after electroweak breaking

Before breaking, the off-diagonal \(e^c\)-like relation has

\[
Y=+1.
\]

After

\[
SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y\to U(1)_{\rm em},
\]

the same weak-singlet field has

\[
Q=T_3+Y=+1.
\]

The Higgs vacuum is electromagnetically neutral and does not change this charge. Hence the surviving photon gauge group still acts as

\[
z\mapsto e^{i\theta}z.
\]

It follows that

\[
\boxed{
\text{electroweak breaking alone does not make the fixed }H_2(\mathbb R)\text{ slice gauge invariant}.
}
\]

The direct fixed-real-form interpretation is incompatible with both pre-Higgs hypercharge and post-Higgs electromagnetism.

## 4. What survives

The algebraic occurrence

\[
H_2(\mathbb R)\subset H_2(\mathbb C)
\]

remains exact. What fails is its interpretation as a fixed charged matter subspace.

Four routes remain:

1. **Gauge fixing:** choose a real representative only after fixing the Abelian phase. This is not itself an observable carrier.
2. **Rotating real subbundle:** let the real line rotate with the \(U(1)\) frame. Its connection and holonomy must then be retained; there is no global fixed subspace.
3. **Gauge-invariant dressing:** replace \(e^c\) by a neutral composite involving its Yukawa partners and Higgs fields, or by a charged-field bilinear.
4. **External neutral copy:** use the unique \(M_2\) factor to select the algebraic pattern while locking it to a distinct gauge-neutral external \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) carrier.

Only the third and fourth routes can directly define gauge-invariant observables without gauge fixing.

## 5. Consequence for the \(Spin(2,3)\) claim

The exact implication is now

\[
\text{matter TRO}
\Longrightarrow
!\,M_2(\mathbb C)
\Longrightarrow
\text{available }H_2(\mathbb R)\text{ real form}.
\]

For the entrywise-real embedding considered here, the physical implication

\[
\text{available real form}
\Longrightarrow
\text{gauge-invariant external carrier}
\]

is false for the undressed charged edge. A different real-form embedding can retain the full charged plane, but it removes diagonal population difference and must be assessed against different physical probes.

Thus the \(Spin(2,3)\) bridge must pass through gauge-invariant observables, a covariant bundle construction or an external lock. It cannot be a literal restriction of the charged particle space to real coefficients.

## 6. Stop-everything and falsification criteria

**Positive:** the Yukawa/bitriplet sector constructs a canonical neutral composite whose two-face response algebra is \(H_2(\mathbb R)\), or an external neutral carrier is locked to the unique \(M_2\) ideal with a forced coefficient.

**Negative:** every dressing is arbitrary, nonlocal, destroys the Jordan triple, or depends only on gauge choice.

**Current result:** direct fixed-real-slice identification is falsified for the charged \(e^c\) edge; composite and external-response bridges remain open.

**Unsafe shortcut:** claim that the Higgs vacuum permits \(e^c\) to be chosen real while ignoring the surviving photon.
