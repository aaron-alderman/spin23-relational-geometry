# Why the Four-Center Linking Algebra Cannot Produce the Full \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) Cone

**Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Branches:** B03, B04 and B08  
**Claim status:** explicit positive linear compression plus general cone-surjectivity obstruction

## 1. Center-to-carrier question

The matter linking algebra is

\[
\mathcal L(M)
\cong
M_5(\mathbb C)\oplus M_5(\mathbb C)
\oplus M_3(\mathbb C)\oplus M_2(\mathbb C).
\]

Its self-adjoint center is

\[
Z(\mathcal L(M))_{\rm sa}\cong\mathbb R^4,
\]

with positive cone

\[
\mathbb R_+^4.
\]

The external rank-two carrier is

\[
H_2(\mathbb R)
=
\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\{I,\sigma_z,\sigma_x\},
\]

whose positive cone is the three-dimensional Lorentz cone.

The first possible bridge is a positive unital map

\[
\Phi:\mathbb R^4\longrightarrow H_2(\mathbb R).
\]

## 2. An explicit positive spanning map

Assign the four central basis vectors to

\[
E_1=\frac14(I+\sigma_z),\qquad
E_2=\frac14(I-\sigma_z),
\]

\[
E_3=\frac14(I+\sigma_x),\qquad
E_4=\frac14(I-\sigma_x).
\]

Every \(E_i\) is positive semidefinite and

\[
\sum_{i=1}^4E_i=I.
\]

Thus

\[
\Phi(x_1,x_2,x_3,x_4)
=
\sum_i x_iE_i
\]

is positive and unital. The four effects span

\[
\operatorname{span}\{I,\sigma_z,\sigma_x\}
=H_2(\mathbb R),
\]

so \(\Phi\) is surjective as a **linear map**.

Its one-dimensional kernel is generated by

\[
(1,1,-1,-1).
\]

No nonzero positive vector lies in the kernel, because

\[
\operatorname{tr}\Phi(x)=\frac12\sum_i x_i>0
\]

for every nonzero \(x\in\mathbb R_+^4\).

## 3. Linear surjectivity is not cone surjectivity

At fixed trace, the positive image has Bloch cross-section

\[
\operatorname{conv}\{+\hat z,-\hat z,+\hat x,-\hat x\},
\]

the diamond

\[
|x|+|z|\le1.
\]

The full normalized \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) cone has the disk

\[
x^2+z^2\le1.
\]

For example, the rank-one state with

\[
x=z=\frac1{\sqrt2}
\]

lies on the circle but outside the diamond.

The failure is general:

\[
\boxed{
\text{a linear image of }\mathbb R_+^4\text{ is polyhedral, while }H_2(\mathbb R)_+\text{ is not}.
}
\]

The Lorentz cone has continuously many extreme rays. No finite classical center can map its positive orthant onto the complete cone.

## 4. Why the map is not Jordan

Distinct central basis idempotents satisfy

\[
e_i\circ e_j=0
\qquad(i\ne j).
\]

Their positive images need not:

\[
E_i\circ E_j\ne0.
\]

Some image effects also fail to commute. Therefore the explicit \(\Phi\) is neither an associative nor a Jordan homomorphism.

This nonmultiplicativity is exactly how a commutative four-coordinate source can generate an off-diagonal response coordinate—but it also means the Jordan product has not been derived from the center.

## 5. Decision

The center-only route gives:

- a positive unital response map;
- a three-dimensional linear span;
- a finite operational subcone of \(H_2(\mathbb R)_+\).

It cannot give:

- the full homogeneous self-dual Lorentz cone;
- the Jordan product by homomorphism;
- a canonical compression;
- the continuous family of rank-one rays.

Hence the full carrier must use noncommutative linking data, an infinite operational completion, or an independently supplied Jordan response law.

## 6. Stop-everything and falsification gates

**Positive:** a noncommutative simple factor of the linking algebra contains \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) as a canonical positive Jordan real form.

**Negative:** every proposed bridge uses only central probabilities and therefore reaches only a polyhedral approximation to the Lorentz cone.

**Current result:** center compression is possible as finite response tomography but insufficient as the fundamental carrier.

**Unsafe shortcut:** call linear surjectivity onto \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) a surjection of positive cones.
