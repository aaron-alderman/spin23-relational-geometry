# Peirce Compression Is a CP Retraction, Not a Unital Full-Algebra Expectation

**Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Branches:** B01, B03, B04, B07, B08 and B11  
**Claim status:** exact operator-algebra and Hamiltonian-invariance classification; full Higgs-dressed dynamical closure remains open

## 1. The compression map

In the completed linking factor

\[
M_3(\mathbb C)_{(E,C,D)},
\]

let

\[
p=p_E+p_D,
\qquad
q=p_C.
\]

The neutrino Peirce compression is

\[
\mathcal E_p(X)=pXp.
\]

It maps the full algebra onto the corner

\[
pM_3p\cong M_2(\mathbb C).
\]

## 2. Exact positive properties

The map has one Kraus operator \(p\), so it is completely positive. It is idempotent:

\[
\mathcal E_p^2=\mathcal E_p.
\]

For all \(a,b\in pM_3p\),

\[
\mathcal E_p(aXb)
=a\mathcal E_p(X)b.
\]

Thus it is a completely positive bimodule retraction onto the hereditary corner.

It also commutes with entrywise response conjugation because \(p\) is real:

\[
\mathcal E_p(\bar X)
=\overline{\mathcal E_p(X)}.
\]

## 3. Why it is not a unital expectation on \(M_3\)

The full unit obeys

\[
\mathcal E_p(I_3)=p\ne I_3.
\]

The \(C\)-face population is discarded. Therefore \(\mathcal E_p\) is not a unital conditional expectation from the full unital algebra onto a unital subalgebra with the same identity.

The unital block-diagonal expectation is instead

\[
\mathcal B(X)=pXp+qXq,
\]

with range

\[
M_2(\mathbb C)\oplus\mathbb C.
\]

This preserves the \(C\)-population rather than removing it.

## 4. Exact Hamiltonian closure condition

For unitary dynamics generated by \(H\), the Peirce subspace is invariant precisely when

\[
\boxed{[H,p]=0.}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
qHp=0=pHq.
\]

Then evolution is block diagonal and a state initially supported on \(p\) remains in the neutrino corner.

Any \(E\)-\(C\) or \(D\)-\(C\) mixing violates this condition and causes leakage.

## 5. What hypercharge guarantees

For an isolated face Hamiltonian satisfying

\[
[H,Y]=0,
\qquad
Y=p_C,
\]

one automatically has

\[
[H,p]=0.
\]

Thus unbroken hypercharge superselection makes the neutrino Peirce corner dynamically invariant at the bare linking-face level.

This explains why the gauge stabilizer cleanly separates the charged line from the neutral plane.

## 6. What gauge invariance does not automatically guarantee

In the full field theory, a charged off-block operator can be multiplied by a compensating Higgs or matter field so that the **total** operator is gauge invariant:

\[
qHp\otimes\Phi_{\rm compensator}.
\]

Then total charge commutes with the Hamiltonian even though the linking-face projector \(p\otimes I\) does not.

Consequently:

\[
\text{total gauge invariance}
\ \not\Rightarrow\
\text{Peirce-factor invariance}.
\]

The four Higgs/Yukawa intertwiners must be tested in the full tensor product, not inferred from the isolated \(M_3\) face algebra.

## 7. Source status of response conjugation

Entrywise conjugation is an exact anti-linear automorphism once the endpoint matrix units and complex coefficient field are fixed. It preserves the neutral corner and commutes with compression.

But Furey's primary block anti-involution is \(\dagger\), and on Hermitian observables \(\dagger\) acts trivially. Entrywise conjugation is therefore additional real-form data, not automatically the same as the source anti-involution.

It is algebraically available and compatible; its status as a dynamical symmetry remains a gate.

## 8. Branch decision

The minimal exact architecture now requires two independent facts:

1. **Peirce closure:** all relevant dynamics commute with \(p\), or leakage is Schur-suppressed.
2. **Response conjugation:** the compressed dynamics preserve the real slice and forbid the imaginary heavy mixing.

Neither follows merely from positivity or from the existence of the corner.
