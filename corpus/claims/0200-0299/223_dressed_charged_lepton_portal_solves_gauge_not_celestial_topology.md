## 1. Revisit of the best existing Furey portal

The corpus already constructed the dressed faces

\[
\widetilde\chi_L=\frac{\sqrt2}{v}H^\dagger L,
\qquad
\widetilde\chi_R=e^{c\dagger}.
\]

Both transform as charged weak singlets with the same electromagnetic phase. Therefore the Gram response

\[
G_{ij}=\langle\widetilde\chi_i^\dagger\widetilde\chi_j\rangle
\]

is gauge neutral in every entry. This remains a valid solution of the internal charged-real-slice obstruction.

The new question is different: can its phase also cancel the Chern-eight relationship transition of the null stress-helicity carrier?

## 2. Internal phase cancellation

If

\[
\widetilde\chi_i\mapsto e^{-i\theta}\widetilde\chi_i,
\]

then

\[
G_{LR}mapsto
e^{+i\theta}e^{-i\theta}G_{LR}
=G_{LR}.
\]

Thus the dressed response has internal \(U(1)\) weight zero. It does not carry an inverse nonzero gauge weight that could be reinterpreted as a compensator.

Gauge neutrality removes internal transition functions; it does not manufacture a celestial winding.

## 3. Massless fermion helicity topology

For a massless particle of helicity \(h\), the helicity line has

\[
c_1(L_h)=-2h
\]

up to the orientation convention. Hence the two chiral fermion helicities \(h=\pm\frac12\) have Chern numbers \(\mp1\).

Their off-diagonal relationship line has

\[
c_1\!\left(\operatorname{Hom}(L_{-1/2},L_{+1/2})\right)
=\pm2.
\]

By contrast, opposite stress helicities \(h=\pm2\) produce relationship Chern number \(\pm8\).

Therefore the dressed two-chirality electron carrier and the stress-helicity carrier have different momentum-space twisting.

## 4. Massive electron branch

After electroweak symmetry breaking, the electron is massive. Its positive-energy mass shell is contractible, and the two-dimensional spin bundle can be globally trivialized. The Yukawa mass couples left and right chiral components inside this trivial massive bundle.

This makes the dressed response a cleaner global \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) candidate, but precisely because the null helicity topology is no longer present. It does not supply inverse Chern class for a separate null stress bundle.

## 5. The exact fourth-power bundle identity

Massless helicity line bundles obey the tensor law \(L_h\otimes L_{h'}\simeq L_{h+h'}\). Consequently, four copies of a fermion relationship line with Chern number \(\pm2\) give the stress relationship line topologically:

\[
c_1(\mathcal R_{1/2}^{\otimes4})
=4(\pm2)=\pm8.
\]

This is an exact bundle-level tensor identity, not merely an arithmetic coincidence. It is still not a dynamical composite or portal. A physical construction would need:

- a Lorentz-scalar or correctly transforming fourfold composite;
- gauge neutrality and acceptable fermion statistics;
- a nonvanishing bundle morphism to the stress relationship line;
- dynamics selecting the fourth tensor power rather than another combination;
- compatibility with flavor and renormalization.

No such action-level morphism follows from the one-generation Yukawa triangle.

## 6. Portal verdict

The dressed charged-lepton response passes:

- internal gauge neutrality;
- reduced spectral positivity;

The two carriers may still be locked dynamically, but they are not the same bundle by default.
