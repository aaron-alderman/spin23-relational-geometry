# Independent Face and Complex Flavor Close Naturally on \(H_6(\mathbb C)\), Not All of \(H_{12}(\mathbb R)\)

**Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Branches:** B01, B03, B04, B06, B08 and B11  
**Claim status:** exact associative-envelope and positive-reduction classification; the physical selector from \(H_2(\mathbb C)\) to \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) remains open

## 1. Tightening the independent-factor repair

Separating relational face from flavor complex doubling gives

\[
\mathbb R^2_{\rm face}\otimes\mathbb C^3_{\rm flavor}
\cong\mathbb C^6
\cong\mathbb R^{12}.
\]

The full real-symmetric ambient algebra \(H_{12}(\mathbb R)\) has dimension 78, but it is unnecessarily large if the flavor complex structure is preserved.

The associative algebras generated by the two factors are

\[
M_2(\mathbb R)_{\rm face}
\quad\text{and}\quad
M_3(\mathbb C)_{\rm flavor}.
\]

Their real tensor product is

\[
\boxed{
M_2(\mathbb R)\otimes_{\mathbb R}M_3(\mathbb C)
\cong M_6(\mathbb C).
}
\]

Its transpose/conjugate self-adjoint part is therefore

\[
\boxed{H_6(\mathbb C),\qquad \dim_{\mathbb R}=36,\qquad \operatorname{rank}=6.}
\]

This is exactly the real-symmetric \(12\times12\) subspace commuting with the independent total complex structure.

## 2. Why the missing complex face direction is generated

The real symmetric face generators \(\sigma_x\) and \(\sigma_z\) generate the full associative algebra \(M_2(\mathbb R)\); their product supplies the real skew generator. The flavor algebra contains the scalar \(iI_3\). Their tensor product yields

\[
i\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}\otimes I_3
=\sigma_y\otimes I_3,
\]

which is Hermitian. Thus the complex \(\sigma_y\) face direction is not an arbitrary addition: it lies in the natural self-adjoint associative envelope once complex flavor scalars and face multiplication are both allowed.

## 3. Positive flavor reduction lands on \(H_2(\mathbb C)\)

The normalized complex flavor trace

\[
E=\operatorname{id}_2\otimes\frac13\operatorname{Tr}_3
\]

is positive and maps \(H_6(\mathbb C)\) onto

\[
H_2(\mathbb C)
=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\{I,\sigma_x,\sigma_y,\sigma_z\}.
\]

All four directions occur already as \(\sigma_\mu\otimes I_3\). Therefore independent factorization solves the CP/face conflation but naturally produces the four-dimensional complex carrier, not the desired three-dimensional real carrier.

The group ladder is consequently

\[
H_6(\mathbb C)
\xrightarrow{\ E\ }
H_2(\mathbb C)
\xrightarrow{\ \text{real-form selector}\ }
H_2(\mathbb R),
\]

with conformal groups

\[
SU(6,6)
\longrightarrow
SU(2,2)\cong Spin_0(2,4)
\longrightarrow
Sp(4,\mathbb R)\cong Spin_0(2,3).
\]

The first arrow is a positive reduction, not a homomorphism of full inverse dynamics. The second arrow is precisely the selector problem already present elsewhere in the programme.

## 4. What this resolves

It replaces the crude 78-dimensional real parent by the smaller 36-dimensional complex-Hermitian parent whenever the independent flavor complex structure is exact.

It also explains why \(Spin(2,4)\) keeps reappearing one rung above \(Spin(2,3)\): complex flavor closure generates the fourth Hermitian face direction. The desired \(Spin(2,3)\) pole is a real-form reduction of that carrier, not the automatic image of flavor trace.

## 5. What remains difficult

Simply deleting \(\sigma_y\) is not stable under the associative envelope. A viable reduction must instead provide one of:

1. an antiunitary real-form selector acting only on the external carrier;
2. a dynamical mass gap for the \(\sigma_y\) response;
3. a boundary or source restriction preventing that direction from being probed;
4. a covariant involution whose fixed points are \(H_2(\mathbb R)\);
5. a theorem that the relevant Schur pivots remain in a smaller inverse-closed real form.

Each option must preserve flavor CP rather than imposing conjugation on the entire flavor algebra.

## 6. Relation to Jordan-triple recursion

The central theorem

\[
H_2(\mathbb R)\leadsto Sp(4,\mathbb R)\cong Spin_0(2,3)
\]

remains exact. This document locates its physical input more carefully. The triple recursion should be applied after the complex carrier has acquired a justified real-form selector, not directly to the whole face–flavor parent.

This sharpens the programme’s core question:

> What physical operation removes the one \(\sigma_y\) carrier direction while leaving complex flavor CP intact?

## 7. Stop-everything and falsification gates

**Stop-everything trigger:** Furey’s left/right or conjugation structure supplies the external antiunitary whose fixed carrier is exactly \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) while acting trivially or covariantly on flavor CP data.

**Complex-parent trigger:** response closure measurably requires the fourth \(H_2(\mathbb C)\) direction, re-rooting the immediate geometry at \(Spin(2,4)\) before reduction.

**Minimality falsifier:** \(H_{12}(\mathbb R)\) is called the minimal independent-factor parent despite exact preservation of the flavor complex structure.

**Real-carrier falsifier:** positive flavor trace alone is claimed to land in \(H_2(\mathbb R)\); the \(\sigma_y\otimes I_3\) counterexample disproves this.
