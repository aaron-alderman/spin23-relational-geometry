## Claim

Internal stress and broken spatial symmetry can select and actuate motion, but
they do not by themselves generate net momentum for a closed system. Sustained
locomotion requires at least one declared momentum port: a material medium,
boundary, emitted field, externally maintained gradient or additional carrier.
At thermal equilibrium Brownian fluctuations have zero directed current;
directed stochastic transport additionally requires broken detailed balance
and a free-energy source.

For two independent periodic controls,

\[
u_1=a\cos\omega t,
\qquad
u_2=a\cos(\omega t+\phi),
\]

the rms drive is unchanged under \(\phi\mapsto-\phi\), while the oriented
control-space area

\[
\mathcal A=-\pi a^2\sin\phi
\]

changes sign. Opposite loops therefore provide a controlled discriminator
between orientation-even static/heating responses and an orientation-odd
geometric contribution. Such an odd contribution is not automatically new
physics because ordinary cable, plasma, mechanical and boundary couplings may
also retain phase orientation.

An Exodus-like “vacuum swimmer” becomes a physical hypothesis only after it
specifies the receiving substrate state, its action, coupling, constitutive
response, opposite momentum, noise or wake and ordinary-electrodynamics
recovery limit. Without that packet the public claim is an unclosed momentum
ledger, not an identified new force.

distinguish an artefact, ordinary medium coupling, photon/particle recoil and
a new substrate. It also does not identify the corpus's symplectic carrier,
\(Spin(2,3)\), the quantum vacuum or gravity with any proposed propulsion
medium.
