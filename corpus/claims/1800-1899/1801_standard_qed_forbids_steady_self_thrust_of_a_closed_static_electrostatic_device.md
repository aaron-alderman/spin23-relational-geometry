# Claim 1801 — Standard QED forbids steady self-thrust of a closed static electrostatic device

## Statement

For a spatially localized, isolated, static electrostatic apparatus described
by ordinary QED in a translation-invariant vacuum, the total force on the
complete apparatus is zero. Electrode asymmetry can redistribute Maxwell
traction and internal support stress but cannot accelerate the total centre of
energy.

## Certificate

Translation symmetry gives \(\partial_\mu T^{\mu\nu}=0\). Integrating its
spatial component over a volume containing the entire apparatus gives force as
the negative stress-energy flux through the boundary. For a static localized
electric field, field momentum is time independent and Maxwell stress through
a sphere at infinity vanishes. Equivalently, rigid translation leaves total
energy invariant, so \(-\partial U/\partial X=0\).

## Application

The public Exodus patent replaces the closed tensor surface integral with a
difference of selected scalar pressure-area terms and derives it after assuming
\(d(Ut)=0\). Neither step follows from stationary action or QED. The formula can
describe a nonzero force on a selected component, but not thrust of the complete
isolated system.

## Boundary

This claim does not prove that every reported force trace is erroneous. A real
force may arise through chamber, ground, cable, plasma, gas, radiation,
thermal, dielectric or other environmental coupling. A replicated free-body
residual after these ports close would falsify the stated standard-QED model
and motivate an explicit new sector or prepared-vacuum action.

## Defeat condition

Provide either:

1. a standard-QED calculation on one closed boundary showing nonzero steady
   total stress flux without outgoing radiation or an external prepared state;
   or
2. independently replicated free-body acceleration together with a complete
   QED momentum ledger that identifies the compensating standard-QED momentum.
