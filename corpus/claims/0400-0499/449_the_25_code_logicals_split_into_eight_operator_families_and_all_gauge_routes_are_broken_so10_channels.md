## 1. Purpose

The vector-\(\mathbf{10}\) factorization established that all 25 minimum logical supports admit a two-bilinear mediator description. That result did not yet say which physical operator family each support belongs to or whether an alternative gauge-boson exchange is a Standard-Model or GUT channel.

An independent enumeration now reproduces the paper's complete eight-family table and sharpens the gauge interpretation.

## 2. Exact eight-family decomposition

The 25 relations divide as follows:

| family | representative relation | multiplicity | scalar \(\mathbf{10}\) | adjoint \(\mathbf{45}\) |

\]

## 3. Scalar-mediator channel split

The exact Standard-Model \(3+2\) classification is:

### Weak-only: 7

- three up-type Dirac products;
- three mixed currents;
- one charged-lepton Dirac product.

### Colored-only: 12

- three right-handed currents;
- six left/mixed \(\Delta B=\Delta L\) relations;
- three right-handed \(\Delta B=\Delta L\) relations.

### Both colored and weak alternatives: 6

- three down-type Dirac products;
- three scalar charged currents.

Thus every one of the nine explicitly baryon/lepton-violating relations and every right-handed current requires the colored-triplet component of the \(SO(10)\) vector.

## 4. Why the 15 gauge routes are not Standard-Model gauge exchange

The 15 mixed-sign relations can also be partitioned into bilinears whose weight differences are roots

\[
\pm2e_i\pm2e_j
\]

of \(so(10)\), hence can be sewn by a gauge boson in the adjoint \(\mathbf{45}\).

However, every logical quartet contains \(\nu^c\). At the vertex containing \(\nu^c\), the relevant root connects the all-plus spinor weight to another spinor weight. Since \(\nu^c\) is a Standard-Model singlet, no nonzero Standard-Model root can make this transition.

The certificate verifies explicitly that all 15 puncture-vertex roots lie outside

\[
su(3)_c\oplus su(2)_L\oplus u(1)_Y.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
\text{all 15 adjoint-exchange alternatives require broken }SO(10)/G_{\rm SM}\text{ gauge bosons}.}
\]

They are UV/GUT channels, not ordinary gluon, weak-boson or hypercharge exchange.

## 5. Cartan neutrality is not full gauge invariance

Each displayed relation is neutral under the five Cartan charges. An individual component expression need not be invariant under the nonabelian gauge group because colour and weak raising/lowering generators mix components.

A physical local operator requires:

1. completion into the appropriate colour and weak multiplet sum;
2. invariant tensors such as \(\delta\) or \(\epsilon\) with declared index placement;
3. Lorentz spinor contractions and fermionic signs;
4. mediator propagators and coupling tensors;
5. generation indices and their required symmetry.

The code determines charge support. It does not supply these contractions automatically.

## 6. Consequences for the Furey linking graph

The completed matter TRO plus its light Higgs-like linking arrows can at most target the 13 supports possessing a weak route. Even there, the current construction must still demonstrate the two vertices and invariant contractions.

The remaining 12 supports require either:

- explicit colored-triplet mediator arrows in a UV extension;
- broken-\(SO(10)\) gauge arrows for the 15 relations that permit them;
- or their absence/suppression in the low-energy physical ideal.

This turns “complete the mediator” into a tiered problem rather than one undifferentiated \(\mathbf{10}\):

\[
\text{light weak doublet}
\quad\oplus\quad
\text{heavy colored triplet}
\quad\oplus\quad
\text{broken adjoint gauge sector}.
\]

## 7. Phenomenological boundary

A light complete vector \(\mathbf{10}\) would generically include colored triplets able to mediate baryon violation. The algebraic completeness of the 25-support system is therefore not automatically a low-energy virtue.

A viable synthesis needs a doublet–triplet separation mechanism:

- weak-doublet components light enough to support observed Yukawa physics;
- colored triplets and broken gauge bosons sufficiently heavy or otherwise suppressed;
- no claim that the code fixes those scales without an action and vacuum.

## 9. Stop-everything and falsification gates

**Stop-everything trigger:** a unique extension of the linking algebra produces the correct \(16\,16\,10\) invariant, its doublet–triplet splitting and a restricted low-energy subset without arbitrary projectors.

**Gauge-route falsifier:** any of the 15 adjoint exchanges is described as an ordinary Standard-Model gauge interaction at the \(\nu^c\) vertex.

**Operator falsifier:** a Cartan-neutral component relation is presented as a full gauge-invariant local operator without its multiplet contraction.

**Phenomenology falsifier:** colored-triplet exchange is retained at low mass without satisfying baryon-violation bounds.
