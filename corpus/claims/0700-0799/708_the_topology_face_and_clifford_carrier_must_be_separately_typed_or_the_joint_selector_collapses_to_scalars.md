## Executive result

The corpus contains three mathematically valid but not automatically identical uses of a two-dimensional factor:

1. the **external topology duplicate** used to make conjugation averaging completely positive;
2. the **observable \(A/B\) face** whose antiunitary fixed real form is \(H_2(\mathbb R)\);
3. the **Clifford face** generated by \(Z\otimes I_8\) and \(X\otimes I_8\) in the spin-factor representation of \(H_2(\mathbb O)\).

It also contains two unrelated eight-dimensional factors:

1. the eight Dirac pairs in the completed Standard-Model generation;
2. the eight-dimensional Clifford multiplicity carrying the real three-rebit Pauli algebra.

Equal dimensions do not establish an intertwiner. If the wrong factors are identified, the combined selector destroys the carrier or the matter algebra.

The exact joint fixed algebras are:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\text{typing}&\text{full fixed algebra}&\text{symmetric fixed range}\\ \hline
\text{topology separate from Clifford face}
&M_2(\mathbb R)\otimes I_8
&H_2(\mathbb R)\\
\text{topology identified with Clifford face}
&\mathbb RI_{16}
&\mathbb RI_{16}
\end{array}
}
\]

Therefore a full topology Pauli twirl and the Clifford multiplicity twirl preserve the \(Spin(2,3)\) carrier only if they act on different factors.

Likewise, applying the Clifford multiplicity depolarizer to the eight Standard-Model Dirac-pair labels would erase every non-scalar matter observable. The Clifford multiplicity must remain a response/dilation factor unless a nontrivial source-derived intertwiner proves otherwise.

## 1. Four factors that must no longer share one name

The programme should use the following notation consistently:

| symbol | name | dimension | role |
|---|---|---:|---|
| \(T\) | external topology duplicate | 2 | makes conjugate-pair exchange a linear CP dilation |
| \(F\) | Clifford face | 2 | carries \(M_2(\mathbb R)\), whose symmetric part is \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) |
| \(C\) | Clifford multiplicity | 8 | auxiliary irreducible multiplicity depolarized by the six-jump selector |
| \(M\) | matter Dirac-pair multiplicity | 8 | labels the eight \((\nu,e,u_i,d_i)\) Dirac pairs |

The safe typed space is

\[
\mathcal H_{\rm safe}
=T\otimes(F\otimes C)_{\rm response}\otimes M_{\rm matter},
\]

with the understanding that \(T\) is included only if the conjugate-pair CP dilation is independently required.

The relevant channels are

\[
\mathcal E_T=\operatorname{Depol}_T,
\qquad
\mathcal E_C=\operatorname{id}_F\otimes\operatorname{Depol}_C,
\]

and both act as the identity on \(M_{\rm matter}\).

## 2. Architecture A: external topology is separate

On

\[
T\otimes F\otimes C
\cong\mathbb R^2\otimes\mathbb R^2\otimes\mathbb R^8,
\]

apply the full topology Pauli twirl to \(T\) and the three-rebit Pauli twirl to \(C\). Their fixed algebra is

\[
\mathbb RI_T
\otimes M_2(\mathbb R)_F
\otimes\mathbb RI_C.
\]

Hence

\[
\operatorname{Fix}(\mathcal E_T\mathcal E_C)
\cong M_2(\mathbb R).
\]

Its symmetric part is

\[
\operatorname{Sym}_2(\mathbb R)
\cong H_2(\mathbb R).
\]

The two Liouvillians commute because they act on distinct factors. In this architecture, the earlier joint exponential error estimates remain valid after relabelling the factors.

The cost is an additional two-dimensional topology duplicate. Its physical or representational origin must be justified independently; it cannot be inferred from the Clifford face because the factors play different algebraic roles.

## 3. Architecture B: topology is the Clifford face

Suppose instead

\[
T=F.
\]

The Clifford selector preserves \(M_2(\mathbb R)_F\). But the full topology Pauli twirl acts irreducibly on that same factor and retains only its scalar commutant:

\[
M_2(\mathbb R)_F
\xrightarrow{\operatorname{Depol}_F}
\mathbb RI_F.
\]

After also depolarizing \(C\), the joint range is

\[
\mathbb RI_F\otimes\mathbb RI_C.
\]

Only the identity remains. Both traceless directions required for the Lorentz cone are lost.

This is an exact stop-everything statement:

\[
\boxed{
T=F
\quad+\quad
\text{full Pauli twirls on }T\text{ and }C
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{no }H_2(\mathbb R)\text{ carrier}.
}
\]

## 4. The antiunitary fixed face is not the full Pauli-twirl kernel

Document 622 obtained

\[
J_z=\operatorname{Fix}(\sigma_xK)
\cong H_2(\mathbb R)
\]

inside Hermitian two-face observables.

That fixed-real-form construction is not the same operation as averaging over both \(X\) and \(Z\) conjugations. The antiunitary condition retains a three-dimensional real Jordan algebra. The full linear Pauli twirl retains only the scalar operator on the same two-level factor.

Therefore the following two statements cannot be substituted for each other:

\[
\operatorname{Fix}(\sigma_xK)\cong H_2(\mathbb R),
\]

and

\[
\operatorname{Fix}(\operatorname{Ad}_X,\operatorname{Ad}_Z)
=\mathbb RI_2.
\]

Both are correct. They answer different questions.

The doubled CP construction can implement the antiunitary real form only by placing the linear exchange on an additional conjugate-copy factor or by using the Clifford conditional expectation that already has the correct small range.

Its CP theorem remains correct. Its exact \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) output is restricted to conjugate-paired encoded inputs; the full twirl range is larger. It should not be reinterpreted as a Pauli twirl on the \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) face itself.

The proposed identification is especially dangerous because the dimensions match:

\[

\]

But the matter \(M\) carries gauge, flavor and particle-label projectors. Full depolarization of \(M\) maps a rank-one pair projector to \(I_8/8\), erasing those distinctions.

## 6. The dimension-eight coincidence is a hypothesis, not a bridge

There is an attractive pattern:

\[
\mathbb O\cong\mathbb R^8,
\qquad
\text{eight Dirac pairs},
\qquad
\text{three binary labels}.
\]

This may ultimately be meaningful. At present it is only a dimension and combinatorics match.

A valid intertwiner

\[
\Xi:M_{\rm matter}\to C_{\rm Clifford}
\]

would need to specify:

- the action of \(SU(3)_c\times SU(2)_L\times U(1)_Y\);
- the Dirac exchange and chirality grading;
- the octonion/Clifford generators;
- the Higgs/Yukawa maps;
- and which operator algebra is physical after reduction.

If \(\Xi\) conjugates the matter algebra into the full Pauli noise algebra, almost all Standard-Model observables are erased. A viable map must instead place matter in a noiseless subsystem or act only on a separate response copy.

## 9. Stop-everything criteria

Stop any proposed unification of these factors if:

- it composes both full Pauli twirls on one two-level factor;
- it sends nontrivial Standard-Model matter projectors to scalars;
- it treats the antiunitary fixed-real-form operation as identical to a full \(X,Z\) conjugation twirl;
- it introduces an unexplained external doubling while claiming no doubling;
- or it lacks a gauge-compatible map from matter observables to the response kernel.

A positive stop-everything result would be an explicit intertwiner \(\mathcal R\) that:

1. preserves the required gauge/Yukawa covariance;
2. lands in the Clifford \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) face;
3. leaves matter in a noiseless subsystem;
4. and makes the external topology duplicate unnecessary or physically derived.
