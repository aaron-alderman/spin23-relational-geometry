## Executive result

The flavor word needed by the Majorana weak-basis invariant is

\[
G_{\ell\nu}=m_\nu H_\ell^*m_\nu^\dagger.
\]

It looks like a ternary product, but its factors do not all inhabit the same undoubled flavor-arrow space. The correct typing appears after Majorana/Nambu doubling.

Define

\[
\mathcal M=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&m_\nu\\
m_\nu^\dagger&0
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\widehat H_\ell=
\begin{pmatrix}
H_\ell&0\\
0&H_\ell^*
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Both are Hermitian. In the doubled linking algebra, use the standard TRO product

\[
\{X,Y,Z\}=XY^\dagger Z.
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
P_+\{\mathcal M,\widehat H_\ell,\mathcal M\}P_+
=m_\nu H_\ell^*m_\nu^\dagger
=G_{\ell\nu}
}
\]

where \(P_+\) projects onto the ordinary flavor face.

Thus the important word is generated by **one** ordinary TRO composition after the physically required Majorana doubling. It is not naturally an internal triple of the original undoubled Furey matter TRO.

## 1. Why the undoubled expression is mistyped

Under a weak-basis transformation,

\[
m_\nu\mapsto W^\dagger m_\nu W^*,
\qquad
H_\ell\mapsto W^\dagger H_\ell W.
\]

The three factors in

\[
m_\nu H_\ell^*m_\nu^\dagger
\]

therefore alternate between the flavor space and its conjugate. Suppressing that distinction makes the product look like a triple inside one rectangular operator space, but that is only coordinate shorthand.

Typed as arrows, the composition is

\[
V
\xrightarrow{m_\nu^\dagger}
\overline V
\xrightarrow{H_\ell^*}
\overline V
\xrightarrow{m_\nu}
V.
\]

The middle conjugate flavor face is essential.

## 2. Nambu doubling repairs the typing

Let

\[
\mathcal D(W)=\operatorname{diag}(W,W^*).
\]

Then

\[
\mathcal M\mapsto
\mathcal D(W)^\dagger\mathcal M\mathcal D(W),
\qquad
\widehat H_\ell\mapsto
\mathcal D(W)^\dagger\widehat H_\ell\mathcal D(W).
\]

Consequently the full TRO triple transforms by ordinary unitary conjugation, and its upper compression transforms as

\[
G_{\ell\nu}\mapsto W^\dagger G_{\ell\nu}W.
\]

The executable certificate verifies the compression identity, doubled covariance, compressed covariance and invariance of

\[
I_2=\operatorname{ImTr}(H_\ell H_\nu G_{\ell\nu})
\]

for a generic complex symmetric two-family mass and a generic complex unitary basis change.

Therefore:

\[
