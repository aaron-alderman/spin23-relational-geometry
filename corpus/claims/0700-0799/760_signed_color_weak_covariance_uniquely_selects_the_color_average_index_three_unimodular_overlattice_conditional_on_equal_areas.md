## 1. The starting lattice and form

Use

\[
b_i=e_i-e_5,
\qquad i=1,\ldots,4,
\]

as an integral basis of \(A_4\). The equal-area form is

\[
J=J_c+J_f
=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&1&-1&1\\
-1&0&1&1\\
1&-1&0&1\\
-1&-1&-1&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Its Pfaffian and determinant are

\[
\operatorname{Pf}J=3,
\qquad
\det J=9.
\]

The Smith elementary divisors are therefore \(1,3\), giving

\[
L^\#/L\cong(\mathbb Z/3)^2.
\]

## 2. Four possible index-three completions

An index-three integral extension is generated by adding

\[
\frac v3,
\]

where the nonzero residue class \(v\bmod3\) lies in the kernel of \(J\bmod3\). This kernel is two-dimensional, so its projective line has four points.

Representatives in the \(b_i\) basis are

\[
\begin{aligned}
v_1&=(0,1,2,1),\\
v_2&=(1,0,2,2),\\
v_3&=(1,1,1,0),\\
v_4&=(1,2,0,1).
\end{aligned}
\]

For every choice the certificate builds an explicit lattice basis, transforms \(J\), verifies integral entries and obtains determinant one. All four are therefore symplectically self-dual lattices.

\boxed{

Replacing \(J_f\) by \(J_f/3\) over \(\mathbb Q\) makes the matrix Pfaffian one, but it obscures the integral carrier. The overlattice construction instead changes the allowed charge/carrier vectors while keeping the form itself integral.

## 6. Relation to \(Sp(4)\) and \(Spin(2,3)\)

The completed lattice \((L_{\rm col},J)\) is an integral unimodular symplectic lattice of rank four. Choosing a symplectic basis identifies it with

\[
(\mathbb Z^4,J_0).

Its realification is therefore a valid carrier for

\[

This is a genuine construction of an arithmetic carrier. It is not yet a derivation from Jordan recursion or Standard-Model dynamics because the equal-area input has not been selected.

## 7. The remaining equal-area gate

\[
