# Oriented Color and Handle Exchange Generate \(C_3\times S_3\), and the Hyperbolic Checkpoint Has a Regular 18-Background Spurion Orbit

## Claim

The integral symplectic group generated by the oriented-color action \(R\) and handle exchange \(H\) is finite:

\[
G_{RH}=\langle R,H\rangle\cong C_3\times S_3,
\qquad |G_{RH}|=18.
\]

The fully hyperbolic checkpoint

\[
N=(UPHR)^2
\]

has trivial stabilizer under conjugation by this group. Its conjugacy orbit therefore contains exactly eighteen response matrices and is a regular \(C_3\times S_3\) torsor.

Thus the spurion-covariant rescue from document 785 closes on a finite discrete background family rather than an uncontrolled infinite moduli set.

## 1. Group classification

The generators obey

\[
R^3=1,
\qquad
H^2=1,
\qquad
(HR)^6=1.
\]

Define

\[
Z=(HR)^2.
\]

Direct calculation gives

\[
Z^3=1
\]

and \(Z\) commutes with all eighteen group elements. It generates the central \(C_3\) factor.

Now set

\[
r=ZR.
\]

Then

\[
r^3=H^2=1,
\qquad
HrH=r^{-1}.
\]

Therefore \(r,H\) generate an \(S_3\) subgroup of order six. Its intersection with \(\langle Z\rangle\) is trivial, and the products

\[
Z^a s,qquad a=0,1,2,quad s\in S_3
\]

give all eighteen elements uniquely. Hence

\[
G_{RH}\cong C_3\times S_3.
\]

## 2. Checkpoint orbit

For each \(g\in G_{RH}\), form

\[
N_g=gNg^{-1}.
\]

Exhaustive exact enumeration gives

\[
|\{N_g:g\in G_{RH}\}|=18.
\]

Only the identity fixes \(N\), so the stabilizer is trivial. The group acts freely and transitively on the orbit.

Every orbit point has the same characteristic polynomial

\[
x^4-7x^3+13x^2-7x+1
\]

and therefore the same two expansion rates, primitive \(Spin(2,3)\) spectrum and fixed-axis norm type. What changes is the embedding of those eigendirections relative to the handle and color frames.

## 3. Spurion interpretation

The eighteen points should be interpreted as symmetry-related backgrounds:

\[
\mathcal O_N\cong C_3\times S_3.
\]

The central \(C_3\) records a three-valued orientation/cycle phase in the carrier action. The \(S_3\) factor records six relational placements generated by a three-cycle and a face reflection.

This is structurally compatible with the language of oriented color plus two-face exchange, but the factors must not be assigned directly to color families, generations or particles without a source map.

## 4. Eigenbundle rather than fixed eigenchannels

At each orbit point \(N_g\), diagonalization produces four scattering eigenlines. Conjugation supplies canonical transport between the underlying carrier eigenspaces:

\[
E_j(N)\longmapsto gE_j(N).
\]

The failure in document 785 arose from asking the generators to permute eigenlines while holding the background \(N\) fixed. The correct covariant object is the eigenbundle over \(\mathcal O_N\), not a representation on one fiber.

Because the orbit is discrete and the stabilizer trivial, there is no local isotropy representation at a point. Any nontrivial holonomy must arise only after adding identifications, antiunitary flux reversal, or paths in a continuous response extension.

## 5. What this advances

- The spurion family closes finitely.
- Its group is explicitly classified.
- Every background retains the fully hyperbolic \(Sp(4)\) and \(Spin(2,3)\) spectral invariants.
- The mixed eigenchannels are understood as broken-background fibers rather than failed field labels.
- A finite comparison with Furey/Higgs orientation data is now possible.

## 6. Boundaries and stop conditions

- Do not count the eighteen backgrounds as particles or generations.
- Do not identify the central \(C_3\) with physical color without an intertwiner.
- Do not claim a Berry phase or holonomy on a discrete regular orbit without specifying connections or identifications.
- Do not include flux reversal in the order-eighteen count; it is antiunitary and may enlarge the normalizer.
- Pin the spurion interpretation if Furey/Higgs data cannot supply an eighteen-point or quotient background structure.
