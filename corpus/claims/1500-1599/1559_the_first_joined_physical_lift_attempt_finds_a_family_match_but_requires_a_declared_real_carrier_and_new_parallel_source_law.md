## Result

The first attempt to execute the six-part physical-lift packet of claim 1555
produces two genuine positive results and three sharp obstructions.

First, the repaired sixteen-state grading is not an arbitrary numerology.  Its
two eight-dimensional halves match exactly one all-left Pati--Salam family,

\[
 (4,2,1)\oplus(\overline 4,1,2),
\]

and this assignment passes the semisimple perturbative and global anomaly
tests.  Second, one real ordering singlet gives a stable open vacuum inside
the anchor-selection chamber, while chiral grading supplies an exact
retarded/advanced energy-reflection identity.  On symmetric energy windows
this gives the ordered real-part sum rule for reciprocal real weights and
the reciprocally symmetrized sum rule for arbitrary complex Hermitian
weights.

Neither result completes the lift.  Ordinary gauge-equivariant reality does
not exchange the two Pati--Salam halves; it requires the 32-state completion

\[
 (4,2,1)\oplus(\overline4,1,2)
 \oplus(\overline4,2,1)\oplus(4,1,2).
\]

A sixteen-state realization is possible only after declaring a
left--right-twisted real structure or disconnected gauge extension.  In
either branch, the seven label edges become bidoublet-valued physical
Yukawa channels and do not contain the same-chirality Majorana or high-scale
breaking carriers.

The common portal coefficient can be protected abstractly by a parallel
one-dimensional master-source bundle.  Its local RG and threshold tests are

\[

 \qquad
 \gamma+\delta-\alpha-\beta=0.
\]

But every sourced symmetry preserves the Dirac/Majorana typing observable
\(K\), whereas the required exchange reverses it.  The left--right twist
found by the representation audit does not cure this: it exchanges the two
weak factors inside the physical family, not the bidoublet Dirac complex

Finally, the ordering singlet makes the chamber dynamically attainable but
adds an orientation bias not derived by Spin(2,3).  The interval result is a
dispersive cancellation,

\[

## 1. What the three tests settle together

The representation audit prevents the label states from being mistaken for
an ordinary real physical Hilbert space.  The Ward audit prevents a formal
coefficient exchange from being mistaken for a sourced symmetry.  The
dynamics audit prevents a stable label vacuum or an energy-integrated
identity from being mistaken for a pointwise physical texture.

Their conjunction is stronger than the three statements separately:

1. the accidental \(8+8\) family match supplies a credible target but no
   ordinary \(J\);
2. the left--right twist repairs that particular \(J\) mismatch but leaves
   the Dirac/Majorana typed-Hom obstruction unchanged;
3. the ordering singlet selects the desired corner but supplies neither the
   missing Clebsch maps nor the portal Ward intertwiner; and
4. the chiral interval identity is exact before the needed diagonal sector
   is added, so it cannot yet serve as the final physical observable.

No component can therefore be borrowed as a silent fix for another.

## 2. The fixed-spectrum physical carrier has exactly two honest reality branches

For the fixed fermion spectrum of one Pati--Salam/Spin(10) family, the next
construction must choose one of two mutually explicit starting points.
This is not exhaustive once the matter spectrum itself may be enlarged.
In particular the chiral E6 \(27=16+10+1\), and its ordinary-real
\(27+\overline{27}\) completion, form a separate parent-enlarged branch
analysed in [claim 1562](1562_the_four_carrier_counts_split_into_fixed_spectrum_and_parent_enlarged_reality_branches.md).

### Ordinary-real branch

Use a 32-state carrier containing the family and its complete conjugate,
with the sixteen-state geometry acting as relation/correspondence data rather
than as the complete physical Hilbert space.  This branch preserves ordinary
gauge-equivariant reality but must explain why the added conjugate sector is
not an unwanted light mirror family.

### Twisted-real branch

Use the sixteen physical states with
\(Jg=\sigma_{LR}(g)J\) for the left--right automorphism, equivalently a
declared disconnected extension.  This branch retains the economical count
but inherits a discrete-extension anomaly test, a symmetry-breaking test,
and the obligation to recover ordinary low-energy reality after the twist is
broken.

Until one branch is declared, a full scalar potential, threshold table and
beta-function calculation is not well posed: their field content and
matching surfaces differ.

Either branch must add complete physical maps rather than seven unrelated

  admitted cross-chiral edge orbit;

The last item remains the hardest.  Adding the correct scalar
representations does not identify their Wilson coefficients.  A free
spurion merely renames \(U\); it is explanatory only if a non-anomalous
symmetry or dynamics fixes its orbit, norm and parallel transport.

## 4. The observable target is now narrower

The exact chiral identity gives a legitimate label-level prediction.  For
reciprocal real weights the ordered same-grade response has an odd
retarded/advanced dispersive part about the chiral energy origin.  With
arbitrary complex Hermitian phases, the phase-robust observable is instead
the reciprocal symmetrization
\(\operatorname{Re}(G^R_{ij}+G^R_{ji})/2\).  A physical use requires
specifying external states, normalization, causal response and a symmetric
experimental energy window.

It also supplies a clear kill test.  After the Majorana, breaking and
endpoint-diagonal sectors are installed, one must either:

1. embed those terms in a larger grading that preserves an analogous
   reflection identity;
2. derive a controlled, quantitatively small violation; or
3. abandon the interval sum rule as a physical prediction and retain only
   the explicitly zero-energy EFT statement.

Demanding a pointwise interval zero is closed in the audited finite
resolvent class.  The surviving target is an integrated response identity,
not absence of propagation.
