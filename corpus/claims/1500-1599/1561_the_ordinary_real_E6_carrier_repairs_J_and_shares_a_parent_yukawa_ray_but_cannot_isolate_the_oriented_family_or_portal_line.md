## Result

The proposed exceptional completion is mathematically clean:

\[
\mathcal H_{54}=27\oplus\overline{27},
\qquad
J:27\longleftrightarrow\overline{27}.
\]

It is the smallest ordinary gauge-equivariant real completion of the full
complex E6 fundamental.  Its four-dimensional perturbative E6 anomaly
vanishes; after restriction, the displayed local subgroup traces and the
Pati--Salam mod-two weak anomalies also vanish.  In the charge convention

\[
27=16_{1}\oplus10_{-2}\oplus1_{4},
\]

the Pati--Salam content is

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
27={}&(4,2,1)_{1}\oplus(\overline4,1,2)_{1}
\oplus(6,1,1)_{-2}\oplus(1,2,2)_{-2}
\oplus(1,1,1)_{4},\\
\overline{27}={}&(\overline4,2,1)_{-1}\oplus(4,1,2)_{-1}
\oplus(6,1,1)_{2}\oplus(1,2,2)_{2}
\oplus(1,1,1)_{-4}.
\end{aligned}}
\]

Thus the 54 branch really does repair the ordinary-\(J\) failure of the
sixteen-state attempt.  It also improves the portal problem in one precise
respect.  For one family, the multiplicity-one invariant

\[
Y\,27_F27_F351'_H
\]

contains both

\[
c_{10}Y\,16_F16_F10_H,
\qquad
c_{126}Y\,16_F16_F\overline{126}_H.
\]

The Dirac and Majorana branches therefore descend from one parent
invariant coefficient \(Y\), with fixed group-theoretic Clebsch
projections.  This does not yet define a ray of broken-phase portal
coefficients, because those projections multiply independent condensates
and normalization factors.

That is the strongest positive result of the branch.  It is not yet the
claimed portal Ward line.  After symmetry breaking the normalized
coefficients are schematically

\[
g_D=Yc_{10}v_{10}Z_D,
\qquad
g_M=Yc_{126}v_{126}Z_M.
\]

E6 fixes neither

\[
c_{10}v_{10}Z_D=c_{126}v_{126}Z_M
\]

nor an exchange of the two typed operator complexes.  It also does not
preserve the selected sixteen-state support.  The irreducible 27 has no
E6-equivariant rank-16 projector, its cubic necessarily uses the complement
through \(16\,16\,10\) and \(10\,10\,1\), and

\[
\operatorname{Hom}_{E_6}(27,\overline{27})=0.
\]

The final branch decision is therefore

\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{rcl}
\text{ordinary real structure on 54}&=&\mathrm{SAT},\\
\text{audited perturbative and Pati--Salam anomaly gates}&=&\mathrm{SAT},\\
\text{one-family parent invariant coefficient}&=&\mathrm{SAT},\\

The 54 is consequently a larger **E6-covariant container** than the 16 or
32 branch, and the shared \(351'\) is a real structural advance.  This is
not a minimality claim.  The price is
an explicit physical quotient/projection and a broken-phase vacuum theorem;

## 1. Complete Pati--Salam inventory and J pairing

The five Pati--Salam pieces of one 27 have dimensions

\[
8+8+6+4+1=27.
\]

The first two pieces are exactly the all-left Pati--Salam family found in
claim 1556.  The \(10_{-2}\) supplies a real colour sextet and a weak
bidoublet.  After further restriction, these contain a vectorlike coloured
triplet pair and a vectorlike weak-doublet pair.  The final direction is a
semisimple singlet carrying nonzero \(U(1)_\psi\) charge.

Ordinary conjugation pairs

\[
\begin{aligned}
(4,2,1)_1&\leftrightarrow(\overline4,2,1)_{-1},\\
(\overline4,1,2)_1&\leftrightarrow(4,1,2)_{-1},\\
(6,1,1)_{-2}&\leftrightarrow(6,1,1)_{2},\\
(1,2,2)_{-2}&\leftrightarrow(1,2,2)_{2},\\
(1,1,1)_{4}&\leftrightarrow(1,1,1)_{-4}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The sextet and bidoublet are self-conjugate as representations of the
semisimple Pati--Salam factors, but their opposite \(U(1)_\psi\) charges and
their locations on opposite E6 sheets still distinguish the two copies.
This is an ordinary \(J\), not the left--right-twisted repair required at
dimension sixteen.

There is also a useful negative statement.  Since 27 and
\(\overline{27}\) are inequivalent complex irreducibles,

\[
\operatorname{End}_{E_6}(27\oplus\overline{27})
=\mathbb C\oplus\mathbb C.
\]

The antilinear sheet exchange exists, but an E6-equivariant **linear**
transporter between the sheets does not.  Ordinary reality therefore does
not itself furnish the oriented correspondence arrow.

## 2. Audited anomaly scope

The E6 triangle anomaly vanishes.  The subgroup ledger makes the same fact
visible without relying only on the parent statement.  For one 27,

\[
\operatorname{Tr}Q_\psi=16(1)+10(-2)+1(4)=0,
\]

\[
\operatorname{Tr}Q_\psi^3=16(1)^3+10(-2)^3+1(4)^3=0.
\]

Using \(T(16)=2T(10)\), the mixed coefficient is

\[
T(16)(1)+T(10)(-2)=2-2=0.
\]

At the Pati--Salam stage, the two weak copies of 4 cancel the two copies of
\(\overline4\) in the \(SU(4)^3\) anomaly.  The real 6 contributes zero.
Each weak factor sees four family doublets plus two doublets from the
bidoublet, so both Witten counts are six and hence even.

This is stronger than saying that the 54 is vectorlike: a single chiral 27
already passes the four-dimensional perturbative parent test, the displayed
local subgroup traces and the Pati--Salam Witten-parity checks.  This audit
does not claim a theorem for arbitrary disconnected extensions or every
possible global anomaly after other quotient choices.  The audited
anomalies do not force the conjugate sheet; ordinary reality does.

## 3. Vectorlike mass and the mirror/exotic fork

The exact-E6 bilinear follows from

\[
27\otimes\overline{27}=1\oplus78\oplus650.
\]

The singlet gives

\[
M\,27_F\overline{27}_F.
\]

It masses the \(16\)-\(\overline{16}\), \(10\)-\(10\), and \(1\)-\(1\)
pairs together.  It cannot lift only the exotic \(10+1\) while preserving
the desired family.  Couplings through E6-breaking vacuum directions,

\[
27_F\overline{27}_F78_H,
\qquad
27_F\overline{27}_F650_H,
\]

can split those eigenvalues, but they expose a sharper chirality problem.
With only one independent all-left \(27+\overline{27}\), making the
\(16\)-\(\overline{16}\) eigenvalue vanish leaves both the family and its
mirror massless.  A single vectorlike pair cannot make only one member
heavy.

There are two interpretations, and they must not be mixed:

1. If all 54 directions are independent Weyl fields, the branch is
   vectorlike and needs extra chiral copies, a boundary/orbifold projection,
   compositeness, or another explicit mechanism to retain one light family.
2. If \(\overline{27}\) is only the \(J\)-image/Nambu copy of 27, it does
   not create an independent mirror family.  But then the formal doubling
   must not be counted as extra physical Weyl matter, and the vectorlike
   bilinear cannot be advertised as a mechanism that independently removes
   the unwanted fields.

The 54-state count fixes representation-theoretic reality, not the
physical quotient.

## 4. Available invariant tensors

The relevant products are

\[
27\otimes\overline{27}=1\oplus78\oplus650,
\]

\[
\operatorname{Sym}^2(27)=\overline{27}\oplus\overline{351'},
\qquad
\Lambda^2(27)=\overline{351}.
\]

They supply the bounded renormalizable inventory:

- vectorlike mass and splitting channels through \(1_H,78_H,650_H\);
- the exceptional cubic \(27_F27_F27_H\), whose selected subgroup terms
  include \(16\,16\,10\) and \(10\,10\,1\); and
- the symmetric \(27_F27_F351'_H\) channel, which contains both the desired
  Spin(10) \(10_H\) and \(\overline{126}_H\) branches.

For two identical one-family fermion multiplets, the antisymmetric 351
channel vanishes.  It becomes available only with antisymmetric family data.
With several families the 27 and \(351'\) channels carry symmetric family
matrices, so the one-dimensional parent coefficient immediately becomes a
multi-entry flavor tensor.

These are available invariant operators, not a proof of a stable vacuum or
a viable mass spectrum.

## 5. Symmetry breaking is a full new packet

A physical realization must provide all of the following:

1. an E6-breaking vacuum selecting \(Spin(10)\times U(1)_\psi\) or a
   Pati--Salam stage, for example through appropriate 78 or 650 directions;
2. breaking of the extra \(U(1)_\psi\);
3. a right-Majorana \((10,1,3)\) direction inside the
   \(\overline{126}\) branch of \(351'_H\);
4. an electroweak \((1,2,2)\) direction inside a Spin(10) 10 branch;
5. doublet--triplet/exotic splitting that lifts \((6,1,1)\) without lifting
   every desired bidoublet; and
6. a declared removal or non-independence of the conjugate mirror sheet.

The known renormalizable Higgs system

\[
27_H\oplus\overline{27}_H
\oplus351'_H\oplus\overline{351'}_H
\]

is evidence that direct E6 breaking can be posed as a complete vacuum
problem.  It does not make the vacuum, hierarchy or support follow from the
54-state carrier alone.

## 6. The oriented correspondence does not close

After choosing the \(Spin(10)\times U(1)_\psi\) direction, the sixteen label
states can be embedded in \(16_1\subset27\), and \(J\) sends them to
\(\overline{16}_{-1}\subset\overline{27}\).  This gives an honest two-sheet
target for the relation geometry.

It is not E6-canonical.  Because 27 is irreducible, Schur's lemma gives

\[
\operatorname{End}_{E_6}(27)=\mathbb C.
\]

Every E6-equivariant idempotent therefore has rank zero or 27, never 16.
Moreover the exceptional cubic fails to close on the selected family:

\[
16\,16\,10,
\qquad
10\,10\,1
\]

make the eleven complement directions active.  The full E6 action also
mixes the three Spin(10) summands.  A rank-16 support projector and the
seven-edge oriented mask can exist only after breaking E6 and adding new
subgroup-level selection data.

This is precisely where the 54 branch differs from a harmless real
completion.  It repairs \(J\) by embedding the desired carrier in a larger
irreducible interaction system, so the forbidden support must be removed
again dynamically or by a declared quotient.

## 7. What the shared 351-prime does and does not prove

For one family, the \(351'\) channel really supplies one parent invariant
coefficient.
It is therefore better than the generic Spin(10) model with independent
\(Y_{10}\) and \(Y_{126}\).  At the unbroken-parent level, the Dirac and
Majorana vertices have Clebsch projections of that one coefficient.  Calling
this a portal ray before the scalar directions and their condensates are
fixed would be too strong.

Three obstructions remain.

First, the broken-phase masses contain independent vacuum and normalization
factors:

\[
(g_D,g_M)=Y(c_{10}v_{10}Z_D,c_{126}v_{126}Z_M).
\]

The diagonal line \(g_D=g_M\) requires an additional relation among those
factors.  Second, the parent invariant contains further E6-related branches;
it does not enforce the selected-null family mask.  Third, E6 does not
exchange the complete Pati--Salam Dirac and Majorana operator complexes.
They remain inequivalent typed contractions after the vacuum is selected.

Ordinary \(J\) or CP only adds the conjugate invariant and conjugates its
coefficient.  It does not turn the Dirac operator into the Majorana
operator.  With three families, \(Y\) is a symmetric matrix and the
selected coefficient entries again require a family intertwiner.

Hence the accurate statement is

\[
\boxed{\text{one parent invariant coefficient: yes; broken-phase portal ray: not derived.}}
\]

## Spin(2,3) status

The ordinary-real 54 gives Spin(2,3)-organized grading and relation data a
legitimate two-sheet exceptional carrier.  Nothing in the audited
Spin(2,3) work, however, selects \(16\subset27\), deletes the active
\(10+1\), creates a linear \(27\to\overline{27}\) intertwiner, or locks
\(v_{10}Z_D\) to \(v_{126}Z_M\).

The next credible Spin(2,3) theorem would have to act **after** E6 breaking:
it would need to define the physical quotient/support projector and make
the surviving counterterm module one-dimensional.  Merely recognizing the
54-state real carrier is not enough.

## Verdict

The 54 branch should remain open, but with a narrower description.  It is
the cleanest ordinary-real exceptional container and the \(351'\) gives the
best current gauge-theoretic near miss to the portal law.  It is not yet a
physical spectrum, an oriented correspondence, or a Ward identity.

Its decisive next test is a single joined construction: choose the
independent-field or Nambu interpretation, write a complete broken-phase
vacuum, derive the rank-16 support projector and exotic spectrum, and then
compute whether the normalized Dirac/Majorana counterterm space is actually
one-dimensional.  Failure of any one of those steps closes the branch as a
physical realization while preserving it as a useful exceptional
container.
