## 1. Hubble tension: a cosmological metric test

Planck under base Lambda-CDM inferred

\[
H_0=67.4\pm0.5\ {\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}},
\]

whereas the SH0ES Cepheid-supernova ladder reported

\[
H_0=73.04\pm1.04\ {\rm km\,s^{-1}\,Mpc^{-1}}.
\]

Treating those quoted uncertainties as independent Gaussians gives the finite
diagnostic

\[
{|73.04-67.4|\over\sqrt{1.04^2+0.5^2}}\simeq4.89.
\]

That number is not a universal significance after shared modelling choices and
dataset updates, but it records why the tension is serious.

A 2026 community distance-network analysis combining multiple overlapping
local indicators reports (73.50\pm0.81\) in the same units and explicitly
propagates shared covariance. Its naive comparison with the Planck number is
about (6.41\) standard deviations. This strengthens the validation target but
still does not identify which cosmological assumption, if any, must change.

The direct Spin(2,3) route has a sign problem: its maximally symmetric metric
model is AdS with negative cosmological constant, whereas late cosmic
acceleration is fitted by positive effective dark energy. The response-space
interpretation avoids that contradiction only because it is not itself the
cosmological metric. A genuine proposal must derive (H(z)), the recombination
sound horizon, perturbation growth, lensing and distance observables and fit
their joint likelihood. Adjusting a single late-time distance scale is not
enough.

## 2. Supersymmetry: an optional graded completion, not an implication

Supersymmetry is the mathematically legitimate graded route around the ordinary
spacetime/internal-symmetry separation, but neither E6 nor Spin(2,3) implies a
superalgebra. The model must declare its spinorial supercharges, multiplets and
breaking mechanism.

Exact boson--fermion degeneracy cancels the elementary fourth-power
supertrace witness,

\[
\sum_b g_bm_b^4-\sum_f g_fm_f^4=0,
\]

but supersymmetry breaking reopens a residual of the breaking scale. SUSY can
therefore reorganize radiative stability and provide dark candidates; it does
not automatically solve vacuum energy.

The corpus already has a sharp local result: claim 1566 proves that one minimal
renormalizable supersymmetric E6 Higgs system cannot both protect the desired
inert bidoublet by a commuting abelian symmetry and acquire the required
nonzero F-flat breaking vacuum. Soft terms, nonrenormalizable operators or an
enlarged supermultiplet sector remain different theories requiring new audits.

## 3. Dark matter: particle and modified-response branches must compete

A flat circular-speed curve gives the elementary Newtonian inference

\[
M(r)={v_c^2r\over G},\qquad
\rho(r)={v_c^2\over4\pi Gr^2}.
\]

This establishes the need for an extended effective source under Newtonian
assumptions, not its microscopic identity. Three Spin(2,3)-programme branches
are admissible:

1. a stable neutral E6 singlet or exotic with computed production, abundance,
   scattering and decay;
2. a topological relic with a derived stress tensor and formation history; or
3. a covariant modified response that fits rotation curves, lensing, clusters,
   the CMB and structure growth with the same parameters.

A coordinate tilt, hidden orientation or MOND-like rotation-curve fit without
lensing and cosmology is not a dark-matter model. No present programme state
selects a particle candidate or modified-gravity law.

## 4. Color confinement: the internal-QFT nonperturbative gate

E6 branching can produce (SU(3)_c) representations, but it does not derive
confinement. In pure gauge theory an area-law rectangular Wilson loop,

\[
W(R,T)\sim e^{-\sigma RT},
\]

implies

\[
V(R)=-\lim_{T\to\infty}{1\over T}\log W(R,T)=\sigma R.
\]

With dynamical quarks the flux tube can break into hadrons, so the pure-gauge
center criterion is not an exact universal order parameter for full QCD.
Four-dimensional Yang--Mills existence and mass gap also remain a mathematical
open problem. Spin(2,3) supplies no shortcut: the programme must inherit or
calculate the nonperturbative QCD spectrum before using light-quark poles or
free thresholds.

## 5. Pentaquarks: a confinement-era spectroscopy benchmark

LHCb resolved narrow structures (P_c(4312)^+), (P_c(4440)^+) and
(P_c(4457)^+) in (J/\psi p), close to charmed meson--baryon thresholds.
Their existence as amplitude structures does not uniquely decide between a
hadronic molecule, a compact five-quark configuration, or a coupled-channel
mixture/threshold mechanism.

For this programme they are a valuable benchmark, not supporting evidence.
Any relational or graph-spectral model claiming composite hadrons must locate
the poles on the correct Riemann sheets and reproduce spin-parity, residues,
widths and channel dependence. Matching a mass or the number five is not a
derivation.

## 6. Gallium anomaly: an anomaly-identification gate

BEST measured observed-to-expected ratios approximately

\[
R_{\rm in}=0.791\pm0.05,
\qquad
R_{\rm out}=0.766\pm0.05.
\]

Their ratio is about (0.968), so the two zones show a common deficit but no
resolved radial wave in that comparison. An eV-scale sterile interpretation
uses

\[
P_{ee}=1-\sin^2(2\theta)
\sin^2\!\left(1.27{\Delta m^2L\over E}\right),
\]

but the large mixing preferred by gallium is in tension with solar, reactor
and beta-decay constraints, while nuclear/electronic capture calculations
remain an active alternative.

The E6 16 contains a sterile-neutrino slot only in the Standard-Model charge
sense. It does not predict an eV mass, the required mixing, cosmological
abundance or this capture deficit. The correct next experiment changes source
or detector technology and resolves energy/baseline dependence; the correct
programme test is a global likelihood, not a fit to BEST alone.

## 7. Diffuse interstellar bands: the positive spectral-inverse control

Diffuse interstellar bands are absorption features from interstellar carriers.
Gas-phase laboratory spectroscopy identified prominent near-infrared bands of
(C_{60}^+): representative laboratory wavelengths (9631.9) and
(9576.7) Angstrom agree with the nominal astronomical bands near (9632)
and (9577) Angstrom. Many other carriers remain unidentified.

This is almost an ideal control for the programme's spectral reconstruction
methods. A credible identification must jointly match rest wavelength, line
profile, width, relative intensities, isotopic/environmental response and sky
correlation. The lesson is that a stable unexplained line can encode unfamiliar
chemistry rather than new fundamental symmetry.

## 8. Consequences of a geopotential spherical-harmonic model

Outside Earth, the classical potential is represented by solid spherical
harmonics,

\[
V(r,\theta,\lambda)={GM\over r}
\left[1+\sum_{\ell\ge2}\left({R\over r}\right)^\ell
\sum_{m=0}^{\ell}\bar P_{\ell m}(\cos\theta)
(\bar C_{\ell m}\cos m\lambda+\bar S_{\ell m}\sin m\lambda)\right],
\]

up to normalization and sign conventions. Its consequences are concrete:

- (J_2) produces secular nodal and apsidal precession, enabling
  sun-synchronous and repeat-ground-track orbit design;
- the full coefficients determine geoid height, gravity anomalies and
  deflection of the vertical, affecting surveying, navigation and orbit
  propagation;
- time-variable coefficients from GRACE/GRACE-FO track movement of ice,
  groundwater, oceans and solid-Earth mass;
- altitude is a low-pass filter because degree (ell) decays relative to the
  monopole as ((R/r)^\ell);
- truncation fixes spatial resolution and covariance: degree 2190 corresponds
  to a nominal half-wavelength near (\pi R/2190\simeq9.14\) km, not uniform
  real accuracy everywhere;
- a complete real scalar basis through degree (L) contains ((L+1)^2)
  modes, so high resolution creates millions of correlated coefficients.

The leading (J_2) nodal rate is proportional to (-\cos i), and the leading
apsidal term vanishes at

\[
5\cos^2 i-1=0,
\qquad i\simeq63.435^\circ.
\]

This model is a precision control for harmonic inference, radial filtering,
truncation and covariance. It is not evidence for an extra dimension or a
failure of GR. Its best use for Spin(2,3) is methodological: any claimed cosmic
or relational multipole should match the same standard of forward modelling,
coefficient covariance and independent validation.
