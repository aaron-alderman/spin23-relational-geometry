\[
\boxed{\Gamma=I-2Z_M,\qquad \Gamma^2=I.}
\]

Conjugation by \(\Gamma\) fixes the Dirac- and Majorana-diagonal connection

If the reflection is absent or broken, a permitted current shifts the

## 1. Charge does not provide the naturalness symmetry

Both relation channels carry charge three, so the compact generator is
\(3I_2\). Its adjoint action on every connection component is trivial. In
particular, an off-diagonal field \(B\) is neutral under this common action.

Therefore a source coupling

\[
J^\dagger B+B^\dagger J
\]

is not forbidden by the known charge alone. Equal charge was already
insufficient kinematically; it is also insufficient as the symmetry that
would protect the dynamical minimum.

## 2. The projector generates a canonical reflection

For

\[
Z_M=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\Gamma=I-2Z_M=
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\0&-1\end{pmatrix},
\]

and a Hermitian connection generator

\[
K=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\b^*&c\end{pmatrix},
\]

one has

\[
\Gamma K\Gamma=
\begin{pmatrix}a&-b\\-b^*&c\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Thus

\[
K_+=\frac12(K+\Gamma K\Gamma)
\]

is precisely the mask-preserving diagonal transport, while

\[
K_-=\frac12(K-\Gamma K\Gamma)
\]

is precisely the second fundamental form/leakage carrier.

The naturalness symmetry is not an unrelated invention. It is canonically
generated by the same idempotent already required by the support theorem.

## 3. Polynomial audit

On one real leakage direction \(b\), the reflection acts as \(b\mapsto-b\).
Through quartic order the unrestricted monomials have degrees

\[
0,1,2,3,4.
\]

Reflection invariance retains \(0,2,4\) and forbids the linear and cubic
terms. A stable even potential

\[
V(b)=m_B^2b^2+\lambda b^4,
\qquad m_B^2>0,
\quad \lambda\ge0,
\]

has its unique leakage minimum at \(b=0\). If \(m_B^2<0\) and \(\lambda>0\),
the same symmetric action instead has two nonzero vacua and spontaneously
breaks the mask.

So symmetry alone is not sufficient. The sign of the quadratic term matters.

## 4. Exact source displacement

For a complex leakage component with the positive quadratic potential

\[
V(B)=m_B^2|B|^2+2\operatorname{Re}(J^*B),
\qquad m_B^2>0,
\]

the exact minimum is

\[
\boxed{B_{\min}=-\frac{J}{m_B^2}.}
\]

The verifier uses \(m_B^2=5\) and \(J=2-3i\), giving
\(B_{\min}=-0.4+0.6i\) and lowering the energy by \(13/5\).

Consequently, positive projector energy gives suppression proportional to
\(m_B^{-2}\), not an exact zero, whenever a background current is allowed.
