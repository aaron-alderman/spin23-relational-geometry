## 1. The tree-level survivor is absence-based

The actual bounded one-mediator census leaves the selected-neutrino Schur
packet and removes the heavy-left and charged-bidoublet alternatives. Hence
the aggregate coupling matrix has one column and no minors.

This does not derive a common family intertwiner. Generation blindness gives

\[
\operatorname{Hom}_{PS}(U\otimes\mathbb C^3,
U'\otimes\mathbb C^3)
\cong
\operatorname{Hom}_{PS}(U,U')\otimes M_3(\mathbb C),
\]

so one gauge Clebsch channel still carries nine complex family coefficients.
The surviving statement is therefore: **if** the central family mask and
minimal matter inventory are realized, tree matching is rank one by absence.

## 2. The phase loophole begins only at three packets

For two nonzero packets,

\[
\det(G_1+G_2)=w_1w_2\Delta_{12}^2
\]

forces alignment even over complex couplings or with opposite weight signs.
Three packets introduce the quadratic minor form

\[
q_w(\Delta)=\sum_{i<j}w_iw_j\Delta_{ij}^2.
\]

Its nonzero complex or real-indefinite null cone permits nonaligned
cancellation. Those points are generically lifted linearly, and no physical
constraint currently forces the minor vector to be null. The cone is a
precise conditional branch, not a naturalness result.

## 3. Tree provenance does not imply radiative tangency

In the smallest loop proxy,

\[
G_{\rm tree}=-\frac1a vv^T,
\qquad
\delta G=\kappa gg^T,
\]

and

\[
\det(G_{\rm tree}+\delta G)
=-\frac{\kappa}{a}\det(v,g)^2.
\]

An independent loop portal is therefore transverse. With analytic momentum
dependence, protection over a domain is the functional identity

\[
\sum_{i<j}w_i(z)w_j(z)\Delta_{ij}^2\equiv0.
\]

A zero at one subtraction momentum is generically isolated. Nonaligned
interval cancellation requires an exact dependent complex/indefinite form
factor relation, which must be supplied by a symmetry or Ward identity.

## 4. The category is consistent but not self-protecting

Complete Schur-complement composition is associative, independent of
elimination order, and compatible with (J) and relation-sheet parity when
the eliminated set is stable. But a factored path and a primitive endpoint
kernel can lower to the same effective matrix. The lowering functor is not
faithful on provenance.

The category can retain the needed distinction only by declaring its
primitive generators and allowed compositions. It cannot infer that
restriction from the endpoint matrix.
