## 1. What 27 buys

The extra eleven fermionic states are complete subgroup representations, not
arbitrary spectators.  Their elementary anomaly traces cancel with the
family branch, and the full Pati--Salam restriction has even Witten parity.
The minimal cubic \(27_F27_F27_H\) supplies
\(16_F16_F10_H\) and \(10_F10_F1_H\), so it contains a Dirac channel and a
standard mechanism for exotic masses.

It does not contain a Spin(10) 126 scalar.  Therefore the minimal 27 cubic
does not supply the conventional right-Majorana triplet.  A \(351'_H\) or a
declared effective operator is required.  The four bidoublet states inside
the fermion 27 are matter and cannot be silently reused as Higgs fields.

Because 27 is irreducible, a nontrivial grading that singles out the H16
family cannot commute with unbroken E6.  The oriented grading must emerge
after symmetry breaking or remain external correspondence data.

## 2. What 54 buys

The sum \(27\oplus\overline{27}\) is the minimum full-E6 carrier on which an
ordinary gauge-equivariant antiunitary can exchange the two summands.  This
is the cleanest exceptional real container.

Its physical meaning must nevertheless be declared.  If the conjugate
summand is the kinematic \(J\)-image, it is not an independent mirror family;
the fermionic action and reality projection must be specified.  If it is an
independent all-left multiplet, the spectrum is vectorlike and a direct mass
removes a 27 together with its conjugate.  One such pair cannot generically
leave exactly one light 16 without an additional support-dependent mass
mechanism.

The distinction is not bookkeeping.  Beta functions, thresholds and the
exotic-decoupling problem differ between the two interpretations.

## 3. The shared 351-prime is the best surviving near miss

For one family, \(27_F27_F351'_H\) contains the desired Dirac and Majorana
branches in one E6 invariant.  This is stronger than the generic Spin(10)
case with independent \(Y_{10}\) and \(Y_{126}\).  What multiplicity one
proves is one ultraviolet invariant coefficient and fixed group-theoretic
projections, not a complete broken-phase ray.

Three gaps remain:

1. with three matter copies, the parent coefficient is a symmetric family
   matrix rather than one number;
2. the E6-breaking vacuum gives independent branch VEVs and normalization
   factors; and
3. threshold maps need not preserve the resulting low-energy ray.

Consequently the exact claim-1557 condition remains unchanged: the completed
operator source line must be parallel under the RG connection and every
matching map.  A common ultraviolet tensor is evidence for such a relation,
not its proof.

## 4. Ranking the routes by decision value

- **16 twisted:** smallest state count; highest disconnected-reality risk.
- **27 chiral E6:** best parent-economy experiment; requires exotic-rank,
  Majorana-scalar and post-breaking grading tests.
- **32 ordinary-real:** most conservative fixed-spectrum finite carrier;
  still requires a declared reality projection and full scalar action.
- **54 ordinary-real E6:** strongest exceptional container and useful
  control; largest support and threshold burden.

The 27 is now the highest-value constructive branch because it adds genuine
parent structure without first doubling the whole E6 spectrum.  The 54 is
the correct control for any claim that ordinary full-E6 reality is essential.

The next E6 calculation should be a single joined rank-and-ray audit, not
another representation count:

1. declare the chiral 27 or ordinary-real 54 interpretation;
2. specify the minimal \(27_H\), \(351'_H\) and breaking VEVs;
3. construct the complete \(16+10+1\) fermion mass matrix and compute its

4. identify the post-breaking rank-16 support projector and H16 grading;
5. compute \((c_{10}v_{10}Z_D,c_{126}v_{126}Z_M)\) through every threshold;

6. test whether the selected family/null-mask sector is a one-dimensional
   counterterm module.

Failure of the mass-rank or support test closes 27 as a realization of the
oriented correspondence.  Failure of the last test preserves E6 as an
elegant unification but closes it as an explanation of the common portal
law.
