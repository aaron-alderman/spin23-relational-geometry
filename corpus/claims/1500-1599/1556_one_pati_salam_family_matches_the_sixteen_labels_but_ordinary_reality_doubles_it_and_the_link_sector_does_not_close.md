## Result

The repaired correspondence has exactly the right complex dimension and
grading balance to imitate one all-left Pati--Salam family:

\[
\mathcal H_+\cong(4,2,1),\qquad
\mathcal H_-\cong(\overline4,1,2),\qquad
\dim_\mathbb C\mathcal H_+=\dim_\mathbb C\mathcal H_-=8.
\]

That candidate also passes the nonabelian anomaly gate. The two weak
components of the (4) and (\overline4) cancel the (SU(4)^3) anomaly,
and each (SU(2)) factor sees four doublets, so both Witten parities are
even.

The ordinary real-structure gate nevertheless fails exactly. An antilinear
gauge-equivariant map from ((4,2,1)) lands in
((\overline4,2,1)), not ((\overline4,1,2)). Therefore

\[
\operatorname{Hom}_{G_{PS}}
\bigl(\overline{(4,2,1)},(\overline4,1,2)\bigr)=0.
\]

The reverse Hom space also vanishes. The smallest ordinary (J)-closed
physical carrier is consequently

\[
(4,2,1)\oplus(\overline4,1,2)
\oplus(\overline4,2,1)\oplus(4,1,2),
\]

of complex dimension (32). The same conclusion holds in the credible
(Spin(10)) parent: its chiral (16) is of complex type, so ordinary real
completion is (16\oplus\overline{16}).

There is one precise reopening at dimension sixteen. If (J) is allowed to
compose complex conjugation with the left--right automorphism
(\sigma_{LR}:SU(2)_L\leftrightarrow SU(2)_R), then

\[
\sigma_{LR}(\overline4,2,1)=(\overline4,1,2)
\]

and the two eight-dimensional halves are exchanged. This is a
left--right-twisted correspondence or a representation of a disconnected
extension, not the ordinary gauge-equivariant real structure assumed by the
six-character geometry.

The scalar gate fails independently. Every one of the seven grading-odd

((10,1,3)) and joins two ((\overline4,1,2)) states. It is grading-even
under the candidate assignment and is absent from the seven grading-odd

## 1. Why the dimension match is unique

The repaired label model contains eight (\gamma=+1) and eight
(\gamma=-1) complex states. A minimal all-left Pati--Salam family is

\[
F_L=(4,2,1),\qquad F_R^c=(\overline4,1,2),
\]

and both summands are irreducible of dimension eight. Once the internal
grading is required to distinguish the two chiral halves, the only
dimension-compatible assignment is the displayed one, up to exchanging
the signs. There is no room inside either eight-dimensional half for an
additional conjugate multiplet.

This is stronger than the earlier statement that the added label sectors
were unsourced: the completed count accidentally equals a physical family,
so mere state counting no longer excludes a lift. The reality and scalar
tests below do.

## 2. The ordinary (J) obstruction

For an ordinary antilinear (G_{PS})-equivariant map (J:V\to W), the
linear representation (W) must be equivalent to (\overline V). But

\[
\overline{(4,2,1)}=(\overline4,2,1)
\not\cong(\overline4,1,2).
\]

The inequivalence is not a phase convention: the nontrivial doublet belongs
to a different simple factor. Schur's lemma therefore makes the relevant
intertwiner space zero. Reversing the assignment gives the same result.

Adding the actual conjugates supplies the minimum ordinary repair. (J)
then exchanges

\[
(4,2,1)\leftrightarrow(\overline4,2,1),\qquad
(\overline4,1,2)\leftrightarrow(4,1,2),
\]

and the KO-6 sign reversal can be assigned between particle and conjugate
copies. No smaller (J)-closed subrepresentation contains the complete
chiral family because both family summands are of complex type.

The (Spin(10)) reformulation does not remove the obstruction. The
branching (16\to(4,2,1)\oplus(\overline4,1,2)) packages the two halves,
but the chiral (16) remains complex rather than real or pseudoreal. An
ordinary real structure again needs (16\oplus\overline{16}).

## 3. What the left--right twist buys

Let (\sigma_{LR}) exchange the two weak factors and fix (SU(4)_C). Then

\[
\sigma_{LR}\!\left(\overline{(4,2,1)}\right)
=(\overline4,1,2),
\]

and similarly in reverse. The twisted intertwiner is one-dimensional by
irreducibility, so an antiunitary exchange can be normalized to square to
one.

This is the closest explicit realization of the label (J) at dimension
sixteen. Its cost must remain visible: (Jg=\sigma_{LR}(g)J), not (Jg=gJ).
It requires a twisted-real formulation or a disconnected
(G_{PS}\rtimes\mathbb Z_2^{LR}) extension. It cannot be silently inserted
into the ordinary finite geometry.

## 4. Nonabelian anomaly audit

In the all-left convention, (F_L) contains two (SU(4)) fundamentals and
(F_R^c) contains two antifundamentals. With the fundamental cubic index
normalized to one,

\[
\mathcal A_{SU(4)^3}=2-2=0.
\]

There is no perturbative cubic anomaly for either (SU(2)). Globally,
(SU(2)_L) sees four doublets from the colour--lepton multiplicity of the
(4), and (SU(2)_R) sees four from the (\overline4). Both counts are
even. The minimal physical representation is therefore anomaly-consistent;
the obstruction is not anomaly matching.

This audit does not identify the label model's anomaly-free central
(U(1)^2) with a physical gauge factor. Doing so would require a specified
central extension and a fresh mixed (G_{PS}^2U(1)) audit.

## 5. Every label edge becomes a physical scalar edge

All fourteen allowed label edges form seven (J)-orbits and are grading
odd. Under the candidate physical assignment they all join (F_L) to
(F_R^c). The fermion product decomposes through

\[
4\otimes\overline4=1\oplus15,
\qquad 2_L\otimes2_R=(2,2),
\]

so the gauge-invariant Yukawa completion requires a singlet or adjoint
bidoublet. There is no constant Pati--Salam-equivariant mass map between the
two halves.

Claim 1539 found one edge orbit neutral under the residual label torus and
six charged link orbits. The physical lift sharpens that count to

\[
\boxed{0\text{ bare Pati--Salam edge orbits},\quad
7\text{ bidoublet-valued edge orbits}.}
\]

This does not prove seven independent bidoublet multiplets are necessary.
One multiplet can feed several component entries through a Clebsch map and
Yukawa tensor. But those maps are precisely the missing physical data; the
seven label coefficients alone do not define them.

## 6. Majorana and staged-breaking mismatch

The conventional right-Majorana carrier is

\[
(10,1,3)\subset126_H,
\]

contracted with (F_R^cF_R^c). Under the only dimension-compatible grading,
this is a same-half, grading-even operation. The repaired label Dirac support
contains only grading-odd cross-half edges, so none of its seven orbits is
the Majorana carrier.

Likewise, the cross-chiral ((1,2,2)) and ((15,2,2)) link candidates do not
by themselves give the conventional high-scale descent that preserves
(SU(2)_L) while breaking (SU(4)_C\times SU(2)_R). Standard carriers such
as ((4,1,2)), ((10,1,3)), or a staged ((15,1,1)) are additional data.

The link potential of claim 1542 can therefore remain a valid label-level
existence proof while failing to be the physical Pati--Salam scalar
potential: its complex link coordinates must first be lifted to complete
nonabelian multiplets, whose extra components and invariants change the
vacuum problem.

## Boundaries

**Reality boundary:** the no-go assumes an ordinary gauge-equivariant real
structure. The explicit left--right-twisted reopening is recorded rather
than excluded.

**Correspondence boundary:** the sixteen states may still label arrows
acting on a separate 32-state physical real Hilbert space. This is a
correspondence lift, not an identification of the label and physical spaces.

**Scalar boundary:** the audit fixes the representation types required by
the cross-chiral bilinear. It does not count independent multiplet copies
without a declared Clebsch/Yukawa map.

**Anomaly boundary:** the result covers the semisimple Pati--Salam factors
for one family. Extra central gauge factors, disconnected-extension
anomalies and three-family flavor assignments remain new tests.

## Verdict

The first physical embedding attempt finds a genuine coincidence and a
genuine obstruction. The repaired model's (8+8) grading is exactly the
one-family Pati--Salam count, and that family is anomaly free. But the
label (J) exchanges the weak factors, whereas ordinary charge conjugation
does not. At sixteen states the only viable realization is therefore
explicitly left--right twisted.

Even that twist does not close the model: all seven edges need physical
bidoublet-valued lifts, while Majorana mass and high-scale Pati--Salam
breaking require different same-chirality or weak-singlet carriers. The
minimal honest next object is now narrower than claim 1555's generic
physical-lift packet: a 32-state ordinary real carrier with the sixteen
labels acting as correspondences, or a declared twisted/disconnected
16-state carrier, together with complete bidoublet, breaking and Majorana
Clebsch maps.
