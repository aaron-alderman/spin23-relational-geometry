# The participatory-remainder programme reduces to nine independent dependency gates

**Date:** 22 October 2026

**Status:** dependency-frontier definition; programme not exhausted

**Depends on:** [claims 1680--1685](1680_the_participatory_remainder_is_a_testable_relational_hypothesis_not_a_residual_proof_of_god.md)

## Result

The research question is not one claim but nine independent gates. Bounded exhaustion requires every gate to end as supported, falsified, reduced to an external datum, or closed as a category error.

| Gate | Question | Present state | Exhaustion condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 textual | Does the canonical Christian corpus support kenosis, election-for-service, incarnation and participation? | locally supported synthesis | adversarial exegesis and rival readings recorded |
| T2 theological | Does the chain cohere without making evil or hiddenness automatically virtuous? | conditional | explicit theodicy and hiddenness liabilities retained |
| P1 epistemic | Can participatory knowledge add warrant without self-authentication? | open | diversity, naturalistic explanation and defeaters integrated |
| H1 historical | What can historical method identify about Jesus, appearances and resurrection belief? | open | facts separated from miracle attribution and prior sensitivity |
| C1 causal | Can practice effects be identified under self-selection and time-varying confounding? | design exists | estimands, positivity and sensitivity boundaries fixed |
| C2 comparative | Is any effect Christian-specific rather than religious, contemplative or social? | open and currently unsupported | active rivals and equivalence tests specified |
| M1 measurement | How much does observation itself change outcomes? | known nonzero nuisance | reactivity arm and estimand incorporated |
| E1 ethics | Can participation be studied without coercion and without hiding harm? | protocol-level only | adverse outcomes and safeguarding become co-primary |
| S1 Spin(2,3) | Is there more than shared selector--realization grammar? | mechanism closed on present input | remains analogy unless a typed map and independent prediction appear |

The gates do not collapse into one another. A positive causal effect cannot prove a resurrection; an early resurrection belief cannot prove present practice effects; theological coherence cannot supply a physical selector; and failure of Christian specificity cannot by itself refute the resurrection.
