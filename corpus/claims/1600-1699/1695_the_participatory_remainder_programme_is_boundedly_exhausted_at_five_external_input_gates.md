## 2. Five irreducible external inputs

No further local redescription closes the following gates:

1. **New comparative data:** preregistered, multisite active-rival longitudinal evidence with behavioural outcomes, harms and held-out replication.
2. **Historical evidence or background theory:** new source evidence, or an independently defended prior model of divine action and miracle likelihoods.
3. **A selected theology of providence:** a declared account connecting kenosis to creaturely and divine causation without absorbing every outcome.
4. **Ethical authorization and transport:** institutional review, independent safeguarding and cross-cultural validation of spiritual-harm measures.
5. **A typed physical bridge:** explicit mathematical objects and maps producing an independent Spin(2,3) consequence; shared vocabulary does not count.

## 3. Finite witnesses

These witnesses close the shortcuts from association to causation, from reactivity to quantum mechanism, and from shared transformation to exclusive truth.

## 4. Surviving claim

The strongest current statement is:

> A particular Christian practice package may produce a distinctive moral profile through participatory mechanisms. This is empirically investigable, presently unestablished as Christian-specific, and insufficient by itself to identify divine action or the truth of the resurrection.

The theological model remains available as an abductive interpretation that must carry all adverse evidence. The Spin(2,3) comparison remains useful only as a discipline of typed middles and selectors.
