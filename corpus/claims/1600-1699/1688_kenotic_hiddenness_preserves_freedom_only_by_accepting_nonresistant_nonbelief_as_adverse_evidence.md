# Kenotic hiddenness preserves freedom only by accepting nonresistant nonbelief as adverse evidence

**Date:** 22 October 2026

**Gates:** T2 and P1

**Status:** philosophical collision; kenotic reply remains coherent but pays an evidential price

**Depends on:** [the theological chain](1681_kenosis_election_incarnation_participation_and_remnant_form_a_coherent_biblical_chain_but_not_a_deduction.md) and [the kill-test protocol](1684_the_kenotic_field_tilt_fails_if_hiddenness_and_fruit_are_allowed_to_explain_every_outcome.md)

## Result

Kenosis can explain why divine disclosure might be non-coercive, but it cannot turn every absence of disclosure into confirmation. A perfectly loving God who seeks relationship appears to predict accessible relationship for at least sincere, capable and nonresistant persons. Their persistent nonbelief is therefore evidence that the model must absorb rather than rename.

The contemporary hiddenness argument focuses precisely on nonresistant nonbelief and on whether naturalism better predicts its demographic and historical distribution ([SEP, "Hiddenness of God"](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/fall2024/entries/divine-hiddenness/)).

## 1. The likelihood collision

Let (K) be kenotic relational theism and (N) naturalism. Let (D) include sincere nonresistant nonbelief, religious disagreement and uneven access to Christian participation. Then

\[
\frac{P(K\mid D)}{P(N\mid D)}
=
\frac{P(K)}{P(N)}
\frac{P(D\mid K)}{P(D\mid N)}.
\]

Kenosis may raise (P(D\mid K)) relative to a coercively obvious deity, but it does not automatically make (D) more expected under (K) than under (N). That comparative likelihood is the missing premise.

## 2. Participation cannot authenticate itself

Religious experience may supply prima-facie warrant to a participant, yet religious diversity and available naturalistic explanations are major defeaters when traditions produce incompatible contents ([SEP, "Religious Experience"](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/sum2022/entries/religious-experience/)). The response "you would know if you participated correctly" is circular when correct participation is inferred from obtaining the approved conclusion.

Admissible participatory warrant must therefore remain:

- corrigible by historical and moral evidence;
- symmetric toward rival traditions;
- capable of recognizing nonresponse and harm;
- unable to classify dissent as resistance merely from the dissent itself.

## 3. Evil is shifted, not solved

The relational model blocks the simple identity between omnipotence and total causal determination. It does not explain the scale, distribution or apparent gratuity of suffering. The problem becomes probabilistic: is this world more expected under kenotic relational theism than under its rivals?

Skeptical theism can challenge inferences from "no reason we know" to "no reason exists," but too much inscrutability also weakens positive inferences from beauty, transformation and providence. A model cannot claim that divine reasons are inaccessible when confronting evil and transparent when interpreting favourable outcomes ([SEP, "Skeptical Theism"](https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/fall2024/entries/skeptical-theism/)).

## Verdict

Kenosis is a coherent constraint on the form of disclosure, not a solution that reverses all counterevidence. Nonresistant nonbelief, diversity and apparently gratuitous suffering remain live adverse evidence. Any final Christian abduction must include rather than erase them.
