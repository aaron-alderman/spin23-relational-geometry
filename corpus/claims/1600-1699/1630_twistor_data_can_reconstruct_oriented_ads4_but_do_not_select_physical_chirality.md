# Twistor Data Can Reconstruct Oriented AdS4 but Do Not Select Physical Chirality

**Date:** 21 October 2026

**Status:** conditional geometric reconstruction; chirality selection open

**Evidence:** [OL-006 source](../../../evidence/sources/spin23-open-letter-answers/ol-006-standalone-chatgpt-answer.md)

## Claim

With a complex two-plane, the appropriate reality condition, cover and retained
orientation data, symplectic twistor geometry can reconstruct an oriented
Lorentzian AdS4 conformal structure and its pair of Weyl spinor bundles.  A
single projective real spinor cannot select a non-null four-plane, and quotienting
away the orientation data removes the global choice.  The incidence geometry
supplies both Weyl sectors; it contains no fixed Spin(2,3)-invariant projector
that makes one of them the physical chirality.

## Subclaims

1. Reconstruction depends on the declared complex and reality structure.
2. Orientation data and physical parity violation are logically different.
3. A chirality preference needs additional dynamics, boundary conditions or a
   separate selector.

## Programme consequence

Record the orientation as geometric input and test any chiral preference in the
action or boundary state rather than attributing it to incidence alone.
