## Claim

For \(J=H_2(\mathbb R)\), \(\mathrm{der}(J)=\mathfrak{so}(2)\),
\(\mathfrak{str}_0(J)=\mathfrak{sl}(2,\mathbb R)\), and the TKK conformal
algebra is \(\mathfrak{sp}(4,\mathbb R)\cong\mathfrak{so}(2,3)\).  Its
\(3+4+3\) Jordan grading must not be conflated with compact, holomorphic or
contact gradings.  The same group has two exact but inequivalent readings here:
as the conformal group of 2+1-dimensional Minkowski space and as linear
canonical transformations of a four-dimensional phase space.  No Hamiltonian
or covariant action in the answer selects one as the programme's spacetime.

## Subclaims

1. The dimensions and brackets of the TKK grading are algebraically fixed.
2. Group isomorphism does not identify the carrier variables or observables.
3. A physical interpretation requires an explicit map, action and operational
   readout, not dimension counting.

## Programme consequence

Keep the conformal and metaplectic branches typed separately until a dynamical
construction relates one of them to measured spacetime observables.
