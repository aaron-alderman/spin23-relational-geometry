# H2(R) Supports a Rebit Operational Theory but Composition Forces a Tomography--Interaction Fork

**Date:** 21 October 2026

**Status:** operational construction established; composite rule undecided

**Evidence:** [OL-004 source](../../../evidence/sources/spin23-open-letter-answers/ol-004-standalone-chatgpt-answer.md)

## Claim

\(H_2(\mathbb R)\) supports a complete single-system rebit theory with positive
unit-trace states, effects, real completely positive instruments and orthogonal
reversible dynamics.  Standard real-Hilbert composition permits nontrivial
reversible interactions but is not locally tomographic; its missing parameter
is accessible at the bilocal level.  A locally tomographic disk-GPT composite
can instead be imposed, but under the standard continuous-symmetry hypotheses
it loses nontrivial continuous reversible interactions.  Complex quantum
theory restores both at the cost of an additional local coordinate.

## Subclaims

1. Single-system operational completeness does not determine composition.
2. Local tomography and reversible interaction are distinct admission gates.
3. The programme's two currents need an operational definition before they can
   be compared with the rebit coordinates.

## Programme consequence

Declare the tensor rule, allowed preparations/effects and tomography protocol;
then test the proposed two-current observables inside that declared theory.
