# The participatory remainder requires active religious and secular rivals, not a belief-versus-unbelief binary

**Date:** 22 October 2026

**Scope:** comparative hypothesis design

**Status:** rival-model matrix and identifiability boundary

**Depends on:** [claim 1680](1680_the_participatory_remainder_is_a_testable_relational_hypothesis_not_a_residual_proof_of_god.md) and [claim 1681](1681_kenosis_election_incarnation_participation_and_remnant_form_a_coherent_biblical_chain_but_not_a_deduction.md)

## Result

A Christian-specific field tilt is not identified by comparing committed Christians with unaffiliated nonparticipants. That contrast simultaneously changes prior disposition, community, expectation, identity, ritual exposure and self-selection. At least six rival models must be kept live.

| Model | Proposed active ingredient | Expected signature | Decisive pressure |
|---|---|---|---|
| (H_C) Christian-specific participation | relation to Christ mediated by Christian practice | distinctive, Christ-patterned profile beyond common factors | no incremental profile after active controls |
| (H_R) generic religious participation | sacred meaning, ritual and transcendence | similar effects across religions | Christian specificity disappears |
| (H_K) contemplative skill | attention, emotion regulation and metacognition | dose-linked effects across contemplative forms | practice mechanics explain the profile |
| (H_S) social embedding | belonging, accountability and material support | effects track network strength | community variables absorb the contrast |
| (H_E) expectancy and identity | demand characteristics and motivated reporting | strongest on self-report, weaker on behaviour | blinded or behavioural endpoints collapse |
| (H_N) null or heterogeneous effects | no stable average; subgroup-specific harms and benefits | high heterogeneity and weak replication | no transportable directional effect |

## 1. Outcome family

The hypothesis should not be scored by institutional growth, doctrinal agreement or intensity of reported experience. Candidate outcomes must be independently defensible and include both benefit and harm:

- costly forgiveness without compelled reconciliation with an unsafe person;
- care across group boundaries;
- reduced domination and retaliatory behaviour;
- durable prosocial action under cost;
- truthful acknowledgment of failure and counterevidence;
- coercion, shame, exclusion, dependency and abuse as adverse outcomes.

No single composite should hide movement in opposite directions.

## 2. Identifiability boundary

Even a well-controlled incremental effect identifies the causal contribution of a practice package, not God. A theological interpretation competes at a different explanatory level with emergence, cultural evolution and naturalistic psychosocial mechanisms. The data may alter comparative plausibility but cannot isolate divine agency as a laboratory treatment.

## 3. Required comparisons

The minimum useful study contains active Christian, active non-Christian religious, structurally matched secular-community and low-intensity control arms. It measures baseline commitment, expectancy, social-network change, instructor effects, prior trauma and concurrent therapy. It retains apostates, nonresponders and harmed participants rather than analysing only successful adherents.

## Verdict

\[
\boxed{\text{belief versus unbelief is confounded};\quad
\text{only active rivals can test specificity}.}
\]
