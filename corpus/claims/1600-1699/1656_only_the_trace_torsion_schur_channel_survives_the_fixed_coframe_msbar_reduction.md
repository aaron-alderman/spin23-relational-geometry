## Result

At fixed coframe, the tensor-torsion source shift and the direct quadratic
trace-torsion source shift are multiplication operators. Their standalone
functional determinants are scaleless in dimensional regularization and
produce no MS-bar logarithmic pole.

The only connection-sector source dependence that survives into a
propagating determinant is the Schur complement generated by the mixing of
the axial fluctuation with auxiliary trace torsion.

## Algebraic trace block

Let the source-independent trace-torsion quadratic density be

\[
{C_t\over2}v_\mu v^\mu,
\qquad C_t\ne0,
\]

where \(C_t\) is the packet's algebraic trace eigenvalue after convention
calibration. Claim 1655 gives the source-linear mixing

\[
v\cdot J_v,
\qquad
J_v={2b_X\over3}\left(\bar p,Ds+\bar X,s\right),
\qquad \bar X=D\bar p.
\]

The source-quadratic trace block also contains

\[
{2b_X\over9}(v\cdot\bar p)^2.
\]

That last term only changes an ultralocal determinant at quadratic source
order. Its correction to the inverse inside \(J_v^T H_v^{-1}J_v\) starts at
fourth source order and is irrelevant here.

Eliminating \(v\) therefore gives the exact quadratic-source Schur term

\[
\boxed{
\Delta\mathcal L_{\rm Schur}^{(\bar p^2)}
=-{2b_X^2\over9C_t}
\left(\bar p_\mu Ds+\bar X s_\mu\right)
\left(\bar p^\mu Ds+\bar X s^\mu\right).
}
\]

This modifies the propagating axial operator and can generate logarithmic
responses.

## Tensor block

The tensor fluctuation appears through

\[
{1\over2}q\,C_Q q+b_X\bar X*(q_a\wedge q^a),
\]

with no derivative on \(q\) and no linear \(q\)-\(s\) mixing. Its determinant
is a background-dependent multiplication determinant. As with the complete
constraint measure, its functional trace is proportional to
\(\int d^dk\,1\) and has no dimensional-regularization/MS-bar pole.

## Reduced fixed-coframe problem

After the measure-preserving auxiliary elimination, the fixed-coframe
connection contribution is reduced to one axial-vector/pseudoscalar operator:

\[
H_{\rm axial}^{\rm red}[\bar p]
=H_{\rm axial}^{(0)}
+\delta H_{\rm Schur}^{(\bar p^2)}.
\]

There is no independent tensor or trace determinant contribution to the
logarithmic running in the frozen scheme. The remaining tasks are:

1. insert the packet-normalized value of \(C_t\);
2. include the coframe completion required by covariance; and
3. evaluate the reduced trace on the three matching backgrounds.

The first source-order bosonic insertion cancels in the reduced axial block:
the auxiliary mixing is linear in \(\bar p\), but its Schur complement begins
at \(\bar p^2\). Coframe mixing may still supply independent first-order
vertices, so this is not yet the complete \(H_{B,1}=0\) statement.
