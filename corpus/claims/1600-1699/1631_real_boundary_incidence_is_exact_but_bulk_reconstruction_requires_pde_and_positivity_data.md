# Real Boundary Incidence Is Exact but Bulk Reconstruction Requires PDE and Positivity Data

**Date:** 21 October 2026

**Status:** incidence theorem established; quantum reconstruction conditional

**Evidence:** [OL-007 source](../../../evidence/sources/spin23-open-letter-answers/ol-007-standalone-chatgpt-answer.md)

## Claim

The real incidence relation between \(\mathbb{RP}^3\) and the Lagrangian
Grassmannian \(\operatorname{Lag}(2,\mathbb R^4)\) is exact boundary geometry.
Lorentzian AdS4 bulk points, however, are represented by complex planes with a
Hermitian reality condition rather than arbitrary real planes.  Incidence alone
does not reconstruct a bulk quantum field: one must specify a PDE, boundary
condition, admissible trace range, inverse modulo homogeneous data, causal
commutator, spectral positivity and microlocal wavefront condition.

## Subclaims

1. Boundary incidence and bulk point reconstruction use different typed data.
2. A kernel formula is not an inverse until its domain, range and null space are
   proved.
3. Locality and positivity are properties of the quantum state/commutator, not
   consequences of projective incidence.

## Programme consequence

The next benchmark is the conformally coupled scalar with an explicit trace
