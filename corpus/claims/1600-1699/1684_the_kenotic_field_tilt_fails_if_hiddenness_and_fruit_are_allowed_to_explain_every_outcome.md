# The kenotic field tilt fails if hiddenness and fruit are allowed to explain every outcome

**Date:** 22 October 2026

**Scope:** anti-circularity and kill-test protocol

**Status:** normative falsifiability gate

**Depends on:** [claim 1680](1680_the_participatory_remainder_is_a_testable_relational_hypothesis_not_a_residual_proof_of_god.md), [claim 1682](1682_the_participatory_remainder_requires_active_religious_and_secular_rivals_not_a_belief_versus_unbelief_binary.md), and [claim 1683](1683_a_preregistered_longitudinal_active_control_design_can_test_participatory_consequences_but_not_divine_agency.md)

## Result

Kenosis cannot be used as a universal solvent. If success is called fruit, failure is called hiddenness, harm is called purification and null evidence is called preservation of freedom, the model has no empirical contrast. The programme therefore fixes failure conditions before evidence is examined.

## 1. Kill conditions

The proposed Christian-specific field-tilt hypothesis is materially weakened when:

1. its preregistered primary profile does not replicate across sites;
2. active religious and secular controls reproduce the complete profile within a declared equivalence margin;
3. expectancy, social embedding and instructor effects absorb the incremental contrast;
4. effects occur only in self-report while blinded behavioural and informant outcomes remain null;
5. adverse outcomes equal or exceed benefits under the same moral weighting fixed in advance;
6. nonresponders, apostates or harmed participants must be excluded to recover the effect;
7. every failed prediction is redescribed as a subtler success;
8. no observation could lower the model's comparative standing.

No single finite study decides Christianity. These conditions instead kill or demote the specific empirical bridge being proposed.

## 2. Anti-circularity ledger

| Forbidden repair | Why it fails | Admissible alternative |
|---|---|---|
| define fruit as whatever Christians exhibit | outcome selected by the result | define moral outcomes before group comparison |
| count only faithful completers | survivorship and conditioning on response | retain all enrolled participants and model attrition |
| invoke kenosis after a null | hypothesis expands to absorb failure | preregister the minimum informative contrast |
| label all remainder divine | residual depends on model misspecification | improve ordinary models and compare explanations |
| dismiss rival transformation as counterfeit | protects uniqueness by definition | score rival arms with identical instruments |
| infer truth from intensity | salience is not veridicality | triangulate behaviour, informants and durability |

## 3. Success condition

Success would be modest: a preregistered, replicated, ethically positive and distinctive outcome profile that survives active controls and sensitivity analysis. It would support the claim that the Christian participation package has unusual consequences. Moving from that result to divine action would still require historical, metaphysical and comparative theological arguments.

## Verdict

\[
\boxed{\text{kenosis may constrain a prediction};\quad
\text{it may not immunize the prediction}.}
\]
