## What was genuinely constructed

### Compact Yang--Mills modules

The tranche now has an exact reflection-positive compact transfer system,
positive GEVP spectra, continuum/finite-volume regression with systematic
envelopes, exact Hankel and support-localizer certificates, certified
one-plaquette Hamiltonian intervals, and convergent positivity-plus-loop-
equation bounds.  These are reusable proof modules.

They also expose the missing quantifier.  Finite noisy moments admit a lighter
state with arbitrarily small overlap; one-plaquette rotor gaps do not scale as
four-dimensional dimensional transmutation; finite loop cutoffs admit positive
solutions that fail the next cutoff; and stochastic-time gaps change under a
mobility rescaling that leaves the equilibrium measure fixed.  None supplies
the uniform support exclusion near zero after (a\to0), (L\to\infty) and
all operator/time cutoffs are removed.

### A viable but critical gravitational sector

The minimal MacDowell--Mansouri/Stelle--West action gives the required two
massless graviton helicities but no massive connection pole.  A curvature-
pseudoscalar extension yields one healthy massive (0^-) torsion mode.  The
published full Poincare-gauge critical class verifies three configuration
degrees of freedom, a positive pseudoscalar mass and nonvanishing nonlinear
constraint determinant in the analyzed block.

That success is a critical surface, not an open quantum region.  Fermion loops
generate transverse torsion kinetics; the complete isolated axial block then
requires opposite signs for healthy spin 1 and spin 0.  Curvature loops also
generate the wider invariant basis.  The sector is therefore a consistent
classical theory or low-energy EFT, not yet a radiatively closed fundamental
Spin(2,3) action.

Entire zero-free form factors preserve the Einstein pole without adding a
massive spin-2 pole.  Full ghost-free bimetric theory can contain positive-
residue massless and massive spin-2 poles, but imports a second metric and its
nonlinear constraint.  Neither structure is derived by the current parent.

### A real shared-scale construction

The Gildener--Weinberg selector portal is the strongest positive joining.  One
radiative vev sets the selector, induced Planck scale, Cartan/AdS scale and
colored threshold.  The scalar Hessian is positive at the finite benchmark,
color is unbroken and threshold matching gives an explicit
(Lambda_{\rm YM}/M_{\rm Pl}) relation.

Its remaining color boundary can be removed in an explicit interacting
gauge--gravity fixed-point model when the color direction is irrelevant.  But
the hierarchy moves by roughly fourteen orders across a modest scan of the
unestablished fixed-point coefficients.  Prediction therefore awaits a
nonperturbatively identified Spin(2,3) universality class and matching map.

The same portal fails its cosmological test: its negative vacuum energy is
about (5.27\times10^{115}) observed dark-energy densities in the benchmark,
and a conservative next-loop term is itself about (3.34\times10^{113})
observed densities.  Sequestering can cancel constant loop shifts
conditionally, but imports global/four-form structure, leaves the residual
unset and removes the direct portal-curvature relation.

### A typed observable

Intrinsic polarization instead sources a tensor Yukawa spin--spin force.  A
non-GR unpolarized frame-dragging signal would falsify the isolated critical
class; a polarized range-dependent torque is the appropriate positive search.

## The five irreducible gates

### 1. Constructive four-dimensional Yang--Mills

Construct the nontrivial reflection-positive continuum theory and prove a
positive gauge-invariant spectral bound uniform in regulator, volume and
operator/time limits.  Every finite tool is now available as a diagnostic;
the infinite-dimensional uniform estimate is not.

### 2. Spin(2,3) quantum completion

Derive a symmetry-protected, radiatively closed nonlinear action from a full
broken Spin(2,3) parent that retains the massless graviton and the healthy
critical auxiliary spectrum.  A new parent symmetry or constraint identity is
required; scanning the same local coefficients cannot protect the surface.

### 3. Predictive color fixed point

Establish the relevant gauge--gravity universality class nonperturbatively,
show the color direction is irrelevant under systematic enlargement, and
match its unique trajectory through all selector and exotic thresholds to a
gauge-invariant low-energy observable.

### 4. Vacuum energy

Supply a radiatively stable mechanism that also predicts the small positive
residual.  Relocating it to an integration constant or cancelling loops while
leaving a boundary residual does not close this gate.

### 5. Matter descent

Complete the anomaly-free E6/Spin(10)/Standard-Model descendant so that
fermion torsion coefficients, flavor rotations, anomaly coefficients and
exotic thresholds are calculated rather than fitted.

## Exhaustion statement

Within the declared local polynomial actions, compact regulators, deformation
methods, fixed-point ansatzes and portal field content, there is no remaining
unperformed finite calculation whose success would close one of these five
gates.  Another lattice size, fit window, moment order, sign scan or group
branching may improve evidence but cannot change the logical type of the
result.

Further progress is real and possible, but it requires a new input class:
an infinite-dimensional estimate, a new UV parent/symmetry, a controlled
nonperturbative fixed point, a predictive vacuum-energy principle, or new
empirical matter data.  The claim explicitly does **not** exhaust all possible
field content, all future mathematics, or the Clay problem.
