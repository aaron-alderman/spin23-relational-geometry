# A Commuting Tensor Module Is Automatic but the Furey Commutant Is the Decisive Test

**Date:** 21 October 2026

**Status:** elementary construction established; programme compatibility unproved

**Evidence:** [OL-003 source](../../../evidence/sources/spin23-open-letter-answers/ol-003-standalone-chatgpt-answer.md)

## Claim

Putting the internal algebra on \(F\) and Spin(2,3) on a spinor \(S\) makes
their actions commute on \(F\otimes S\) by construction.  That construction
does not prove that the programme's actual Furey action has the required
non-scalar commutant, ideal projection or chirality.  An irreducible real
Spin(2,3) four-spinor has scalar commutant, and a sufficiently large
octonionic left-chain action can exhaust \(\operatorname{End}_{\mathbb R}(O)\).
Dual-pair or \(\mathfrak{osp}(n|4)\) routes therefore require declared enlarged
modules rather than an implicit compatibility claim.

## Subclaims

1. Separate tensor factors provide existence, not uniqueness or economy.
2. The commutant depends on the represented algebra, real structure and ideal.
3. Chirality cannot be inferred from commuting actions alone.

## Programme consequence

Write the actual generators as matrices on one declared real module and compute
the full commutant, grading and invariant subspaces before coupling an action.
