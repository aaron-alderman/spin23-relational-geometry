# The Rank-Two Peirce Criterion Selects H2(R) but Not Its Physical Carrier Role

**Date:** 21 October 2026

**Status:** established algebraic criterion; physical selection open

**Evidence:** [OL-001 source](../../../evidence/sources/spin23-open-letter-answers/ol-001-standalone-chatgpt-answer.md) and [adjudication ledger](../../../evidence/data/spin23-open-letter-answer-assessment.json)

## Claim

A positive rank-two Euclidean Jordan algebra with a complete pair of primitive
orthogonal idempotents and a one-dimensional real off-diagonal Peirce space is
isomorphic to \(H_2(\mathbb R)\).  Its effective inner Tits--Kantor--Koecher
algebra is \(\mathfrak{sp}(4,\mathbb R)\), whereas the multiplicity-two positive
neighbor \(H_2(\mathbb C)\) gives \(\mathfrak{su}(2,2)\).  These facts establish
a sharp classification boundary, not a reason that the programme's recursion
must choose that boundary.  Convention-matched certificates are still needed
for the asserted zero-coupling \(\mathfrak{iso}(2,2)\) and literal Q-map
closures.

## Subclaims

1. Positivity, rank two, a complete primitive frame and real Peirce
   multiplicity one are jointly sufficient for the \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) result.
2. Dropping positivity or changing the Peirce multiplicity opens neighboring
   real forms and Jordan/triple systems.
3. TKK closure identifies an algebraic symmetry; it does not select a physical
   Hilbert space, observables or action.

## Programme consequence

The next carrier step is a machine-checked Peirce/TKK certificate on the actual
recursive generators, including all neighboring closures and the exact
quotient by ineffective centers.
