## Result

The proposed theological model can be made precise enough to investigate without claiming to measure God. Let (X) denote a person-in-context, (M) a declared participatory practice, (M_0) a matched comparator, (O) preregistered outcomes, and (Z) measured confounders. Define

\[
\Delta_O(Z)=
P(O\mid M,Z)-P(O\mid M_0,Z).
\]

The **participatory-remainder hypothesis** says that sustained participation may change the conditional distribution of independently specified human outcomes while leaving ordinary causality, refusal and uncertainty intact. It does not identify any nonzero (Delta_O) with grace. The residual

\[
R=O_{\rm observed}-\widehat O_{\rm ordinary\ model}
\]

is a location for model criticism, not a divine variable.

## 1. Four claims that must remain separate

1. **Semantic claim:** Christianity describes salvation as participation in Christ.
2. **Causal claim:** Christian practices can change participants and communities.
3. **Comparative claim:** their outcome profile may differ from matched secular and religious alternatives.
4. **Theological claim:** a surviving difference is best explained by divine action.

The first is textual and theological. The second is empirically ordinary. The third is testable but difficult. The fourth is abductive and requires comparison with psychosocial, institutional and rival-theological explanations. No earlier claim entails a later one.

## 2. Non-coercive tilt

The proposed kenotic form is not deterministic control:

\[
0<P(O\mid M,Z)<1.
\]

Freedom and contingency remain live, so failures are expected. This makes the hypothesis compatible with many observations and therefore at risk of low empirical content. Its content comes only from preregistered directional predictions, calibrated effect-size floors, active controls and retained negative cases.

## 3. Measurement is intervention, not quantum magic

Participation changes the system because it is an intervention: forgiveness cannot be tested without an act of forgiveness, and trust cannot be tested without vulnerability. This is structurally similar to measurement as coupling, but no quantum state, Born rule, collapse dynamics or consciousness-causes-reality claim follows.

The correct minimal map is

\[
(X,M,C,t)\longmapsto X',
\]

where (C) records community and context and (t) duration. Calling this map an operator is notation, not a physical identification.

## 4. Remainder without a gaps inference

Three remainders must not be conflated:

- a statistical residual left by a fitted model;
- an epistemic remainder left by incomplete evidence;
- an ontological distinction preserved within participation.

The first may shrink under better causal models. The second calls for humility. The third is a theological interpretation of communion without identity collapse. None proves the others.

## Verdict

\[
\boxed{\text{participation is an admissible intervention hypothesis},\qquad
R\neq\text{God},\qquad
\text{QM identity claim}=\text{false}.}
\]
