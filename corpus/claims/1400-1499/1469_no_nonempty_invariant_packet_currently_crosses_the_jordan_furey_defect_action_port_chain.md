## Result

The priority speculative arc from claim 1468 has a definite baseline result. Across the declared chain

\[
\text{Jordan}\to\text{Furey}\to\text{defect/index}
\to\text{effective action}\to\text{calibrated port},
\]

no nonempty packet among positive-cone order, \(Spin(2,3)\)/symplectic action, integer \(U(1)\) charge, chirality/index, anomaly class and calibrated spectral pole is presently transported through every stage and arrow.

This does not prove that no such functor exists. It proves that the corpus has not yet constructed one and identifies the first square on which it must act.

## 1. Why endpoint recurrence is not transport

The positive Jordan carrier supports cone order and the \(Spin_0(2,3)\cong Sp(4,\mathbb R)\) completion. Calibrated two-port response can support the same positive-cone language and reconstruct the same group at the opposite end. That is a real structural recurrence.

It is not yet a functorial derivation. Between the endpoints, the Furey description tracks integer charge and chirality, the defect description tracks bundle degree, index and anomaly, and the action tracks local fields and response poles. The present maps do not carry a common invariant through all of them.

## 2. The four arrows

1. **Jordan to Furey:** no physical charge-graded intertwiner is constructed. The primitive Peirce arrow and charge-minus-two Majorana coefficient are inequivalent \(U(1)\) representations.
2. **Furey to defect:** integer charge and chirality can be compared, but no natural map generates the asymmetric \(\mathcal O(3)\) selected / \(\mathcal O(0)\) spectator assignment.
3. **Defect to action:** this is the strongest current arrow. The restricted anomaly-free parent conditionally preserves charge, chirality/index and anomaly accounting. Sterile pairing nevertheless needs a new \(\mathcal O(-6)\) sector.
4. **Action to port:** a spectral pole can be carried conditionally, but no matched generator--state--port packet fixes its physical shift, residue, linewidth and calibrated observable.

Three arrows therefore retain free-map risk. An arbitrary basis conjugator, spurion or independently fitted port map can always manufacture formal commutativity; such a repair does not count.

## 3. The smallest repair

The first square to attack is Jordan--Furey--defect. The required object is a charge-graded natural intertwiner whose image canonically distinguishes \(\mathcal O(3)\) selected matter from \(\mathcal O(0)\) spectators.

The next finite calculation is

\[
\operatorname{Nat}_{U(1),\,\deg}
(\mathsf J\!\to\!\mathsf F,\,\mathsf J\!\to\!\mathsf D),
\]

with integer rephasing weights and \(\mathbb{CP}^1\) bundle degree retained. The route fails if this space is zero or if every nonzero element requires a freely chosen charge-two spurion or the very bundle split it was meant to derive.

## 4. Beautiful result and honest alternative

The beautiful result would be one packet that changes mathematical language without losing identity, eventually making the two endpoint appearances of \(Spin(2,3)\) consequences of one transport law.

The honest alternative is also strong: \(Spin(2,3)\) may be a recurrent response geometry reached independently at different scales, while charge, chirality and apparatus maps belong to genuinely separate layers. Claim 1469 cleanly distinguishes those possibilities.
