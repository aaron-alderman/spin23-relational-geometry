# Any source can seed an ansatz, but only typed translation, corpus collision and kill tests can promote it

**Date:** 20 October 2026

**Scope:** Claimipedia research method

**Status:** normative protocol; not a physical claim

**Depends on:** [the salvage-and-type rule for generative material](../0000-0099/25_older_llm_claim_audit_and_reconciliation.md), [living mediation as an explicitly heuristic search programme](../1100-1199/1185_living_mediation_as_the_missing_transport_from_jordan_distinction_to_physical_realization.md), and [the dependency-frontier definition of exhaustion](../1200-1299/1231_genuine_exhaustion_is_a_dependency_frontier_not_a_document_count.md)

**Evidence:** [machine-readable protocol](../../../evidence/data/claimipedia-speculative-ansatz-playground.json) and [executable verifier](../../../verification/verify/verify_claimipedia_speculative_ansatz_playground.mjs)

## Result

Claimipedia may use any human source as a search-direction generator: established mathematics, theology, scripture, myth, art, dreams, altered-state reports, visionary or highly associative narratives, failed theories, anomalies and coincidences. Admission as inspiration grants no evidential weight. A source may choose **where to look**, never **what to conclude**.

The resulting idea enters a staged ansatz playground:

1. record the raw signpost without sanitizing its provenance;
2. strip its authority and extract a typed motif;
3. translate that motif into at least two non-equivalent mathematical forms;
4. collide both forms with a named corpus gate or missing middle;
5. write the smallest explicit objects, maps, action or equations;
6. run the cheapest discriminating calculation and a negative control;
7. promote the survivor by one epistemic level, or archive the failure as a result.

No path runs directly from evocative material to truth.

## 1. Permissive input and strict output

Discovery is not justification. A biblical image, a dream, a psychedelic report, a hallucination-like narrative, a diagram or a failed calculation may suggest recursion, triality, hidden/manifest duality, descent, mediation, boundary or nested rotation. Those motifs can be productive precisely because translation is allowed to wander.

But the source story is removed before testing. If an ansatz works only while its sacred, visionary or aesthetic interpretation is visible, it has not yet become mathematics or physics. Altered-state reports are phenomenological material about experience; they do not establish an external ontology. Highly associative or disorganized narratives can be mined for structures without being treated as pathology, revelation or measurement.

This lets the corpus preserve hope without laundering it into evidence.

## 2. Promotion ladder

The allowed labels are **SIGNPOST**, **ANALOGY**, **TYPED-ANSATZ**, **EXACT-LOCAL**, **CONDITIONAL-BRIDGE** and **EMPIRICAL**. A signpost cannot jump directly to exact, conditional or empirical status, and analogy cannot jump directly to empirical status. Each promotion must cite the calculation or observation that earns it.

## 3. Beauty is a search prior, not a likelihood update

Beauty may rank what to calculate first. Useful components are compression, naturality, unification, few free parameters and strong falsifiability. The corresponding debts are untyped arrows, fitted coefficients, post-hoc repair and evidence leakage. A schematic queueing score is

\[
Q=B_{\rm compression}+B_{\rm unification}+B_{\rm naturality}+B_{\rm falsifiability}
-D_{\rm parameters}-D_{\rm types}-D_{\rm leakage}.
\]

This is not a posterior probability. Its own calibration is empirical: source-blind rankings must beat complexity-matched random rankings on held-out consequences.

## 4. Mandatory controls

Every promoted arc must include source-blind reconstruction, a competing translation, a scrambled baseline, a held-out consequence and an explicit failure value. This controls apophenia: repeated structure is interesting only when an invariant survives relabeling and predicts something outside the pattern used to notice it.

## 5. Salami slicing used constructively

Small claims are useful when each slice reduces a dependency: one Hom-space, anomaly polynomial, commuting square, spectrum, identifiability rank or calibrated null. They are not useful when they merely restate the same analogy. The unit of progress is the smallest result that changes the dependency graph.

## 6. Verdict

The earlier exhaustion result closed untyped continuation from the then-current inputs. It did not prohibit new hypothesis generation. This protocol reopens exploration as a controlled queue while leaving every physical-admission flag unchanged.

\[
\boxed{\text{wild source}\longrightarrow\text{typed motif}\longrightarrow\text{killable ansatz},
\qquad \text{never}\quad \text{wild source}\longrightarrow\text{evidence}.}
\]
