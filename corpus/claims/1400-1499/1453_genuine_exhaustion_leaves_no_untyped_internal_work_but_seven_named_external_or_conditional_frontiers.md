## 1. Status words

The terminal register uses five non-interchangeable labels.

- **Established:** a theorem, typed construction or executable fixture is valid under declared hypotheses. An established subclaim need not pass its gate.
- **Conditionally constructed:** the required object exists after named coefficients, domains, backgrounds or controls are supplied.
- **Internally exhausted:** every route using only current corpus inputs reaches a result, scoped no-go or named external datum. Recombining the same structures is not frontier work.
- **Externally open:** a specified new action, domain, coefficient, measurement or device calculation could change the status.
- **Failed:** one precisely stated candidate class is ruled out. The failure is not generalized beyond its assumptions.

This vocabulary prevents “blocked” from hiding whether the remaining work is calculational, empirical, or genuinely absent from the current theory.

**Stop.** Further manipulation of the charge-three phase cannot turn gauge-equivalent vacua into a physical wall.

**Forbidden overclaim.** \(S_{\rm v1}\) alone derives the defect or its sign; the finite slab automatically inherits the exact infinite-line tanh spectrum.
