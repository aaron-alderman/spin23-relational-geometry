## Result

Allowing the up and down sectors to see the shared loop connection with
different charges is the smallest apparent repair of claim 1484.  It fails
as a natural Standard Model identification.  Normalize the up-sector flux
weight to one and call the down/up weight ratio \(q\).  In the simultaneous
light-hierarchy and third-family-decoupling limit, the observed Cabibbo
overlap requires

\[
\boxed{
q\le\left(2|V_{us}|^2-1\right){r_d\over r_u}
=-23.1503335.
}
\]

The equal-magnitude conjugate choice \(q=-1\) is impossible in this limit.
So is the quark electric-charge ratio
\(Q_d/Q_u=(-1/3)/(2/3)=-1/2\).  A sector character can rotate the light
doublet far enough only by introducing an unexplained charge hierarchy of
at least 23.15 relative to the up normalization.

## Derivation

Retain the democratic expansion of claim 1484.  Let

\[
A={1-2\eta\over3}
\]

be the scale of the edge-plane part of the down Bloch vector.  If the down
sector sees flux \(q\) times the up flux, then

\[
d_{\rm edge}=A u_{\rm edge},
\qquad
d_{\rm flux}=\eta q u_{\rm flux}.
\]

Let \(f=|u_{\rm flux}|^2/|u|^2\) and let \(\theta\) be the Bloch angle.
Hierarchy matching gives

\[
{|d|\over|u|}=\eta R,
\qquad R={r_d\over r_u},
\]

while the physical upper-state overlap gives

\[
C=\cos\theta=2|V_{us}|^2-1.
\]

Taking the dot product and dividing by \(\eta|u|^2\) yields the exact
first-order relation

\[
CR=\left({1\over3\eta}-{2\over3}\right)(1-f)+qf.
\]

The right side is a convex combination of \(q\) and
\(1/(3\eta)-2/3\), and the latter is always greater than \(-2/3\) for
\(\eta>0\).  The data give

\[
C=-0.8987409998,
\qquad R=25.7586262,
\qquad CR=-23.1503335.
\]

Since \(CR<-2/3\), the other endpoint of the convex combination must obey
\(q\le CR\).  This proves the bound without selecting the flux fraction or
the value of \(\eta\).

fluxes, two edge perturbations, the down degree/hopping ratio and the maximal
two-phase diagonal orbit.  Three independent seeds converged to the same

\[

\[
(|V_{us}|,|V_{cb}|,|V_{ub}|,|J|)
\simeq(0.6073,0.0380,0.00437,3.05\times10^{-5}).

The search did not find a joined solution and is not a global-minimum
certificate.  Its flight to a very large negative charge ratio is, however,
consistent with the analytic lower bound on \(|q|\).

## What this removes and what survives

The result removes two superficially attractive middles:

- opposite orientation with equal charge magnitude;
- electromagnetic charge weighting of the same Abelian loop.

It does not forbid a large emergent representation weight, but no declared
Spin(2,3), Furey, Standard Model or defect datum supplies one near 23, much
less the numerical basin near 135.  Installing it would move rather than
explain the hierarchy.

The smallest surviving fan is therefore not another scalar charge.  It is a
noncentral endomorphism of the common \(A_2\) doublet that preserves the
centroid singlet.  The next top-down question is whether such an intertwiner
is generated by the defect superconnection, Peirce action or an \(S_3\)
orientation tensor; the next bottom-up test is whether one coefficient then
fits the Cabibbo angle while leaving a held-out third-family or CP invariant.
Physical admission remains false.
