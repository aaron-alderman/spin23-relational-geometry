# Anomaly Cancellation Cannot Distinguish a CAR Adjoint from an Independent Mirror Generation

**Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Branches:** B03, B06, B08 and B11  
**Claim status:** exact one-generation anomaly and beta-function audit; complete threshold phenomenology of a possible mirror sector remains open

## 1. The tempting but invalid test

One might try to decide whether the conjugate half of the completed Furey carrier is a CAR adjoint or an independent mirror by checking anomalies.

That test cannot work by itself because one Standard-Model generation is already anomaly free. A conjugate mirror generation is separately anomaly free, and their direct sum remains anomaly free.

## 2. One-generation anomaly inventory

Using left-handed Weyl fields

\[
Q_{1/6},\quad u^c_{-2/3},\quad d^c_{1/3},
\quad L_{-1/2},\quad e^c_{1},
\]

the Abelian cubic sum is

\[
6\left(\frac16\right)^3
+3\left(-\frac23\right)^3
+3\left(\frac13\right)^3
+2\left(-\frac12\right)^3
+1=0.
\]

The mixed gravitational sum is

\[
6\left(\frac16\right)
+3\left(-\frac23\right)
+3\left(\frac13\right)
+2\left(-\frac12\right)+1=0.
\]

Likewise,

\[
[SU(3)]^2U(1)=0,
\qquad
[SU(2)]^2U(1)=0,
\qquad
[SU(3)]^3=0.
\]

There are four weak doublets after color multiplicity, so the Witten \(SU(2)\) parity test also passes.

## 3. Why an independent mirror also passes

The conjugate representation reverses every perturbative chiral-anomaly sign. Since each original total is zero, its conjugate total is also zero. Adding both gives

\[
0+0=0.
\]

The weak-doublet count becomes eight, still even. Therefore all standard anomaly checks give the same answer for:

1. one chiral generation represented self-dually with adjoints;
2. one chiral generation plus an independent conjugate mirror generation.

Anomaly freedom is necessary for both and distinguishes neither.

## 4. What does distinguish them

### CAR basis projection

In the self-dual description, a rank-15 basis projection selects 15 independent annihilation modes. The conjugate half is fixed as their adjoints.

An independent mirror has another 15 annihilation modes. This changes finite-mode Fock dimension from

\[
2^{15}
\quad\text{to}\quad
2^{30}.
\]

### Gauge running

For one generation, the Weyl-fermion contributions to one-loop gauge coefficients are, in ordinary hypercharge normalization,

\[
\Delta b_1=\frac{20}{9},
\qquad
\Delta b_2=\frac43,
\qquad
\Delta b_3=\frac43.
\]

An independent mirror doubles all three matter contributions above its mass threshold. A CAR adjoint does not: adjoint field operators are not an additional species in loops.

### Chiral spectrum and masses

An independent conjugate generation permits gauge-invariant vectorlike mass pairings and changes electroweak precision, Higgs and flavor observables. A Nambu adjoint merely implements the ordinary field/adjoint relation.

### Operator algebra

The decisive algebraic test remains

\[
B(F)^*=B(\Gamma_{\rm CAR}F)
\]

with a complementary basis projection—not a vanishing anomaly polynomial.

## 5. Consequence for the Furey programme

The earlier anomaly-free TRO classification remains valuable: it removes genuinely anomalous chiral support patterns. It cannot certify that an exchange-completed carrier contains no mirrors.

The no-mirror claim requires all of:

- conjugate gauge and Lorentz action on the second half;
- a self-dual CAR involution;
- a rank-15 basis projection;
- no independent mirror creation operators;
- Standard-Model rather than doubled beta-function contributions;
- the correct chiral kinetic and Yukawa spectrum.

## 6. Threshold caveat

A very heavy independent mirror could decouple from low-energy running, but it would still be a physical sector. Its threshold corrections, mass-generation mechanism and effect on naturalness would have to be included. “Not yet observed” is not equivalent to “CAR redundant.”

Conversely, matching low-energy beta functions does not by itself prove CAR identification if a mirror lies above the measured range. The operator-algebra test remains primary.

## 7. Stop-everything and falsification gates

**Stop-everything trigger:** the completed Furey kinetic theory exhibits the rank-15 CAR basis projection and reproduces undoubled Standard-Model running.

**Mirror trigger:** an additional threshold contributes another \((20/9,4/3,4/3)\) per generation to the fermionic beta coefficients.

**Anomaly falsifier:** vanishing anomaly sums alone are cited as proof that the conjugate carrier half is not an independent mirror.

**Decoupling falsifier:** a heavy mirror is removed from the theory without matching its threshold and mass-generation effects.
