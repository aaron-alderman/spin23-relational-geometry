## 2. Static positive kernel

Let

\[
\gamma=\frac54\sqrt{\frac{11}{9}}
=\frac{5\sqrt{11}}{12}.
\]

Define

\[
K_0=
\begin{pmatrix}
4&1-i\gamma&1\\
1+i\gamma&4&1\\
1&1&3
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

It is Hermitian and strictly diagonally dominant with positive diagonal, hence

\[
K_0>0.
\]

Its loop product is

\[
K_{12}K_{23}K_{31}=1-i\gamma,
\]

so the static inverse-propagator kernel already carries nonzero oriented
holonomy.

## 3. Causal continuation

Take

\[
\boxed{K(z)=K_0-zI_3.}
\]

For (operatorname{Im}z>0),

\[
\operatorname{Im}K(z)=-(\operatorname{Im}z)I_3<0.
\]

Thus (K) is an anti-Herglotz inverse propagator. Its Green matrix

\[
G(z)=K(z)^{-1}
\]

satisfies

\[
\operatorname{Im}G(z)
=(\operatorname{Im}z)G(z)^\dagger G(z)>0.
\]

The corresponding correlator is strictly causal/passive in the adopted
convention.

## 4. Eliminate the third port at kernel level

Partition with

\[
A(z)=
\begin{pmatrix}

1+i\gamma&4-z

\qquad

\qquad
d(z)=3-z.

\[
K_{\rm eff}(z)

\[

The raw oriented coefficient is (gamma).

## 5. Canonical residue normalization

Expand at low frequency:

\[
K_{\rm eff}(z)
=K_{\rm eff}(0)-zZ+O(z^2),
\]

where

\[
Z=I_2+\frac{BB^\dagger}{9}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
10/9&1/9\\1/9&10/9
\end{pmatrix}>0.
\]

Its determinant is

\[
\det Z=\frac{11}{9}.
\]

Canonical fields are obtained with (x=Z^{-1/2}x_c), giving

\[
H_c=Z^{-1/2}K_{\rm eff}(0)Z^{-1/2}.
\]

For any real (2\times2) matrix (A),

\[
A\sigma_yA^T=(\det A)\sigma_y.
\]

Therefore the canonically normalized oriented coefficient is

\[
\boxed{

=\frac{\gamma}{\sqrt{\det Z}}
=\frac54=1.25.}
\]

This is exactly one of the finite-lattice checkpoints where both deck sectors
had negative reference/defect relative Pfaffian.
