## 3. Meaning of the missing population coordinate

An exchange-fixed vector obeys

\[
v_B=\overline{v_A}.
\]

Consequently

\[
\|v_A\|^2=\|v_B\|^2.
\]

At the same time, \(v_A\) may retain arbitrary complex coefficients. The relationship coordinate between the survivors remains complex and supplies the two spacelike directions of the fixed \(H_2(\mathbb R)\).

## 6. What remains unproved

Three identifications are still missing.

### 6.1 Source derivation

The exchange does not extend to an automorphism of the native real direct-sum division algebra. It is exact on the completed complex carrier because the two neutral blocks become isomorphic there.

### 6.2 Physical face identification

The pair index labels two surviving TRO topologies. It has not been proved that these are the same two faces measured by the external stress-response carrier.

### 6.3 Dynamical preservation

Gauge generators, cyclic traces, Higgs locking, CAR/Fock completion and radiative corrections must all preserve or covariantly transport the exchange.

Accordingly, the result is an exact emergent-carrier intertwiner candidate, not yet a microscopic derivation.

## 7. Relation to flavor CP

Exchange fixing pairs a coefficient \(y\) with \(\bar y\); it does not require \(y\in\mathbb R\). This is compatible with complex parameter data at the paired-carrier level.

However, three-generation CP survival still requires an explicit basis-invariant calculation after the survivor pairing, gauge action and Yukawa matrices are all included. The result prevents a premature reality no-go but does not by itself prove a nonzero CKM/PMNS invariant.
