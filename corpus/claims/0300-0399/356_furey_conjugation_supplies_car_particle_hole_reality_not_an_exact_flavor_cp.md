# Furey Conjugation Supplies CAR Particle–Hole Reality, Not an Exact Flavor CP

**Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Branches:** B01, B03, B05, B06, B08, B09 and B11  
**Claim status:** exact separation of intrinsic Furey/CAR conjugation from the optional BDI/generalized-CP reality; the selector-to-physical-coordinate map remains open

## 1. A necessary correction to the previous gate

The preceding analysis asked whether one fixed antiunitary could make the
selector symbol reflection-real while remaining compatible with all Yukawa
couplings. That phrasing risks conflating two different operations:

1. **BdG/CAR particle–hole conjugation**, which is an exact redundancy of a
   self-dual fermionic description;
2. **time reversal or generalized CP**, which is a physical symmetry and may be
   broken by complex flavor data.

Class D requires the first, not the second. The integer BDI lift uses both.

This distinction reopens the branch that looked blocked by flavor CP.

## 2. What Furey actually supplies

In Furey's complex-quaternionic construction, ordinary complex conjugation

\[
*:\ i\mapsto-i
\]

maps a left-handed Weyl generalized ideal to the right-handed one and satisfies
\(*^2=1\). Majorana generalized ideals are the fixed and anti-fixed real
combinations

\[
\Psi_{M_1}=\frac12(\Psi_D+\Psi_D^*),
\qquad
\Psi_{M_2}=\frac12(\Psi_D-\Psi_D^*).
\]

In the complex-octonionic ladder construction, the same complex conjugation
relates the particle ideal to its antiparticle ideal. Thus the source algebra
already contains a canonical conjugating operation; it is not necessary to
invent a flavor CP transformation to obtain particle/adjoint exchange.

## 3. The self-dual CAR carrier

For a charged representation \(V\), use

\[
\mathcal K=V\oplus\bar V,
\qquad
\rho_{\rm CAR}(g)=
\begin{pmatrix}\rho(g)&0\\0&\bar\rho(g)\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The intrinsic CAR antiunitary is

\[
\boxed{J_0=\tau_xK.}
\]

It obeys

\[
J_0^2=1,
\qquad
J_0\rho_{\rm CAR}(g)J_0^{-1}=\rho_{\rm CAR}(g).
\]

This remains true for complex charged representations because coefficient
conjugation and particle/adjoint exchange occur together. This \(J_0\) is the
algebraic counterpart of BdG particle–hole redundancy. It does **not** say that
the Yukawa matrices are CP invariant.

## 4. The second antiunitary has a different job

A second antiunitary

\[
J_W=\begin{pmatrix}0&W\\W^T&0\end{pmatrix}K
\]

can encode a multiplicity flag, time-reversal-like structure or generalized CP.
Its relative product with \(J_0\) is the linear grading

\[
J_WJ_0=\begin{pmatrix}W&0\\0&W^T\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Unlike \(J_0\), a nontrivial \(J_W\) is generally broken by misaligned Yukawas
and the exceptional CP triangle. This breaking removes the BDI integer as an
exact physical classification, but it need not remove class-D parity.

The corrected hierarchy is

\[
\begin{array}{ccl}
J_0&:&\text{exact CAR/BdG particle--hole reality},\\
J_W&:&\text{optional BDI or generalized-CP lift},\\
J_W\text{ broken},\ J_0\text{ exact}
&:&\mathbb Z\longrightarrow\mathbb Z_2.
\end{array}
\]

## 5. Explicit BDI-breaking, class-D-preserving deformation

Start from the odd-winding representative

\[
H_m(k)=\cos(mk)\tau_z+\sin(mk)\tau_y.
\]

Add

\[
\delta H(k)=\lambda\sin k\,\tau_x.
\]

For \(\lambda\ne0\), this breaks \(T=K\) and the chiral symmetry generated by
\(\tau_x\). But it preserves

\[
C=\tau_xK,
\qquad
CH(k)C^{-1}=-H(-k).
\]

The spectrum remains gapped because

\[
E(k)^2=1+\lambda^2\sin^2k>0.
\]

At \(k=0,\pi\), the perturbation vanishes, so the class-D Pfaffian invariant is
unchanged:

\[
\boxed{\nu_D=(-1)^m.}
\]

This demonstrates directly that the CP/time-reversal-like reality may break
while odd Majorana parity survives.

## 6. Consequence for the exceptional CP texture

The matrix

\[
M=
\begin{pmatrix}
S&r&\alpha\\
r&T&i\beta\\
\alpha&i\beta&O
\end{pmatrix}
\]

breaks ordinary entrywise flavor reality when \(\beta\ne0\), and breaks the
quarter-turned flag reality when \(r\ne0\). Nonzero \(r\alpha\beta\) therefore
prevents both candidate generalized-CP operations from being exact.

It does not break \(J_0\), because \(J_0\) exchanges every coefficient-bearing
particle block with its adjoint block. The BdG Hamiltonian is constructed so
that this exchange is exact even when its pairing amplitudes are complex.

Hence the exceptional CP phase is compatible in principle with class-D parity.
It removes an integer refinement; it does not automatically remove the parity.

## 7. What Takagi factorization does—and does not—mean

Takagi factorization can diagonalize one complex symmetric Majorana matrix. It
is useful for constructing a canonical BDI representative of that isolated
block.

But the mass-dependent Takagi basis is not the source of exact BdG
particle–hole symmetry. That role belongs to \(J_0\). Nor does Takagi
factorization make physical flavor CP disappear, because CP resides in relative
misalignment among several coupling matrices.

This removes the apparent contradiction between a complex CP-violating
Majorana texture, exact CAR self-duality and a surviving class-D
\(\mathbb Z_2\) invariant.

## 8. What cross-branch dependency has been resolved

The previous branch required a single antiunitary to serve simultaneously as
CAR conjugation and exact generalized CP. That was too strong.

The Furey source and the existing CAR analysis together show

\[
\boxed{\text{exact }J_0\text{ is available without exact flavor CP}.}
\]

Therefore generic observed CP violation is no longer a direct obstruction to
the selector's class-D parity.

## 9. What remains genuinely unresolved

Three physical dependencies survive:

1. **Coordinate:** selector angle must become a momentum, defect or adiabatic
   coordinate.
2. **Locality:** the corresponding BdG/Toeplitz operator must be local or arise
   from a controlled effective limit.
3. **Mass sewing:** the selector-derived mass must obey the antipodal law while
   remaining gapped after all permitted Standard-Model couplings are included.

The antiunitary itself is no longer the principal blocker.

## 10. Stop-everything and falsification gates

**Conflation falsifier:** breaking generalized CP is claimed to break the exact

**Doubling falsifier:** \(V\oplus\bar V\) is counted as twice as many independent

**Gauge falsifier:** the unitary swap \(\tau_x\) is used alone on a charged
sector; gauge equivariance requires the antiunitary \(\tau_xK\).

**Topology falsifier:** class-D parity is claimed to survive a bulk gap closure
or a failure of particle–hole sewing.
