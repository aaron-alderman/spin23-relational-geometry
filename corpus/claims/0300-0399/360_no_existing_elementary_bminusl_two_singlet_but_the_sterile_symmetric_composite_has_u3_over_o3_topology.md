# No Existing Elementary \(B-L=\pm2\) Singlet, but the Sterile Symmetric Composite Has \(U(3)/O(3)\) Topology

**Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Branches:** B01, B02, B03, B05, B06, B08, B09, B11 and B13

## 1. Inventory question

The \(B-L\) vortex completion needs a complex field with

\[
(\mathbf1,\mathbf1)_0,
\qquad B-L=\pm2.
\]

The question is not whether such a representation can be added. It is whether
one has already been derived somewhere in the Jordan/Furey programme.

## 2. External Jordan locking sector

The full lock contains

\[
\Delta\sim(\mathbf1,\mathbf3)_1,
\qquad
\Delta^\dagger\sim(\mathbf1,\mathbf3)_{-1},
\qquad
\xi\sim(\mathbf1,\mathbf3)_0.
\]

If the allowed \(LL\Delta\) portal is assigned \(B-L\) covariantly, then

\[
B-L(\Delta)=+2.
\]

The desired charge occurs, but \(\Delta\) is a weak triplet with hypercharge
one, not an SM singlet. Its vacuum breaks electroweak symmetry and cannot serve
as the pure \(B-L\) scalar.

The custodial singlet appearing after symmetry breaking is a mixture of neutral
triplet components. It is not an independent gauge-singlet field before
breaking and does not repair this mismatch.

## 3. Furey Higgs and endomorphism sectors

The identified Higgs-like candidates are

\[
(\mathbf1,\mathbf2)_{1/2},
\]

so they are also excluded.

The corrected endomorphism algebra contains seven
\((\mathbf1,\mathbf1)_0\) gauge representations. Four additional weak/color
singlets have hypercharge \(\pm1\) and are not full gauge singlets. The
inventory does not assign the seven neutral copies:

- bosonic statistics;
- a fundamental scalar kinetic term;
- \(B-L=\pm2\);
- a unique source projector.

Gauge singlet multiplicity is therefore not evidence for the required scalar.
Assigning one of the seven entries the desired role would be a hand-picked
completion.

## 4. Exceptional and Jordan diagonal coordinates

Real trace or diagonal Jordan coordinates can be gauge singlets, but a real
coordinate cannot carry a continuous nonzero \(B-L\) charge by itself. Two real
coordinates could be assembled into a complex field only after deriving:

1. a complex structure on that pair;
2. the \(B-L\) action;
3. the correct scalar statistics and kinetic term;
4. a Majorana Yukawa map to \(\nu^c\nu^c\).

None is currently supplied by the diagonal Jordan coordinates.

## 5. Exhaustive conclusion for elementary candidates

Among the scalar representations presently derived and assigned a physical
role,

\[
\boxed{
N_{\rm elementary}\big[(\mathbf1,\mathbf1)_0, |B-L|=2\big]=0.}
\]

The minimal fundamental \(B-L\) scalar in the previous document is genuinely
new relative to the current corpus.

## 6. The composite candidate is unavoidable

For three sterile left-handed fields \(\nu^c_a\), the Lorentz scalar

\[
\boxed{
\Sigma_{ab}\sim\nu^c_a\nu^c_b}
\]

is symmetric in flavor:

\[
\Sigma_{ab}=\Sigma_{ba}.
\]

It has

\[
(\mathbf1,\mathbf1)_0,
\qquad B-L=+2,
\]

and spans

\[
\operatorname{Sym}^2(\mathbb C^3),
\qquad
\dim_{\mathbb C}=6.
\]

Its conjugate or Hubbard--Stratonovich auxiliary field has \(B-L=-2\), exactly
the charge needed to couple to \(\nu^c\nu^c\).

This candidate is not added representation theory. It is already present as a
fermion bilinear. What is absent is a derivation that makes it a propagating or
condensing order parameter.

## 7. Isotropic condensate and Takagi orbit

Suppose, conditionally, that approximately \(U(3)\)-symmetric sterile dynamics
produces a full-rank isotropic condensate

\[
\langle\Sigma\rangle=vI_3.
\]

Under

\[
\Sigma\longmapsto U\Sigma U^T,
\qquad U\in U(3),
\]

the stabilizer of \(I_3\) is

\[
U U^T=I
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
U\in O(3).
\]

The vacuum orbit is therefore

\[
\boxed{\mathcal M_\Sigma=U(3)/O(3).}
\]

Its real dimension is

\[
9-3=6,
\]

matching the six angular directions of a fixed-singular-value complex symmetric
matrix.

## 8. Fundamental group and primitive vortex

The symmetric-unitary realization of the orbit is

\[
\Sigma/v=UU^T.
\]

For \(n=3\),

\[
\boxed{\pi_1(U(3)/O(3))\cong\mathbb Z.}
\]

A primitive loop is

\[
\Sigma_1(\varphi)
=v\operatorname{diag}(e^{i\varphi},1,1),
\qquad 0\leq\varphi\leq2\pi.
\]

Its determinant winds once. A Takagi lift is

\[
U(\varphi)=\operatorname{diag}(e^{i\varphi/2},1,1),
\]

which ends at

\[
U(2\pi)=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1)\in O(3).
\]

The path is open in \(U(3)\) but closed in the quotient—precisely the structure
that the selector deck discussion kept encountering.

## 9. The common-phase vortex is charge three

The isotropic phase loop

\[
\Sigma_3(\varphi)=ve^{i\varphi}I_3
\]

has

\[
\det\Sigma_3=v^3e^{3i\varphi}.
\]

Therefore it represents class three, not the primitive class, in
\(\pi_1(U(3)/O(3))\):

\[
\boxed{[e^{i\varphi}I_3]=3[\operatorname{diag}(e^{i\varphi},1,1)].}
\]

This explains the index-three vortex structurally. It is three coincident
primitive flavor vortices. In a flavor-anisotropic potential it may split into
separate unit defects; in a locking potential it may remain bound.

## 10. Trace plus fiveplet decomposition

After the condensate selects \(O(3)\), symmetric flavor perturbations decompose
as

\[
\operatorname{Sym}^2(\mathbf3)
=\mathbf1\oplus\mathbf5.
\]

The trace is the isotropic singlet direction; the traceless symmetric tensor is
a fiveplet.

This mirrors the \(1+5\) decomposition seen in the custodial locking sector, but
the groups act on different spaces:

- here \(O(3)\) is sterile flavor;
- there custodial \(SO(3)\) acts on electroweak locking indices.

The matching dimensions are a candidate locking clue, not an identification.

## 11. Dynamical and symmetry caveats

The orbit \(U(3)/O(3)\) requires an approximately \(U(3)\)-symmetric potential
for \(\Sigma\). Generic Dirac Yukawas, charged-lepton alignment and the
exceptional flavor texture explicitly reduce that symmetry.

Moreover, a fermion bilinear does not automatically become a weakly coupled
elementary scalar. One would need:

1. an attractive four-fermion or gauge channel;
2. a gap equation or effective potential favoring condensation;
3. a kinetic term and compositeness scale;
4. control of gauge and gravitational anomalies;
5. a demonstration that the desired vortex remains stable after explicit
   flavor breaking.

None has yet been derived.
