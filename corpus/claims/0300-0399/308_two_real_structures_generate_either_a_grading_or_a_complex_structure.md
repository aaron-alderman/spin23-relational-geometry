# Two Real Structures Generate Either a Grading or a Complex Structure

**Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Branches:** B01, B03, B04, B06, B08 and B11  
**Claim status:** exact finite-dimensional antiunitary classification; the commutation sign for the completed Furey selector and CAR involution remains undetermined

## 1. The new compatibility question

The programme now requires two square-(+1) antiunitaries:

\[
\Theta_{\rm sel}^2=1,
\qquad
\Gamma_{\rm CAR}^2=1.
\]

The first selects the population-normal response; the second identifies particles with CAR adjoints. Their relative algebra determines whether the retained complex structure is independent or generated automatically.

## 2. General product theorem

Let \(A\) and \(B\) be antiunitary involutions on a complex Hilbert space. Their product

\[
C=AB
\]

is complex linear and unitary.

### Commuting branch

If

\[
AB=BA,
\]

then

\[
\boxed{C^2=+1.}
\]

The product is an involutive linear grading. It decomposes the carrier into \(C=\pm1\) sectors but does not supply multiplication by \(i\) on an underlying real space.

### Anticommuting branch

If

\[
AB=-BA,
\]

then

\[
\boxed{C^2=-1.}
\]

The product is a linear complex structure. Both real structures reverse it:

\[
ACA^{-1}=-C,
\qquad
BCB^{-1}=-C.
\]

Thus two real structures can generate the missing complex orientation, but only in the anticommuting branch.

## 3. Explicit two-dimensional models

With \(K\) standard conjugation:

### Commuting example

\[
A=K,
\qquad
B=\sigma_xK.
\]

Both square to one and

\[
AB=BA=\sigma_x,
\qquad \sigma_x^2=1.
\]

### Anticommuting example

\[
A=\sigma_xK,
\qquad
B=\sigma_zK.
\]

Both square to one, while

\[
AB=\sigma_x\sigma_z
=\begin{pmatrix}0&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
(AB)^2=-I.
\]

The product is the standard real complex structure on \(\mathbb R^2\).

## 4. Application to the Furey programme

The candidate operations have different origins:

\[
\Theta_{\rm sel}=sK_0,
\qquad
\Gamma_{\rm CAR}=sC_F.
\]

Their product is the linear operator

\[
\Theta_{\rm sel}\Gamma_{\rm CAR}
=K_0C_F
\]

when the survivor swap commutes with both. This product measures the difference between bare coefficient conjugation and Furey’s physical gauge/Lorentz conjugation.

The corpus has not yet constructed both maps on one explicit basis, so it has not established whether they commute, anticommute or have a more general unitary product.

## 5. Relation to the retained CP complex structure

The three-family exchange construction retained

\[
\mathcal J=\operatorname{diag}(iI_3,-iI_3).
\]

At present \(\mathcal J\) is an independently specified exchange-invariant operator. It must not be identified with \(\Theta_{\rm sel}\Gamma_{\rm CAR}\) without checking:

\[
(\Theta_{\rm sel}\Gamma_{\rm CAR})^2=-1
\]

and matching its action on gauge, Lorentz and flavor generators.

There are two live outcomes:

1. **generated-complex-structure outcome:** the antiunitaries anticommute and their product is \(\mathcal J\) up to an allowed unitary equivalence;
2. **independent-complex-structure outcome:** they commute or have a nontrivial unitary product, so \(\mathcal J\) is extra geometric data requiring its own preservation law.

## 6. Why this resembles—but does not yet prove—a KO-dimension structure

Relations among a real structure, grading, complex structure and Dirac operator are central in real spectral triples and Clifford-module classifications. The signs found here are of that type.

However, no KO dimension should be assigned until the programme specifies:

- the full Hilbert/Fock space;
- the Dirac or kinetic operator;
- grading parity;
- the actions of both antiunitaries on that operator;
- the exact sign table.

Recognizing the pattern is useful; naming a spectral triple prematurely would be overclaiming.

## 7. Stop-everything and falsification gates

**Stop-everything trigger:** an explicit common Furey basis gives \(\{\Theta_{\rm sel},\Gamma_{\rm CAR}\}=0\) and identifies their product with the CP-retaining \(\mathcal J\).

**Grading trigger:** the maps commute and their product supplies a physically meaningful chirality or survivor grading compatible with the kinetic operator.

**Complex-structure falsifier:** \(\mathcal J\) is declared generated by the two antiunitaries without verifying the anticommutation sign and generator action.

**Spectral-triple falsifier:** a KO dimension is assigned without a Dirac operator and full sign table.
