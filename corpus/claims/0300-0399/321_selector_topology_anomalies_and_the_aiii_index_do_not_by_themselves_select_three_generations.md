# Selector Topology, Anomalies and the AIII Index Do Not by Themselves Select Three Generations

**Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Branches:** B01, B03, B06, B08, B09, B10 and B11  
**Claim status:** exact no-go for generation selection from the present selector topology, Standard-Model anomaly equations and multiplicative AIII index; exceptional/cubic generation mechanisms remain open

## 1. A necessary honesty check

The textured joint operator has

\[
\operatorname{ind}\mathcal D=N_g.
\]

For the observed Standard Model this equals three. But \(N_g\) entered through the number of copies of the \(8+7\) internal Yukawa complex.

Therefore the index currently **records and protects** the generation count; it does not derive that count.

This document asks whether the selector topology or anomaly constraints supply the missing equation \(N_g=3\). They do not.

## 2. The selector bundle sees parity, not three

The selector director has

\[
\pi_1(\mathbb{RP}^2)=\mathbb Z_2.
\]

One exchange bundle has nontrivial \(\mathbb Z_2\) holonomy. For \(N_g\) identical copies, the stable torsion class adds modulo two:

\[
\boxed{
[E_{N_g}]=N_g\pmod2.}
\]

Consequently

\[
[E_1]=[E_3]=[E_5],
\qquad
[E_2]=[E_4].
\]

The nontrivial selector loop can distinguish odd from even multiplicity, but not one from three.

The raw vector-bundle rank of course reveals how many copies were inserted. That is bookkeeping, not a topological derivation.

## 3. Perturbative anomalies cancel generation by generation

For one left-handed Standard-Model generation,

\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
 &Q&u^c&d^c&L&e^c\\
\hline
Y&1/6&-2/3&1/3&-1/2&1
\end{array}
\]

with the usual color and weak multiplicities.

The mixed gravitational anomaly is

\[
6\left(\frac16\right)
+3\left(-\frac23\right)
+3\left(\frac13\right)
+2\left(-\frac12\right)
+1=0.
\]

The cubic hypercharge anomaly is

\[
6\left(\frac16\right)^3
+3\left(-\frac23\right)^3
+3\left(\frac13\right)^3
+2\left(-\frac12\right)^3
+1=0.
\]

Likewise,

\[
[SU(3)]^2U(1)=0,
\qquad
[SU(2)]^2U(1)=0,
\qquad
[SU(3)]^3=0
\]

within each generation.

Multiplying zero by \(N_g\) imposes no condition on \(N_g\).

## 4. The Witten anomaly also fails to select three

Each generation contains four left-handed \(SU(2)\) doublets after color multiplicity:

\[
3Q_L+L_L.
\]

For \(N_g\) generations the number is

\[
4N_g,
\]

which is even for every integer \(N_g\). Thus the global \(SU(2)\) anomaly gives no generation-count condition.

## 5. Why the AIII index is still valuable

The failure to derive three does not make the index empty. Once a chiral internal complex is given, it proves:

- the net number of textured defect zeros cannot change under AIII-preserving deformations;
- complex flavor mixing and CP phases do not remove them;
- Dirac completion balances the index;
- Majorana masses break its protecting grading;
- a wall can transport the generation multiplicity into localized modes.

What it cannot say is why the internal complex occurs three times.

The logical direction is

\[
N_g
\Longrightarrow
\chi_Y=N_g
\Longrightarrow
\operatorname{ind}\mathcal D=N_g,
\]

not the reverse.

## 6. What could still select three?

A genuine derivation needs additional structure that is intrinsically ternary or rank three. Candidate branches already present in the programme include:

1. the cubic norm of \(H_3(\mathbb O)\) or \(H_3(\mathbb C)\);
2. three primitive idempotents in a rank-three Jordan frame;
3. a triality orbit with a canonical, nonduplicated matter assignment;
4. an arithmetic orbit count fixed by an integral exceptional order;
5. a flavor bundle whose Chern, spectral-flow or monodromy data has minimal nontrivial rank three;
6. a cubic consistency equation whose three roots correspond to inequivalent generations.

None of these has yet been shown to reproduce three copies of the complete chiral Standard-Model representation without extra states.

## 7. Interface with the cubic-norm branch

The present result gives the exceptional branch a precise burden of proof.

It is not enough to point out that a Jordan algebra has rank three or a cubic determinant. One must construct:

\[
\{\text{three canonical exceptional sectors}\}
\longrightarrow
\{\text{three isomorphic }8+7\text{ chiral complexes}\}
\]

such that:

- the sectors are inequivalent enough not to collapse into one by symmetry;
- they carry identical observed gauge quantum numbers;
- their Yukawa matrices may differ and mix;
- no fourth equivalent sector appears;
- the selector/CAR construction acts consistently on all three;
- the resulting index is three without inserting a three-dimensional flavor factor by hand.

That is the generation problem in a falsifiable form.

## 8. Branch status

The generation-selection branch is pinned at:

\[
\boxed{
\text{construct a canonical rank-three exceptional-to-Yukawa functor}.}
\]

The selector topology and local anomaly equations cannot unlock it. Progress must come from the exceptional, arithmetic, flavor-monodromy or source-multiplication branches.

Meanwhile, the AIII defect branch remains productive because it determines what happens to any supplied generation multiplicity under walls, textures and neutrino-mass deformations.

## 9. Stop-everything and falsification gates

**Stop-everything trigger:** a rank-three Jordan frame or cubic-norm construction produces exactly three complete \(8+7\) chiral complexes and their mixing data without an inserted flavor triplet.

**Arithmetic trigger:** an integral exceptional orbit has exactly three physically admissible primitive sectors after all gauge and chirality constraints.

**Monodromy trigger:** a rank-three flavor bundle is forced as the minimal bundle compatible with selector, CAR and CP holonomies.

**Index falsifier:** \(\operatorname{ind}\mathcal D=3\) is advertised as a derivation when three generations were used to build \(H_Y\).

**Topology falsifier:** a \(\mathbb Z_2\) selector invariant is claimed to distinguish one from three.

**Anomaly falsifier:** generation-independent anomaly cancellation is claimed to determine \(N_g=3\).

**Rank-three numerology falsifier:** the word “cubic” or the existence of three idempotents is treated as a generation derivation without constructing the full representations and mixing.
