\[

\[

\[

## 5. Three-flavor Majorana index

The right-handed-neutrino mass is

\[
M_R(r,\varphi)=y,S(r,\varphi).
\]

For three sterile flavors and invertible \(y\),

\[
\det M_R\propto S^3\det y,
\]

so a vortex of winding \(n\) has

\[
\boxed{w[\det M_R]=3n.}
\]

In the chiral Jackiw--Rossi limit, a unit string therefore has index three. With
only exact CAR particle-hole symmetry retained, its protected class-D parity is

\[
\boxed{3n\equiv n\pmod2.}
\]

Odd \(B-L\) strings carry odd neutral Majorana parity.

This is not a novel phenomenon invented for the programme: heavy Majorana
neutrino zero modes on \(B-L\) cosmic strings are an established field-theory
possibility. The new point is that the programme's three-flavor selector winding
lands on the same index structure.

## 6. Relation to the antipodal law

For a unit string,

\[

\]

and hence

\[
M_R(\varphi+\pi)=-M_R(\varphi).
\]

The conventional \(B-L\) vortex therefore realizes the same mass antipodality
used in the selector family.

It still does not prove that the \(B-L\) vacuum circle **is** the Jordan selector

share a sewing equation.
