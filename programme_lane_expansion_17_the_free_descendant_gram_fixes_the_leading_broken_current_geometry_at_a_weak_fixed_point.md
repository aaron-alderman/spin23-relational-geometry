## 6. The running-Standard-Model boundary

The theorem used here assumes conformal covariance and evaluates descendant norms at a weak fixed point. The four-dimensional Standard Model has running gauge, Higgs and Yukawa couplings rather than the required fixed point.

Three additional structures must be audited before calling this the Standard-Model anomalous-dimension matrix:

1. background-source counterterms and local RG;
2. hypercharge EOM representatives and contact terms;
3. threshold matching when the top, Higgs and electroweak particles are removed.

The descendant Gram should be viewed as the universal positive part fixed by nonconservation. A local-RG calculation must determine whether extra scheme-dependent terms rotate its eigenvectors or merely change representatives.
