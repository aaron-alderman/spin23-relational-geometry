## Executive result

The requested signed integral distance-five \(P\)-lattice with \(|N|=1\) exists, reduces at \(p=3\) to the ternary Golay \(P\)-space, and doubles the fifteen-coordinate Standard-Model lattice to an explicit weighted integral \([[37,1,5]]_3\) construction.

Take

\[
\boxed{\mu=(2,2,2,2,2,2,-4,-4,-1,-1,-1),\qquad N=\sum_\ell\mu_\ell=1}

and let \(\mathcal S\subset\mathbb Z^{11}\) be generated by

\[

Then, exactly over the integers,

\[
\boxed{

G\operatorname{diag}(\mu)G^T=0,\qquad
\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}G=5.}

Moreover,

\[
\boxed{d_{\mathbb Q}(\mathcal S)=5}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{\mathcal S\bmod3=C_{11}^{\perp},}
\]

\[
\boxed{d_3(\mathcal S)=5.}
\]

This is the branch's strongest arithmetic result so far.

## 1. Why this coefficient vector is the first viable one

Every entry of \(\mu\) is \(-1\pmod3\), so the weighted \(P\)-conditions reduce to the ordinary scalar Golay conditions. Its actual signs are six positive and five negative. Since \(N=1>0\), the all-one complement has real signature

\[
(5,5),
\]

which is large enough to contain a rank-five isotropic space.

The competing minimum-\(L^1\) multiset

\[
(2,2,2,2,2,2,-7,-1,-1,-1,-1)
\]

has the right real signature but the wrong rational discriminant. Its complement has determinant square class \(-7\), whereas a rational hyperbolic \(H^5\) has determinant square class \(-1\). It cannot contain a rational maximal isotropic five-space.

For the chosen two-\(-4\) multiset,

\[
\det\operatorname{diag}(\mu)=-1024=-(32)^2.
\]

Because \(\mathbf1\) has norm one, the complement has the same determinant square class \(-1\). Its Hasse invariant agrees with \(H^5\); the form is split over \(\mathbb Q\).

The construction begins with the following five exact isotropic rows:

\[

\]

They satisfy

\[
E\mu=0,\qquad E\operatorname{diag}(\mu)E^T=0.
\]

An exact rational dual frame \(F\) is obtained by first solving

\[
\langle E_i,W_j\rangle_\mu=\delta_{ij},
\qquad
\langle\mathbf1,W_j\rangle_\mu=0
\]

and then applying the standard isotropic correction

\[
F=W-\frac12(W\operatorname{diag}(\mu)W^T)E.
\]

This gives

\[
\langle E_i,F_j\rangle_\mu=\delta_{ij},
\qquad
\langle F_i,F_j\rangle_\mu=0.
\]

Thus \((E,F)\) is an exact rational \(H^5\) basis of \(\mathbf1^\perp\).

## 3. Forcing the Golay reduction

Reduce the hyperbolic basis modulo three and express the ternary Golay dual in its coordinates. After one finite skew adjustment of the complementary Lagrangian, the Golay space is transverse and is the graph of

\[

The matrix is skew, so the exact rational graph

\[

This is constructive, not an appeal to weak approximation: every frame, skew adjustment, denominator and final row is emitted by the certificate.

## 4. Exact distance

The certificate tests the ordinary coordinate dual of the integer row space. No support of size at most four contains a logical relation with nonzero coordinate sum, while a support of size five does. Therefore

\[
d_{\mathbb Q}=5.
\]

Modulo three, row reduction gives the \([11,5,6]_3\) dual of the ternary Golay code. Its ordinary dual is the \([11,6,5]_3\) Golay code, and a weight-five Golay word has nonzero coordinate sum. Hence

\[
d_3=5.
\]

The integral lattice therefore succeeds simultaneously over \(\mathbb Q\) and at the exceptional prime that defeated the previous unit-signed and large-prime constructions.

## 6. What question this answers

The paper states that an integral \(P\)-lattice of distance five with \(|N|=1\) is the missing input needed to double the fifteen-qudit Standard-Model code to distance five. The displayed \((G,\mu)\) is such an input.

It also repairs the precise defect of the ordinary all-\(+1\) Golay realization:

## 7. Essential physical caveat

The coefficients are not unit chiralities. They are

\[
2,\ -4,\ -1,
\]

not only \(\pm1\). Therefore this is an exact weighted integral triorthogonal lattice and code construction, but not yet a literal spectrum with one ordinary Weyl fermion at each of the thirty-seven coordinates.

Expanding a coefficient into repeated unit-chirality fermions changes the physical coordinate set and introduces parallel columns, so it is not automatically the same distance-five code. A physical interpretation must explain the nonunit weights as multiplicities, indices, boundary degrees, orbifold weights or another genuine carrier datum without destroying the code.

This caveat is not cosmetic. Mathematically, the paper's integral \(P\)-lattice input is solved. Physically, the coefficient-selection problem has become sharper.

## 8. Relation to \(Spin(2,3)\), Jordan triples and the exceptional branch

No \(Spin(2,3)\) or Jordan theorem was used to find \(G\). The construction uses rational hyperbolic geometry, the ternary Golay dual and skew-graph composition.

The result nevertheless creates a concrete bridge target. The relational-carrier programme no longer needs to “produce Golay” in the abstract. It must explain the specific weighted polarization

\[

\]

or prove that an equivalent canonical vector is selected by a Jordan, exceptional or Leech structure.

The occurrence of a split \(H^5\), a skew graph, two paired copies and one linking row is structurally compatible with the programme's carrier language. Compatibility is not derivation. The next decisive test is whether these weights or this hyperbolic frame arise from the existing Jordan/Furey data without being inserted by hand.

## 9. Stop-everything and falsification gates

### Stop-everything result achieved

An explicit nonunit integral distance-five \(P\)-lattice with \(N=1\) and ternary-Golay reduction exists and produces a verified weighted integral \([[37,1,5]]_3\) doubling.

### Next stop-everything tests

1. A canonical algebraic structure selects \((2^6,-4^2,-1^3)\) or an equivalent coefficient orbit.
2. The nonunit weights admit a defensible particle, index or boundary interpretation.
3. The same coefficient data maps non-arbitrarily into the programme's \(Spin(2,3)\) relational carrier.

### Falsification boundaries

- This does not give a unit-chirality thirty-seven-fermion spectrum.
- It does not derive the coefficients from Furey, Jordan, octonionic, Leech or Monster data.
- It does not make \(Spin(2,3)\) the automorphism group of the code.
- It does solve the weighted integral \(P\)-lattice existence problem posed by the doubling theorem.
