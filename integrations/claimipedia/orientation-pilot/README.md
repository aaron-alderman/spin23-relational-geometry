# Orientation–Claimipedia pilot

This directory contains the proposal-only initial tranche for representing *The Architecture of Orientation* in Claimipedia and testing its relationships with Sourcery's live `scientific_derivation_compose` operation.

No Claimipedia admission is asserted. The Sourcery operation explicitly grants no entailment, empirical authority, corpus admission, or application activation.

## Pilot outcome

The tranche contains eight deliberately separated claims:

1. orientation as a relation;
2. attention as a mechanism of affordance disclosure;
3. nonlinear response to identity-central anomaly;
4. the separation of meaning from warrant;
5. corrigibility as a quality rather than a definition of orientation;
6. mythic transformation invariants as an empirical hypothesis;
7. moral truth as a frame-transformation research proposal;
8. the Christian Logos as a metaphysical interpretation.

The live Sourcery run found:

- three proposal paths from the relational definition to review: direct, through attention, and through attention plus identity-level anomaly;
- two paths from the meaning/warrant distinction to review: direct and through the corrigibility proposal;
- one direct review path each for mythic invariants, moral invariance, and the Logos interpretation;
- no derivation from mythic recurrence to moral truth;
- no derivation from mythic recurrence to Christian metaphysical truth;
- four invalid rules rejected across every query: myth-to-moral truth, myth-to-Logos proof, meaning-to-warrant collapse, and psychologizing defensive response into epistemic falsity.

The result is therefore not “the architecture is true.” It is a receipt that the initial typed graph preserves several important category boundaries while exposing the assumptions and losses on admissible proposal paths.

### Interpretive limitation

The composer did not discover the semantic boundaries unaided. The pilot author declared four transformations to be analogy-only or category-collapsing fixtures, and Sourcery enforced that declaration by excluding them from candidate paths. Positive paths likewise demonstrate consistency with the authored graph, not independent support for the claims or transformations. The substantive work remains in adversarial review of the signatures, assumptions, losses, and edge types.

## MECE boundary

The claims are mutually differentiated by their primary question and epistemic class. They are not exhaustive of the entire manuscript. In particular, this tranche does not yet represent embodiment and tacit knowledge, culture as normative exoskeleton, power and institutional legibility, totalitarian basin dynamics, or the clinical thesis. These omissions are recorded as a coverage ledger rather than silently absorbed into the eight claims.

## Triage recommendation

- Retain O1 and O4 as definitional guardrails.
- Retain O2, O3, and O6 for empirical specification before evidential review.
- Retain O5 and O7 as contestable normative research proposals.
- Retain O8 as a metaphysical interpretation only.
- Reject the four collapsed derivations as currently formulated.
- Do not admit any claim until at least one independent challenge and one third-order audit have been reviewed by a human.
