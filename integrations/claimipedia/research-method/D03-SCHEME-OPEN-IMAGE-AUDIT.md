# D-03 scheme, gauge and open-image audit

## Stable result

For an invertible source change `O'=S O`, the absorptive matrix obeys

`rho'=S rho S^T`, `det rho'=(det S)^2 det rho`.

The state ratio is a projective coordinate and transforms by a Möbius map. Thus rank, positivity and the distinctness of source directions are basis-invariant. A stress-tensor improvement is a momentum-dependent triangular source change; at fixed invariant mass it shifts every state ratio equally and cannot manufacture or remove the third direction.

Hermitian local counterterm polynomials contribute to the real contact layer, not the ordinary absorptive cut. This is why the spectral matrix—not a chosen real part of the correlator—is the primary scheme-stable object.

## Gauge and real-structure limits

The Abelian strike is not a completed gauge-fixed amplitude computation. Gauge independence belongs to the BRST-cohomology quotient after the gauge, Goldstone and ghost sectors are included. BRST-exact and equation-of-motion terms cannot simply be deleted diagram by diagram.

The canonical positive object is generally `H_2(C)`. Reduction to `H_2(R)` requires a common antiunitary symmetry that preserves the state and makes both sources even in a compatible basis. CP/T violation, chemical potentials and unresolved absorptive phases are explicit failure modes.

## Open-image no-go

A vacuum Lorentz-invariant two-point function depends on one continuous invariant, `s`. Its local image therefore has dimension at most one, even if matrices sampled at three separated energies linearly span all of `H_2`. The current result is:

- full global linear span in the simplified interacting model;
- no three-dimensional open vacuum image;
- an open-image re-entry programme requiring three independently effective controls and a rank-three Jacobian.

Temperature, spatial momentum and a Higgs background are candidate controls, not a completed proof.
