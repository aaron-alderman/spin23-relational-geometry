# D-03 projective spectral-support theorem

## Result

The full-carrier question reduces to a census of projective source-amplitude ratios.

For a resolved intermediate state or state class `n`, define

`v_n = (<n|O_g|0>, <n|O_H|0>)`.

Real reciprocity makes its spectral contribution a positive rank-one matrix `v_n v_n^T`. If the Higgs amplitude is nonzero, its direction is fixed by

`r_n = <n|O_g|0> / <n|O_H|0>`.

The corresponding coordinate vector in `H_2(R)` is proportional to

`(r_n^2, 1, sqrt(2) r_n)`.

For three ratios the coordinate determinant is

`sqrt(2) (r_2-r_1)(r_3-r_1)(r_3-r_2)`.

Therefore three state classes span the complete Jordan carrier exactly when their real projective source ratios are pairwise distinct. A state seen only by the metric source is the projective point at infinity and obeys the corresponding limiting theorem.

## Why this matters

The free Higgs rung has one ratio. The leading gauge anomaly adds the point at infinity, producing a plane. A third state class produces the missing Jordan coordinate only if its metric/Higgs amplitude ratio differs from both.

This is stricter than counting sectors. Three named particle sectors, thresholds or representations can still collapse to one ray when their source ratios coincide.

## Span is not yet dynamics

Three distinct projector directions establish linear span. To explore an open part of the cone, their nonnegative weights must vary independently under physical controls or have separately resolved spectral support. Threshold separation, selection rules, temperature, momentum or background fields can provide that independence.

Full `Spin(2,3)` response covariance remains a later gate: the transformations must act on the complete spectral family, not merely on its ambient span.

## Real-structure gate

If relative spectral amplitudes carry irreducible complex phases, their projectors naturally lie in `H_2(C)`, whose conformal geometry is larger. Reduction to `H_2(R)` requires a common real structure, reciprocity or an antiunitary alignment theorem. Positivity alone does not supply it.
