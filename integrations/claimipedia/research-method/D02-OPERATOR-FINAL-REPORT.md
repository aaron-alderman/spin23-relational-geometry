# D-02 bounded-operator final report

## Outcome

The finite-dimensional Schur–Herglotz programme extends to bounded operators, but support rank is replaced by a richer stratum:

`support × spectral gap × Douglas class × complementability × topology × ideal × approach net × scalarization`.

The final dispatcher is ordered:

1. ordinary bounded inverse;
2. closed-range Moore–Penrose/support inverse;
3. certified factorized or form complement;
4. topology-labelled limit on an approach-net groupoid;
5. retain/refuse.

## Strongest positive result

If

`D=T^(1/2) Dhat T^(1/2)` and `B=C T^(1/2)`,

with bounded positive `T`, bounded `C`, and norm-holomorphic boundedly invertible Herglotz `Dhat`, then

`Sigma=C Dhat^(-1) C*`

is bounded and norm-holomorphic, and `A-Sigma` remains Herglotz. Closed range and Fredholmness are not necessary.

Operator Harnack inequalities additionally show that the dissipative support and square-root range class are frequency-invariant on compact upper-half-plane patches. They do not automatically factor the full complex interior; that certificate remains explicit.

## Strongest negative results

- Equal support does not imply equal gap or range class.
- Kernel annihilation does not imply Douglas square-root compatibility.
- Norm-convergent block endpoints can have different strong Schur limits at a closing kernel gap.
- Strong convergence need not be norm convergence; weak positive convergence need not be strong.
- A positive operator-valued residue need not be trace class.
- No finite probes plus total trace determine a general infinite-dimensional residue.

## New structural insight

At an emerging kernel the possible finite residues form the whole bounded positive cone `R=LL*`. The passive response quotients the coupling by `L -> L U`; orientation in the coupling square root is invisible unless retained separately. This is the operator analogue of the finite-dimensional approach-residue decoration, but with an infinite-dimensional fibre.

## Governed review

The admitted Sourcery v19 composer accepted one four-step proposal-only path:

`operator Herglotz family -> Harnack/Douglas range class -> eight-coordinate stratum -> five-way dispatcher -> Triage`.

It rejected support-only classification, kernel-only compatibility, strong-to-norm promotion and finite-probe residue reconstruction.

## Re-entry programmes

Unbounded operator domains, real-axis boundary values, frequency-dependent/nonreciprocal couplings and Hilbert-module/Banach categories remain separate. Each needs additional domain, topology or factorization structure and cannot silently alter this bounded interior result.
