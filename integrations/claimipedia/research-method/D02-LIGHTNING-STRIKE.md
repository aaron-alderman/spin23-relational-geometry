# D-02 lightning strike: support-stratified Schur composition

## What was already present

The research programme already contained nearly all the necessary method primitives, but not one object joining them:

- the **Open-Branch Research Method** protects alternatives, records discomfort and assigns stop/re-entry conditions;
- Claimipedia's **lightning-strike principle** works backward from a conditional terminal claim and requires every necessary link to close independently;
- the programme's **landscape and closure ledgers** prevent useful mathematical structure from being mistaken for closure of the physical claim;
- Sourcery's admitted **scientific derivation composer** performs bounded typed-path search with explicit assumptions, losses and rejection receipts.

The new meta² process composes those methods without merging their responsibilities. It makes the process itself typed, inspectable and testable.

## The conditional strike

> **If** one support-stratified Schur law covers fixed Ward null spaces, rank-changing hydrodynamic supports and domain-wall resolvent poles, **then** a continuous nine-link chain must connect the domain responses to a reviewable theorem candidate without an illegal inverse, hidden symmetry loss or silent change of explanatory layer.

One failed necessary link breaks the terminal proposition. It does not erase upstream results: a valid fixed-null theorem remains useful even if global gluing or decoration transport fails.

## The layers that must not collapse

| Layer | D-02 question | Typical category error prevented |
|---|---|---|
| Formal | What is the partial composition operation and its output type? | Treating every block formula as everywhere defined |
| Analytic | Where do inverses, limits, poles and positivity exist? | Replacing a singular limit by an ordinary inverse |
| Geometric/topological | How do support patches and singular strata glue? | Assuming one basis works globally |
| Symmetry | Which actions are preserved, broken, quotiented or anomalous? | Calling a common carrier a common physical symmetry |
| Physical | Which modes are observable or lawfully eliminable? | Discarding a pole or null mode because it is inconvenient |
| Computational | Which diagnostic selects which handler? | Hiding tolerance- or conditioning-dependent choices |
| Evidential | Is the result a proof, witness, counterexample or conjecture? | Promoting a finite fixture to a general theorem |
| Governance | Who may retain, reject or promote the result? | Letting derivability become scientific authority |

Crossing between layers requires a named bridge such as `represents`, `constrains`, `implements`, `witnesses` or `falsifies`. “Same mathematics” is not a bridge type.

## Symmetry census for the first bounded strike

The phrase “all symmetries” is made operational rather than absolute. For the declared finite-dimensional scope, the strike must classify:

1. independent block-basis changes and congruence covariance;
2. Ward/gauge null directions and the physical quotient;
3. reciprocity and analytic-face exchange;
4. domain-wall chiral grading and orientation reversal;
5. local support-frame changes and stratum-chart transitions;
6. KMS as a compatible thermal decoration rather than part of the common carrier.

Each record names the acted-on object, action, exactness, domain, preservation rule, breaking rule, quotient, obstruction, test and unresolved frontier. Non-Abelian holonomy, essential spectrum, interacting-QFT anomalies and emergent dualities are explicitly outside the first strike rather than silently ignored.

## Options retained rather than collapsed

The first strike selects a **local projector/partial-inverse baseline**, **constant-rank patches with named pole loci**, a **typed multi-handler dispatcher**, and a **finite-dimensional theorem first**. It does not delete the alternatives:

- quotient-bundle and linear-relation/dilation carriers remain live;
- Whitney strata and residue-sheaf descriptions remain live;
- retention, residue output, patch switching, regulator limits and refusal remain lawful singular handlers;
- bounded-operator and unbounded-QFT extensions remain dependent or dormant rather than being treated as already solved.

This matters because a later obstruction may cause a `SPLIT` or `RE-ROOT` rather than an improvised patch.

## First execution package

The existing executable witness already distinguishes:

- fixed Ward null + range condition → support pseudoinverse;
- null coupling → refusal;
- wall pole → residue or retain;
- rank change → patch or retain;
- regular off-locus point → ordinary inverse.

That closes part of LS4 and LS8 only. It does **not** establish the positivity theorem, global gluing, complete covariance or decoration preservation.

The next bounded calculation is deliberately two-sided:

1. Prove the finite-dimensional local theorem for a matrix-valued Herglotz block family on a constant-rank eliminated support with range-compatible coupling.
2. Construct the smallest transverse rank-change counterexample showing why that theorem cannot simply be continued through the singular locus.

This pairs a positive theorem with the neighboring failure that fixes its exact boundary.

## Stop and re-entry rule

Stop claiming one global D-02 law if two admissible approaches to the same singular point give inequivalent external responses after transition and residue data are included. Split the work into stratum-specific calculi connected by explicit comparison morphisms.

Re-enter the unified branch only if a canonical groupoid, dilation or measure-valued construction removes that path dependence.

## How this applies elsewhere

For a new research question, change the terminal proposition and domain objects, but keep the control structure:

```text
bound the claim
→ separate layers
→ census symmetries for the declared scope
→ retain alternatives at every choice
→ enumerate every necessary linkage
→ choose positive, uncomfortable and pressure-test calculations
→ execute with negative cases
→ reconcile without collapsing edge types
→ triage without automatic authority
```

The reusable output is not merely a list of tasks. It is an option-preserving dependency graph that says what would count as success, what would break the strike, which results survive failure, and what new evidence would reopen a stopped branch.
