# D-03 mixed four-scalar cut

## Result

The leading scalar-interaction topology generically supplies the missing third projective source direction.

In a real component of the Abelian-Higgs scalar sector, take

`O_H=phi^2/2`, `O_4=phi^4/4!`.

The Higgs source reaches a four-scalar state at order `lambda` through a propagator diagram. The trace contains a running-coupling term proportional to `beta_lambda O_4`, whose four-scalar form factor is local. Up to common normalization factors,

`r_4 = 2m^2 + beta_lambda / [lambda sum_i ((q-p_i)^2-m^2)^(-1)]`.

For nonzero `beta_lambda`, this ratio is generically distinct from the two-scalar value `2m^2`. Because the propagator sum varies over four-body phase space, the new ratio is not merely a fixed source relabelling.

## Three-direction result

The leading state classes are:

1. gauge pair: metric-only, the projective point at infinity;
2. scalar pair: finite ratio `2m^2`;
3. four-scalar cut: finite kinematics-dependent ratio `r_4`.

Their thresholds are distinct in the simplified massless-gauge rung:

`0 < 4m^2 < 16m^2`.

The projective coordinate determinant of these three directions is nonzero whenever `r_4 != 2m^2`. Thus the leading threshold-resolved spectral structures span `H_2(R)` generically.

## What is and is not closed

Closed structurally:

- a nonzero quartic beta function gives a third projective direction;
- that direction varies across physical four-body kinematics;
- the three leading threshold classes span the real Jordan carrier;
- triangular source mixing does not erase the ratio difference.

Still open:

- complete charged-scalar combinatorics;
- BRST-consistent gauge, Goldstone and ghost completion;
- operator-mixing normalization;
- loop-induced phases and the common-real-structure gate;
- rank three of the physical control Jacobian;
- transformation closure on the spectral family.

At a scalar fixed point with `beta_lambda=0`, the contact contribution disappears and this particular third-direction mechanism collapses.
