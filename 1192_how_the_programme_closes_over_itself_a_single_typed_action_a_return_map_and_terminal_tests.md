# How the programme closes over itself: a single typed action, a return map, and terminal tests

## Executive answer

The programme will not close by finding one more correspondence. It closes only when its present one-way chain becomes a return loop:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Jordan observables}
\to
P
\to
X=I-3P
\to
\text{matter/gauge action}
\to
\text{charge-three vacuum}
\to
\mathbb Z_3\text{ defect}
\to
\text{physical response}
\to
\text{Jordan observables}.
}
\]

At present, many individual arrows are established, but the loop does not yet generate its own starting data. The decisive next task is to construct one typed variational system whose equations:

1. select or dynamically stabilize a primitive idempotent \(P\);
2. induce the relative connection generated by \(X=I-3P\);
3. act faithfully on the fundamental fermion module while remaining projectively well defined on Jordan observables;
4. generate the charge-three link/vacuum rather than append it;
5. reproduce the correct \(\mathbb Z_9\) lift and colour-centre quotient to \(\mathbb Z_3\);
6. transgress to the oriented untwisted level-three boundary theory;
7. return an observable stress-energy/response operator whose distinguished spectral idempotent is the same \(P\).

The final requirement is the closure condition. It converts \(P\) from an initial choice into a fixed point of the physical response it creates.

## 1. Three meanings of closure

### Algebraic closure

All allowed compositions remain inside the declared typed structure. The programme must use Jordan/triple closure rather than silently generate the full forbidden associative tensor algebra.

### Dynamical closure

Every field and coefficient essential to the result must arise from one action, its bundle data, or quantization. In particular, the following may no longer be independent insertions:

\[
P,
\qquad
a_X,
\qquad
S_{q=3},
\qquad
k_{\mathrm{BF}}=3,
\qquad
\text{boundary orientation}.
\]

### Reflexive closure

The dynamics sourced by \(P\) must produce a response that selects the same \(P\). Schematically,

\[
P
\longmapsto
\mathcal R[P]
\longmapsto
\Pi_1(\mathcal R[P])
=P,
\]

where \(\mathcal R[P]\) is a Hermitian response operator and \(\Pi_1\) extracts its distinguished rank-one spectral projector.

This is the mathematical version of the programme “closing over itself.”

## 2. The two Jordan layers must be kept typed

The programme contains two Jordan structures with different jobs.

### Spacetime/observable layer

\[
J_{\mathrm{obs}}=H_2(\mathbb R),
\qquad
\operatorname{Conf}(J_{\mathrm{obs}})
\cong Sp(4,\mathbb R)
\cong Spin_0(2,3).
\]

This layer concerns causal/observable phase geometry.

### Family/selection layer

\[
J_{\mathrm{fam}}=H_3(\mathbb C),
\]

with primitive-idempotent orbit

\[
\mathbb{CP}^2
\cong
SU(3)/S(U(1)\times U(2)).
\]

This layer supplies the \(1\oplus2\) family split, Peirce relations, and the generator \(I-3P\).

Closure does **not** mean identifying these algebras. It requires a typed coupling between them. The original stress-energy sandwich intuition

\[
A\,X\,B
\]

should become a map whose outer slots belong to the observable/spacetime response sector and whose middle slot transports family/module data:

\[
\{A,\mathcal X_P,B\}
\in
J_{\mathrm{obs}}
\quad\text{or an explicitly declared response module}.
\]

The domain, codomain, positivity rule, and covariance of this map must be stated. Without that typing, the Spin\((2,3)\) and family-Jordan branches remain parallel analogies rather than one theory.

## 3. The minimal field package

A closure attempt needs no larger speculative algebra than the following package:

\[
\mathfrak F=
(g,\ P,\ \mathcal A,\ \Psi,\ S,\ b),
\]

where:

- \(g\) is spacetime/causal geometry or its \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) observable replacement;
- \(P\) is a primitive rank-one section of the family Jordan bundle;
- \(\mathcal A\) is a connection compatible with the projector geometry and its faithful fundamental lift;
- \(\Psi\) is the Standard Model fermion module;
- \(S\) is not an arbitrary scalar but a cross-Peirce/link field carrying relative charge three;
- \(b\) is the compact two-form field or differential-cohomology datum required by the finite topological response.

The associated typed bundles are

\[
E=L\oplus Q,
\qquad
T\mathbb{CP}^2\cong\operatorname{Hom}(L,Q),
\qquad
X=I-3P.
\]

The connection must have both:

- an adjoint/projective component acting on \(P\), \(DP\), and curvature;
- a fundamental component acting on \(L\oplus Q\) and retaining \(\omega I_3\).

An adjoint-only construction cannot close because it loses the central \(\mathbb Z_3\).

## 4. The single-action requirement

The action should have the schematic form

\[
S_{\mathrm{tot}}
=S_{\mathrm{obs}}[g]
+S_P[P,\mathcal A,g]
+S_F[\Psi,P,\mathcal A,g]
+S_{\mathrm{link}}[S,P,\mathcal A]
+S_{\mathrm{top}}[b,P,\mathcal A]
+S_{\mathrm{compat}}.
\]

The terms are not allowed to be independent decorations. Their variations must cross-source one another.

### Variation with respect to \(P\)

Should produce a rank-one selection equation,

\[
\frac{\delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}}{\delta P}=0,
\qquad
P^2=P,
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}P=1,
\]

and orient \(P\) relative to the fermion/Yukawa response.

### Variation with respect to the connection

Should yield the faithful family current, including the module contribution invisible to the adjoint geometry:

\[
\frac{\delta S_{\mathrm{tot}}}{\delta\mathcal A}
=J_{\mathrm{adj}}[P]+J_{\mathrm{fund}}[\Psi].
\]

### Variation with respect to the link field

Should produce a vacuum whose stabilizer has the already audited integral global form:

\[
\operatorname{SNF}
\begin{pmatrix}
3&-1\\
0&3
\end{pmatrix}
=(1,9),
\]

followed by the colour-centre quotient

\[
\mathbb Z_9/K\cong\mathbb Z_3.
\]

### Variation with respect to \(b\)

Should impose the level-three flatness/quantization condition rather than assume it.

### Variation with respect to observable geometry

Should produce the complete stress-energy/response tensor:

\[
T_{\mu\nu}
=-\frac{2}{\sqrt{|g|}}
\frac{\delta S_{\mathrm{matter+relation}}}{\delta g^{\mu\nu}}.
\]

That response must then feed back into the projector equation.

## 5. The return map

The cleanest closure test is to define a Hermitian family-response operator from the physical state. A generic form is

\[
\mathcal R_{ij}
=\int_\Sigma
\mathcal K^{\mu\nu\rho\sigma}
\left\langle
T^{(i)}_{\mu\nu}
T^{(j)}_{\rho\sigma}
\right\rangle_{!c}
+\text{fermion/link/topological response terms},
\]

where the kernel \(\mathcal K\) must be derived from the observable Jordan geometry rather than chosen to force an answer.

The induced selection map is

\[
\mathfrak C(P)
:=
\Pi_{\mathrm{dist}}\big(\mathcal R[P]\big),
\]

where \(\Pi_{\mathrm{dist}}\) is the rank-one spectral projector associated with a nondegenerate distinguished eigenvalue.

Closure requires

\[
\boxed{\mathfrak C(P)=P.}
\]

Stability requires more:

\[
\rho\!\left(D\mathfrak C\big|_P\right)<1
\]

on directions transverse to the residual orbit, where \(\rho\) is spectral radius. This says that nearby selections flow back toward the same physical projector rather than away from it.

If the response remains proportional to the identity,

\[
\mathcal R[P]\propto I,
\]

then no family is selected and the programme remains underdetermined. If the response has a unique rank-one eigendirection but it does not coincide with the input \(P\), the proposed dynamics is inconsistent. A stable fixed point is the desired closure.

The factor-three projector curvature is not enough. Closure requires the full sequence

\[

c_1(T\mathbb{CP}^2)=3h
&\longrightarrow

Each arrow needs an integral differential-cohomology map, not merely equality of local forms.

The central question is whether the connection induced by the projector geometry possesses all independent flat holonomy sectors. If it remains purely composite, the level may be explained while the nine-state carrier is absent. Closure therefore requires either:

- an independent but compatible connection on the lifted fundamental bundle;
- an emergence theorem showing that the connection becomes autonomous after gapped modes are integrated out;
- or a boundary theorem showing that independent edge holonomies are liberated by transgression.

## 9. What must be derived versus what may remain input

### May remain foundational input

- the choice of spacetime dimension and signature;
- the Standard Model fermion representation, unless the wider Furey branch derives it;
- the Jordan axioms and positivity cone;
- compactness/integrality conventions for gauge fields.

### Must be derived for programme closure

- the \(1\oplus2\) rank split as a stable vacuum or fixed point;
- the generator \(I-3P\) from unimodularity;
- faithful action on the fundamental module;
- the charge-three relation/link representation;
- the global \(\mathbb Z_9\to\mathbb Z_3\) quotient;
- the BF level and discrete twist;
- boundary orientation and gap conditions;
- the return from physical response to the same projector.

If the first list grows to contain the decisive choices, the programme has not closed; it has only packaged them.

## 10. Ordered execution plan

### Step 1: Freeze the typed ontology

Write one table of every space, bundle, field, symmetry, and morphism. Reject any formula whose domain or codomain is implicit.

### Step 2: Construct the minimal lifted connection

Build the smallest connection acting simultaneously on projective/Jordan data and the faithful module \(L\oplus Q\). Prove that it does not regenerate the forbidden associative gauge algebra.

### Step 3: Classify admissible coupling terms

Classify local real terms up to a fixed derivative order built from

\[
P,\ DP,\ F,\ \Psi,\ S,\ b,
\]

subject to gauge covariance, Jordan positivity, and the known anomaly constraints.

### Step 4: Solve the projector vacuum equation

Determine whether the action produces a stable rank-one orbit and whether Yukawa/fermion response selects a unique \(P_i\) or leaves a family permutation ambiguity.

### Step 5: Perform the integral global-form calculation

Compute the character/cocharacter lattice of the combined lifted bundle and rederive \(\mathbb Z_9/K\cong\mathbb Z_3\) inside the same model.

### Step 6: Derive the defect response

Integrate out the appropriate gapped modes. Compute the BF level, Dijkgraaf–Witten twist, orientation sign, and charged boundary spectrum.

### Step 7: Construct and test the return map

Calculate \(\mathcal R[P]\), extract its distinguished spectral projector, and test

\[
\mathfrak C(P)=P,
\qquad
\rho(D\mathfrak C|_P)<1.
\]

### Step 8: Extract one discriminating observable

The closed loop must yield at least one quantity not independently fitted: a selection rule, mixing relation, response coefficient, defect statistic, or no-go result.

## 11. Terminal outcomes

### Closed

One typed action yields a stable projector, faithful lifted connection, charge-three breaking, correct global quotient, oriented gapped level-three defect, and a response map returning the same projector, with at least one nontrivial prediction.

### Mathematically closed but physically underdetermined

The action and return map are consistent but possess several equivalent stable fixed points or free coefficients not resolved by current data.

### Effective closure only

The low-energy BF and family structures close after certain fields are assumed heavy, but no microscopic action derives that spectrum.

### No-go

Locality, positivity, anomaly cancellation, faithful central lifting, or independent BF holonomy cannot coexist without regenerating the excluded associative enlargement or unwanted light states.
