## Reframed question

Wave 4 excluded a canonical static orientation in the three-copy mediator space from the established data. Wave 5 asks whether the missing selector is path dependent rather than static.

Construct or exclude a physically derived bundle and connection

\[
E_W\to B_{\rm physical},
\qquad \nabla_W=d+A,
\]

whose holonomy around an independently defined loop

\[
U_\gamma=\mathcal P\exp\left(-\oint_\gamma A\right)
\]

has a protected nondegenerate spectral subspace on (W\cong\mathbb C^3).

## Candidate provenances

1. Berry/Wilczek--Zee transport for a family of mixed heavy mediator eigenstates;
2. transport generated by a family of finite Dirac operators or represented Jordan-triple maps;
3. vacuum or symmetry-breaking parameter loops with nontrivial monodromy;
4. boundary/anomaly-inflow transport;
5. experimentally realizable source cycles whose interference reads the holonomy.

## Pass conditions

The construction must provide a typed base, fiber, connection and loop; noncentral holonomy; a robust spectral gap; frame-independent projector; counterterm stability; relation to an incidence ray; and an observable phase or transport effect.

## No-go condition

If every admissible connection is gauge-equivalent to scalar transport on (W), or every physical loop has central holonomy, then path dependence cannot repair the static selector obstruction.

## Epistemic boundary

This is a new hypothesis generated by reconstructing the recurring illegible motif. It is not implied by the existing (Spin(2,3)), Furey, Jordan or exceptional results.
