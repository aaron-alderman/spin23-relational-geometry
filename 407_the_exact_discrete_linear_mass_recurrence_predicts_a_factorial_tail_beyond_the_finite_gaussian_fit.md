# The Exact Discrete Linear-Mass Recurrence Predicts a Factorial Tail Beyond the Finite Gaussian Fit

**Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Branches:** B01, B03, B04, B05, B06, B08, B09 and B11  
**Claim status:** exact recurrence structure plus superior finite factorial fit; rigorous asymptotic bound remains open

## 1. Why the Gaussian interpretation needed another check

The width-six shell density is very well fit by

\[
\log w_y\sim-a y^2.
\]

That matches the continuum Dirac equation with linear mass. But the regulated
lattice has fixed nearest-neighbor hopping and an onsite term whose norm grows
linearly with \(|y|\). Its exact far-tail recurrence can have a different
asymptotic form.

## 2. Exact transverse recurrence

Before radial folding, the zero-energy lattice equation has the form

\[
T_+\psi_{y+1}+T_-\psi_{y-1}
+\left(\mu y\,\beta_2+V_0\right)\psi_y=0,
\]

where

\[
T_+=-\frac{i}{2}\alpha_y-\frac r2 M_W,
\qquad
T_-=T_+^\dagger.
\]

The Clifford relations imply

\[
T_+^2=\frac{r^2-1}{4}I.
\]

At the benchmark \(r=0.8\),

\[
T_+^2=-0.09I,
\qquad
T_+^{-1}=\frac{4}{r^2-1}T_+.
\]

The executable matrix audit verifies both identities exactly within numerical
tolerance.

## 3. Formal far-tail balance

For a minimal decaying solution at large positive \(y\), the growing onsite term
dominates. Neglecting subleading bounded terms gives the transfer balance

\[
\psi_y\approx
-\left(\mu y\,\beta_2\right)^{-1}T_-\psi_{y-1}.
\]

Therefore

\[
\|\psi_y\|\lesssim\frac{C}{y}\|\psi_{y-1}\|,
\]

which iterates formally to

\[
\|\psi_y\|\lesssim\frac{C^y}{y!}.
\]

The density should ultimately behave like a factorially suppressed sequence,
up to matrix-channel mixing and subleading factors.

This is faster than a fixed-rate exponential and is not exactly Gaussian.

## 4. The finite data prefer a factorial coordinate

On shells \(y=2,\ldots,6\), regress \(\log w_y\) against
\(\log(y!)\). The result is

\[
\log w_y\approx a-1.51086\log(y!),
\qquad
R^2\approx0.999974.
\]

The Gaussian regression over the same shells has

\[
R^2\approx0.996919.
\]

Thus the log-factorial coordinate fits the measured tail better.

The fitted exponent \(-1.51\) is not the asymptotic density exponent \(-2\)
suggested by the simplest scalar balance. The available shells remain
pre-asymptotic and mix several internal channels.

## 5. Ratio diagnostic

If

\[
w_y/w_{y-1}\sim C/y^2,
\]

then \(y^2w_y/w_{y-1}\) should approach a constant. The measured values for
\(y=3,4,5,6\) are approximately

\[
1.79,\ 1.90,\ 2.18,\ 2.45.
\]

They are not yet constant, but they move slowly compared with the raw ratios and
are consistent with approach to a transfer-product regime.

## 6. Reconciliation with the continuum Gaussian

The continuum differential equation produces a Gaussian because a derivative
balances the linear mass. The fixed-spacing difference equation instead
produces a product of inverse onsite matrices in its extreme far tail.

Both descriptions can be correct in different regimes:

- continuum/intermediate regime: Gaussian-like envelope;
- extreme lattice tail: factorial transfer-product decay.

The current widths may already begin the crossover, which explains why both
fits are excellent and the factorial coordinate is slightly better.

## 7. Correction to the previous checkpoint

The previous document correctly called the measured tail “Gaussian-like” and
did not claim an asymptotic theorem. The stronger interpretation that the
lattice tail is asymptotically Gaussian is now explicitly rejected.

The preferred statement is:

> The finite profile has a Gaussian-like continuum envelope, while the exact
> discrete recurrence predicts factorial/super-exponential ultimate decay.

## 8. Toward a rigorous bound

The recurrence suggests a direct proof strategy. For sufficiently large
\(|y|\), establish

\[
\|(\mu y\beta_2+V_0)^{-1}\|\le\frac{C_0}{|y|}
\]

and control the two-step transfer system generated by \(T_\pm\). Iteration can
then give a discrete Agmon/product bound of the form

\[
\|\psi_y\|\le C_1\frac{C_2^{|y|}}{|y|!}.
\]

The remaining difficulty is uniform control of internal-channel mixing and the
backward component of the two-step recurrence.
