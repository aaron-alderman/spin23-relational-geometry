## 3. Solving the kernel

Triviality on \(d^c\) gives

\[
\omega_3^{-a}e^{i2k\pi/3}=1,
\]

so

\[
a\equiv k\pmod3.
\]

Triviality on \(L\) gives

\[
(-1)^b e^{-ik\pi}=1,
\]

so

\[
b\equiv k\pmod2.
\]

These conditions also make the action trivial on \(Q\), \(u^c\) and \(\nu^c\).

Hence the kernel is

\[
\left\{
(\omega_3^k,(-1)^k,e^{ik\pi/3}):k=0,\dots,5
\right\}.
\]

It is cyclic of order six, generated by

\[
\boxed{
(\omega_3,-1,e^{i\pi/3}).
}
\]
