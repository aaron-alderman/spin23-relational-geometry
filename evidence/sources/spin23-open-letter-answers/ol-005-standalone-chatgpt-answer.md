**The metaplectic realization is an exact physical model of the chosen bosonic quadratic sector. It can preserve substantial Jordan spectral and order data, but it does not automatically preserve the Jordan product, and it does not by itself furnish a local relativistic carrier.** The missing step is a construction connecting those oscillator observables to spacetime translations, a forward energy–momentum spectrum, and causal localization.

There is one necessary qualification: **“rank two” does not specify the Jordan algebra or its quadratic embedding.** For example, \(\operatorname{Herm}_2(\mathbb C)\), represented through polarization observables, and \(\operatorname{Sym}_2(\mathbb R)\), represented through commuting position quadratics, give different answers about determinant preservation. The distinction between these realizations is essential to judging what the programme has actually established.

## 1. What the metaplectic construction preserves exactly

Let
\[
\widehat R=(\widehat q_1,\widehat q_2,\widehat p_1,\widehat p_2)^T,
\qquad
[\widehat R_a,\widehat R_b]=i\hbar\Omega_{ab},
\qquad
\Omega=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&I_2\\
-I_2&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
For a real symmetric matrix \(G\), define the Weyl-ordered quadratic
\[
q_G(z)=\frac12z^TGz,
\qquad
\widehat Q_G=\operatorname{Op}^{W}(q_G).
\]

Choose the convention
\[
U_{\widetilde S}^{\dagger}\widehat R\,U_{\widetilde S}=S\widehat R,
\qquad
\widetilde S\in Mp(4,\mathbb R),\quad S\in Sp(4,\mathbb R).
\]
Then
\[
\boxed{
U_{\widetilde S}^{\dagger}\widehat Q_GU_{\widetilde S}
=\widehat Q_{S^TGS}
}
\]
and
\[
\boxed{
\frac{1}{i\hbar}[\widehat Q_G,\widehat Q_H]
=\widehat Q_{G\Omega H-H\Omega G}.
}
\]

These identities hold on the usual common invariant domain. The correspondence between quadratic Poisson brackets and quantum commutators is exact: higher Moyal corrections vanish. It is not a semiclassical approximation, and it does not require the oscillator state to be Gaussian. This is the rigorous basis for the realization of mixing, squeezing, and dynamics generated by quadratic Hamiltonians. [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/0509027?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The natural matrix associated with a quadratic is its Hamiltonian matrix
\[
F_G=\Omega G.
\]
Under metaplectic conjugation,
\[
F_G\longmapsto
\Omega S^TGS=S^{-1}F_GS.
\]
Consequently, several invariants follow directly:

| Object | What survives under the full symplectic action |
|---|---|
| Canonical structure | The CCR and the symplectic form \(\Omega\) |
| Quadratic coefficient matrix \(G\) | Rank, inertia, and positive semidefiniteness |
| Hamiltonian matrix \(\Omega G\) | Its similarity class, including spectrum and Jordan structure |
| Polynomial invariants | \(\det G\) and \(\operatorname{tr}[(\Omega G)^{2k}]\) |
| Pairs of quadratics transformed together | The bilinear invariant \(\operatorname{tr}(\Omega G\Omega H)\) |
| Quantum observable \(\widehat Q_G\) | Its operator spectrum and positivity, by unitary equivalence |

For \(G>0\), Williamson’s theorem gives two positive symplectic frequencies \(\omega_1,\omega_2\). They are invariant, with
\[
-\frac12\operatorname{tr}[(\Omega G)^2]
=\omega_1^2+\omega_2^2,
\qquad
\det G=\omega_1^2\omega_2^2.
\]
These frequencies determine the normal-mode form of the positive quadratic Hamiltonian. Ordinary matrix eigenvalues of \(G\), and \(\operatorname{tr}G\), generally change under squeezing. [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2106.11965?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

There is also a precise qualification to *faithfulness*. The metaplectic representation on the full oscillator Hilbert space distinguishes its group elements, but conjugation on homogeneous quadratic observables does not: \(U\) and \(-U\) act identically, as do the symplectic matrices \(S\) and \(-S\). That observable action therefore factors through
\[
PSp(4,\mathbb R)=Sp(4,\mathbb R)/\{\pm I\}.
\]
Faithfulness on Hilbert-space vectors and faithfulness of the action on quadratic observables are different claims.

## 2. Which rank-two Jordan invariants survive depends on the embedding

For a Euclidean rank-two spin factor, write
\[
x=(t,\mathbf x).
\]
Its spectral data have the familiar form
\[
\lambda_\pm(x)=t\pm\|\mathbf x\|,
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}_Jx=2t,
\qquad
\det_Jx=t^2-\|\mathbf x\|^2.
\]
The positive cone is
\[
J_+=\{(t,\mathbf x):t\geq\|\mathbf x\|\}.
\]

Two bilinear forms must be distinguished. Polarizing the determinant gives a Lorentz-signature form,
\[
b_J(x,y)
=\frac12\bigl[\det_J(x+y)-\det_Jx-\det_Jy\bigr],
\]
whereas the Jordan trace pairing
\[
\langle x,y\rangle_J=\operatorname{tr}_J(x\circ y)
\]
is positive definite in the Euclidean case. They have different invariance groups. A Jordan automorphism preserves the unit, product, trace, determinant, and spectral decomposition. A determinant-preserving structure transformation need not preserve the unit or trace.

A quadratic encoding must specify which of these structures it intertwines.

### The polarization embedding: \(\operatorname{Herm}_2(\mathbb C)\)

Take
\[
A=tI+\mathbf x\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma,
\]
so that
\[
\det A=t^2-\|\mathbf x\|^2,
\qquad
A\succeq0\iff t\geq\|\mathbf x\|.
\]

For the following oscillator formulas, set \(\hbar=1\). The standard number-preserving realization is
\[
d\Gamma(A)=a^\dagger A a.
\]
Its Weyl-ordered counterpart is
\[
Q_A=a^\dagger A a+\frac12\operatorname{tr}A\,I.
\]
The Jordan–Schwinger construction preserves matrix commutators as oscillator commutators; this is the algebraic reason it realizes the corresponding Lie symmetries. [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.04918v1?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Both maps are injective and preserve and reflect order:
\[
A\succeq0
\iff d\Gamma(A)\succeq0
\iff Q_A\succeq0.
\]
One can see this by diagonalizing \(A\). If its eigenvalues are \(\lambda_+,\lambda_-\), then
\[
Q_A\sim
\lambda_+\left(n_1+\frac12\right)
+\lambda_-\left(n_2+\frac12\right).
\]
A negative eigenvalue produces arbitrarily negative expectations at large occupation number; nonnegative eigenvalues produce a positive operator.

More strongly, this embedding retains the positive Jordan spectrum as symplectic data. Write \(A=B+iC\), with \(B^T=B\) and \(C^T=-C\). Its real quadratic coefficient matrix is
\[
G(A)=
\begin{pmatrix}
B&-C\\
C&B
\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
Q_A=\frac12\widehat R^TG(A)\widehat R.
\]
Direct calculation gives
\[
\det G(A)=(\det A)^2.
\]
For \(A>0\), the Williamson frequencies of \(G(A)\) are precisely
\[
\omega_1,\omega_2=\lambda_+,\lambda_-
\]
up to ordering. Hence the source Jordan trace and determinant remain recoverable:
\[
\operatorname{tr}_JA=\omega_1+\omega_2,
\qquad
\det_JA=\omega_1\omega_2.
\]

**That is a substantive survival result:** arbitrary metaplectic transport does not destroy these encoded positive spectral invariants.

What fails is the claim that the full group acts by Jordan automorphisms on the *original fixed copy* of \(\operatorname{Herm}_2(\mathbb C)\). Passive \(U(2)\) transformations preserve the number-preserving subspace. A generic Bogoliubov transformation,
\[
a\longmapsto ua+va^\dagger,
\]
introduces \(aa\) and \(a^\dagger a^\dagger\) terms. The transformed quadratic generally lies outside that four-dimensional subspace.

Thus the original eigenvalues can survive as symplectic invariants of the transported quadratic even though the transported observable is no longer another number-preserving \(Q_{A'}\). Recovering the source trace from \(\omega_1+\omega_2\) is different from taking half the ordinary trace of the squeezed coefficient matrix.

### A different embedding: \(\operatorname{Sym}_2(\mathbb R)\)

Consider instead
\[
A\longmapsto \frac12\widehat q^TA\widehat q.
\]
Here
\[
G(A)=
\begin{pmatrix}
A&0\\
0&0
\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Every such \(G(A)\) has zero four-dimensional determinant, and \(\Omega G(A)\) is nilpotent. Those symplectic spectral invariants cannot recover \(\det A\).

Indeed, the canonical transformation
\[
q\longmapsto Dq,\qquad p\longmapsto D^{-T}p
\]
gives
\[
A\longmapsto D^TAD,
\qquad
\det A\longmapsto(\det D)^2\det A.
\]
The source determinant is a relative invariant under this subgroup, becoming invariant when \(\det D=1\). Rank, inertia, and cone membership still survive.

These two examples show why the assertion “the rank-two Jordan determinant survives \(Mp(4,\mathbb R)\)” needs an explicit encoding and an explicit meaning of “survives.”

## 3. What does not survive: the ordinary Jordan product on the full oscillator space

The central algebraic limitation is
\[
\boxed{
d\Gamma(A\circ B)
\neq
\frac12\{d\Gamma(A),d\Gamma(B)\}
}
\]
in general.

Products of quadratic observables contain quartic terms. Those terms cancel in the commutator, which explains the exact Lie representation, but survive in the anticommutator.

The unit already proves the failure:
\[
d\Gamma(I)=N,
\]
so
\[
d\Gamma(I\circ I)=N,
\qquad
d\Gamma(I)^2=N^2.
\]
Likewise, a primitive Jordan idempotent maps to a mode-number operator, rather than an operator projection.

This has several consequences for the programme.

**Jordan rank is not Hilbert-space operator rank.** A rank-one positive matrix \(A\) can map, under Weyl ordering, to
\[
\lambda\left(n+\frac12\right),
\]
which has a strictly positive lower bound despite \(\det A=0\). The boundary of the source Jordan cone does not become the boundary defined by the existence of an operator kernel.

**Effect normalization is not automatic.** If
\[
0\preceq A\preceq I,
\]
then
\[
0\preceq d\Gamma(A)\preceq N.
\]
This does not make \(d\Gamma(A)\) an effect bounded by the Hilbert-space identity. Positive source observables and normalized quantum measurement effects are different structures here.

**The Jordan trace pairing is not an oscillator operator trace.** Quadratic oscillator observables are unbounded and generally not trace class. The source cone’s self-duality with respect to its finite-dimensional trace pairing therefore does not automatically become self-duality under \(\operatorname{Tr}_{\mathcal H}\).

There is a useful exception. On the one-particle sector,
\[
\mathcal H_{N=1}\simeq\mathbb C^2,
\]
the normal-ordered map \(d\Gamma(A)\) is exactly the original matrix \(A\), including its Jordan product and unit. But squeezing does not preserve this sector. One therefore cannot use the one-particle restriction to justify a faithful Jordan representation while simultaneously claiming the unrestricted metaplectic dynamics preserves it.

### The determinant acquires ordering corrections when made into an operator polynomial

Classically, a two-component amplitude \(\alpha\) gives Stokes data
\[
s_0=\alpha^\dagger\alpha,
\qquad
s_i=\alpha^\dagger\sigma_i\alpha,
\]
satisfying
\[
s_0^2-\sum_i s_i^2=0.
\]

Quantum mechanically, put
\[
T_0=N,\qquad T_i=a^\dagger\sigma_i a.
\]
The Schwinger identity is
\[
\sum_iT_i^2=N(N+2).
\]
Thus
\[
T_0^2-\sum_iT_i^2=-2N.
\]
With Weyl centering, \(S_0=N+1\) and \(S_i=T_i\), this becomes
\[
S_0^2-\sum_iS_i^2=I.
\]
The quantum Stokes relation explicitly contains the occupation-dependent correction. [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0108098?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The lesson is precise: **a classical determinant or null-cone relation may survive as an ordering-corrected Casimir relation, rather than as the unchanged polynomial obtained by replacing coordinates with operators.** Exact quadratic commutators do not remove this distinction.

## 4. The positivity that survives is substantial—but has several layers

### Positive quadratic forms and positive operators

For a homogeneous real Weyl quadratic on the full oscillator Hilbert space,
\[
\boxed{
G\succeq0\iff\widehat Q_G\succeq0.
}
\]
The forward implication follows by factoring \(G=C^TC\):
\[
\widehat Q_G
=\frac12\sum_\alpha(c_\alpha\cdot\widehat R)^2.
\]
For the converse, displacing a coherent state arbitrarily far along a negative direction of \(G\) makes its expectation arbitrarily negative.

This is a particularly clean positivity property of homogeneous quadratics. It does not extend unchanged to arbitrary Weyl symbols, and adding a scalar ordering shift changes the lower-bound question.

Metaplectic conjugation preserves this operator order exactly. It also preserves positivity and normalization of density operators. Gram matrices and variance matrices of encoded observables remain positive whenever the required moments exist.

However, positivity of one chosen oscillator Hamiltonian is not the relativistic spectrum condition. The latter concerns a jointly commuting energy–momentum four-vector and its joint spectrum.

### Quantum covariance positivity

Restore \(\hbar\), and define
\[
V_{ab}
=\frac12\left\langle
\Delta R_a\Delta R_b+\Delta R_b\Delta R_a
\right\rangle.
\]
Physical covariance matrices satisfy
\[
\boxed{
V+\frac{i\hbar}{2}\Omega\succeq0.
}
\]
Under a metaplectic state transformation,
\[
V\longmapsto SVS^T,
\]
and
\[
V+\frac{i\hbar}{2}\Omega
\longmapsto
S\left(V+\frac{i\hbar}{2}\Omega\right)S^T.
\]
Thus the full Robertson–Schrödinger uncertainty condition is preserved. Equivalently, the two symplectic eigenvalues obey
\[
\nu_1,\nu_2\geq\frac{\hbar}{2}.
\]
For two modes, this is a stronger requirement than a determinant bound alone. [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1110.3234?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The two basic symplectic invariants are
\[
I_2=-\frac12\operatorname{tr}[(\Omega V)^2]
=\nu_1^2+\nu_2^2,
\qquad
I_4=\det V=\nu_1^2\nu_2^2.
\]
For Gaussian states these determine the global entropy, while
\[
\operatorname{Tr}\rho^2
=\frac{\hbar^2}{4\sqrt{\det V}}
\]
gives the purity. Unitary evolution preserves purity and entropy for arbitrary states too, but covariance data determine them only within the Gaussian class. [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0307073?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

This also explains exactly what quadratic measurements access:
\[
\langle\widehat Q_G\rangle
=\frac12\operatorname{tr}(GV)+\frac12d^TGd,
\qquad d=\langle\widehat R\rangle.
\]
Expectations of all quadratic observables are controlled by first and second moments. Symmetrized products of quadratics probe fourth moments; their evaluation does not become a Jordan-product identity merely because Gaussian states allow those moments to be reconstructed.

### Coherency positivity survives, but its determinant need not be invariant

The optical coherency matrix
\[
C_{ij}=\langle a_j^\dagger a_i\rangle
\]
is positive semidefinite. Writing
\[
C=\frac12\left(\bar s_0I+\bar{\mathbf s}\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma\right)
\]
therefore gives
\[
\bar s_0\geq\|\bar{\mathbf s}\|,
\qquad
\det C=\frac14\left(\bar s_0^2-\|\bar{\mathbf s}\|^2\right)\geq0.
\]

This is a genuine surviving Lorentz-cone inequality for expectation values. It is not the same statement as an operator determinant identity, nor is \(\det C\) invariant under all squeezing.

For example, two-mode squeezing takes the vacuum’s \(C=0\) to
\[
C=\sinh^2r\,I_2.
\]
Its coherency determinant changes from zero to \(\sinh^4r\), while the global Gaussian state remains pure and its covariance symplectic eigenvalues remain \(\hbar/2\). The additional information resides in anomalous correlations such as \(\langle a_i a_j\rangle\). The complete covariance matrix closes under the symplectic action; the coherency matrix alone does not.

Similarly, global metaplectic transformations can create mode entanglement. Separability is preserved under transformations local to the chosen mode partition, not under arbitrary \(Sp(4,\mathbb R)\). The relevant additional positivity test for two-mode Gaussian separability is positivity after partial transposition, rather than ordinary state positivity alone. [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/html/0902.1502v2?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## 5. The obstruction to a local relativistic interpretation

A local relativistic carrier requires more than a unitary representation, a positive cone, and a Lorentz-shaped determinant.

In Minkowski language, one needs a spacetime representation with translation generators \(P_\mu\), a forward joint spectrum, and localized fields or observable algebras:
\[
[P_\mu,P_\nu]=0,
\qquad
\operatorname{spec}(P)\subseteq\overline V_+,
\]
\[
U(a,\Lambda)\mathcal A(O)U(a,\Lambda)^{-1}
=\mathcal A(\Lambda O+a),
\]
\[
[\mathcal A(O_1),\mathcal A(O_2)]=0
\quad\text{for spacelike-separated regions.}
\]
These localization and covariance conditions are independent structural ingredients of relativistic quantum field theory. They are not consequences of positivity on an oscillator Hilbert space. [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/0112041?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The four entries of \((q_1,q_2,p_1,p_2)\) are phase-space coordinates. Their preserved form is alternating. Four real coordinates and a positive cone of observables do not, by themselves, identify spacetime events, spacelike separation, or a causal propagation law.

There are two concrete ways to make the obstruction sharper.

### A \(3+1\)-dimensional Poincaré algebra cannot fit inside the ten quadratics

Suppose the programme requires all Poincaré generators to be realized by the ten homogeneous quadratic observables. Their Lie algebra is
\[
\mathfrak{sp}(4,\mathbb R).
\]
A faithful Poincaré realization would require
\[
\mathfrak{iso}(3,1)\hookrightarrow\mathfrak{sp}(4,\mathbb R).
\]
Both algebras have dimension ten, so such an injection would be an isomorphism. But \(\mathfrak{sp}(4,\mathbb R)\) is simple, whereas \(\mathfrak{iso}(3,1)\) has the nonzero abelian translation ideal \(\mathbb R^{3,1}\).

**That is an explicit algebraic obstruction under the restriction to these quadratics.** It is not repaired by observing that the parameter counts agree.

The natural Stokes operators illustrate the problem directly:
\[
[T_i,T_j]=2i\epsilon_{ijk}T_k.
\]
They cannot be the spatial components of a commuting momentum four-vector. A Lorentzian-looking expression formed from \(N,T_1,T_2,T_3\) therefore does not establish a relativistic mass shell.

Additional operators or a different spacetime construction can change this conclusion. The obstruction concerns the proposed identification within the specified quadratic algebra.

### Direct finite-mode field truncation obstructs exact microcausality

Suppose instead that the two oscillator modes are interpreted directly as spacetime field modes. More generally, consider a finite positive-energy plane-wave expansion
\[
\Phi_M(x)=\sum_{j=1}^{M}
\left(
c_je^{-ip_j\cdot x}a_j+
\overline c_je^{ip_j\cdot x}a_j^\dagger
\right),
\qquad p_j^0>0.
\]
Its commutator is
\[
[\Phi_M(x),\Phi_M(y)]
=-2i\sum_{j=1}^{M}|c_j|^2
\sin\bigl(p_j\cdot(x-y)\bigr)\,I.
\]

This finite sum is real analytic in \(x-y\). If it vanished on the open set of spacelike separations, it would vanish identically. Yet its time derivative at zero is
\[
-2i\sum_j|c_j|^2p_j^0\,I\neq0
\]
for a nontrivial field. Hence a field of this finite-mode form cannot satisfy exact microcausality.

This derivation identifies a genuine obstruction to the direct field-mode interpretation. It is not a theorem that an abstract oscillator Hilbert space can never enter relativistic physics: what matters is the representation of translations and localized observables on that space.

## 6. The singleton connection prevents an absolute “only an analogue” verdict

There is a legitimate relativistic connection:
\[
\mathfrak{sp}(4,\mathbb R)\cong\mathfrak{so}(3,2).
\]
But the associated Jordan geometries must be kept straight:

| Jordan algebra | Determinant signature | Conformal Lie algebra |
|---|---:|---|
| \(\operatorname{Sym}_2(\mathbb R)\) | \((1,2)\) | \(\mathfrak{sp}(4,\mathbb R)\cong\mathfrak{so}(3,2)\) |
| \(\operatorname{Herm}_2(\mathbb C)\) | \((1,3)\) | \(\mathfrak{su}(2,2)\cong\mathfrak{so}(4,2)\) |

Thus the natural Jordan-conformal interpretation of \(Sp(4,\mathbb R)\) concerns \(2+1\)-dimensional conformal geometry, or the related \(AdS_4\) symmetry—not the full \(3+1\)-dimensional conformal group. [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9301050?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The oscillator representation decomposes into even and odd sectors,
\[
\mathcal H_{\mathrm{osc}}
=\mathcal H_{\mathrm{even}}\oplus\mathcal H_{\mathrm{odd}},
\]
which realize the singleton modules
\[
\mathcal H_{\mathrm{even}}\simeq D\!\left(\frac12,0\right)
\quad\text{(Rac)},
\qquad
\mathcal H_{\mathrm{odd}}\simeq D\!\left(1,\frac12\right)
\quad\text{(Di)}.
\]
These supply scalar and spinor conformal representation data in three spacetime dimensions. [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.07102?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The crucial change of interpretation is that the oscillator basis can then label states of a **one-particle conformal representation**. The auxiliary oscillator creation operators are not being identified with the creation operators of a two-mode spacetime field. Constructing a local field requires the appropriate spacetime realization and field quantization.

Nor does the metaplectic double cover establish fermionic statistics. Schwinger oscillators can produce half-integer \(SU(2)\) representation labels while obeying bosonic CCR. A rotation sign, oscillator parity, and exchange statistics are distinct. The spin-statistics connection for local relativistic fields depends on covariance, positivity, spectrum, and localization assumptions; it is not derived from the existence of a double cover alone. [arxiv.org](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math-ph/0102035?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

The programme can therefore claim an **exact and experimentally meaningful realization of its specified quadratic observables, their canonical dynamics, and their verified order and spectral invariants**. That is stronger than a merely suggestive resemblance. Its spacetime claim remains a separate mathematical obligation: identify the relevant Jordan embedding and spacetime symmetry, construct the translation spectrum and localized observables, and establish causal commutativity and the appropriate statistics. For a \(3+1\)-dimensional construction confined to the ten homogeneous quadratics, the Poincaré-algebra obstruction already prevents that promotion.
