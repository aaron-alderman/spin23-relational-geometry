# Furey's Three Lepton-Doublet Candidates and the Triplet Portal

**Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Branches:** B03 and B11  
**Claim status:** exact block-map origin count and coupling multiplicity; identification with physical generations is not established

## 1. The three doublet origins

In the natural endomorphism convention

\[
Y_{\rm particle}=Y_{\rm source}-Y_{\rm target},
\]

the three \((\mathbf1,\mathbf2)_{-1/2}\) sectors arise from

\[
\boxed{
O_1\to H,
\qquad
R\to H,
\qquad
H\to C.
}
\]

These are distinct ordered block-map origins, not three copies inserted by hand.

## 2. Coupling to the external-index complex triplet

The full Jordan lock supplies

\[
\Delta\sim(\mathbf1,\mathbf3)_1.
\]

If the three doublet candidates are denoted \(L_A\), then gauge symmetry allows

\[
y_{AB}L_A L_B\Delta,
\]

where the weak-triplet Weyl contraction makes

\[
y_{AB}=y_{BA}.
\]

For three candidates this is a symmetric \(3\times3\) matrix with

\[
\boxed{6\text{ independent complex entries}}
\]

before reality, flavor or source constraints.

## 3. The tempting generation interpretation

This is the first point in the current bridge where the number three appears in a matter-sector multiplicity and directly feeds a familiar three-by-three flavor object. It is therefore worth retaining as an open path.

It is not evidence of three generations by itself:

- the quark-doublet type occurs only once in the same inventory;
- the down-conjugate, charged-singlet and neutral-singlet multiplicities are \(2,2,7\), not three;
- the full endomorphism algebra is mirror-complete;
- no source projector pairs the three lepton candidates with three complete anomaly-free generations;
- the three block origins have different positions in the carrier algebra.

Promoting them to generations would be a selective reading of one favorable multiplicity.

## 4. Custodial and chirality implications

The \(LL\Delta\) portal selects the charged complex triplet part of

\[
\mathfrak{su}(2)_{\rm adj}\otimes H_2(\mathbb R),
\]

not the neutral real triplet supplied by the trace coordinate. It therefore breaks the accidental right \(SO(3)_R\) unless the matter states are completed into appropriate right multiplets.

Moreover, the full endomorphism inventory contains mirror doublets. A physical portal must first specify the chiral projector; otherwise both orientations can couple and the result is vectorlike rather than a Standard-Model neutrino sector.

## 5. Decision tree

The branch should proceed only through explicit tests:

1. identify a source-native projector selecting one orientation of the three doublets;
2. determine whether the three selected states participate in three complete anomaly-free generations;
3. calculate the induced symmetric matrix \(y_{AB}\), rather than assigning it;
4. test compatibility with nonzero flavor CP violation;
5. compute whether the same portal destabilizes custodial alignment beyond the allowed range.

**Stop-everything positive:** one source projector simultaneously produces three complete chiral generations and constrains the symmetric triplet coupling.

**Negative:** the three lepton candidates cannot be paired with corresponding quark and singlet sectors without arbitrary copy choices.

**Current result:** three exact lepton-doublet block origins exist, but their interpretation as generations is unearned.

**Unsafe shortcut:** identify multiplicity three with the three observed families while ignoring every other sector's incompatible multiplicity.
