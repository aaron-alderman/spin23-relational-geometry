# The General Jordan-Native Potential for the Full Lock

**Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Branches:** B01, B04, B05 and B11  
**Claim status:** complete renormalizable invariant basis under connected \(SO(3)_L\times SO(2)_R\); quantum running and phenomenological parameter bounds remain open

## 1. Field split

Write the full lock as three weak-adjoint vectors,

\[
L=(u,v,a).
\]

The external Jordan automorphism \(SO(2)_R\) rotates \((u,v)\), the two traceless coordinates, while \(a\), the trace-axis column, is a singlet. The left \(SO(3)_L\) rotates all three vectors simultaneously.

Define

\[
\begin{aligned}
A&=a\cdot a,\\
B&=u\cdot u+v\cdot v,\\
P&=a\cdot(u\times v)=\det L,\\
D&=(u\cdot u-v\cdot v)^2+4(u\cdot v)^2,\\
C&=(a\cdot u)^2+(a\cdot v)^2.
\end{aligned}
\]

All five quantities are invariant under connected \(SO(3)_L\times SO(2)_R\).

## 2. Complete renormalizable potential

Every invariant scalar through degree four is generated by

\[
A,quad B;qquad P;qquad A^2, AB, B^2, D, C.
\]

Thus the general renormalizable potential is

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
V={}&-\mu_a^2A-\mu_b^2B+\kappa P\\
&+\lambda_aA^2+\lambda_{ab}AB+\lambda_bB^2
+\lambda_DD+\lambda_CC.
\end{aligned}
}
\]

If an additional reflection or \(L\mapsto-L\) symmetry is imposed, the determinant coupling \(\kappa\) vanishes. That reflection is extra structure; connected rotations alone permit \(P\).

The completeness follows because left-rotation scalars are generated by dot products and the scalar triple product. Right \(SO(2)\) then leaves exactly the combinations displayed above through quartic order.

## 3. Custodial subspace of couplings

Full right \(SO(3)_R\) invariance permits the structures

\[
I_1=(\operatorname{tr}L^TL)^2=(A+B)^2,
\]

\[
I_2=\operatorname{tr}[(L^TL)^2]
=A^2+2C+\frac12(B^2+D),
\]

as well as \(\det L\). Therefore the Jordan-native couplings lie on the custodial locus only if

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\mu_a^2&=\mu_b^2,\\
\lambda_a&=\lambda_1+\lambda_2,\\
\lambda_{ab}&=2\lambda_1,\\
\lambda_b&=\lambda_1+\frac12\lambda_2,\\
\lambda_D&=\frac12\lambda_2,\\
\lambda_C&=2\lambda_2.
\end{aligned}
}
\]

The determinant coefficient \(\kappa\) is unrestricted because \(\det(R_LLR_R^T)=\det L\) for connected rotations.

These relations are not implied by \(SO(3)_L\times SO(2)_R\). Custodial symmetry is a special coefficient surface.

## 4. Aligned stationary ansatz

Take

\[
u=ke_1,qquad v=ke_2,qquad a=we_3.
\]

Then

\[
A=w^2,quad B=2k^2,quad P=wk^2,quad C=D=0,
\]

and

\[
V(w,k)
=-\mu_a^2w^2-2\mu_b^2k^2+\kappa wk^2
+\lambda_aw^4+2\lambda_{ab}w^2k^2+4\lambda_bk^4.
\]

For nonzero \(w,k\), stationarity requires

\[
\mu_a^2
=2\lambda_aw^2+2\lambda_{ab}k^2
+\frac{\kappa k^2}{2w},
\]

\[
\mu_b^2
=\frac{\kappa w}{2}+\lambda_{ab}w^2+4\lambda_bk^2.
\]

Nothing in these equations forces \(w=k\). The certificate supplies an explicit locally stable radial stationary point with \(w=2k\), demonstrating that misalignment is not merely formal.

## 5. The determinant-metric tension in the potential

The Jordan determinant distinguishes trace and traceless axes through

\[
q_J=t^2-x^2-y^2.
\]

At the full-lock level, the analogous quadratic distinction is

\[
A-B.
\]

It is allowed by the Jordan-native symmetry but forbidden by custodial \(SO(3)_R\). Thus a potential that remembers the determinant geometry naturally contains precisely the kind of trace/traceless splitting that can misalign the custodial vacuum.

This does not prove misalignment is unavoidable; it proves alignment is not protected by the Jordan automorphism group.
