That is evidence for a novelty lead, not proof of novelty.

### 2. Standard-Model two-current response versus Furey topology — one calculation short

The source action, vacuum \(H_2(\mathbb R)_+\) closure, affine frame torsor and UV metric form a clean story. But most analytic ingredients are standard QFT or response theory. It becomes a genuine paper when the interacting operator-mixing matrix and at least one renormalized correlator are calculated.

### 3. Freudenthal inverse graph and scale no-go — specialist novelty uncertain

The paired-source quartic critical locus and scale obstruction are exact and elegant. They may support a focused mathematical note. The risk is that the result is a coordinate expression of known Freudenthal rank geometry. A specialist literature audit and coordinate-free proof are required before calling it novel.

### 4. Programme-level falsifiability atlas — perspective rather than discovery

The dependency graph and no-go atlas could become a useful methods or perspective paper about type errors in algebraic reconstruction programmes. Its value is critical synthesis. It should not be presented as a new fundamental theory.
