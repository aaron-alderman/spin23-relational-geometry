# D-03 electroweak lift and closure audit

## The brave conclusion

The existence of a full `H_2` carrier is probably generic in an interacting gauge–Higgs theory. The Standard Model trace contains several gauge beta-function operators, the quartic beta-function operator, masses, Yukawa-running representatives and improvement/EOM terms. Once two sources see three distinct projective state directions, `H_2` span follows. The electroweak theory has an abundance of candidate thresholds.

This weakens—not strengthens—the case that full span signals a new symmetry. Carrier existence is cheap. A meaningful Spin(2,3) claim must instead produce a physical transformation law or a nontrivial invariant relation.

## Transformation-closure no-go

Physical source relabellings act by `GL(2)` congruence. The full ambient `Sp(4,R)` action is fractional:

`M -> (A M+B)(C M+D)^(-1)`.

For the positive Herglotz fixture

`M(z)=diag(-1/z,-1/(z-2))`,

the lower symplectic shear `[[I,0],[I,I]]` maps the poles from `{0,2}` to `{1,3}`. It preserves the ambient analytic-positive domain but not the fixed physical spectral support. Generic `Sp(4,R)` therefore does not close on the QFT response family. The established physical action is only the congruence/contact subgroup.

The current classification is consequently:

- `Spin(2,3)` remains a useful ambient response geometry;
- it is not established as a dynamical symmetry of the metric–Higgs family.

## A2 bridge

Three channel labels can always be centered into a two-dimensional plane and decorated with the abstract permutation representation of `S_3=W(A_2)`. That is bookkeeping, not physics. Gauge-pair, scalar-pair and four-scalar channels have unequal invariant thresholds, so the dynamics supplies no permutation symmetry between them. There are no roots, reflections or typed intertwiners.

The A2/Higgs/code bridge is rejected at the present gate. It may re-enter only with a threshold-preserving `S_3` action and explicit intertwiners.

## Constrained observable

The simplified scalar rung does yield a falsifiable leading-order relation. With `v(r)=(r^2,1,sqrt(2)r)` and the gauge direction `v_infinity=(1,0,0)`, define

`Q=(1/sqrt(2)) det[v_infinity,v(r_2),v(r_4)]`.

Then

`Q=r_4-r_2=beta_lambda/[lambda sum_i D_i]`.

This “quartic-anomaly projective orientation” vanishes at the scalar fixed point and is invariant under a common triangular improvement shear at fixed kinematics. It is a constrained perturbative target, not yet a normalized electroweak prediction.

## Remaining external frontiers

- BRST-complete electroweak form factors and spectral densities;
- a three-control thermal/background Jacobian;
- a physical non-congruence map preserving thresholds and positivity;
- an exact threshold-preserving `S_3` action;
- a renormalized experimental or lattice protocol for `Q`.

## Evidence boundary

The BRST and spectral qualifications are consistent with explicit one-loop Higgs-model studies of [renormalized BRST-invariant composites in U(1)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.01770) and [positive spectral densities of gauge-invariant composites in SU(2) Yang–Mills–Higgs theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.07813). These papers support the need for the completion; they do not supply the metric–Higgs mixed form factor calculated here in simplified form.
