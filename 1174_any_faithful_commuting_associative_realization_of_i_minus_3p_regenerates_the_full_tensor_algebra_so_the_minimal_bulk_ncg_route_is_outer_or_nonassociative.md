## Executive result

The intermediate \(U(1)_X=\exp(it(I-3P))\) cannot be installed as an ordinary faithful commuting associative coordinate symmetry while avoiding the tensor-algebra enlargement found in document 1171.

The reason is elementary and exact. Since

\[
X=I-3P,
\]

the projector and its complement are polynomials in \(X\):

\[
P=\frac{I-X}{3},
\qquad
Q=\frac{2I+X}{3}.
\]

Thus the unital complex associative algebra generated by \(X\) is already

\[
\mathbb C P\oplus\mathbb C Q\cong\mathbb C\oplus\mathbb C.
\]

If this algebra acts independently and commutes with a faithful generation-blind Standard-Model representation on

\[
H_{\rm int}\otimes H_f,
\]

then the algebra generated by both actions is the faithful image of

\[
\mathcal A_{\rm SM}\otimes_{\mathbb R}(\mathbb C\oplus\mathbb C).
\]

Its real dimension is \(24\cdot4=96\), and its Wedderburn decomposition is the enlarged algebra already proved in document 1171.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{“put }I-3P\text{ in the associative multiplicity commutant”}
\Longrightarrow
\text{the full tensor algebra}.
}
\]

The desired middle structure is not an undiscovered ordinary associative subalgebra. The surviving bulk routes are genuinely different:

1. gauge a selected outer/normalizer subgroup without adjoining its entire associative span as coordinates;
2. use a groupoid or correspondence rather than one algebra acting on both slots;
3. use a Jordan/conditional selector whose physical closure is not the full associative tensor closure;
4. find a nonfaithful quotient and compute its kernel explicitly;
5. move the symmetry to a boundary or defect.

This is the first strict no-go in the new embedding sequence.

## 1. The generator already contains the two-point algebra

Because \(X\) has two distinct eigenvalues, \(-2\) and \(+1\), its spectral idempotents are degree-one polynomials:

\[
P=\frac{I-X}{3},
\qquad
Q=I-P=\frac{2I+X}{3}.
\]

Every polynomial in \(X\) is block scalar:

\[
f(X)=f(-2)P+f(1)Q.
\]

Conversely every pair of complex block scalars can be obtained by interpolation. Hence

\[
\operatorname{Alg}_{\mathbb C}(I,X)
=
\mathbb CP\oplus\mathbb CQ.
\]

It is impossible to adjoin the charge generator associatively but somehow omit the family idempotents that cause the tensor decomposition.

## 2. Faithful independent actions generate the tensor product

Let

\[
\pi_{\rm int}:\mathcal A_{\rm SM}\to\operatorname{End}(H_{\rm int})
\]

and

\[
\pi_f:\mathcal F_2\to\operatorname{End}(H_f)
\]

be faithful finite-dimensional real representations. On the tensor-product Hilbert space define

\[
\pi(a\otimes f)=\pi_{\rm int}(a)\otimes\pi_f(f).
\]

Choose real vector-space bases \(\{A_i\}\) and \(\{F_\alpha\}\) whose represented matrices are linearly independent. Their Kronecker products

\[
\{A_i\otimes F_\alpha\}
\]

are linearly independent. Therefore \(\pi\) is faithful and

\[
\dim_{\mathbb R}\pi(\mathcal A_{\rm SM}\otimes_{\mathbb R}\mathcal F_2)
=
\dim_{\mathbb R}\mathcal A_{\rm SM}\,
\dim_{\mathbb R}\mathcal F_2
=24\cdot4=96.
\]

The commuting images generate this tensor-product image, not merely the product subgroup of their unitary groups.

## 3. Why selecting only the central \(U(1)\) does not evade it

One might try to adjoin only

\[
\left\{\exp(itX):t\in\mathbb R\right\}
\]

rather than all of \(\mathbb C\oplus\mathbb C\). But the complex linear and multiplicative span of that group contains \(I\) and \(X\), hence \(P,Q\), hence the full two-point algebra.

As a group action, \(U(1)_X\) is smaller. As an associative coordinate-algebra extension, its generated algebra is not.

This distinction is the heart of the current fork:

\[
\boxed{
\text{a selected group of operators may be small even when its associative envelope is large.}
}
\]

Ordinary inner NCG gauge transformations are derived from algebra unitaries, so using only the small group needs additional structure that selects it from the larger envelope.

## 4. Scope of the no-go

The theorem assumes:

- the Standard-Model and family actions commute;
- both actions are faithful;
- the family action is independent on a tensor-factor multiplicity space;
- the combined coordinate object is a unital associative real algebra.

It does not exclude:

- a representation with a nontrivial tensor-product kernel;
- a linking algebra whose family operator appears only on selected corners;
- a bimodule correspondence or groupoid with restricted composability;
- a real Jordan algebra of observables;
- an outer automorphism group promoted independently to gauge data;
- a boundary algebra different from the bulk coordinate algebra.

But each escape must exhibit its actual closure law. Calling the same commuting matrices a “normalizer” does not avoid the theorem if their associative span is silently restored.

## 5. Consequence for the represented one-form route

The minimal two-point finite triple of documents 1100–1103 genuinely produces a charge-three one-form. Attempting to tensor that triple faithfully with the Standard-Model triple regenerates the 96-dimensional algebra and its enlarged unitary structure.

Therefore a bulk represented-one-form completion has only three honest options:

1. **accept the enlarged algebra** and prove that \(uJuJ^{-1}\), unimodularity and junk remove all unwanted gauge/scalar directions;
2. **construct a nonfaithful/linking representation** whose kernel removes the unwanted mixed algebra factors while retaining the \(P|Q\) edge;
3. **leave ordinary associative inner fluctuations** and realize the charged relation through an outer, Jordan or correspondence calculus.

The first is now disfavoured but not formally excluded until its physical gauge kernel is computed. The second is constructive and testable. The third is the leading conceptual branch.

## 6. Why Jordan language is now substantive

The Jordan selector branch is no longer merely a preferred vocabulary. It may avoid a proven associative closure problem.

The Peirce decomposition needs:

\[
P,\quad Q,\quad PJQ\oplus QJP,
\]

and its triple product controls relational composition. It does not automatically grant every independent unitary in the associative tensor envelope the status of a gauge transformation.

This does not mean a Jordan model automatically works. It must define:

- its derivation or structure group;
- the charged phase lift;
- the analogue of represented one-forms;
- positivity and spectral action;
- coupling to the associative Standard-Model algebra.

But the reason to pursue it is now exact: the ordinary faithful commuting associative alternative necessarily overcloses.
