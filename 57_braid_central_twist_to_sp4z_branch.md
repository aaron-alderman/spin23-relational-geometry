# Three-Strand Braid Central Twist to (Sp(4,\mathbb Z))

**Date:** 16 October 2026  
**Branches:** B09 braid/Hopf/modular route, with links to B07 and B12  
**Claim status:** explicit homomorphism established; physical braid interpretation and genus-two modular dynamics remain open

## 1. The explicit representation

Let the generators of \(B_3\) be \(\sigma_1,\sigma_2\), with

\[
\sigma_1\sigma_2\sigma_1
=\sigma_2\sigma_1\sigma_2.
\]

Define

\[
\rho(\sigma_1)=
\begin{pmatrix}1&1\\0&1\end{pmatrix},
\qquad
\rho(\sigma_2)=
\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

These matrices lie in \(SL(2,\mathbb Z)\) and satisfy the braid relation exactly.

Embed \(SL(2,\mathbb Z)\) into \(Sp(4,\mathbb Z)\) by

\[
\iota(g)=
\begin{pmatrix}
g&0\\0&g^{-T}
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Thus

\[
\boxed{
B_3\xrightarrow{\rho}SL(2,\mathbb Z)
\xrightarrow{\iota}Sp(4,\mathbb Z)
}
\]

is a concrete representation of the three-strand braid group inside the integral symplectic group relevant to the programme.

## 2. The full twist becomes the spinorial sign

The center of \(B_3\) is generated by

\[
z=(\sigma_1\sigma_2)^3.
\]

Direct multiplication gives

\[
\rho(z)=-I_2,
\qquad
\iota\rho(z)=-I_4.
\]

Therefore the central full twist acts by a sign on the four-dimensional symplectic carrier.

But on the Jordan carrier,

\[
X\mapsto(-I_2)^{-T}X(-I_2)^{-1}=X.
\]

It is likewise invisible on two-forms and hence on the five-dimensional orthogonal carrier.

This yields an exact three-level statement:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{carrier}&\text{full-twist action}\\
\hline
W\cong\mathbb R^4&-I\\
J\cong\operatorname{Sym}_2(\mathbb R)&+I\\
\Lambda^2_0W\cong\mathbb R^{2,3}&+I
\end{array}
}
\]

The twist is visible to the spinorial/symplectic carrier and invisible to the observable and vector carriers. This is precisely the kind of “hidden phase after a loop” the narrative had been seeking, now as a proved representation fact.

## 3. Relation to modular quotients

Because \(z\) maps to \(-I\), its square maps to identity. Projectivizing kills \(-I\) and gives the familiar quotient toward \(PSL(2,\mathbb Z)\).

The representation does **not** fill \(Sp(4,\mathbb Z)\). Its image lies in the embedded \(SL(2,\mathbb Z)\) Levi subgroup. Thus it provides a genus-one modular rung inside the genus-two group, not a derivation of genus-two dynamics.

## 4. Relation to Lagrangian phase space

The embedded group acts on the Lagrangian chart by

\[
X\mapsto g^{-T}Xg^{-1}.
\]

The central twist fixes every \(X\). A loop can therefore return the relational matrix to itself while changing the sign of a lifted symplectic/spinor state.

This is an exact mathematical version of:

> the observable relation closes, but the carrier remembers a twist.

## 5. What has and has not been achieved

### Established

- an explicit \(B_3\to Sp(4,\mathbb Z)\) homomorphism;
- the central twist maps to \(-I_4\);
- the sign is invisible on \(J\) and \(\Lambda^2_0W\);
- the image is only an embedded \(SL(2,\mathbb Z)\), not the full genus-two modular group.

### Open

- what physical objects are braided;
- whether the representation is selected rather than chosen;
- whether a metaplectic lift produces a measurable Berry/Maslov phase;
- how a second independent modular handle enlarges the image toward \(Sp(4,\mathbb Z)\);
- whether any partition function or anomaly transforms under this group.

## 6. Effect on the Moonshine branch

B09 has advanced from analogy to an explicit representation. B12 does not yet reopen fully: a group homomorphism is not a modular partition function, character, or sewing law.

The new unlock condition is narrower:

> construct a physical state space whose braid action is the displayed representation and whose two-handle sewing extends it beyond the Levi \(SL(2,\mathbb Z)\) subgroup.

## 7. Stop-everything and falsification tests

**Positive:** the same \(-I\) twist appears as a measured metaplectic, fermionic, Berry or Maslov phase while all Jordan observables close, and the representation extends canonically to a genus-two modular object.

**Negative:** the embedding remains one arbitrary copy of \(SL(2,\mathbb Z)\) among many, with no physical braid carrier or observable phase.
