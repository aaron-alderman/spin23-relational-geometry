## 1. Left, right and custodial generators

The full-lock fluctuation transforms as

\[
X\longmapsto U_LXU_R^T.
\]

Infinitesimally, the Hermitian left and right generators act as

\[
T_L^iX=iJ_iX,
\qquad
T_R^iX=-iXJ_i.
\]

The unbroken custodial generator is

\[
T_C^i=T_L^i+T_R^i=i[J_i,X].
\]

When projected onto the symmetric-traceless fiveplet,

\[
\boxed{
P_5T_L^iP_5
=P_5T_R^iP_5
=\frac12T_C^i.
}
\]

This identity is exact and fixes the complete gauge-generator algebra internal to the fiveplet.

## 2. Neutral vertices

Let

\[
s=\sin\theta_K=\frac{g_E}{\sqrt{g^2+g_E^2}},
\qquad
c=\cos\theta_K=\frac{g}{\sqrt{g^2+g_E^2}},
\qquad
e=gs=g_Ec.
\]

For the state \(H_q\),

\[
T_C^3H_q=qH_q,
\qquad
T_L^3H_q=T_R^3H_q=\frac q2H_q
\]

after projection within the fiveplet. Therefore

\[
\boxed{g_{\gamma q}=eq,}
\]

and

\[
\boxed{
g_{Zq}
=\frac{e}{sc}\left(\frac q2-s^2q\right)
=\frac{eq(c^2-s^2)}{2sc}.
}
\]

The neutral component has no diagonal derivative coupling to \(\gamma\) or \(Z\) within the fiveplet. This does not exclude off-diagonal or two-vector couplings generated by the vacuum and the full kinetic term.

## 3. Charged-current ladder

The custodial spin-two raising operator satisfies

\[
T_C^+|2,q\rangle
=\sqrt{(2-q)(3+q)},|2,q+1\rangle.
\]

Because the weak gauge field acts through \(T_L^+=T_C^+/2\) after projection, the group-theory magnitude of the \(W^+H_qH_{q+1}\) vertex is

\[
\boxed{
\frac{g}{2\sqrt2}
\sqrt{(2-q)(3+q)},
}
\]

with the usual momentum factor and an overall sign determined by all-momenta-incoming conventions.

Thus

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
q\to q+1&\text{coefficient magnitude}\\ \hline
-2\to-1&g/\sqrt2\\
-1\to0&g\sqrt3/2\\
0\to1&g\sqrt3/2\\
1\to2&g/\sqrt2.
\end{array}
\]

The lowering vertices are Hermitian conjugates.

## 4. What is and is not predicted

The following are representation-protected:

- all photon charges;
- the diagonal \(Z\) coefficients above;
- ratios of adjacent-charge \(W\) ladder matrix elements;
- charge conjugation between \(q\) and \(-q\).

The following are not fixed by this calculation:

- the common fiveplet mass;
- custodial-breaking mass splittings;
- vertices involving a separate Higgs doublet;
- scalar cubic and quartic self-couplings;
- Yukawa couplings and decay branching fractions;
- singlet–fiveplet mixing away from the custodial point.
