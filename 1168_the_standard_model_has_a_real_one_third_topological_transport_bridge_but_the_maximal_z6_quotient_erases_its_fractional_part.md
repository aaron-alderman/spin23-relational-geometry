# The Standard Model Has a Real One-Third Topological-Transport Bridge, but the Maximal \(\mathbb Z_6\) Quotient Erases Its Fractional Part

**Date:** 19 October 2026

## Executive result

The search for physical transport of the internally derived prime three has found a better candidate than an invented colour-centre BF sector.

The renormalizable Standard Model has:

- a \(U(1)_{B-L}\) zero-form global symmetry;
- a magnetic \(U(1)\) one-form symmetry inherited from hypercharge;
- a \(\mathbb Z_3\) selection rule relating fractional \(B\!-\!L\) charge to hypercharge;
- a scheme-independent fractional topological response \(\xi\) between the two currents.

For

\[
G_{\rm SM}
=
\frac{SU(3)_C\times SU(2)_W\times U(1)_Y}{\Gamma},
\qquad
\Gamma=\mathbb Z_n\subseteq\mathbb Z_6,
\]

and fractionalization class \(k\), the response is

\[
\boxed{
\xi=\frac n3+\frac{kn}{6}\pmod1,
\qquad
k=0,\ldots,\frac6n-1.
}
\]

The induced currents obey

\[
J^{(2)}=\xi\,\star\frac{dA}{2\pi},
\qquad
J^{(1)}=\xi\,\star\frac{dB}{2\pi},
\]

where \(A\) is the \(B\!-\!L\) background and \(B\) is the background for the magnetic one-form symmetry.

This is an actual Standard-Model source/response relation involving compact zero- and one-form symmetry data. It is much closer to the programme's relational-carrier language than equality of Gram matrices alone.

But the global-form table creates a sharp discriminator:

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
\Gamma&\text{electric one-form symmetry}&\text{possible }\xi\bmod1&1/3\text{ possible?}\\ \hline
1&\mathbb Z_6&0,\frac16,\frac13,\frac12,\frac23,\frac56&\text{yes}\\
\mathbb Z_2&\mathbb Z_3&0,\frac13,\frac23&\text{yes}\\
\mathbb Z_3&\mathbb Z_2&0,\frac12&\text{no}\\
\mathbb Z_6&1&0&\text{no}
\end{array}
\]

The especially attractive case is \(\Gamma=\mathbb Z_2\): it has an exact electric \(\mathbb Z_3\) one-form symmetry and permits \(\xi=\pm1/3\). The maximally quotiented \(\Gamma=\mathbb Z_6\) theory instead has no electric one-form symmetry and has fractionally trivial \(\xi\).

Thus the Furey matter representation's \(\mathbb Z_6\) kernel and the desired one-third transport cannot both be promoted silently to the same global gauge-group claim. Matter quantum numbers allow all four global forms; heavy line operators distinguish them.

This does not yet produce the two-qutrit \(A_2\) Weil carrier. It supplies one physical mixed response. A further finite reduction, gauging, Higgsing or defect construction must turn the magnetic \(U(1)\) one-form sector into a paired \(\mathbb Z_3\) algebra.

## 1. The Standard-Model symmetry structure

The exact global form is not fixed by the Lie algebra and observed local multiplets. It may use

\[
\Gamma=1,\ \mathbb Z_2,\ \mathbb Z_3,\ \mathbb Z_6.
\]

David Tong's [line-operator analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.01853) shows that these choices have different spectra of Wilson, 't Hooft and dyonic lines despite sharing the same local Standard-Model fields.

For \(\Gamma=\mathbb Z_n\), the one-form symmetry is

\[
U(1)_{\rm magnetic}\times(\mathbb Z_6/\Gamma)_{\rm electric}.
\]

The continuous magnetic factor comes from the hypercharge Bianchi identity. The discrete electric factor measures central Wilson lines not screened by the declared matter/line spectrum.

The maximally faithful quotient divides out the full kernel acting trivially on known local fields. But local matter does not determine whether that kernel has been gauged in the global definition. This corrects any earlier use of “faithful” as if it experimentally fixed \(\Gamma=\mathbb Z_6\).

## 2. The exact \(\mathbb Z_3\) selection rule

Normalize \(B\!-\!L\) so gauge-invariant baryon and lepton operators have integral charge. Quarks then carry fractional \(B\!-\!L\) charge and obey

\[
\boxed{
Q_{B-L}=\frac13q_Y\pmod1.
}
\]

Equivalently, the zero-form symmetry has global structure

\[
\frac{G_{\rm SM}\times\widetilde U(1)_{B-L}}{\mathbb Z_3}.
\]

This explicitly transports prime three from quark fractionalization into a relation between gauge charge and global conserved charge. It does not rely on three generations.

Hsin and Gomis derive this structure and its response in [*Detecting Standard Model Gauge Group from Generalized Fractional Quantum Hall Effect*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.18160).

## 3. The response coefficient

Let \(A\) be a background one-form field for \(B\!-\!L\), and \(B\) a background two-form field for the magnetic one-form symmetry. The mixed response is visible in

\[
\langle J^{(1)}_\mu(x)J^{(2)}_{\nu\lambda}(0)\rangle
=
i\frac{\xi}{2\pi}
\epsilon_{\mu\nu\lambda\rho}\partial^\rho\delta^{(4)}(x)
+\cdots.
\]

Integer shifts of \(\xi\) can be produced by local counterterms, so its fractional part is invariant.

For zero fractionalization class,

\[
\xi=\frac n3\pmod1.
\]

More generally,

\[
\xi=\frac n3+\frac{kn}{6}\pmod1,
\qquad
k\in\mathbb Z_{6/n}.
\]

The discrete \(k\) specifies how \(B\!-\!L\) defects are decorated by electric one-form defects.

## 4. Exact global-form table

For \(\Gamma=1\), the electric one-form symmetry is \(\mathbb Z_6\), and

\[
\xi\in\left\{0,\frac16,\frac13,\frac12,\frac23,\frac56\right\}.
\]

For \(\Gamma=\mathbb Z_2\), the electric one-form symmetry is exactly \(\mathbb Z_3\), and

\[
\xi\in\left\{0,\frac13,\frac23\right\}.
\]

For \(\Gamma=\mathbb Z_3\), the electric one-form symmetry is \(\mathbb Z_2\), and

\[
\xi\in\left\{0,\frac12\right\}.
\]

For \(\Gamma=\mathbb Z_6\), the electric one-form symmetry is trivial, and

\[
\xi=0\pmod1.
\]

This is an actual branch filter: a fractional transport measurement would distinguish global forms invisible to ordinary local scattering.

## 6. Why it is not yet the \(Sp(4,\mathbb F_3)\) carrier

The response contains one zero-form background and one magnetic one-form background:

\[
A\in\Omega^1,
\qquad
B\in\Omega^2.
\]

The certified two-qutrit carrier requires two finite conjugate pairs with exponent module \(\mathbb F_3^4\). The response does not automatically provide:

1. a dynamical \(\mathbb Z_3\) gauge field rather than a background;
2. a second independent \(\mathbb Z_3\) pair;
3. the nondegenerate hyperbolic commutator;
4. the self and mutual quadratic refinements;
5. the full \(Sp(4,\mathbb F_3)\) action.

Therefore

\[
\text{one-third SM topological transport}
\neq
\text{two-qutrit Weil carrier}.
\]

The former is nevertheless a credible physical input from which the latter might be produced on a compactification or defect.

## 7. The \(\Gamma=\mathbb Z_2\) branch

For \(\Gamma=\mathbb Z_2\), the Standard Model has an electric \(\mathbb Z_3\) one-form symmetry. An appropriate fractionalization class gives \(\xi=\pm1/3\).

This is the unique global form for which

\[
(\mathbb Z_6/\Gamma)_{\rm electric}\cong\mathbb Z_3,
\qquad
\xi=\pm\frac13
\]

coexist cleanly.

Necessary next steps are:

1. compactify or place the theory on a defect with nontrivial cycles;
2. determine the transgressed zero-mode algebra of \(A\) and \(B\);
3. decide whether gauging or Higgsing leaves a finite magnetic \(\mathbb Z_3\) partner;
4. calculate the intersection pairing;
5. compare its quadratic refinement with \(q(a)=a^2/3\);
6. test whether two independent cycles yield \(\mathbb F_3^4\).

## 8. The colour–flavour-centre alternative

QCD with \(N\) colours and \(n_f\) equal-mass flavours can admit

\[
\mathbb Z_{\gcd(N,n_f)}
\]

colour–flavour-centre symmetry under engineered flavour-twisted boundary conditions. For \(N=n_f=3\), this gives \(\mathbb Z_3\). See [Cherman et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.05385).

This does not hold generically in the physical Standard Model: quark masses differ, Yukawa matrices break the required cyclic flavour symmetry, and the boundary twist must be engineered. It remains a controlled deformation, but it is weaker as a direct derivation than the \(B\!-\!L\)/magnetic response.

## 9. Revised status of the \(\mathbb Z_6\) claim

The corpus correctly proved that diagonal \(\mathbb Z_6\) acts trivially on all known Standard-Model multiplets. Therefore the local matter representation factors faithfully through the maximal quotient.

The stronger global statement

\[
G_{\rm SM}
=
\frac{SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1)}{\mathbb Z_6}
\]

is not fixed by those multiplets alone. It also specifies allowed heavy line operators and bundles. The other quotients remain compatible with present local data.

Choosing the maximal quotient removes the electric one-form symmetry and the fractional part of \(\xi\). Choosing \(\Gamma=\mathbb Z_2\) preserves an electric \(\mathbb Z_3\) and permits one-third transport. The programme has reached an empirical/global-topological fork, not an algebraic contradiction.
