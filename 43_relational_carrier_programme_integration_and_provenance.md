## 7. Why the provenance uncertainty is scientifically useful

The idea should not be discarded because its origin is uncertain, nor promoted because it feels remembered.

Instead it receives two independent ledgers:

- **provenance ledger:** what source actually said what;
- **warrant ledger:** what mathematics and physics support each statement.

A reconstructed idea can earn warrant through proof even if its historical source remains unknown. Conversely, locating an old LLM passage would establish provenance but would not establish truth.

That distinction prevents source memory from becoming evidence.
