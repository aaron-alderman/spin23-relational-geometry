# A Quarter-Turn Maps the Real Domain-Wall Carrier to the Population-Normal Furey Carrier

**Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Branches:** B01, B03, B04, B06, B07, B08, B11 and B13

## 1. Two different \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) embeddings

The finite domain-wall construction uses real boundary probes and therefore produces

\[
J_y=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\{I,\sigma_x,\sigma_z\},
\]

the entrywise-real copy of \(H_2(\mathbb R)\).

The charge-compatible Furey survivor exchange produces

\[
J_z=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\{I,\sigma_x,\sigma_y\},
\]

the equal-population copy.

These are abstractly isomorphic, but their physical coordinate meanings differ. There is nevertheless an exact fixed transformation between them.

## 2. The quarter-turn

Define

\[
\boxed{
U=e^{-i\pi\sigma_x/4}
=\frac1{\sqrt2}(I-i\sigma_x).}
\]

Its adjoint action rotates Bloch vectors through \(\pi/2\) about the \(x\)-axis:

\[
U\sigma_xU^\dagger=\sigma_x,
\qquad
U\sigma_zU^\dagger=-\sigma_y.
\]

Therefore

\[
\boxed{U J_y U^\dagger=J_z.}
\]

The determinant, positive cone and rank are preserved by unitary congruence.

## 3. Domain-wall zero-mode residue

The real domain-wall zero mode gives a boundary residue

\[
R_{\rm wall}=pp^T,
\qquad p=(p_1,p_2)^T\in\mathbb R^2.
\]

After the quarter-turn,

\[
q=Up
=\frac1{\sqrt2}
\begin{pmatrix}p_1-ip_2\\p_2-ip_1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

The two component norms are equal:

\[
|q_1|^2=|q_2|^2=\frac12(p_1^2+p_2^2).
\]

Hence

\[
UR_{\rm wall}U^\dagger=qq^\dagger
=\begin{pmatrix}t&c\\\bar c&t\end{pmatrix}
\in J_z^+,
\]

with determinant zero. The domain-wall boundary ray is exactly an exchange-fixed population-normal ray in the rotated probe basis.

## 4. The selector antiunitary also transports correctly

Standard conjugation \(K\) fixes \(J_y\). Under the probe rotation,

\[
UKU^\dagger
=(-i\sigma_x)K.
\]

The overall phase of an antiunitary cancels in its action on observables, so this is the same fixed algebra as

\[
\sigma_xK,
\]

the Furey survivor exchange selector.

Thus both the cone and its real structure match under the same quarter-turn.

## 5. Herglotz and Schur covariance

If the wall response is

\[
G_y(z)=P(H-zI)^{-1}P^\dagger,
\qquad \operatorname{Im}G_y(z)\geq0,
\]

then

\[
G_z(z)=UG_y(z)U^\dagger
\]

satisfies

\[
\operatorname{Im}G_z(z)geq0
\]

and takes values in the complexification of \(J_z\). Applying \(U\) to the boundary block and boundary–bulk coupling also transports the exact Schur-complement formula. No causal or sewing information is lost.

Therefore the domain-wall and Furey carriers are exactly equivalent as response categories.

## 6. Why this is not merely a basis change physically

The wall probes were originally real local sources. The matrix \(U\) forms complex quadratures with a fixed relative phase of \(\pi/2\). Operationally implementing it requires:

- phase-coherent boundary sources;
- an oriented causal or frequency convention;
- or an internal complex structure supplying the quadrature.

If only real local probes are admissible, \(J_y\) is the physical embedding and \(J_z\) is not directly measured. If phase-resolved probes are natural, the population-normal Furey form may be the better description.

This is why abstract unitary equivalence does not settle which real form nature selects.
