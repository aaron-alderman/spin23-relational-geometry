# The 25 Minimum Anomaly-Code Logicals Are Not Single Furey Ternary Products

**Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Branches:** B03, B04, B09, B11 and B14  
**Claim status:** exact finite enumeration and structural incidence no-go

## 1. Question

Prakash and Ramya find 25 minimum weight-three logical supports after puncturing \(\nu^c\). Including the removed coordinate, each support is a neutral four-field charge relation. The completed Furey matter TRO has 80 nonzero oriented products

\[
[x,y,z]=xy^\dagger z.
\]

The strongest immediate possibility was:

> Are the 25 minimum code-logical quartets exactly a distinguished subset of the TRO's ternary-composable quartets?

The answer is no at the level of one direct ternary product.

## 2. Exact logical-support enumeration

Let

\[
q_0=q(\nu^c)=(1,1,1,1,1)
\]

and let \(q_a,q_b,q_c\) be three distinct weights among the remaining fifteen. The certificate enumerates every triple for which

\[
q_0=s_aq_a+s_bq_b+s_cq_c,
\qquad s_a,s_b,s_c\in\{\pm1\}.
\]

It recovers exactly 25 supports, independently matching the paper's count.

Representative relations include

\[
q(\nu^c)=q(e^c)+q(u_i^c)-q(d_i^c)
\]

and

\[
q(\nu^c)=q(u_i)+q(u_i^c)-q(\nu).
\]

These are charge-lattice relations. They do not yet assert a local vertex.

## 3. Direct ternary-composition test

Write a matter state as a matrix unit \(E_{ab}\). Then

\[
E_{ab}E_{cd}^\dagger E_{ef}
\]

is nonzero exactly when

\[
b=d,
\qquad
c=e,
\]

in which case the output is \(E_{af}\). Its Cartan weight automatically satisfies

\[
q_{af}=q_{ab}-q_{cd}+q_{ef}.
\]

For each of the 25 four-state supports, the certificate tries every choice of output and every ordering of the other three inputs. The result is

\[
\boxed{
25\text{ minimum logical supports},
\qquad
0\text{ direct ternary-product supports}.}
\]

## 4. Why the mismatch is structural

The completed neutral state is the unique arrow

\[
\nu^c:E\leftarrow D.
\]

Its right endpoint \(D\) is unique in the relevant incidence position. A nonzero product involving four distinct matter states cannot route through that arrow with the source/target matching required by \(xy^\dagger z\). Because every minimum logical quartet contains \(\nu^c\), none can be a single distinct-state TRO product.

The failure is therefore not a numerical accident and not a failure of the five-bit Cartan intertwiner. The weight rule is necessary for ternary composition, but it is not sufficient: the TRO also remembers incidence.

## 5. What this rules out

The following proposed implication is false:

\[
\text{minimum anomaly-code logical}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{one native matter-TRO ternary product}.
\]

Consequently:

- code distance does not directly select the local matter-TRO interaction topology;
- charge neutrality cannot replace source/target composability;
- the exact Cartan/ternary intertwiner does not mean every charge relation is a ternary vertex;
- the 25 logical supports cannot be used as 25 derived local couplings without extra mediator data.

## 6. What remains open

The paper notes that the logical quartets have the charge structure of operators generated by exchange of an \(SO(10)\) \(\mathbf{10}\). That points to the correct next level:

\[
\text{matter bilinear}
\longrightarrow
\text{mediator/linking block}
\longrightarrow
\text{matter bilinear},
\]

not a single matter-only ternary product.

Three higher bridges remain credible:

1. a pair of ternary vertices joined through a Higgs or \(SO(10)\)-\(\mathbf{10}\) mediator;
2. a linking-algebra composite rather than a TRO product internal to the matter subspace;
3. a sum of oriented ternary channels whose net Cartan relation is one of the 25 code logicals.

These possibilities must retain gauge representation, chirality and source/target incidence; equality of total charge is not enough.

## 7. Updated research decision

- **Closed:** all 25 minimum integer logical supports are recovered from the five-bit weights.

- **Closed:** anomaly-code minimality alone does not determine native matter-TRO vertices.

- **Open:** whether the existing Higgs-augmented linking graph realizes any or all 25 supports.
- **Open:** whether a smaller symmetry principle selects a proper physical subset.

## 8. Stop-everything and falsification gates

**Stop-everything trigger:** one canonical mediator representation realizes exactly the 25 code-logical supports, with no additional charge-allowed quartets, through two composable ternary/linking vertices.

**Incidence falsifier:** any claimed direct product must exhibit the matrix-unit endpoint equalities explicitly.

**Interaction falsifier:** a charge relation without Lorentz and gauge contraction data is not yet a local operator.

**Uniqueness falsifier:** if many mediator choices realize arbitrary subsets, the code supplies arithmetic organization but no interaction prediction.
