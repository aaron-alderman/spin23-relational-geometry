# Projector stationarity is the return map, and (Spin(2,3)) belongs to the (2+1)-dimensional defect

## Main results

Two major pieces of the closure problem can be sharpened.

First, the proposed return map need not be appended by hand. If \(\Gamma[P]\) is the effective action obtained after the matter, connection, link, and topological fields are integrated out, then variation over the manifold of rank-one projectors gives the exact stationarity equation

\[
\boxed{
[P,\mathcal R[P]]=0,
\qquad
\mathcal R[P]:=\frac{\delta\Gamma}{\delta P}.
}
\]

When the relevant eigenvalue of \(\mathcal R[P]\) is nondegenerate, this is equivalent to the fixed-point equation

\[
\boxed{
P=\Pi_\lambda(\mathcal R[P]).
}
\]

Thus reflexive closure is the ordinary Euler–Lagrange condition for a dynamical primitive idempotent.

Second, the two Jordan layers acquire a clean dimensional relation. The family projector lives in the four-dimensional bulk, while the level-three BF carrier lives on a \(2+1\)-dimensional defect. The causal Jordan algebra of \(2+1\)-dimensional Minkowski space is

\[
H_2(\mathbb R),
\]

whose conformal group is

\[
\operatorname{Conf}(H_2(\mathbb R))
\cong Sp(4,\mathbb R)
\cong Spin_0(2,3).
\]

Therefore \(Spin(2,3)\) need not be directly identified with the \(H_3(\mathbb C)\) family algebra. It is the natural conformal/causal symmetry of the defect on which the family-derived topological remnant becomes observable.

## 1. Constrained variations of a projector

Let

\[
P^2=P,
\qquad
P=P^\dagger,
\qquad
\operatorname{rank}P=1.
\]

The space of such projectors is \(\mathbb{CP}^2\). A tangent variation cannot be an arbitrary Hermitian matrix because it must preserve \(P^2=P\) to first order. Differentiating gives

\[
P\,\delta P+\delta P\,P=\delta P.
\]

Consequently

\[
P\delta PP=0,
\qquad
Q\delta PQ=0.
\]

Every tangent variation is cross-Peirce and can be written

\[
\delta P=[K,P]
\]

for an anti-Hermitian generator \(K\), modulo generators commuting with \(P\).

This is the variational version of

\[
T_P\mathbb{CP}^2
\cong
\operatorname{Hom}(L,Q).
\]

## 2. Effective response and the closure equation

Let \(\Phi\) denote all fields other than \(P\):

\[
\Phi=(g,\mathcal A,\Psi,S,b,\ldots).
\]

Define the effective action by

\[
e^{-\Gamma[P]}
=
\int \mathcal D\Phi\;e^{-S[P,\Phi]}
\]

in Euclidean notation, or by the corresponding quantum effective action in Lorentzian signature.

Let the Hermitian response operator be

\[
\mathcal R[P]
:=
\frac{\delta\Gamma}{\delta P}.
\]

Then

\[
\delta\Gamma
=\operatorname{Tr}(\mathcal R\,\delta P)
=\operatorname{Tr}(\mathcal R[K,P]).
\]

Using cyclicity,

\[
\delta\Gamma
=\operatorname{Tr}([P,\mathcal R]K).
\]

Since the cross-Peirce component of \(K\) is arbitrary, stationarity requires

\[
\boxed{[P,\mathcal R[P]]=0.}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
Q\mathcal R[P]P=0,
\qquad
P\mathcal R[P]Q=0.
\]

The response generated by the physical state must contain no force pushing the selected line into its complement.

## 3. Spectral fixed-point form

If \(\mathcal R[P]\) has a nondegenerate eigenvalue \(\lambda\) associated with the selected line, then

\[
P=\Pi_\lambda(\mathcal R[P]).
\]

This is precisely the previously proposed return equation, but now it is derived:

\[
\boxed{
P
\longrightarrow
\text{physics conditioned by }P
\longrightarrow
\mathcal R[P]
\longrightarrow
\Pi_\lambda(\mathcal R[P])
=P.
}
\]

Several outcomes are distinguished.

### No selection

If

\[
\mathcal R[P]\propto I,
\]

every projector is stationary. The theory has an unlifted \(\mathbb{CP}^2\) vacuum orbit and associated massless or nearly massless modes.

### Orbit but no family choice

If \(\mathcal R[P]\) is covariant and depends only on \(P\) through

\[
\mathcal R[P]=\alpha P+\beta Q,
\]

then every conjugate rank-one projector is a stationary solution. The action stabilizes the rank but not its orientation in family space.

### Unique stable selection

If external physical data such as Yukawa response break the orbit and give a nondegenerate eigenline, a specific \(P_i\) is selected.

### Inconsistent feedback

If the distinguished eigenspace of \(\mathcal R[P]\) differs from the input \(P\), the assumed configuration is not self-consistent and must flow.

## 4. Stability is an eigenvalue-gap/Hessian condition

Stationarity is not enough. Let \(V:P\mathbb C^3\to Q\mathbb C^3\) represent a tangent variation. For a response that is locally independent of \(P\), the second variation of

\[
\Gamma(P)=\operatorname{Tr}(P\mathcal R)
\]

is controlled by eigenvalue differences between the selected line and complement.

More generally, the Hessian contains both the instantaneous spectral gap and the feedback derivative

\[
D\mathcal R[P].
\]

The stability requirement is positivity on the half-Peirce tangent space:

\[
\delta^2\Gamma[P](V,V)>0
\qquad
\text{for all nonzero }V\in J_{1/2}(P).
\]

In fixed-point language this corresponds to contraction of the return map transverse to the residual symmetry orbit.

Thus the physical mass matrix of the projector/Goldstone modes is the linearized test of reflexive closure.

## 5. A minimal bulk action skeleton

The smallest action capable of realizing the established descent has the schematic form

\[
\begin{aligned}
S_{\mathrm{bulk}}
=\int_{M_4}\Big[&
\frac{f_P^2}{2}\operatorname{Tr}(D P\wedge *DP)
+V_{\mathrm{eff}}(P,\Psi,Y)\\
&+\frac{1}{2e_X^2}f_X\wedge *f_X
+|D_{3a}S|^2+V_S(S,P)\\
&+\mathcal L_{\mathrm{SM}}(\Psi,A_{\mathrm{SM}},g;P,a)
+\cdots
\Big].
\end{aligned}
\]

Here:

- \(a\) is the compact relative connection generated by \(X=I-3P\);
- the fundamental module sees charges \((-2,1,1)\);
- \(S\) lies in an oriented cross-Peirce representation and therefore has charge \(3\);
- \(V_{\mathrm{eff}}\) is not arbitrary once the fermions and Yukawas are specified; its derivative contributes to \(\mathcal R[P]\).

The skeleton is not yet a completed action because the global construction of \(a\) for varying \(P\), and the removal of unwanted projector/link modes, remain to be derived.

## 6. Why a charge-three link produces autonomous BF holonomies

The previous projector-curvature calculation derived a local coefficient three but left the connection composite. The charge-three link provides the more standard path to an autonomous topological gauge field.

Suppose the radial and directional modes of \(S\) are gapped and its remaining phase is \(\theta\). Then

\[
S\sim ve^{i\theta},
\qquad
D S\sim iv(d\theta-3a)e^{i\theta}.
\]

The low-energy phase action is

\[
S_\theta
=\frac{v^2}{2}\int(d\theta-3a)\wedge*(d\theta-3a).
\]

Dualizing the compact phase introduces a compact two-form gauge field \(b\) and produces

\[
S_{\mathrm{IR}}
=\frac{3}{2\pi}\int_{M_4}b\wedge da
+\text{irrelevant kinetic terms}.
\]

This resolves the earlier composite-holonomy problem conditionally:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Peirce geometry derives }q(S)=3,
\quad
\text{the independent }a\text{ supplies all compact holonomies},
\quad
\text{Higgs duality derives level }3.
}
\]

The remaining burden is to derive the independent compatible connection \(a\) without generating the forbidden enlarged family gauge algebra.

## 7. Where \(Spin(2,3)\) belongs

Let \(\Sigma_3\) be the \(2+1\)-dimensional defect or orbifold fixed locus supporting the reduced topological carrier.

A vector \((t,x,y)\in\mathbb R^{2,1}\) can be represented by a real symmetric matrix

\[
Z=
\begin{pmatrix}
t+x&y\\
y&t-x
\end{pmatrix}
\in H_2(\mathbb R),
\]

with determinant

\[
\det Z=t^2-x^2-y^2.
\]

Thus the positive cone of \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) is the causal cone of \(2+1\)-dimensional spacetime. Its conformal group is

\[
\operatorname{Conf}(H_2(\mathbb R))
\cong Sp(4,\mathbb R)
\cong Spin_0(2,3).
\]

This matches the defect dimension exactly. The family Jordan geometry resides in the bulk and produces a finite remnant; the \(Spin(2,3)\) Jordan geometry organizes the causal and conformal observables on the boundary where that remnant is carried.

The bridge is therefore not an equality

\[
H_3(\mathbb C)=H_2(\mathbb R),
\]

which would be false. It is a bulk-to-boundary map:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
H_3(\mathbb C)\text{ family projector geometry in }M_4
&\longrightarrow
\mathbb Z_3\text{ topological response on }\Sigma_3,\\
H_2(\mathbb R)\text{ causal Jordan geometry on }\Sigma_3
&\longrightarrow
Spin_0(2,3)\text{ conformal organization}.
\end{aligned}
}
\]

This gives \(Spin(2,3)\) a precise role without claiming it is literally the family gauge group or the centre of the four-dimensional Standard Model.

## 8. Return to the original stress-energy sandwich

On the defect, let \(T_{ab}\) be the \(2+1\)-dimensional stress-energy tensor. Its generating functional depends on the boundary causal geometry and on the boundary restrictions of the family/topological sources.

The original intuition

\[
A\,X\,B
\]

can now be typed as a response construction in which:

- \(A\) and \(B\) are boundary observable or stress-response insertions organized by the \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) causal cone;
- the middle operation \(\mathbb S_P\) transports the bulk family selection and its faithful module lift to the defect;
- the resulting connected response contributes to \(\mathcal R[P]=\delta\Gamma/\delta P\);
- projector stationarity returns the same \(P\).

Schematically,

\[
\mathcal R[P]
\sim
\int_{\Sigma_3}
\left\langle
T\,\mathbb S_P\,T
\right\rangle_c
+\text{fermion and link response}.
\]

This is not yet a definition—the tensor contractions and source derivatives must be made explicit—but its types are now coherent.

## 9. The remaining closure bottleneck

The programme has reduced the next obstruction to one bundle-theoretic question:

> Can a varying primitive projector \(P\), a faithful fundamental module \(L\oplus Q\), and an independent compact relative connection \(a\) be combined locally and globally without gauging the entire family stabilizer or regenerating the forbidden associative envelope?

If yes, then:

- Peirce geometry derives the charge-three link;
- ordinary Higgs duality derives autonomous level-three BF theory;
- the defect naturally carries the \(Spin(2,3)\) observable geometry;
- the effective projector equation supplies the return map.

If no, the closure programme stops at the connection step.
