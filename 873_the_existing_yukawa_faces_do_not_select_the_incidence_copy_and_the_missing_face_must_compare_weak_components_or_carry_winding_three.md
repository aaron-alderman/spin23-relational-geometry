# The Existing Yukawa Faces Do Not Select the Incidence Copy, and the Missing Face Must Compare Weak Components or Carry Winding Three

**Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Branches:** Furey ribbon faces, local-coefficient selection, Higgs/Yukawa topology, weak-doublet sewing, and factor-three arithmetic  
**Claim status:** exact no-go for ordinary unit-coefficient Yukawa-face flatness; two sharply defined replacement routes remain open

## Executive result

The existing planar Furey/Higgs Yukawa faces cannot supply both constraints needed to select the incidence copy.

After projection to color-augmentation coefficients:

- the up Yukawa triangle supplies exactly the useful condition \(u+u^c=0\);
- the down Yukawa triangle supplies \(d+d^c=0\) in the published path orientation;
- the charged-lepton and neutrino triangles project to zero because all their edges are color singlets.

But the incidence-selected copy obeys

\[
(u,d,u^c,d^c)=(w,w,-w,3w).
\]

Thus

\[
d+d^c=4w\ne0.
\]

More strongly, no orientation choice for an ordinary unit-coefficient down triangle can work:

\[
\pm d\pm d^c=(\pm1\pm3)w\in\{\pm2w,\pm4w\}.
\]

The second selecting face is therefore not the ordinary down Yukawa triangle. It must instead implement one of two equivalent kernel-level conditions:

\[
\boxed{u-d=0}
\]

or

\[
\boxed{3d-d^c=0.}
\]

The first is a primitive seam comparing weak-doublet components. The second is a nonprimitive winding-three face and makes the unexplained coefficient three explicit.

## 1. Projection of the four Yukawa faces

The Higgs-augmented Furey graph has four triangular cycles:

\[
\gamma_u,quad\gamma_d,quad\gamma_e,quad\gamma_\nu.
\]

The color-augmentation coefficient system retains only color-standard components. Higgs, charged-lepton and neutrino edges have zero augmentation component. Therefore

\[
\begin{aligned}
\gamma_u&\longmapsto u+u^c,\\
\gamma_d&\longmapsto d+d^c,\\
\gamma_e&\longmapsto0,\\
\gamma_\nu&\longmapsto0,
\end{aligned}
\]

up to independent overall face orientation signs.

The local cycle equation at the resolved color vertex remains

\[
d^c=u+d-u^c.
\]

Using \((u,d,u^c)\) as coordinates, the two nonzero published face rows are

\[
D_{\rm Yuk}=
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&1\\
1&2&-1
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

## 2. What the published faces actually select

The kernel of \(D_{\rm Yuk}\) is generated by

\[
(u,d,u^c)=(1,-1,-1).
\]

Cycle closure then gives

\[
d^c=1.
\]

Thus the published pair of face-flatness equations would select

\[
\boxed{(u,d,u^c,d^c)=(w,-w,-w,w),}
\]

not the incidence relation \((w,w,-w,3w)\).

The Smith invariants of \(D_{\rm Yuk}\) are

\[
(1,2).
\]

Its cokernel has a \(\mathbb Z/2\) defect. This differs arithmetically as well as geometrically from the primitive selector matrix, whose Smith invariants are \((1,1)\).

## 3. Orientation cannot repair the mismatch

Allow every independent sign choice

\[
\alpha u+\beta u^c,
\qquad
\gamma d+\delta d^c,
\qquad
\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta\in\{\pm1\}.
\]

There are sixteen cases. On the target incidence vector, the down row evaluates to

\[
\gamma+3\delta.
\]

Its possible values are

\[
-4,-2,2,4.
\]

Zero never occurs. The obstruction is therefore independent of how the two triangular faces are oriented.

## 4. Primitive repair: a weak-component seam

Keep the genuine up-face condition

\[
u+u^c=0.
\]

Replace the down triangle by

\[
u-d=0.
\]

The resulting matrix is

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
1&0&1\\
1&-1&0
\end{pmatrix},
\]

which is row-equivalent to the minimal selector of document 872. Its kernel is exactly

\[
(u,d,u^c)=(w,w,-w).
\]

This row compares the two colored components of the weak doublet rather than closing one component with its conjugate singlet. A physical derivation would therefore have to come from weak-doublet transport, Higgs sewing, a charged-current seam, or a Jordan relation tying the two components together.

It cannot be asserted as an unbroken low-energy symmetry: \(u\) and \(d\) have different electric charge and different Yukawa response. The relation would need to live before electroweak breaking, in a dressed response quotient, or as a topological cochain rather than a pointwise field equality.

## 5. Winding-three repair

Alternatively, keep a down-sector face but replace its unit incidence by

\[
3d-d^c=0.
\]

Together with \(u+u^c=0\) and cycle closure, this gives

\[
2(d-u)=0.
\]

Because the integral augmentation ideal is torsion free, \(d=u\), and the incidence copy follows.

This route is algebraically correct but nonprimitive. It asks for a face that traverses the \(d\) edge three times relative to \(d^c\), or an equivalent local-coefficient transfer carrying degree three.

That coefficient could conceivably arise from:

- the three-sheet color cover;
- the group-ring norm \(1+g+g^2\);
- a degree-three covering or wound seam;
- cubic anomaly/Jordan norm data.

No such physical face has yet been derived. The appearance of three is nevertheless now a concrete target rather than a numerological coincidence.

## 6. Consequence for genus-two sewing

The earlier genus-two construction sews the four Yukawa boundary circles in the pairs \((u,e)\) and \((d,\nu)\). That topological pairing remains valid for the color-singlet ribbon.

The present calculation says that those ordinary boundaries do not, after augmentation projection, select the active color-standard copy. The genus-two carrier and the active augmentation selector therefore live at different layers unless an additional weak seam or wound local-coefficient face is added.

This prevents a premature identification:

\[
\text{Yukawa boundary sewing}
\ne
\text{incidence-copy selection}.
\]

They may still become compatible in an enriched ribbon with both ordinary faces and color-local-coefficient seams.

## 7. Stop-everything and falsification criteria

**Weak-seam trigger:** an oriented Furey/Higgs/Jordan construction produces the primitive evaluation \(u-d\) without imposing an unphysical low-energy equality of fields.

**Winding trigger:** a canonical degree-three transfer produces \(3d-d^c\), with the coefficient three fixed by color-cover topology or cubic norm rather than inserted manually.

**Unified trigger:** the same enriched ribbon yields the selecting cochain and the genus-two handle intersection form.

**Negative trigger:** every gauge-typed weak seam is broken by the Higgs/Yukawa structure and every canonical color transfer annihilates rather than multiplies the augmentation ideal.

**Falsifier:** claiming that reorienting the ordinary down Yukawa triangle is sufficient. All sixteen unit-sign possibilities have been exhausted.

## 8. Verdict

One half of the minimal selector is already present:

\[
\gamma_u^{\rm aug}=u+u^c.
\]

The other half is genuinely new. It must compare weak partners or encode winding three:

\[
\boxed{
\gamma_{\rm missing}^{\rm aug}=u-d
\quad\text{or, at kernel level,}\quad
3d-d^c.
}
\]

This is a productive obstruction: it identifies exactly what the existing Furey faces cannot do and what an enriched Jordan/Furey topology must contribute.
