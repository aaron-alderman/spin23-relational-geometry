## Executive result

The explicit six-cycle from [checkpoint 04](frontier_parallel_topology_04_degree_three_seam_calculus_and_junk.md) does not acquire a safe physical seam merely by replacing “ordinary form” with “mixed DGA” or “superconnection.”

Let

\[
x=E_{PN,PS},
\qquad
y=E_{PS,NS},
\qquad
z=E_{NS,NP}.
\]

Their associative represented product is

\[
\boxed{xyz=E_{PN,NP}.}
\]

The right-hand side is exactly the reversed-anchor operator that the original null mask forbids as a direct degree-one edge. Mediation changes the factorization, but it does not change the endpoint representation type.

This gives a minimal dichotomy:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{retain the mixed word}&\Longrightarrow\text{retain anchor mixing},\\
\text{quotient the anchor map}&\Longrightarrow\text{kill the Möbius seam}.
\end{array}}
\]

The minimal Quillen/holonomy reading does not evade the result. The open half-cycle is endpoint covariant rather than gauge invariant and has the same gauge character as the forbidden direct map. Its Hermitian completion is an odd Dirac-type anchor block. Closing the path with its KO-real partner gives

\[
abc\,\bar a\bar b\bar c=|abc|^2,
\]

a positive orientation-even Wilson loop.

Therefore the smallest mixed DGA and smallest KO-real superconnection alternatives are closed. A more elaborate transport representation remains logically possible, but it needs new type data not present in the current module and is now pinned as an explicit underdetermination rather than an active calculation on existing data.

## 1. The endpoint-type theorem

The forward elementary edges have the established types

\[
PN\overset{R}{\longrightarrow}PS
\overset{L}{\longrightarrow}NS
\overset{R}{\longrightarrow}NP.
\]

In the concrete representation their matrix units compose uniquely:

\[
E_{ij}E_{jk}=E_{ik}.
\]

Consequently

\[
E_{PN,PS}E_{PS,NS}E_{NS,NP}=E_{PN,NP}.
\]

Let \(P_D=P_{PN}\) and \(P_M=P_{NP}\) denote the two anchor projectors. Then

\[
P_D(xyz)P_M=xyz.
\]

There is no nonzero component orthogonal to the forbidden anchor block. The represented seam space is one dimensional.

Hence any linear projection \(Q\) satisfying

\[
Q(E_{PN,NP})=0
\]

also satisfies

\[
Q(xyz)=0.
\]

To retain “transport” while erasing its operator projection, the theory must introduce a second representation of the formal path distinct from multiplication in \(\operatorname{End}(H)\). That distinction is additional structure.

## 2. Gauge typing is unchanged by mediation

Under a diagonal change of local frames, an elementary path telescopes. The open product transforms as

\[
xyz\longmapsto u_{PN}(xyz)u_{NP}^{-1}.
\]

This is exactly the transformation law of a direct \(PN\to NP\) block. Intermediate phases cancel.

Thus mediation does not turn the seam into a gauge scalar. It remains an endpoint-covariant operator carrying the forbidden reversal type.

This is the finite-geometry analogue of an elementary fact about Wilson lines: an open Wilson line transforms at its endpoints. Only a closed loop is gauge invariant.

## 3. Two mixed completions with identical degree-one data

The existing module structure supplies the left and right degree-one bimodules. It does not specify their mixed higher products or mixed junk ideal.

Two completions agree on all present degree-one data:

### Free mixed tensor completion

Take the tensor algebra generated by \(\Omega_L^1\oplus\Omega_R^1\), with the displayed matrix representation. The word \(RLR\) survives and maps to \(E_{PN,NP}\).

This completion realizes the seam but violates anchor protection at the represented operator level.

### Differential-ideal quotient completion

Quotient by the differential graded ideal generated by the \(RLR\) word and its \(J\)-adjoint. Because the generator begins in degree three, the degree-one module is unchanged. The anchor mask is preserved, but the seam vanishes.

Therefore:

\[
\boxed{
\text{the degree-one module and KO data do not determine mixed degree three.}
}
\]

Choosing retention or deletion is an extra axiom unless an action, universality property or physical representation selects it.

## 4. The minimal superconnection test

Define the Hermitian open-seam insertion

\[
S=E_{PN,NP}+E_{NP,PN}.
\]

It obeys

\[
\gamma S=-S\gamma,
\]

so it is an odd endomorphism of precisely the type that enters a finite Dirac operator. Moreover,

\[
S^2=P_{PN}+P_{NP}.
\]

Thus the choices are:

- include \(S\): restore the forbidden direct anchor block as an effective Dirac coupling;
- retain only \(S^2\): obtain an even endpoint population projector with no orientation sign.

The anti-Hermitian orientation combination

\[
K=E_{PN,NP}-E_{NP,PN}
\]

has

\[
K^2=-(P_{PN}+P_{NP}).
\]

Its square likewise forgets orientation.

The superconnection vocabulary therefore supplies no third outcome in the minimal representation.

## 5. KO reality trivializes the closed-loop phase

Let the first-half edge weights be \(a,b,c\). The real structure and \(JD=DJ\) fix the partner-half weights to their complex conjugates in the explicit witness.

The full six-cycle Wilson product is

\[
W=a b c\,\bar a\bar b\bar c
=|abc|^2.
\]

It is:

- gauge invariant;
- real;
- nonnegative;
- unchanged by orientation reversal.

The only gauge-invariant loop in the minimal cycle therefore loses the phase that might have distinguished the two orientations.

The open half-product retains phase information, but is not gauge invariant and is the forbidden anchor map. The closed product is safe, but orientation blind.

## 7. What would genuinely reopen the branch

A reopening must add at least one datum not present in the current finite module:

1. a transport Hilbert space distinct from the matter Hilbert space;
2. a central extension whose loop phase is not paired to its conjugate by \(J\);
3. a higher categorical representation in which the formal path is nonzero while its matter-endomorphism shadow vanishes;
4. a boundary action making the open endpoint transformation physical rather than gauge redundant;
5. a protected interference observable comparing two independent paths before they collapse to the same matrix unit.

Each proposal must explain why the new datum is derived rather than installed to preserve the desired seam.
