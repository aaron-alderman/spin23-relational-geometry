# Does the Weak Real Form Define Electroweak Breaking? A Stabilizer Obstruction

**Date:** 17 October 2026  
**Branches:** B01, B03 and B11  
**Claim status:** exact infinitesimal stabilizer calculation; physical Higgs realization remains open

## 1. The question forced by the middle rung

The Jordan inclusions

\[
H_2(\mathbb C)\supset H_2(\mathbb R)\supset\mathbb R\oplus\mathbb R
\]

are exact. That does not yet mean they form a symmetry-breaking sequence. A physical intermediate must be preserved by a definite subgroup of the weak symmetry, and that subgroup must relate consistently to the final electromagnetic \(U(1)\).

Write a Hermitian matrix in the Pauli basis:

\[
X=tI+x\sigma_1+y\sigma_2+z\sigma_3.
\]

Then

\[
H_2(\mathbb R)=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}
\{I,\sigma_1,\sigma_3\}
\]

is the plane \(y=0\), while the chosen diagonal algebra is

\[
\mathbb R\oplus\mathbb R
=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb R}\{I,\sigma_3\}.
\]

## 2. Infinitesimal weak action

Conjugation by \(SU(2)\) acts on the traceless Pauli vector \(\mathbf r=(x,y,z)\) through the ordinary \(SO(3)\) rotation. Infinitesimally,

\[
\delta\mathbf r=\boldsymbol\omega\times\mathbf r.
\]

Preserving the plane \(y=0\) for every vector in that plane forces

\[
\boldsymbol\omega\parallel\mathbf e_y.
\]

Hence the connected stabilizer of \(H_2(\mathbb R)\) inside the adjoint weak action is the one-parameter subgroup generated by \(T_2\).

Preserving the diagonal line \(\mathbb R\sigma_3\) instead forces

\[
\boldsymbol\omega\parallel\mathbf e_z,
\]

so its connected stabilizer is generated by \(T_3\). The two Lie algebras have zero intersection:

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak{stab}_{\mathfrak{su}(2)}H_2(\mathbb R)
\cap
\mathfrak{stab}_{\mathfrak{su}(2)}(\mathbb R\oplus\mathbb R)
=0.
}
\]

## 3. Why this is uncomfortable

The algebra inclusions are nested, but their connected stabilizers are not. The first restriction chooses a normal to a real plane in Pauli space; the second chooses an axis lying inside that plane. Rotations about the plane normal move the final axis, while rotations about the final axis mix the retained and discarded off-diagonal directions.

Therefore the sequence

\[
H_2(\mathbb C)\to H_2(\mathbb R)\to\mathbb R\oplus\mathbb R
\]

does **not**, by itself, implement a nested chain \(SU(2)\to U(1)\to U(1)\) under ordinary conjugation.

The central hypercharge phase also acts trivially by conjugation on the \(2\times2\) observable block, so simply appending \(U(1)_Y\) does not repair this adjoint-level mismatch. On particle states the combined generator \(Q=T_3+Y\) acts nontrivially, but that is a different representation and must be built explicitly.

## 4. What survives

The \(Spin(2,3)\) middle rung remains an exact Jordan/conformal intermediate. What fails is the strongest easy interpretation: it cannot be declared an intermediate electroweak vacuum merely from the inclusions.

Three possibilities remain open:

1. the real form is selected by a discrete antiunitary symmetry such as a CP-like operation, not by a continuous weak stabilizer;
2. the physical group acts on a larger state/operator module, where hypercharge and the Higgs intertwine the two stabilizers;
3. the middle rung belongs to the external response geometry and only couples to the weak block without being a stage of electroweak breaking.

The third option is currently the least assumption-heavy.

## 5. CP and basis dependence

Entrywise complex conjugation flips the \(\sigma_2\) coordinate and fixes \(H_2(\mathbb R)\). This looks CP-like, but a matrix real structure is basis-dependent until tied to a physical antiunitary operation.

The Standard Model also contains physical CP violation. A universal restriction to real observables must therefore explain whether it applies only to the bosonic weak response block, complex phases re-enter through flavor/Yukawa intertwiners, the real form is approximate or spontaneously violated, or the identification is incompatible with observed CP violation.

## 6. Revised stop-everything test

**Positive:** a Higgs or antiunitary selector on the full particle module fixes the same \(H_2(\mathbb R)\), leaves \(Q=T_3+Y\) unbroken, and reproduces the observed mass and CP structure.

**Negative:** the real form can be moved arbitrarily by weak basis changes and no covariant field or antiunitary symmetry selects it.

**Current result:** the bare adjoint weak action supplies an obstruction, not the desired derivation.
