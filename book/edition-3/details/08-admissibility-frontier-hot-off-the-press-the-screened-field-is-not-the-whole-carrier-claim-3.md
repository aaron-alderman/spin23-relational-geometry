# Hot off the press — the screened field is not the whole carrier — assertion 3

## Exact assertion

> In the minimal local scalar completion of a static kernel \(1/(q^2+\kappa^2)\), the electric-gradient share of mode energy is \(q^2/(q^2+\kappa^2)\), while the non-gradient screening sector holds \(\kappa^2/(q^2+\kappa^2)\). At \(q\ll\kappa\), adding Maxwell \(E^2\) alone omits almost all the energy in that completion. A physical model must identify the sector represented by the \(\kappa\) term and give it dynamics, energy and flux. The look-here calculation is to build matched finite-speed and explicit-screening completions, recover the same Ewald kernel, and compare total KMS transport under one order of limits.

## What this commits us to

Screening must be represented by a physical sector with its own stored energy, noise and flux. The displayed fractions belong to the declared minimal local completion; a phenomenological denominator alone is not a complete transport model.

## What would move or defeat this claim

Specify the polarization field or medium, derive its energy current and KMS noise, integrate it out, and reproduce the screened matter kernel before taking the dc and long-wavelength limits.
