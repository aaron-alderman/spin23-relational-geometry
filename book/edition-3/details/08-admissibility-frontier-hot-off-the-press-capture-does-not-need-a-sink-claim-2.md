## Exact assertion

> Backward evolution of the complete final state reconstructs the initial excited state, showing that the apparent capture is coherent redistribution, not a concealed absorber. The finite-temperature upward/downward factors reproduce the exact Boltzmann ratio. A source-shaped unitary environment can therefore produce a long capture window without a Markov sink or a freely drawn spectral envelope.

## What this commits us to

Population transfer must be accompanied by energy, norm, complex-state reversal and detailed-balance checks. A plateau shorter than the recurrence time is not automatically permanent capture.

## What would move or defeat this claim

Demonstrate convergence of the pre-recurrence plateau as the rail approaches a continuum, or show that recurrence or a bound state of the coupled Hamiltonian retains a nonvanishing excited fraction.
