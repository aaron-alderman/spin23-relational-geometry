## Exact assertion

> The overall bond-strain matrix element is still declared rather than evaluated from the AZ-055 bound and scattering states, and a finite harmonic rail ultimately recurs. The look-here calculation is to compute that matrix element from one bond-modulation operator, attach it to an infinite or converged rail, and demand a rail-size-stable plateau and emitted spectrum across gap and temperature.

## What this commits us to

The mechanism becomes source-native only when the operator, state normalization, material scale and continuum limit are all explicit.

## What would move or defeat this claim

Compute the normalized scattering-to-bound transition form factor and show that the gap lies inside a coupled phonon band with nonzero spectral weight.
