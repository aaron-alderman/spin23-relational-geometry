# The Furey interface

_A real operator bridge with a hard boundary_

## Why Furey enters

Furey's division-algebra programme provides chiral ideals, particle representations, ladder operations and a Jordan hierarchy. In the present carrier, a continuous Furey product rotation acts as the missing channel generator needed for full sp(4,R) closure.

This is stronger than analogy: it is an explicit operator participation in a declared finite-dimensional model.

## Why it is not yet unification

The operator remains effective. Its microscopic origin, charge completion and relationship to the full division-algebra construction are not derived. The result says that a Furey-shaped channel flow can close the algebra—not that Furey's programme has derived spacetime or that Spin(2,3) has derived the Standard Model.
