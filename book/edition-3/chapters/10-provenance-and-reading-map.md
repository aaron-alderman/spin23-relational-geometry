## How to challenge a claim

> **Section status — [▦ Ledger / governance](../reading-key/status-governance.md)** — Preserves provenance, status, stopping rules and navigation.

> **Research move — [⌕ Auditor](../reading-key/move-auditor.md)** — checks assumptions, certificates, counterexamples and stopping rules.

Start with the claim's boundary sentence, then open its controlling source and executable verifier. Check whether the mathematical object is the same on both sides of every arrow: the same field space, symmetry action, domain and normalization. Many attractive false bridges change one of those silently.

> **Expanded claim page →** [Inspect the claim’s scope, logic, verification route, boundary and sources](../details/10-provenance-and-reading-map-how-to-challenge-a-claim-claim-1.md)

For a physical claim, also ask what prepared the state, what the measuring port couples to, which limits are taken and whether the quoted response belongs to the stated dynamics. The QWZ audit is the model example: the numerical quadrature was correct, but the stationary state of the written bath made the claimed response vanish.

> **Expanded claim page →** [Inspect the claim’s scope, logic, verification route, boundary and sources](../details/10-provenance-and-reading-map-how-to-challenge-a-claim-claim-2.md)
