## What a representation does

> **Section status — [◎ Interpretive map](../reading-key/status-interpretive.md)** — Organizes thought or workflow but is not evidence for a physical claim.

> **Research move — [⇄ Translator](../reading-key/move-translator.md)** — moves a structure between mathematical, physical or ordinary languages.

A representation is a way for an abstract symmetry to act on a concrete list of states. The familiar example is rotating an arrow by multiplying its coordinate vector by a matrix. Furey's division-algebra constructions similarly organize particle-like states and ladder operations using complex numbers, quaternions and octonions.
