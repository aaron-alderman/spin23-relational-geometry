# The revised spine

_Response data to Spin(2,3)_

> response data → H₂(R) → Peirce–Schur triple → TKK generators → sp(4,R) ≅ spin(2,3)

## What closes

The programme no longer reaches Spin(2,3) only from a top-down list of low-rank coincidences. Positive two-face response data supplies H₂(R); Schur elimination becomes a Jordan-triple operation; Tits–Kantor–Koecher prolongation supplies the graded generators; and the resulting ten-dimensional real Lie algebra is sp(4,R).

The global low-rank isomorphism then identifies Sp(4,R) with Spin₀(2,3). This is the firm mathematical middle rung.

## What remains open

The derivation is conditional on the carrier and generator typing. Nature has not yet selected the action, state, vacuum, quantum measure or observed quantity that would make this algebra the dynamical symmetry of a physical system.
