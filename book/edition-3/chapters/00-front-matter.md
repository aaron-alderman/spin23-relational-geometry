## Scope and method

> **Section status — [▦ Ledger / governance](../reading-key/status-governance.md)** — Preserves provenance, status, stopping rules and navigation.

> **Research move — [▤ Steward](../reading-key/move-steward.md)** — preserves provenance, memory, status and continuity.

This book asks how positive composition, typed carriers, transformation, dynamics and observation can be held in one research architecture without being declared identical. It begins from ordinary measuring ideas, states each mathematical object and then follows only the bridges for which a map or explicit test is available.

> **Expanded claim page →** [Inspect the claim’s scope, logic, verification route, boundary and sources](../details/00-front-matter-scope-and-method-claim-1.md)

The labels established, conditional, obstruction and open are part of the result. A beautiful chain of mathematics is not converted into a physical theory by typography or narrative momentum.

<a id="a-book-of-layers"></a>
## A book of layers

> **Section status — [◎ Interpretive map](../reading-key/status-interpretive.md)** — Organizes thought or workflow but is not evidence for a physical claim.

> **Research move — [⇄ Translator](../reading-key/move-translator.md)** — moves a structure between mathematical, physical or ordinary languages.

A ruler can tell you the length of a table without telling you why the table exists. A compass can preserve a direction without choosing which direction should count as north. Much of this programme has the same layered character. It contains exact measuring structures, conditional mechanisms and proposed physical interpretations. The reader should not flatten those layers into one claim.

> **Expanded claim page →** [Inspect the claim’s scope, logic, verification route, boundary and sources](../details/00-front-matter-a-book-of-layers-claim-1.md)

Each chapter therefore moves in the same order: ordinary picture, defined object, exact result, and authority boundary. A term such as symplectic is introduced only after the familiar idea it formalizes: preserving oriented area in a phase-space plane. A term such as chirality is introduced only after the familiar idea: left- and right-handed patterns cannot always be superposed by rotation.

> **Expanded claim page →** [Inspect the claim’s scope, logic, verification route, boundary and sources](../details/00-front-matter-a-book-of-layers-claim-2.md)

<a id="four-labels-used-throughout"></a>
## Four labels used throughout

> **Section status — [▦ Ledger / governance](../reading-key/status-governance.md)** — Preserves provenance, status, stopping rules and navigation.

> **Research move — [▤ Steward](../reading-key/move-steward.md)** — preserves provenance, memory, status and continuity.

Established means that a calculation follows from its written assumptions and has an executable or inspectable certificate. Conditional means that a construction works after a named field, coefficient, boundary condition or control is supplied. Externally open means that the missing ingredient is not yet supplied by the declared model or evidence. Internally exhausted means that further rearrangement of the same ingredients no longer addresses the missing datum.

> **Expanded claim page →** [Inspect the claim’s scope, logic, verification route, boundary and sources](../details/00-front-matter-four-labels-used-throughout-claim-1.md)

None of these labels means true of nature. Even an exact theorem can describe the wrong physical system. Conversely, a blocked physical programme can contain useful mathematics, simulation tools and experimentally transferable methods.

> **Expanded claim page →** [Inspect the claim’s scope, logic, verification route, boundary and sources](../details/00-front-matter-four-labels-used-throughout-claim-2.md)

<a id="the-route-through-the-book"></a>
## The route through the book

> **Section status — [▦ Ledger / governance](../reading-key/status-governance.md)** — Preserves provenance, status, stopping rules and navigation.

> **Research move — [⌖ Cartographer](../reading-key/move-cartographer.md)** — maps territory, distinctions, dependencies and interfaces.

The admissibility frontier comes first because it identifies the changed route to discovery: recover distinctions that ordinary measurements erase, then trace their admission, dynamics and detection. The following chapters build one exact Jordan-to-Lie rank-two hinge from response measurements and relational loops before asking whether it can carry Standard-Model matter, safe interactions or exceptional structure. The result ledger records positive findings and reversals; the research frontier ends in controlled experiments and explicit reopening conditions rather than in a larger symmetry slogan.

## Things before names

> **Research move — [▤ Steward](../reading-key/move-steward.md)** — preserves provenance, memory, status and continuity.

Begin with the preparation, response table, path, boundary, update or detector record before introducing the algebraic name that compresses it. Show what can be done to the thing and what remains unchanged. Technical language then becomes earned precision rather than an entrance fee.

> **Expanded claim page →** [Inspect the claim’s scope, logic, verification route, boundary and sources](../details/00-front-matter-things-before-names-claim-1.md)

For example, two coupled readouts and their nonnegative response come before H₂(ℝ); eliminating a hidden component comes before the Schur complement and Jordan triple; transport around a loop and a changed return come before holonomy.

<a id="every-bridge-carries-a-reason"></a>
## Every bridge carries a reason

> **Research move — [▤ Steward](../reading-key/move-steward.md)** — preserves provenance, memory, status and continuity.

Two technical nouns placed beside one another do not explain their relationship. Every material bridge must say why the things are alike, why they differ, what produces the difference, whether its mathematical and physical parts are open or closed, what follows, what does not follow and which calculation or observation would decide the remainder.

> **Expanded claim page →** [Inspect the claim’s scope, logic, verification route, boundary and sources](../details/00-front-matter-every-bridge-carries-a-reason-claim-1.md)

The compact grammar is because—but—therefore—still open. An unlabeled arrow, shared name, repeated number or similar diagram is a prompt to investigate, not a completed bridge.

> **Expanded claim page →** [Inspect the claim’s scope, logic, verification route, boundary and sources](../details/00-front-matter-every-bridge-carries-a-reason-claim-2.md)

## Pictures and speculation are optional

> **Research move — [▤ Steward](../reading-key/move-steward.md)** — preserves provenance, memory, status and continuity.

Landscape, grammar, network, workshop, ecological, mythic and theological pictures may be offered after the reason-bearing core. They form a menu, not a ladder of belief: a reader may use the theorem or experiment while rejecting every optional interpretation.

> **Expanded claim page →** [Inspect the claim’s scope, logic, verification route, boundary and sources](../details/00-front-matter-pictures-and-speculation-are-optional-claim-1.md)

A picture must pass deletion and substitution tests. Removing it or replacing it with another picture must leave the typed objects, reason, status, implication and test unchanged; otherwise it carries a hidden premise.

> **Expanded claim page →** [Inspect the claim’s scope, logic, verification route, boundary and sources](../details/00-front-matter-pictures-and-speculation-are-optional-claim-2.md)
