## A compact vocabulary

> **Section status — [▦ Ledger / governance](../reading-key/status-governance.md)** — Preserves provenance, status, stopping rules and navigation.

> **Research move — [⇄ Translator](../reading-key/move-translator.md)** — moves a structure between mathematical, physical or ordinary languages.

Carrier: the concrete space on which transformations act. Selector: a physical rule that chooses one option from a symmetric family. Domain: the boundary and regularity conditions that make an operator well defined. Residue: the strength with which a mode appears in a response. Anomaly: an obstruction that appears when a classical symmetry is required to survive quantization.

> **Expanded claim page →** [Inspect the claim’s scope, logic, verification route, boundary and sources](../details/11-concept-atlas-a-compact-vocabulary-claim-1.md)

These words name different jobs. A carrier does not select itself; a positive spectrum does not define a boundary domain; an index does not cancel an anomaly; and an experimental sensitivity does not predict a residue.
