# Concept atlas

_The programme as a navigable typed graph_

The interactive atlas renders the programme as a three-dimensional typed graph. Its central chain is the established mathematical spine; physical realization, projective topology and the exceptional horizon remain spatially and epistemically distinct.

## Epistemic rule

A path inherits its weakest edge status. In particular, an analogy does not become a derivation because it sits near an established node.
