# The exceptional horizon

_Jordan, Leech and Monster without overclaim_

## What the local result changes

The H₂(R) construction makes the Spin(2,3) middle rung concrete. It clarifies how response geometry, causal positivity and relational composition can meet.

## What it does not collapse

J₃(O), E₆, E₈, the Golay code, the Leech lattice, Conway symmetry and moonshine remain distinct structures with distinct jobs. The Leech pair-Gram construction reaches H₂(R) and genus-two symplectic geometry, but needs an orientation twist and affine theta shift. No group-name chain substitutes for a carrier-preserving map.
