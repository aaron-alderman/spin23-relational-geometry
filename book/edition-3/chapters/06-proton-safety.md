# Proton safety, Pati–Salam and triality

_Selection is the physics_

## Complete multiplets are not automatically safe

Unified representations carry wanted and unwanted channels together. The proton-safe projector exists only after reducing the colour–lepton symmetry that made the complete multiplet attractive in the first place.

## The surviving route

A finite Pati–Salam reconstruction supplies a concrete source split, while an all-orders rank-one normal form protects part of it. The simplest U(1) and universal Z₂ protections fail. The first surviving family-universal cyclic witness is a Z₉ lift whose faithful low-energy quotient is baryon triality.

This is a live conditional route, not closure: global anomalies, heavy thresholds, the full finite triple and the third edge remain decisive gates.
