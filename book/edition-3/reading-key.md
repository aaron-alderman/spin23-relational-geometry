# Reading-key index

_A front door to one page per status and one page per research move._

The two badge families answer different questions. A **status** asks how far a claim has been established. A **research move** asks what the passage is doing—mapping, building, auditing, translating or another temporary function. Neither badge is evidence by itself; open its dedicated page to see the definition, reading rule and every current use.

## Claim-status pages

- **[✓ Established](reading-key/status-established.md)** — Follows from declared assumptions and has an inspectable certificate.
- **[◇ Conditional](reading-key/status-conditional.md)** — Works after a named field, coefficient, boundary condition or control is supplied.
- **[↗ Externally open](reading-key/status-external.md)** — Needs an ingredient, calculation or observation not contained in the current corpus.
- **[■ Internally exhausted](reading-key/status-exhausted.md)** — Rearranging the present ingredients no longer addresses the missing datum.
- **[⊘ Obstruction / no-go](reading-key/status-obstruction.md)** — A stated candidate class or identification has been ruled out under explicit assumptions.
- **[⌁ Experiment / protocol](reading-key/status-experiment.md)** — Defines a measurement, sensitivity calculation or empirical decision rule.
- **[◎ Interpretive map](reading-key/status-interpretive.md)** — Organizes thought or workflow but is not evidence for a physical claim.
- **[▦ Ledger / governance](reading-key/status-governance.md)** — Preserves provenance, status, stopping rules and navigation.

## Research-move pages

- **[✦ Scout](reading-key/move-scout.md)** — opens a plausible new route or searches beyond the present ingredients.
- **[⌖ Cartographer](reading-key/move-cartographer.md)** — maps territory, distinctions, dependencies and interfaces.
- **[⌕ Auditor](reading-key/move-auditor.md)** — checks assumptions, certificates, counterexamples and stopping rules.
- **[⚒ Builder](reading-key/move-builder.md)** — constructs a model, mechanism, calculation, protocol or intervention.
- **[◉ Witness](reading-key/move-witness.md)** — retains observation, preparation history, stakes and situated evidence.
- **[▤ Steward](reading-key/move-steward.md)** — preserves provenance, memory, status and continuity.
- **[⇄ Translator](reading-key/move-translator.md)** — moves a structure between mathematical, physical or ordinary languages.
- **[⊕ Integrator](reading-key/move-integrator.md)** — joins several typed results while letting the weakest edge set the claim.
