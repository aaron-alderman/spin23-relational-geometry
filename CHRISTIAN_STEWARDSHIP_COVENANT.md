# Christian Stewardship Covenant

## Purpose

This work is offered in a spirit of truth-seeking, humility, service and shared stewardship.

We ask those who study, reuse or extend it to:

- seek truth rather than prestige, certainty or rhetorical advantage;
- distinguish established results, model-local calculations, conditional constructions, conjectures, analogies and failed tests;
- credit sources and contributors faithfully;
- disclose modifications and material disagreements clearly;
- correct errors openly and preserve an auditable history of those corrections;
- treat collaborators, critics and affected communities with dignity;
- resist deception, exploitation, dehumanization and claims that outrun the evidence;
- share useful improvements with generosity;
- remain conscious that scientific insight confers responsibility rather than ownership over other people.

The covenant is inspired by Christian commitments to love of God and neighbour, truthful witness, care for the common good, humility about human knowledge and faithful stewardship of gifts.

## Legal status

This covenant is a voluntary statement of purpose and conduct. It is **not a license condition**, an acceptable-use restriction or an additional legal term. The permissions and obligations governing reuse are stated in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md). No one loses those permissions merely by declining or failing to follow this covenant.

Keeping the covenant nonbinding is deliberate: conscience cannot be manufactured by a copyright clause, and an ambiguous religious or moral restriction would undermine the clarity and equal availability of the open licenses.
