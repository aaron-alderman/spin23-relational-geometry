# Reflection checkpoint — Herglotz convergence campaign

## Claim and scope

The campaign claims only that three bounded, source-grounded routes can be mapped into a common causal-passive two-face response interface accepted by the admitted scientific derivation composer. It does not claim physical equivalence, a microscopic coupling, semantic completeness, novelty in the external literature, or empirical confirmation.

Basis: Claimipedia Outcome Dynamics source ledger snapshot `6b7c7f…7542`, compiled graph `5b7ad6f6…c575`, live docket `08d98c40…54dd`, and verification receipt `6039b529…6d34`.

These are executable checks of bounded models and implementation behavior. They are not a proof of physical correspondence among the models.

## Strongest counterexample

The convergence may be vacuous because every route reaches the target only after discarding the very decorations that carry physical meaning. This counterexample is not hypothetical: every live candidate reports nonempty information loss. The result therefore supports a common implementation seam, while directly refusing an unqualified unification interpretation.

## Meaning and surface-area audit

The compiler adds a reviewed extraction manifest and generated graph rather than a second claim ontology. Its triage packet names existing `EpistemicClaim` and `EpistemicFrontier` meanings. However, “decorated Herglotz interface” and “support-stratified sewing” are new proposed scientific/computational meanings and have not yet been collapsed into the admitted common grammar.

The implementation adds campaign-specific code and data. It demonstrates reusable source binding, ranking and verification patterns but does not yet prove net production simplification.

## Open frontiers

- A decoration-preserving composition algebra is not implemented or proved.
- The wall model lacks full gauge/spinor/Standard-Model completion.
- The thermal route lacks a microscopic Standard Model correlator with all normalizations fixed.
- The synthesis certificate is only a finite contract witness; a general analytic patch/residue theorem and its full executable representation remain open.
- External-literature novelty was not assessed.
- The triage packet is reviewable but has not been promoted into the Claimipedia corpus.

## Disposition

Retain D-01 through D-03 for Claimipedia Triage and retain D-04 as a no-go guard. The evidence is sufficient for a bounded discovery docket and insufficient for corpus promotion or a physical-unification claim.
